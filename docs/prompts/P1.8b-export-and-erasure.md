# P1.8b — Privacy II: "download my data", account deletion and ward erasure

The second half of P1.8 (the first is
[`P1.8a-consents-leaving-retention.md`](P1.8a-consents-leaving-retention.md)).
Anyone downloads everything the system holds about them and their children
as a file within minutes; a person deletes their account and is anonymised
everywhere, permanently, also after a restore; a full guardian erases a
child without a login. The decisions are in ADR 0041 (written in P1.8a,
extended here).

## Context
Read first: `CLAUDE.md`, P1.8a and everything it lists, ADR 0041,
`docs/security.md` (member register exports), ADR 0018 (handing over the
last manager role), ADR 0028 (mobile app: account deletion inside the app).

What exists already (after P1.8a):
- `privacy.erase_person` (0020) covers every table up to P1.8a, including
  events (registrations and sealed sensitive answers), notifications
  (notices, deliveries, push devices, `about_person_ids`), join requests,
  waiting lists, guardianship notes, invitations and consents; the
  deletion ledger and its replay after restores (ADR 0030).
- The worker's retention handle (`deps.retentionDb`,
  `DATABASE_RETENTION_URL`) and `privacy:retention`, which erases orphaned
  people without login after `ORPHAN_RETENTION_MONTHS`.
- Leaving a club (`POST /me/clubs/:clubId/leave`) with its blockers:
  `last_club_manager`, `outstanding_invoices` (the `OutstandingObligations`
  providers; none registered yet) and `roles_must_be_removed`.
- `/me/privacy` on the web and "Privacy" in the app's Settings (consents,
  leaving, policies).
- **Export pattern to copy (P1.2):** `member_exports` with an insert
  trigger queueing `members:export`, which writes to object storage
  (`exports/<club_id>/<id>.csv`, the `ObjectStorage` of
  `@clubadmin/server-kit`) in the requester's language; download through a
  pre-signed link valid five minutes for the requester only; a bucket
  lifecycle rule deletes `exports/` after two days as a backstop.
- **Sessions and identity:** `SessionsService` revokes BFF sessions in
  Valkey, ends Zitadel sessions (the `clubadmin-api` machine user) and
  deny-lists mobile sessions; revoking a mobile session removes its push
  device (P1.7). Nothing deletes a Zitadel user yet.
- **Notifications (ADR 0039)** for every account-bound message; the
  request budgets and cache-first rules of ADR 0040.
- Next free migration: 0021.

## Goal
A member downloads everything about themselves and their children in a
readable and a machine-readable form, ready within minutes. A person
deletes their account with a few days to change their mind; afterwards
nothing identifying remains, not even in a restored backup. A full
guardian erases a child without a login. Neither is possible while the
person still has club roles to hand over or invoices to settle.

## Users and roles involved
Members with their own login; full guardians with `act_on_behalf` (export
and erasure of wards); other full guardians of a ward (told and may
cancel); the worker as system and as `clubadmin_retention`; Zitadel.

## User stories
- As a member, I want to download everything about me (and my children),
  so that I can check it or take it elsewhere.
- As a person, I want to delete my account, with a few days to change my
  mind, and afterwards nothing that identifies me should remain.
- As a person with a club role, I want to be told which roles to hand over
  before I can delete my account.
- As a full guardian, I want to erase my child (without a login) from the
  system when we stop using it, and the other parent should be told and
  able to stop it.

## Data model
- **`data_exports` (global):** `subject_person_id`,
  `requested_by_person_id`, `status` (`queued`, `ready`, `failed`,
  `expired`), `object_key` (`exports/people/<id>.zip`), `size_bytes`,
  `locale`, `expires_at` (seven days after ready), `error_code`. RLS:
  SELECT and INSERT for the requester (subject self or a ward per
  `app.is_full_guardian_of`), `app.is_system()` for the worker. Insert
  trigger `app.enqueue_data_export` queues `privacy:export`. Index
  `(requested_by_person_id, created_at DESC)`. History. Rows kept 12
  months as the record that an access request was answered.
- **`erasure_requests` (global):** `person_id`, `requested_by_person_id`,
  `kind` (`account_deletion`, `ward_erasure`), `status` (`scheduled`,
  `cancelled`, `running`, `done`, `failed`), `scheduled_for`,
  `include_ward_ids` (uuid[]), `cancelled_by_person_id`, `cancelled_at`,
  `done_at`, `error_code`. One open request per person (partial unique
  index on `scheduled`/`running`); index `(scheduled_for) WHERE status =
  'scheduled'`. RLS: the person and their full guardians; system. IDs
  only. History. Kept 24 months after `done_at` (the ledger keeps the
  fact).
- `privacy.erase_person` (CREATE OR REPLACE in 0021 if the export rows or
  files need it): deletes the person's `data_exports` rows; files are
  deleted by the job (object storage is not reachable from SQL).

## API
Under `/api/v1`. Problem codes: `export_limit_reached`, `export_expired`,
`erasure_pending`, `erasure_blocked`, `ward_has_login`,
`recent_login_required`.

| Method | Path | Permission | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| POST | `/me/data-exports` | own data | `{ personId? }` (self or a ward). At most 3 per requester and day. `@RecentAuth(15)` |
| GET | `/me/data-exports` | own data | The caller's requests (newest first, keyset), status, expiry |
| GET | `/me/data-exports/:exportId/download` | own data | Pre-signed link valid five minutes, requester only, while the file exists; `privacy.export.downloaded`. `@RecentAuth(15)` |
| GET | `/me/account/deletion` | own data | Preview: blockers per club (`last_club_manager`, `roles_must_be_removed` with the roles and groups, `outstanding_invoices` with whom to contact), wards without login and without another full guardian with a login (choose per ward), the open request if any |
| POST | `/me/account/deletion` | own data | `{ eraseWardIds: [] }`; schedules erasure after the grace period. `@RecentAuth(5)`; `erasure_blocked` while any blocker remains (also for each chosen ward) |
| DELETE | `/me/account/deletion` | own data | Cancel during the grace period |
| POST | `/me/wards/:personId/erasure` | own data | Full guardian of a ward without login (`ward_has_login`); the ward's blockers apply; every other full guardian gets a notice and may cancel. `@RecentAuth(5)` |
| DELETE | `/me/wards/:personId/erasure` | own data | Cancel (the requester or any full guardian of the ward) |
| GET | `/me` (changed) | own data | Gains `pendingDeletion` (`scheduledFor`), a new optional field |

Each endpoint the web or the app calls gets a request budget (ADR 0040).

## Account deletion and erasure
- **Blockers (decided 2026-11-01):** a profile is not removed while the
  person (or a ward to be erased) has outstanding invoices from any club
  (`OutstandingObligations`; settled or written off first) or holds a club
  role, a leader function or a group ownership anywhere (handed over or
  removed first; the last manager hands over per ADR 0018). The preview
  lists each blocker with the club and who can resolve it; the request is
  refused with `erasure_blocked` until none remains, and the job checks
  again before erasing (a role given during the grace period stops it with
  `error_code = blocked` and a notice to the person).
- `POST /me/account/deletion` creates the request (`account_deletion`,
  grace period `ERASURE_GRACE_DAYS`, default 14, 0–30, an installation
  setting), audits `account.deletion.scheduled` and sends the notice
  `account.deletion.scheduled` (category `account`, mandatory email,
  immediate: "your account will be deleted on …; log in to cancel"). The
  account works during the grace period; the web and the app show a
  banner with "Cancel deletion".
- Wards without login whose only full guardian with a login is the
  caller: the caller chooses per ward to erase them too (default) or keep
  them (they stay without a guardian with a login; P1.8a's orphan period
  applies once no club keeps them).
- **`privacy:erase`** (every ten minutes) takes due requests (`FOR UPDATE
  SKIP LOCKED`, status `running`) and, idempotently, per person (the
  requester, then each chosen ward), one transaction at a time: re-checks
  the blockers; in each of the person's clubs leaves as in P1.8a with the
  system as actor; ends open guardianships (`guardianship.ended`); deletes
  export files and rows; then, as `clubadmin_retention`,
  `privacy.erase_person(person, 'account_deleted' | 'ward_erased')`; then,
  after commit, deletes the Zitadel user (`pnpm auth:configure` grants the
  machine user the user-delete permission), revokes BFF sessions and
  deny-lists mobile sessions (their push devices go with them); then marks
  the request `done`. A failure after the database part leaves
  `error_code` and is retried; the database erasure is never rolled back.
  After a restore the ledger replay erases the person again; a Zitadel
  user that still exists cannot log in and the replay report lists it.
- No notice after the erasure (the address is gone).
- Ward erasure follows the same path; the other full guardians get
  `person.erasure.scheduled` (category `family`, mandatory email,
  immediate) and may cancel.
- Erased people disappear from the register, the presenter shows "Deleted
  user" where an ID remains, and they are counted in nothing new.

## "Download my data"
- `privacy:export` (per request) builds a ZIP in the requester's language:
  `data.json` (documented, versioned schema `docs/api/data-export.md`) and
  a self-contained `index.html` (no external resources, no scripts) with
  a README explaining each section, the controller of each part (the
  club, or the platform for the account), and how to ask a club for more.
  ZIP through one dependency with an allowed licence (`fflate`, MIT).
- Contents for the subject: the person row, the account (email, locale,
  time zone, notification settings; never the IdP subject or session
  handles), per club where the subject has or had a relationship: club
  profile (member number, shared fields, emergency contacts, protected
  flag), memberships, group memberships with functions, roles,
  employments, consents with the texts they agreed to, join requests and
  waiting-list entries, answers (with notes), attendance and call-ups,
  **event registrations with their answers** (sensitive answers decrypted
  only into the subject's or their guardian's file; never in logs),
  guardianships with the other party's name, invitations sent, **the inbox
  of the last 90 days, notification settings and push devices** (platform,
  app variant, last used; never the token), data exports and erasure
  requests, the record history of the subject's own rows (actors through
  the presenter). Not included: club notes (`club_profiles.notes`; the
  README says the club answers a full access request), other people's
  contact details, audit IP hashes, backups.
- Wards: a full guardian's export of a ward contains the ward's data; one
  request per subject ("me and my children" as several requests).
- Written in batches to a multipart upload, never held in memory. Deleted
  after seven days (`privacy:expire_exports`, hourly) and by the `exports/`
  lifecycle rule; the row stays. The ready notice `privacy.export.ready`
  (category `account`, immediate) links to the page, never to the file.

## Worker tasks
| Task | Schedule | Does |
|---|---|---|
| `privacy:export` | per export (insert trigger) | Writes a "download my data" file |
| `privacy:expire_exports` | hourly | Deletes files seven days after ready; fails exports still queued after two hours |
| `privacy:erase` | every 10 minutes | Runs due erasure requests |

`privacy:retention` (P1.8a) also deletes `data_exports` rows after 12
months and `erasure_requests` after 24 months.

## UI
- **Web, `/me/privacy`** gains "Download my data" (request for me and each
  ward, list with status, download, expiry) and "Delete my account"
  (preview with blockers and what to do, the wards' choice, a
  confirmation that names the date, then the banner with "Cancel
  deletion"); a ward's page gains "Erase {name}" for full guardians of a
  ward without login.
- **Mobile:** "Delete my account" in Settings → Privacy (preview with
  blockers, confirm, cancel; app stores require account deletion inside
  apps that create accounts); the banner while deletion is pending;
  "Download my data" opens the web page. Cache first; the preview always
  refreshes before confirming.
- Every string in sv, en, nb.

## Privacy and security
- Legal bases: Art. 15 and 20 (export), Art. 17 (erasure).
- The export file is the most sensitive artefact: only the requester
  downloads it, with a recent login, through a five-minute link; never
  emailed; never logged; the object key holds only the export ID.
- Audit events: `privacy.export.requested`, `privacy.export.downloaded`,
  `account.deletion.scheduled`, `account.deletion.cancelled`,
  `person.erasure.scheduled`, `person.erasure.cancelled`, `person.erased`.
- Notification types: `privacy.export.ready`,
  `account.deletion.scheduled`, `person.erasure.scheduled` (recipient
  tests per type).
- Personal data never in logs, job payloads or outbox events.

## Protected identity
The export names other people (guardians, co-guardians, those who
recorded attendance) only as the subject may see them, through the
presenter, as the API would.

## Performance
- Request budgets for every new endpoint (ADR 0040).
- Export of a person with five years of attendance (about 400 marks) and
  a full inbox in under a minute; memory bounded by batches.
- `privacy:erase` for a person in three clubs < 5 s including Zitadel.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] "Download my data" produces a ZIP (JSON and HTML) in the requester's
      language within minutes, for self and wards, including event
      registrations, the inbox, notification settings and devices;
      downloadable only by the requester with a recent login; deleted
      after seven days.
- [ ] Account deletion is refused while roles, leader functions,
      ownerships or outstanding invoices (fake provider) remain, with each
      blocker named; the job re-checks before erasing.
- [ ] A 14-day grace period that can be cancelled, then leaving every
      club, ended guardianships, `privacy.erase_person`, deleted export
      files, the Zitadel user deleted and all sessions revoked; chosen
      wards erased too; idempotent on retry; the replay erases again after
      a restore.
- [ ] Ward erasure notifies the other full guardians, who can cancel.
- [ ] Request budgets, tenant isolation, allow/deny and audit for every
      new endpoint; translations complete; Maestro flow for account
      deletion and cancelling it.
- [ ] Docs: `gdpr.md` (rights as implemented), `security.md` (exports,
      erasure), `architecture.md` (tasks), `docs/api/data-export.md`, ADR
      0030 (erasure job implemented), ADR 0041 (extended), `seed-data.md`.

## Out of scope
- Restorable club profiles and recovery codes (deferred, P1.8a).
- Erasing or exporting chat, documents, accounting records and
  background-check metadata (their modules add themselves).
- An operator console for erasure requests; a club manager erasing a
  person.
