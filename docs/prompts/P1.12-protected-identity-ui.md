## Context

What exists already:
- **Flags (P0.5):** `people.protected_identity` (global; check constraint
  `people_protected_no_address`) and `club_profiles.protected_identity_club`
  (club level), with partial indexes `people_protected_idx` and
  `club_profiles_protected_idx` (migration 0003). The effective flag is
  either of them.
- **Services, no endpoints:** `PeopleService.setProtectedIdentity(context,
  personId, value)` (the person or a full guardian; turning it on deletes
  the stored address) and `ClubProfilesService.setClubProtection(context,
  personId, value)` (club managers). Both write the audit event
  `person.protection.changed` (changed fields only) and publish the domain
  event `person.protection.changed` (`{ personId, clubId? }`), which
  `authorizationCacheHandler` (`packages/db/src/authorization-cache.ts`)
  turns into bumps of `authz:prot:v:<clubId>` / `authz:prot:v:global`.
  Nothing else consumes it: club managers are **not** notified yet, although
  `domain-model.md` says they are.
- **Permission `person.protection.set`** is in `packages/shared/src/
  permissions.ts` with its policy in `authorization.policy.ts` (global flag:
  the person or a guardian with `act_on_behalf`; club flag with `?clubId`:
  club managers, with MFA), but **no endpoint uses it**.
- **How flags are set today:** only by the seed (`seed/steps/core.ts`: Samira
  and Nadia globally, one generated SVK Herr senior player at club level by
  Anna), by "Add member" and the import (P1.3b: the "Protected identity in
  the club" checkbox and the `protected` column set the club flag and drop
  the address), and nowhere else. A person or guardian cannot protect
  themselves or a child in the UI; `UpdatePersonSchema` omits the flag and
  `AddChildSchema` has no field for it. The notice after accepting a
  guardian invitation for a protected child
  (`family.notices.acceptedProtected`) tells the guardian to protect
  themselves "on your profile", where there is no such control.
- **Visibility:** `PersonVisibility` (`person-visibility.service.ts`:
  `full` / `protected_visible` / `hidden`, cached protected set per club),
  `PersonPresenter` (summaries with `protected: boolean`; `actor()` turns a
  hidden actor into `{ kind: "protected" }`), the family, member-import and
  profile presenters, `app.visible_group_members` and the register's view
  as database backstops, the lint rule `clubadmin/person-names-in-presenters`.
- **Icon:** `ProtectedBadge` (crossed camera, tooltip `protected.tooltip`)
  in `packages/ui` and `apps/mobile/src/components/protected-badge.tsx`,
  rendered by `PersonName` on the web (rosters, register, family, person
  page, activity, attendance, invitees, duplicates, join requests) and in
  the app. `useProtectScreen` sets `FLAG_SECURE` / the app-switcher blur on
  the person, group, activity and attendance screens; the web shows
  `protectedNotice` above lists with protected people.
- **Access log:** `GET /people/:personId` calls
  `PeopleService.recordProtectedView`, which writes the audit event
  `person.protected.viewed` (resource `person`) for every view by someone
  other than the person, in the club's tenant context when `?clubId` is
  given, otherwise with `club_id` null. Nobody can read these events: there
  is no endpoint, `audit.events` is readable only per club (`club_read`
  policy) and the audit/history UI is P1.9.
- **Chat:** no chat tables or module yet (Phase 2). The seed-data table
  says Samira "accepted the IKN F2015 'Föräldrar' chat room only"; that is
  not seeded.

## Goal
A parent who learns that her family has protected personal data turns
protection on for herself and her child in two steps, on the web or in the
app, and is told exactly what changes: other members no longer see them,
leaders see the crossed camera and know not to photograph or share, no
address is stored. A club manager who has been informed sets protection in
the club with a second factor, and every club manager is told (without the
name in the email) when a member's protection changes. The protected person
or their guardian can see who has opened their profile and when, and club
managers can review the same for their club, so misuse by an insider is
visible. Removing protection is deliberate and cannot be done quietly by
the other parent. The rules for joining chat rooms by explicit acceptance
are decided and written down, so Phase 2 chat only implements them.

## Users and roles involved
Adults with their own login (global flag on themselves); full guardians
(`parent`, `legal_guardian`, `step_parent`, `foster_parent` with
`act_on_behalf`) for their wards; teenagers from the country pack's
`consent_age` for themselves (see Open decisions); club managers (club
flag, the club's access log, notifications); registrars, owners and leaders
(see the icon, appear in the access log); platform operators with support
access (never see protected people; unchanged).

## User stories
- As an adult member, I want to turn on protected identity for myself, so
  that other members cannot see me anywhere in the system.
- As a parent, I want to turn on protection for my child, and be offered to
  protect myself too, so that my name does not reveal my child.
- As a parent, I want to add a child with protection already on, so that
  the child is never visible unprotected, not even for a moment.
- As a club manager who has been informed by a family, I want to set
  protection for a member in our club (with a second factor), so that the
  member is protected even if the family has not done it themselves.
- As a club manager, I want to be told when a member's protection is turned
  on or off, and to list the club's protected members with the source of
  the flag, so that leaders can be briefed.
- As a protected person or the guardian of one, I want to see who has
  opened the profile, from which club and when, so that I can react to
  misuse.
- As a club manager, I want to see the access log of the club's protected
  members, so that I can follow up on a leader who looks without reason.
- As a parent, I want to be sure that the other parent cannot remove my
  child's protection without me and the club knowing.
- As a leader, I want to understand what the crossed camera means wherever
  I see it, on the web and in the app.

## Data model
Club-owned tables keep `club_id`, tenant RLS, record history and
`updated_at` (CLAUDE.md rule 3). New person references are declared in
the owning module's `*.references.ts` (merges and erasure).

- **`people`** (expand only): `protected_identity_set_by_person_id`
  (nullable; who turned the global flag on last) and
  `protected_identity_set_at` (timestamptz). Cleared when the flag is
  turned off. Used by the removal rule below; backfilled as null (seeded
  flags are re-set through the service by the seed).
- **`club_profiles`** (expand only): `protected_identity_club_set_by_person_id`
  and `protected_identity_club_set_at`, shown to club managers ("set by
  Anna, 3 March").
- **Club flag and address:** setting the club flag removes `address` from
  that club's `club_profiles.shared_fields` in the same transaction (the
  club stops seeing an address it should not hold; the global address is
  the person's own and stays unless the global flag is set).
- **Access log: no new table.** The audit event `person.protected.viewed`
  is the log (one source, the audit log's 2-year retention, append-only).
  - A `SECURITY DEFINER` function `app.protected_view_log(p_person_id,
    p_before_at, p_before_id, p_limit)` returns the events for one person
    across clubs (`id`, `created_at`, `club_id`, `actor_person_id`,
    `on_behalf_of_person_id`, `actor_kind`) only when
    `app.current_person_id()` is the person or `app.is_full_guardian_of`
    them; otherwise it returns nothing. `REVOKE ALL … FROM PUBLIC`, grant to
    `clubadmin_app`. Keyset by `(created_at, id)` through the existing
    `events_resource_idx (resource_type, resource_id, created_at DESC)`.
  - Club reads go through the normal `club_read` RLS policy, with a new
    partial index `audit.events (club_id, created_at DESC, id DESC) WHERE
    action = 'person.protected.viewed'` (the club index alone would scan
    every event of a busy club).
  - Views are still recorded on every `GET /people/:personId` by someone
    else (guardians included: a guardian may be the risk). Reading a log
    is not itself logged as a view.
- **Chat acceptance (decision only, no tables):** ADR 0022 is extended with
  the acceptance rules Phase 2 chat must implement: a protected person is
  never added to a room automatically; the room's owner may *offer* the
  room; the person (or a full guardian for a child below `consent_age`)
  accepts or declines, optionally with a display name per club (never
  their real name if they choose one); members of the room then see them
  only by that name or real name, as chosen; leaving or withdrawal hides
  their past messages' author as "a member with protected identity";
  removing protection does not add them to rooms. `domain-model.md` gets the
  same rules under "Protected identity". The tables
  (`chat_room_invitations` or similar) and the display name come with the
  chat package (see Open decisions).
- **Retention:** flags live with the person and profile; access-log events
  follow the audit log (2 years, `docs/gdpr.md`); the `set_by` columns are
  scrubbed by erasure like other person references.

## API
Under `/api/v1`. Writes are refused for clubs that are not active
(ADR 0031) only for the club flag; the global flag is not club data and is
always allowed. Problem codes, never values: `protection_removal_not_allowed`,
`protected_address` (existing), `not_a_full_guardian`, `not_a_club_member`.

| Method | Path | Permission | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| PUT | `/people/:personId/protection` | `person.protection.set` | Global flag `{ protected: boolean }`. The person or a full guardian (the service checks the kind, as `setProtectedIdentity` does). Turning on: no step-up, deletes the stored address (the UI warns first). Turning off: `@RecentAuth(15)` and the removal rule below. Idempotent; returns the new state |
| PUT | `/people/:personId/protection?clubId=` | `person.protection.set` (MFA) | Club flag `{ protected: boolean }`; the person must have a profile in the club (`not_a_club_member`). Club managers only |
| GET | `/people/:personId` (changed) | `person.profile.read` | Adds an optional `protection` object for the person, full guardians and (with `clubId`) club managers: `global`, `club` (with `clubId`), `setAt` per flag, `setBy` (PersonPresenter, club managers only for the club flag), `canChangeGlobal`, `canRemoveGlobal`, `canChangeClub`. Absent for everyone else (ADR 0029: new optional field) |
| POST | `/me/wards` (changed) | own data | `AddChildSchema` gains optional `protectedIdentity` (default false); the child is created protected in the same transaction, no address accepted |
| GET | `/me/wards` (changed) | own data | Each ward gains `protectedIdentity` (full guardians only) so the family page can show the state and the prompt |
| GET | `/people/:personId/access-log?cursor=` | `person.access_log.read` | The person and full guardians with `act_on_behalf`: views across all clubs, newest first, keyset (`cursor` = last event ID), 50 per page: `viewedAt`, `club` (ID and name), `viewer` (PersonPresenter's `actor()` in that club: a viewer the reader may not see is `{ kind: "protected" }`; an operator is `{ kind: "operator" }`), `onBehalfOf` when set, `viewerRole` (the relationship that allowed the view: `club_manager`, `registrar`, `group_owner`, `group_leader`, `guardian`) computed at read time |
| GET | `/clubs/:clubId/protected-access-log?personId=&cursor=` | `club.protected_access_log.read` (MFA) | Club managers: the club's views of protected people, optionally for one person; same item shape plus `person` |
| GET | `/clubs/:clubId/members?protected=only` (changed) | `club.members.read` | New filter for the full view (club managers, registrars): only members with an effective flag, each with `protectionSource` (`global`, `club`, `both`); ignored for the limited board view (it never contains protected members) |

The PUTs publish `person.protection.changed` as today (the payload gains
`enabled: boolean` and `scope: "global" | "club"`, IDs and booleans only).

**Removal rule for a child's global flag** (service, tested): a full
guardian may turn it off only if they turned it on
(`protected_identity_set_by_person_id`), or if the guardian who did is no
longer an active full guardian; otherwise `protection_removal_not_allowed`
and the UI says to talk to the other guardian or the club. The person
themselves may always turn off their own flag once they have their own
login and have reached `consent_age`. A club flag is independent and can
only be removed by a club manager. (Default; see Open decisions.)

## Permissions
New permissions in `packages/shared/src/permissions.ts`, one policy each
in `authorization.policy.ts`, allow and deny tests, entries in the
permission matrix (`scenarios.ts` "matrix"; tenant isolation: a user of
club B gets 404 for club A):
- `person.protection.set` (exists; first endpoint): allow the person, a full
  guardian with `act_on_behalf`; with `clubId` a club manager with MFA.
  Deny: relatives and contact persons, registrars, leaders, club managers
  without `clubId`, a club manager of another club (404), anyone acting
  `X-On-Behalf-Of` for the club flag. Add the matrix rows now.
- `person.access_log.read` (resource `person`): the person, guardians with
  `act_on_behalf` (the service requires a full guardian). Deny: relatives,
  club managers (they use the club log), leaders, registrars, operators.
- `club.protected_access_log.read` (resource `club`): club managers, with
  MFA. Deny: registrars, the board roles, leaders, operators with support
  access (they never see protected people).
- The `protection` object in the profile and the `protected=only` filter
  need no new permission (they follow `person.profile.read` /
  `club.members.read` with the rules above); test that a leader and a
  registrar never receive `protection` or `setBy`.

## Privacy and security
- The flag itself is sensitive (it suggests the family has protected
  personal data). Legal basis: the person's or guardian's request, and for
  the club flag the club's legitimate interest in protecting the member
  (and, where it is based on information about *skyddade personuppgifter*,
  the duty of care). Never logged, never in push or email bodies, never in
  exports to other members (unchanged), never in job payloads beyond IDs and
  booleans.
- **Notifications (email, P1.7 adds in-app/push later):** a worker handler
  on `person.protection.changed` emails every active club manager of each
  affected club: for a club flag, that club; for a global flag, every club
  where the person, or a ward of the person, has an active or paused
  membership. The email names neither the person nor the guardian: "A
  member's protected identity was turned on/off in {club}. Open ClubAdmin
  to see who." with a link to the protected members page. When a guardian
  turns a child's flag off, the other active full guardians get the same
  kind of email. Idempotent by the event's idempotency key; templates in
  sv, en, nb.
- **Audit:** `person.protection.changed` stays (changed fields only); the
  record history keeps the values. Removal attempts refused by the removal
  rule are audited as `person.protection.removal_refused` (no values).
- **Caches:** the existing version bumps make the change apply on the next
  request after the event is handled (under a second; the existing test
  covers both flags; extend it to the new endpoints). The web renders on
  the server and holds no person cache. The app's persisted caches of
  *other* users may still hold the name until their next fetch: the app
  refetches on foreground and after login, and `docs/security.md` records
  this residual window.
- **Step-up:** turning protection on is never harder than a click
  (protecting must be easy); turning it off needs a recent login; the club
  flag needs MFA both ways.
- Rate limits per account on the PUTs and the log reads (P0.10).
- Child safety: a child below `consent_age` cannot change their own flag;
  the guardian's actions are recorded as on behalf where applicable
  (ADR 0006).

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] A person turns their own protection on and off (web and app); turning
      on deletes the address after a warning; turning off needs a recent
      login.
- [ ] A full guardian protects a child, including when adding the child;
      relatives and contact persons get `403`; the guardian is prompted to
      protect themselves.

      club's shared fields.

      managers read their club's log with MFA; nobody else can (allow/deny

- [ ] After a flag change, other members stop seeing the person on the next

      Phase 2.

## Open decisions
1. **Who may remove a child's global flag.** Default: only the full
   guardian who set it (or any full guardian once the setter is no longer
   one), or the child from `consent_age` with their own login; the other
   full guardians are emailed on removal. Alternative: any full guardian,
   with notification only.
2. **From what age a teenager controls their own global flag.** Default:
   from the country pack's `consent_age` (SE 13) with their own login; until
   then only guardians. Alternative: only from adulthood (18).
3. **Who reads the access log.** Default: the person and full guardians
   (all clubs, viewer names shown), and club managers for their club with
   MFA. Alternatives: hide viewer names from the family (show role only),
   or add registrars to the club log.
4. **Access log store.** Default: the existing `person.protected.viewed`
   audit events read through a `SECURITY DEFINER` function, with the audit
   log's 2-year retention. Alternative: a dedicated `protected_profile_views`
   table with its own retention (e.g. 5 years).
5. **Chat acceptance in this package.** Default: rules only (ADR 0022 and
   `domain-model.md`); the invitation/acceptance tables and the per-club
   display name are built with chat in Phase 2. Alternative: add the
   per-club display name (`club_profiles.protected_display_name`) and its
   settings UI now.
6. **Step-up for the global flag.** Default: none to turn it on, a login in
   the last 15 minutes to turn it off. Alternative: MFA to turn it off.
7. **Manager notifications.** Default: email without the person's name to
   every active club manager of the affected clubs. Alternative: also
   registrars, or only an in-app notice once P1.7 lands.
