# P2.2 — Announcements: the board in the app, email and push in one message

## Context
Read first: `CLAUDE.md`, ADR 0039 (notifications: inbox, push, email modes,
bounces, unsubscribing), ADR 0049 (audiences: the same list decides who
receives an announcement), ADR 0022 (protected identity), ADR 0040
(responsiveness), `docs/gdpr.md`.

What exists already:

- Notifications (P1.7): categories (`schedule`, `responses`, `leader`,
  `events`, `membership`, `club_admin`, `family`, `account`, `waitlist`),
  channels `email` and `push`, email modes `always` | `no_app` | `never`,
  quiet hours, the daily email, bounce handling and one-click unsubscribe,
  the type → category map in `packages/shared/src/notifications.ts`.
- Events have an audience (`EVENT_AUDIENCES`) with audience groups; groups,
  group roles and club roles (P1.15).
- No board, no announcements.
- Audiences as a shared list (ADR 0049) are built in P2.4; if P2.2 comes
  first, it builds the audience model and P2.4 reuses it.

## Goal
A club or a group sends one message (a newsletter, "klubbstugan är stängd",
the date of the annual meeting) to the people it concerns and chooses how it
reaches them: the **board in the app**, **push** and/or **email**, one text.
The message board and bulk email are one feature. Members read
announcements in one place ("Anslagstavlan", across their clubs and groups)
and never miss the important ones.

## Users and roles involved
Club managers, the secretary, group leaders (senders); members and
guardians of all ages (recipients); protected people.

## User stories
- As a club manager, I write "Årsmöte 12 mars" to the whole club, pin it
  on the board and send it by email and push, so that everyone knows.
- As a coach, I tell my team's players and guardians that the field is
  closed this week, by push and on the board.
- As a parent, I see the announcements of my children's groups and of the
  club on one board, unread first, and pinned ones on top.
- As a member who never opens the app, I get announcements by email and
  can unsubscribe from the club's newsletters without losing activity
  notices.

## Decisions
1. **One message, chosen channels.** The sender picks any of board, push,
   email; the text is the same everywhere (Markdown limited to what email
   and push render; push shows the title).
2. **Audience** = the ADR 0049 list: the whole club (members and their
   guardians), groups (optionally some roles in them), club roles.
   Guardians receive what is sent to their children's groups. Public
   announcements belong to the public pages (Phase 3).
3. **The board** keeps announcements per club and group: pinned first, then
   newest; read/unread per person; an expiry date hides an announcement from
   the board (it stays in the archive). The board is an aggregated view
   ("Anslagstavlan" across clubs and groups, and each group's own).
4. **Email** follows P1.7: bounces stop sending to an address, unsubscribe
   per club and category, the email modes apply; announcements are a new
   notification category `announcements` so people can turn them off
   separately from activity notices; **proposed:** except announcements the sender marks
   **important** (e.g. the annual meeting notice), which are sent to everyone
   regardless of the category setting (still respecting bounces and
   `never`).
5. **Push** follows the recipients' notification settings and quiet hours.
6. **Proposed — sending later**: an announcement may be scheduled; until sent it can
   be edited or withdrawn. After sending, editing changes the board and the
   archive (marked "redigerat"), not the emails already sent.
7. Kept until deleted by the sender or a club manager; erasure anonymises
   the sender.

## Data model
Club-owned, tenant RLS, history, `updated_at`, person references declared.

- `announcements` (club_id, group_id nullable, title, body, channels,
  important, pinned_until, expires_at, scheduled_at, sent_at, created_by,
  withdrawn_at).
- `announcement_audiences` (the ADR 0049 entries).
- `announcement_reads` (announcement_id, person_id, read_at).
- Recipients resolved when sent into the notification outbox (IDs only);
  delivery through the P1.7 pipeline.

## API
| Method | Path | Permission | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| GET | `/me/announcements` | authenticated | The board across clubs and groups, keyset |
| POST | `/me/announcements/:id/read` | authenticated | Mark read |
| GET | `/clubs/:clubId/announcements` | `club.announcements.send` | The club's archive incl. drafts and scheduled |
| POST | `/clubs/:clubId/announcements` | `club.announcements.send` | Draft, schedule or send |
| PATCH/DELETE | `/clubs/:clubId/announcements/:id` | sender or `club.announcements.send` | Edit, withdraw |
| GET/POST | `/clubs/:clubId/groups/:groupId/announcements` | `group.announcements.send` | Group announcements |
| GET | `/clubs/:clubId/announcements/:id/recipients` | sender's permission | Counts per channel, delivery and read status (counts, not names, for large audiences) |

## Permissions
| Permission | Scope | Allows | Default |
|---|---|---|---|
| `club.announcements.send` | club | Announcements to the club, any group or club role | `club_manager`, `chair`, `secretary` |
| `group.announcements.send` | group | Announcements to the group's members and guardians | `head_coach`, `assistant_coach`, `team_manager`; owners |

Allow/deny tests; a group sender cannot address people outside the group.

## UI
- App and web: "Anslagstavlan" (unread first, pinned on top), a group's
  board on the group page, an announcement's page.
- Composer: audience picker, channels, important, pin, expiry, schedule,
  preview per channel, the number of recipients per channel before sending.
- The archive with delivery and read counts.

## Privacy and security
Recipients' addresses never shown to the sender; delivery data kept as in
P1.7; audit events for send, edit and withdraw. Rate limit per club on
email volume (operator setting).

## Protected identity
Protected people receive announcements but appear in no recipient list; the
sender's name through `PersonPresenter`.

## Locale and country packs
sv, en, nb; the category and the email footer translated.

## Performance
Fan-out in the worker in batches; the board query starts from the person's
groups and clubs with a supporting index; budgets for `/me/announcements`.

## Seed data
IKN: a pinned club announcement about the annual meeting (important, all
channels), a group announcement for F2015, one scheduled draft; Anna (a
parent) has one unread.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Board, push and email from one message; important bypasses the
      category setting, not bounces
- [ ] Audiences resolve guardians; group senders limited to their group
- [ ] Scheduling, editing, withdrawing
- [ ] Tenant isolation, allow/deny, matrix rows, budgets, audit, history
- [ ] Translations complete for sv, en and nb; docs updated
      (`domain-model.md`, ADR 0039 amendment for the category)

## Out of scope
- Public announcements on the club's website (Phase 3).
- Comments and reactions on announcements (use the chat).
- Email to people who are not members (mailing lists).
