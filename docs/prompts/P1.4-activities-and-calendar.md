# P1.4 — Activities: recurring trainings, matches, locations and the in-app calendar

## Context
Read first: `CLAUDE.md`, `docs/domain-model.md` ("groups", "seasons",
"group_memberships", "guardianships" and the rights of linked kinds,
"Protected identity", "Effective permissions"), ADR 0006 (authorization and
`X-On-Behalf-Of`), ADR 0009 (jobs in Postgres), ADR 0017 (record history),
ADR 0019 (groups, owners, seasons), ADR 0021 (calendar subscriptions: what
calendar data may contain), ADR 0022 (protected identity), ADR 0025
(authorization engine), ADR 0028 (mobile app, persisted cache), ADR 0029 (API
only grows), ADR 0031 (club access state), `docs/performance.md` (the "my
schedule" budget and the `person_schedule` read model), `docs/seed-data.md`
("Demo scenario: activity and other data").

What exists already:
- **Groups (P0.5, P1.2):** `groups` with kinds, parent groups, owners
  (`group_owners`), `group_memberships` with functions (`member`,
  `head_coach`, `assistant_coach`, `team_manager`, `physio`,
  `other_leader`), `seasons` and `group_seasons`. Endpoints for groups,
  rosters (keyset pages through the PersonPresenter) and "my groups"
  (`GET /me/groups`, also the wards' groups).
- **Families (P1.3, P1.3b):** guardianships with rights per link;
  `view_schedule` is defined but nothing reads it yet ("each later feature
  enforces the right it needs: schedule P1.4"). Linked kinds (`relative`,
  `contact_person`, `step_parent`, `foster_parent`) see a ward's schedule
  only with `view_schedule`; full guardians always.
- **Clubs:** `clubs.time_zone` (IANA), `default_locale`, the country pack
  (`packages/country-packs`), the access state (`active`, `read_only`,
  `suspended`; writes refused when not active, ADR 0031).
- **Platform:** `withTenant`/`withActor`, the outbox (domain events with IDs
  only) and the authorization cache invalidated by `membership.changed` and
  `guardianship.changed`; the worker's crontab; the web BFF with server
  actions that work without JavaScript; the Expo app with a persisted,
  per-account encrypted query cache and a home screen without content yet.
- **Nothing yet** for activities, locations, schedules, responses (P1.5),
  events with sign-up (P1.6), notifications (P1.7) or calendar feeds
  (P1.11).

## Goal
A coach plans the season in a few minutes: "F2017 trains Tuesdays 17:30–19:00
and Thursdays 18:00–19:30 in Strandvikens sporthall from 19 August to
5 December, not during the autumn break", adds the matches as they are
announced, moves or cancels a single session when the hall is taken, and
everyone concerned sees it at once: players and their guardians in "My
schedule" on the web and in the app (all children in one list, even across
clubs), leaders in the group calendar, club managers in the club calendar.
Times are right across daylight-saving changes. No calendar view ever lists
who takes part, so nothing reveals a protected member.

## Users and roles involved
Group owners and the group's leaders (`head_coach`, `assistant_coach`,
`team_manager`) plan and change activities; club managers do it for any
group and manage the club's locations; members, their full guardians and
linked people with `view_schedule` see the schedule; board members and
registrars see the club calendar (no names).

## User stories
- As a coach, I want to create a recurring training (weekdays, time,
  duration, location, start and end date or the season), so that the season's
  schedule exists without entering every session.
- As a coach, I want to skip dates (holidays, a list of dates, or the
  country's public holidays), so that the series has no sessions on them.
- As a coach, I want to change or cancel one session, or "this and all
  following" (a new time from November), or the whole series, so that the
  schedule follows reality; a cancelled session stays visible as cancelled
  with an optional reason.
- As a coach, I want to add a match (opponent, home or away, kick-off,
  meeting time and place, location or a free-text address), so that players
  and parents know where to be and when.
- As a coach of two groups, I want one activity for both (a joint training),
  so that it appears once in each group's calendar and once in a family's
  schedule.
- As a club manager, I want to keep the club's locations (name, address,
  map link, notes like "entrance B") and see when two activities use the
  same location at the same time, so that double bookings are noticed.
- As a member or guardian, I want "My schedule" with all my activities and my
  children's, filterable per person and club, with what changed since I last
  looked, so that I plan the family week in one place.
- As a relative with `view_schedule`, I want to see my grandchild's schedule,
  and as a relative without it I want to see nothing of it.
- As a member using the app, I want my schedule to open instantly, also
  offline, and to show cancellations as soon as I am online.

## Data model
All club-owned tables: `club_id`, tenant RLS, record history, `updated_at`
(CLAUDE.md rule 3). Times in `timestamptz` (UTC); local times and rules in
the club's time zone; all-day activities as `date`.

- `locations`: `name`, `address` (street, postal code, city, country; all
  optional), `latitude`/`longitude` (optional), `directions` (free text,
  e.g. "Entrance B, hall 2"), `archived_at`. Unique name per club among the
  active ones.
- `activity_series`: the recurrence of a training (or any regular
  activity): `kind`, `title`, its groups (`activity_series_groups`, copied to
  each occurrence's `activity_groups`), `weekdays` and `local_start_time`/`duration_minutes` per weekday (a
  series may have different times on different days), `starts_on`,
  `ends_on` (or `season_id`), `skip_dates` (`date[]`),
  `skip_public_holidays` (country pack), `location_id`, `location_text`,
  `notes`, `created_by_person_id`. No RRULE strings in the database: the
  supported rules are weekly ones; ADR 0034 records why and how they map to
  RRULE for P1.11.
- `activities`: one row per occurrence (materialised, see below): `kind`
  (`training`, `match`, `meeting`, `other`; events with sign-up are P1.6 and
  get their own kind then), `series_id` (nullable), `title`, `starts_at`,
  `ends_at` (or `all_day_on`/`all_day_until` dates), `meet_at` and
  `meet_place` (optional "samling"), `location_id` or `location_text`,
  `status` (`scheduled`, `cancelled`), `cancelled_reason` (shown to
  everyone; never personal data, say so in the UI), `notes` (shown to the
  group), `leader_notes` (leaders and owners only), match fields
  (`opponent`, `home_away`, `competition`), `detached` (edited apart from
  its series), `sequence` (incremented on every change that a calendar
  client must see, for P1.11 and iMIP later), `created_by_person_id`,
  `changed_at` (the last change members are told about).
- `activity_groups`: `activity_id`, `group_id` (one or more groups per
  activity, same club); index `(club_id, group_id, starts_at)` through a
  denormalised `starts_at` or an equivalent plan.
- **Materialisation:** occurrences exist from the series' start up to the
  earlier of its end and a rolling horizon (default 400 days); the worker
  extends horizons nightly. Editing a series rewrites its future,
  non-detached occurrences in one transaction; past occurrences are never
  rewritten (attendance in P1.5 refers to them). "This and following"
  splits the series at that date (the old series ends, a new one starts).
- **`person_schedule`** (the read model in `docs/performance.md`): one row
  per (person, activity) for every upcoming and recent activity (e.g. the
  last 30 days) of a person through their active group memberships,
  maintained by the worker from domain events (`activity.changed`,
  `membership.changed`, group membership changes) and rebuilt per person on
  demand. It holds IDs, `starts_at` and the group(s) only. Global table
  (person-owned, not club-owned): RLS through the `app.*` helpers (the
  person, their full guardians, linked people with `view_schedule`;
  ADR 0023), reasons for the choice in ADR 0034. A guardian's schedule is
  the union over their wards at read time (no copy per guardian), so a
  right that is revoked takes effect at once.
- Person references (`created_by_person_id`, `person_schedule.person_id`)
  declared in `activities.references.ts` (merging duplicates).
- Retention: activities and series are kept as long as the club exists
  (attendance, LOK-stöd and annual reports refer to them); `person_schedule`
  rows older than 30 days are deleted by the worker.

ADR 0034 decides: materialised occurrences versus computing them on read,
the supported recurrence rules, the horizon, how DST and skipped dates are
applied, the schedule read model and its RLS, and the event and sequence
semantics that P1.11 (feeds) and P1.7 (notifications) build on.

## API
Under `/api/v1`. Times in ISO 8601 with offset; every response that holds
local times also names the club's time zone.

| Method | Path | Permission | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| GET | `/clubs/:clubId/locations` | `club.read` | Active locations (archived with `?archived=true` for managers), keyset |
| POST | `/clubs/:clubId/locations` | `club.locations.manage` | |
| PATCH | `/clubs/:clubId/locations/:locationId` | `club.locations.manage` | Edit or archive (activities keep their location) |
| GET | `/groups/:groupId/activities?from=&to=&cursor=` | `group.activities.read` | The group calendar: occurrences in a range (max 400 days), keyset by start |
| POST | `/groups/:groupId/activities` | `group.activities.manage` | One activity (training, match, meeting, other); `groupIds` for joint activities (the caller must manage every group) |
| POST | `/groups/:groupId/activity-series` | `group.activities.manage` | A recurring series; returns the series and the number of occurrences created |
| GET | `/activity-series/:seriesId` | `group.activities.read` | |
| PATCH | `/activity-series/:seriesId` | `group.activities.manage` | Changes the whole series from a date (`from`, default today): future non-detached occurrences are rewritten |
| POST | `/activity-series/:seriesId/split` | `group.activities.manage` | "This and following" from an occurrence, with the changes |
| GET | `/activities/:activityId` | `group.activities.read` (any of its groups) | Detail: time, place, notes, the leaders of its groups (PersonPresenter); never participants |
| PATCH | `/activities/:activityId` | `group.activities.manage` | One occurrence: time, place, notes, match fields; marks it detached |
| POST | `/activities/:activityId/cancel` | `group.activities.manage` | With an optional reason; `/restore` undoes it |
| DELETE | `/activities/:activityId` | `group.activities.manage` | Only an activity created in error with no responses yet (P1.5 adds that check); otherwise cancel |
| GET | `/clubs/:clubId/activities?from=&to=&groupId=&locationId=&cursor=` | `club.activities.read` | The club calendar (managers, registrars, board roles) |
| GET | `/clubs/:clubId/locations/:locationId/conflicts?from=&to=` | `club.locations.manage` | Overlapping activities at a location (warnings, never blocking) |
| GET | `/me/schedule?from=&to=&personId=&clubId=&cursor=` | own data | Me and my wards (full guardians; linked people with `view_schedule`), each entry naming whose it is; `changedSince` marks entries changed since a time the client sends |

Problem codes (`activity_in_past`, `series_too_long`, `invalid_recurrence`,
`group_not_managed`, `location_archived`, `activity_has_responses`, …),
never values. Writes are refused for clubs that are not active (ADR 0031).
`GET /me/schedule` and the group calendar answer with ETags (`304`).

## Permissions
New permissions, one policy each, allow and deny tests, entries in the
permission matrix (tenant isolation: 404 for club B):
- `group.activities.read`: active members of the group (and of its child
  groups for a parent group's activities), their full guardians, linked
  people with `view_schedule` (through the ward), the group's leaders and
  owners (also of parent groups), club managers. Not other members of the
  club, not linked people without `view_schedule`.
- `group.activities.manage`: owners of the group (and of parent groups),
  members with the functions `head_coach`, `assistant_coach` or
  `team_manager`, club managers. Not `physio`, `other_leader` or members.
  Joint activities: the caller must manage every group named.
- `club.activities.read`: club managers, registrars and the board roles
  (the club calendar, no names).
- `club.locations.manage`: club managers (and later a facilities role;
  out of scope).
- `GET /me/schedule` is the caller's own data (and the wards the caller may
  see the schedule of): no `X-On-Behalf-Of` needed; with it, only that
  ward. The service enforces `view_schedule` for linked kinds (P1.3).

## UI
- **Web, group page:** a calendar tab (agenda list by week, a month grid on
  wide screens), "New activity" and "New series" for those who manage the
  group (the series form shows a preview of the dates it creates, the
  skipped ones struck through, and how many there are), per-occurrence
  actions (edit, cancel with reason, restore), and for series "this
  session", "this and following", "the whole series".
- **Web, "My schedule"** (home for members and guardians): today, this week
  and later, one list for the whole family with a chip per person, filters
  per person and club, cancelled sessions struck through with the reason,
  "changed" marks since the last visit.
- **Web, club calendar** for managers, registrars and the board: all groups,
  filter by group and location, location conflicts highlighted.
- **Web, locations** under club settings: list, create, edit, archive, map
  link (OpenStreetMap; no embedded map, no third-party script).
- **Mobile:** "My schedule" as the home screen (agenda list, pull to
  refresh, offline from the persisted cache, the family chips), and the
  activity detail. Planning stays on the web in this package.
- Loading, empty ("No activities this week") and error states; times in
  the club's time zone with the zone shown when it differs from the
  device's; translations in sv, en, nb.

## Privacy and security
- Activities hold no personal data beyond who created them; notes and
  cancellation reasons are free text visible to the group: the forms say
  not to write about individuals (health, behaviour). Leader notes are for
  leaders only.
- No calendar view or response lists the participants (ADR 0021, ADR 0022);
  the activity detail names only the leaders of its groups, through the
  PersonPresenter (protected leaders are left out for viewers who may not
  see them).
- Audit events for creating, changing, cancelling, restoring and deleting
  activities and series and for location changes (`activity.created`,
  `.updated`, `.cancelled`, …; changed field names only).
- Domain events with IDs only: `activity.changed` (with the kind of change:
  created, updated, cancelled, restored, deleted), `activity_series.changed`;
  P1.7 turns them into notifications, so no email or push is sent in this
  package.
- A member who leaves a group or club loses the schedule at once (the read
  model is updated from `membership.changed`; the API also checks the
  membership on read).

## Protected identity
Schedules, calendars and activity details contain no participants. Leaders
named on an activity go through the PersonPresenter. Counts are not shown
in this package (responses are P1.5). The club calendar lists groups and
locations only.

## Locale and country packs
- Strings in sv, en and nb (activity kinds, home/away, weekdays, recurrence
  descriptions such as "Every Tuesday and Thursday until 5 December",
  durations, the "changed" mark).
- Dates, times and weekdays formatted with the viewer's locale and the
  club's time zone; the first day of the week from the locale.
- Public holidays per country in the country packs (`se`, `no`): a pure
  function `publicHolidays(year)` with fixed and movable dates (Easter-based
  ones computed, not listed), used by `skip_public_holidays`. Tests for
  several years in both packs.
- DST: a series at 18:00 stays at 18:00 local time across the changes in
  March and October (tests for both, in Europe/Stockholm and
  Europe/Oslo); a non-existent local time (02:30 on the spring change) is
  refused with a code.

## Performance
- `GET /me/schedule` p95 < 50 ms for a family of three children in two
  clubs with 400 days of activities (read model, one statement plus names
  of leaders only on the detail endpoint); the group calendar for a season
  < 100 ms; the club calendar for a week of a 40-team club < 100 ms.
- Creating a series of 40 weeks × 2 days in < 200 ms (bulk insert, one
  statement for the occurrences and one for `activity_groups`); rewriting a
  series' future in < 500 ms.
- The worker updates `person_schedule` within seconds of a change, in bulk
  (one statement per activity or per membership change, never per person).
- Load scenario: add activities for 5 years (`seed-data.md`, "Load
  scenario"), and query-plan tests for the schedule, the group calendar and
  the club calendar on it (indexes only, no sequential scans on
  `activities` or `person_schedule`).

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] A coach creates a twice-weekly series for a season with skipped
      dates and public holidays; the preview and the result agree.
- [ ] Changing "this and following" splits the series; past sessions and
      detached ones are untouched; the whole-series change rewrites only
      the future.
- [ ] Times stay correct across both DST changes in SE and NO.
- [ ] A guardian's "My schedule" shows all children across clubs; a linked
      relative sees a ward's schedule only with `view_schedule`, and loses
      it at once when the right is removed.
- [ ] A member who leaves a group no longer sees its activities.
- [ ] No schedule, calendar or activity response contains participants;
      protected leaders are left out for viewers who may not see them.
- [ ] Location conflicts are shown, never blocking.
- [ ] Writes refused for read-only and suspended clubs.
- [ ] The mobile app shows "My schedule" from the cache offline and
      refreshes when online.
- [ ] Performance budgets met (query-plan tests on the load scenario).
- [ ] Tenant-isolation test for every new endpoint
- [ ] Allow/deny tests for every new policy
- [ ] Audit events for every change
- [ ] Translations complete for sv and en (and nb)
- [ ] Docs updated: `domain-model.md` (activities, locations, schedule),
      `performance.md`, `seed-data.md`, `architecture.md` (module, worker
      tasks), ADR 0034

## Out of scope
- Responses (attending / not attending) and attendance (P1.5); the
  "has responses" check on delete arrives with them.
- Events with sign-up forms, capacity and waiting lists, cup weekends
  (P1.6).
- Notifications of changes by email or push (P1.7); this package publishes
  the domain events only.
- Calendar feeds and answer links (P1.11), iMIP invitations (Phase 2).
- Facility booking and a facilities role (Phase 3); LOK-stöd reporting
  (Phase 2).
- Planning from the mobile app; training plans (Phase 3).
