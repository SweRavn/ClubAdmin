# P2.4 — Documents II and meetings: governing documents, agendas, minutes, signing

## Context
Read first: `CLAUDE.md`, ADR 0049 (decisions 1B, 3–5, 8–9), ADR 0037
(settings decided by the club's governing documents), ADR 0034
(activities, kind `meeting`), ADR 0046, ADR 0052 (tasks), ADR 0022, P2.2 (file archive and
audiences, which this builds on).

What exists already:

- After P2.2: folders, documents, versions, audiences, attachments to
  notices.
- The settings registry (`club-settings.ts`, `decidedBy`) and board
  decisions (`club_setting_decisions`, ADR 0037).
- Groups of kinds `board` and `committee`; activities of kind `meeting`
  with notices and answers; MFA and recent-auth step-up.
- `PaymentProvider` in server-kit as the model for a provider interface.

## Goal
The club writes and adopts its governing documents in ClubAdmin, runs board
and annual meetings with a structured agenda, records decisions per item,
assembles the minutes into one document and has them approved ("justerade")
and signed in the app. Adopted documents set the club's settings where they
mark them (ADR 0037).

## Users and roles involved
Chair, secretary, board and committee members (ordinary group members,
adjunct members too), adjusters ("justerare"), members (readers).

## User stories
- As the secretary, I draft the new statutes in Markdown, mark the
  membership-fee setting, and the chair records them as adopted at the
  annual meeting.
- As the chair, I create a board meeting from the template, add items and
  papers; the notice carries the agenda as a link.
- During the meeting, the secretary writes per item, checks decisions and
  adds action items with an assignee and a date.
- After the meeting, the minutes are assembled; the chair and the
  adjusters approve and sign them in the app with their second factor.

## Decisions (ADR 0049, 0037)
1. **Governing documents** in series (statutes, annual meeting minutes,
   board minutes, policies, the club's own), Markdown with marked settings,
   draft → adopted (date, meeting, effective date) → superseded. An
   adopted version is locked; changes mean a new version adopted again. A
   scanned signed PDF may be attached. Adopted documents cannot be deleted,
   only archived.
2. **Boards and committees are ordinary groups**, members added by hand; no
   derived membership. The annual meeting is a club-wide meeting.
3. **Meetings** are activities of kind `meeting`. Agenda items (number,
   title, presenter, papers, time) come from a template: the country pack's
   default (SE: "Mötets öppnande", "Val av ordförande", "Val av justerare",
   …), which the club adjusts into its own templates.
4. During the meeting each item has its Markdown field; **decisions** per
   item with text (may mark settings); **action items** are **tasks** (ADR 0052):
   one assignee or more, a due date, done or not, in the assignee's "Mina
   uppgifter" together with duty shifts (P2.6). This prompt builds the
   shared task tables and "Mina uppgifter"; P2.6 adds shifts to them.
5. **Minutes assembled** from the items into one Markdown document (one §
   per item) in the group's series; then approved and signed.
6. **Signing in the app (simple electronic signature)**: recent login with
   a second factor, SHA-256 of exactly the signed version, timestamp, audit
   event; the signed version is locked and gets a signature page (PDF)
   with the hash. Each signature records its level (`simple`).
   `SignatureProvider` is an interface (plugin capability, ADR 0051) for
   BankID, Freja and the EU wallet later. A club cannot require a level yet.
7. Erasure does not rewrite adopted documents or signed minutes (the
   privacy notice says so); `@` mentions render through `PersonPresenter`;
   the editor warns about free-text names.
8. Push when new minutes or statutes are adopted (to the document's
   audience).

## Data model
Club-owned, tenant RLS, history, `updated_at`, person references declared.

- `document_series` (club_id, group_id nullable, kind, name).
- `documents` (P2.2) with `kind = governing`: series_id, status, adopted_at,
  adopted_meeting_id, effective_from, superseded_by.
- `document_versions`: Markdown body for governing documents; settings
  markers parsed into `club_setting_decisions` on adoption (ADR 0037).
- `agenda_templates` (club_id nullable for the pack default, items JSON).
- `meeting_agenda_items` (activity_id, position, title, presenter, minutes
  Markdown, papers via `notice_attachments`).
- `meeting_decisions` (item_id, text).
- **Tasks (ADR 0052)**, shared with duty shifts: `tasks` (club_id,
  group_id nullable, kind `action_item` here, title, description Markdown,
  due_date or starts_at/ends_at, needed, source: meeting item, activity,
  event session or none, created_by) and `task_assignments` (task_id,
  person_id, counts_for_person_id nullable, source `assigned` | `signup` |
  `swap`, status `planned` | `done` | `missed`, marked_by, marked_at).
- `document_signatures` (version_id, signer, role `chair` | `secretary` |
  `adjuster` | other, level, sha256, signed_at, provider).

## API
| Method | Path | Permission | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| GET/POST | `/clubs/:clubId/document-series` | read: audience; write: `club.governing_documents.manage` | Series |
| POST/PATCH | `/clubs/:clubId/governing-documents[/:id]` | `club.governing_documents.manage` | Drafts, versions |
| POST | `/clubs/:clubId/governing-documents/:id/adopt` | `club.governing_documents.adopt` (MFA) | Lock, settings decisions |
| GET/PUT | `/clubs/:clubId/activities/:id/agenda` | can edit the meeting | Items, decisions, actions |
| POST | `/clubs/:clubId/activities/:id/minutes` | can edit the meeting | Assemble the minutes |
| POST | `/clubs/:clubId/documents/:id/versions/:v/signatures` | named signer, recent auth with MFA | Sign |
| GET | `/clubs/:clubId/documents/:id/versions/:v/signature-page` | audience | PDF |
| GET/PUT | `/clubs/:clubId/agenda-templates` | `club.governing_documents.manage` | Templates (from the country pack's default) |
| GET | `/me/tasks` | authenticated | "Mina uppgifter" (action items now, duty shifts from P2.6), keyset |
| POST | `/me/task-assignments/:id/status` | the assignee or whoever may edit the meeting | Done / not done |

## Permissions
| Permission | Scope | Allows | Given to these template roles |
|---|---|---|---|
| `club.governing_documents.manage` | club | Draft governing documents, series, templates, minutes | `secretary`, `chair` |
| `club.governing_documents.adopt` | club | Record a document as adopted (MFA) | `chair`, `secretary` |

A meeting's agenda is edited by whoever may edit the activity (group
leaders of a board group). Signing is open to the persons named as signers
of that version, never to others.

## UI
- Governing documents per series; Markdown editor with setting markers and
  preview; adoption dialog; version history with differences.
- Meeting page: agenda editor, live minutes per item, decisions, action
  items; assemble; signing status per signer.
- "Mina dokument" shows documents waiting for my signature; "Mina
  uppgifter" (app and web) shows my action items.

## Privacy and security
Minutes contain personal data; audience controls reading; signatures are
audited; the signature page detects later change. A lawyer reviews the
simple-signature claim before the hosted service relies on it (ADR 0049).

## Protected identity
Signers, presenters and assignees through `PersonPresenter`; protected
people shown as allowed by their visibility in printed minutes too.

## Locale and country packs
`agendaTemplates` in the country pack (SE annual and board meeting; NO
årsmøte, styremøte); sv, en, nb.

## Performance
Agenda and minutes loaded in one query set; budgets per endpoint.

## Seed data
IKN: adopted statutes with a marked setting; a board group with a meeting
from the template, decisions, an action item; minutes signed by the chair
and waiting for one adjuster.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Series, drafts, adoption with settings decisions, supersession
- [ ] Meetings with agenda, decisions, actions, assembled minutes
- [ ] In-app signing with MFA, hash, signature page; `SignatureProvider`
      interface
- [ ] Adopted documents archived, never deleted; erasure leaves them
- [ ] Tenant isolation, allow/deny, matrix rows, budgets, audit, history
- [ ] Translations sv, en, nb; docs (ADR 0037 and 0049 "As implemented",
      `domain-model.md`, privacy notice)

## Out of scope
- BankID, Freja, EU wallet providers; clubs requiring a level.
- Automatic board membership from club roles.
- Voting in the app.
