# P2.7b — Web fixes from testing (navigation, search, forms, demo data)

To do after P2.7 is complete. Findings from manual testing of the web app
with the demo data on 2026-11-19.

## Context
Read first: `CLAUDE.md`, `docs/seed-data.md`, ADR 0022 (person visibility),
ADR 0046 (roles and permissions), ADR 0055 (updates in place, one tap).
What exists already: the app frame (`apps/web/src/components/app-frame.tsx`:
main navigation, the "Viewing as" menu from `/me/wards`), the roles pages
(`apps/web/src/app/(app)/clubs/[clubId]/roles/`, "Who can …?" in
`roles/holders`), the role name schema (`RoleTextsSchema` in
`packages/shared`: `sv`, `en`, `nb`), the event form
(`apps/web/src/components/event-fields.tsx`, `RowList`).

## Goal
The problems seen in testing are fixed, each with a test that would have
caught it.

20. **Leaders cannot answer whether they are coming.** Today (ADR 0035,
    `apps/api/src/modules/activities/attendance.service.ts`) only the
    roster's *participants* may answer: those whose group role holds
    `group.participate` (ADR 0046; in the built-in template the role
    `member`). Those whose role holds `group.lead` (head coach, assistant
    coach, team manager, physio, other leader) are *leaders*, who can
    neither answer nor are asked. So coaches cannot say they are coming to
    a training or match, and in a board or committee group only those with
    a participating role are asked to a meeting. Rule from now on: **everyone called to
    an activity, in any role and for every kind (training, match, meeting,
    other), answers whether they are coming**, as long as the activity asks
    for answers (`response_mode` `optional` or `requested`). Do:
    - Amend ADR 0035 (a section "Leaders answer too", decided 2026-11-19):
      the roster's leaders answer for themselves (and a guardian for a
      ward who is a young leader, with `sign_up`); answers keep the role
      they were given in. Record why leaders are not simply given the
      function `member` as well (considered and rejected 2026-11-19):
      a participating role (`group.participate`) means *participant*, and places and waiting lists (only
      participating roles take a place), grant reports (participants by age;
      a session needs a leader), attendance statistics per member,
      call-ups, duty and car-pool quotas all rely on that; a second
      group role per leader would also have to be kept in step. *Who is
      asked* is separated from *the role on the roster* instead: every role
      answers, the role stays as it is, counts are shown per role. In a
      board or committee group where everyone is an equal member, the
      members simply hold a participating role (possible today).
    - API: accept answers from the roster's leaders; the summary counts
      participants and leaders separately (`counts.leaders` with attending,
      not attending, not answered; existing fields unchanged, ADR 0029);
      the leaders' list shows the leaders' answers; reminders for answers
      (P1.7, `requested`) and the "not answered" notices include leaders;
      call-ups (#19, `set-invitees`) may include leaders, and an activity
      whose call-up leaves leaders out does not ask them.
    - Attendance: a leader's "coming" pre-fills their mark as present, "not
      coming" as excused, as for participants (ADR 0035).
    - Web and mobile: leaders see the one-tap answer (finding 18) on the
      activity, in "Mitt schema" and on the home page; the activity page
      shows "Ledare: 2 av 3 kommer" next to the participants' counts.
    - Grant reports and attendance hints are unchanged (they read marks,
      not answers).
    - Tests: a head coach answers a training and a match; a board member
      with a leader function answers a board meeting; counts split by role; a leader left out of
      a call-up is not asked; the reminder reaches a leader who has not
      answered; matrix rows unchanged (same endpoints). Playwright: Erik
      (SVK F2017 head coach) taps "Kommer" on next training.
    - Docs: ADR 0035, `docs/domain-model.md` (responses), `docs/seed-data.md`
      (Erik's and Sara's answers in the demo, and a board meeting where the
      board answers).

22. **No overview of roles and permissions in the documentation.** Since
    P1.15 (ADR 0046) what a person may do comes only from permissions held
    through roles (club roles and group roles, "functions" in older text);
    a participant is a group role with `group.participate`, a leader one
    with `group.lead`. The catalogue and the built-in template are in code
    (`packages/shared/src/authorization/permission-catalogue.ts`,
    `BUILT_IN_ROLE_TEMPLATE`), but no document lists them. Do:
    - Generate `docs/roles-and-permissions.md` from the catalogue and the
      template (a script in `packages/devtools`; a unit test fails when the
      file is out of date): every permission with its scope, area, the
      sentence and example in Swedish and English, and a table of the
      template's club roles and group roles with their permissions; link it
      from `docs/domain-model.md` and `README.md`.
    - Use "group role" consistently in the docs where "function" still
      means a group role (keep `function` only for the legacy column).
    - Role keys that still decide something are intentional and listed in
      the document with their reason: the locked `club_manager` (the last
      administrator cannot leave or be removed, the club's creator gets it,
      the invitation preset "administrator", ADR 0018/0046 A3) and `chair`
      (recovering a club without administrators). Everything else that
      names a role only labels it (the access log, "ask the treasurer",
      audit wording); `clubadmin/no-role-names` keeps it that way. Check
      the club page's "managers" list (`clubs.controller.ts`, by the role
