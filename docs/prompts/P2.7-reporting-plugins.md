# P2.7 — Plugins for grant reporting and background checks; LOK and LAM

## Context
Read first: `CLAUDE.md`, ADR 0051 (this prompt's decisions), ADR 0015
(country packs), ADR 0047 (background checks for writing to children, a
club may only be stricter), ADR 0050 (duties that need a check), ADR 0035
(attendance), ADR 0041 (privacy, settings registry), ADR 0012 and
`docs/licensing.md` (what may be added), `docs/gdpr.md`, `docs/security.md`.

What exists already:

- Activities and series (ADR 0034), attendance with leaders and grant
  hints in the attendance view (P1.5: `AttendanceService` asks
  `getCountryPack(club.country).grantRules.check(session)`, `GrantHint` in
  `packages/shared` `attendance.ts`).
- The Swedish pack's LOK rules (`packages/country-packs/src/se/index.ts`)
  follow RF's rules of 15 January 2023 (ages 7–25, three participants, 45
  minutes, a leader of 13). **They are out of date**: RF's rules of 22 May
  2025 apply to activities from 1 July 2025 (below).
- Members with birth dates, encrypted personal identity numbers (field-level
  encryption), groups with `sport`, club roles and group roles (P1.15),
  `PaymentProvider` in `packages/server-kit` as a model of a provider
  interface.
- No background-check tables yet (`docs/gdpr.md` lists them as planned).

## Goal
A plugin contract that we and third parties use to provide grant reporting
and background checks per country, region or municipality, and the first two
grant plugins: Sweden's LOK-stöd (with the 2025 rules) and Norway's LAM
(membership counts for "samordnet rapportering"). A club enables the plugins
it needs; its registrar sees, per period, what counts for the grant and why,
fixes missing data before the deadline, locks the period and exports or
submits the report. The background-check side of the contract is built and
proven with test plugins; the Swedish and Norwegian check plugins and their
screens follow in P2.8.

## The contract (`packages/plugin-api`)
Design it from ADR 0051; this is the minimum. Types are Zod schemas (shared
with the OpenAPI where they cross the API) so a plugin's input and output
can be validated at the boundary.

- **Manifest:** `id` (reverse-DNS, e.g. `se.rf.lok`), `version`,
  `publisher`, `licence` (SPDX), `pluginApi` (semver range), `capabilities`,
  `regions` (ISO 3166-1/-2 codes, municipality codes allowed), `data`
  (declared fields from a closed list: `birthDate`, `sex`,
  `personalIdentityNumber`, `disability`, `homeMunicipality`,
  `membershipStatus`, `membershipFeePaid`, `groupSport`, `attendance`,
  `leaders`, `clubRoles`, `groupRoles`), `configSchema` (per-club
  settings, e.g. the RF organisation number or a municipality),
  `secrets` (names only), `network` (allowed hosts), `texts` (sv, en, nb
  for every label and reason code).
- **Host context** given to every call: the club (ID, country, time zone,
  locale), its validated configuration, decrypted secrets, a clock, a
  logger that rejects personal data, an HTTP client limited to `network`,
  a key-value state per club and plugin, and the declared data only.
- **`grant-reporting`:** `schemes(ctx)` → scheme definitions (key, name,
  basis `session` | `membership_snapshot` | `leader`, `periods(year)` with
  from, to, deadline and late rules, required fields, outputs);
  `evaluateSession(ctx, scheme, session)` → reason codes for the live hint;
  `evaluatePeriod(ctx, scheme, period, sessions, people)` → per session and
  person what counts and why, totals, and missing data per person and
  field; `snapshot(ctx, scheme, referenceDate, people)` for membership
  schemes → counts per age band, sex, sport, residence;
  `export(ctx, scheme, report, format)` → files (name, media type, bytes);
  `submit(ctx, scheme, report)` and `submissionStatus(ctx, receipt)` for
  integrations.
- **`background-checks`:** `checkTypes(ctx)` (initiator, maximum age when
  presented, re-check interval, minimum age, copy retention, verification
  methods, cost, integration status); `requirements(ctx)` per duty category
  with level and basis; `startRequest`, `requestStatus` (club-initiated,
  with consent states); `verifyPresented(ctx, input)` (person-initiated:
  any code is used during the call and returned to nobody); results carry
  only the metadata ADR 0051 allows.
- **Registry and runner** in the API: plugins installed for the
  installation (configuration lists them; operators enable), checked against
  the licence allow list and the plugin-API version at start; calls run with
  a timeout, validated input and output, errors contained and audited.
  In-process only in this prompt; keep the interface transport-agnostic.

## Data model
- `club_plugins` (club_id, plugin_id, enabled, config jsonb validated by the
  manifest's schema, secrets encrypted with the field-level helpers):
  tenant RLS, history (secrets redacted), updated_at.
- `plugin_state` (club_id, plugin_id, key, value jsonb): tenant RLS,
  history.
- `grant_reports` (club_id, plugin_id, scheme_key, period_from, period_to,
  status `draft` | `locked` | `exported` | `submitted` | `accepted` |
  `rejected`, the evaluation (jsonb, IDs and codes only), totals,
  locked_by/at, submission receipt and status): tenant RLS, history.
  Changes to attendance in a locked period are flagged on the report.
- `background_checks` (club_id, person_id, check_type, issuing_country,
  issued_on, verified_on, verified_by_person_id, method, valid_until,
  superseded_by_id, closed_reason_code): tenant RLS, history, person
  references for merging; never documents, pictures, reference codes or
  negative outcomes (ADR 0051).
- `background_check_requests` (club_id, person_id, plugin_id, check_type,
  status, plugin reference, IDs only): tenant RLS, history.
- The club's stricter background-check setting (ADR 0047) in the settings
  registry: a club may raise a requirement level or shorten an interval,
  never lower or lengthen.
- Every table: `history.enable_history`, `app.enable_tenant_rls`,
  `app.enable_updated_at`; erasure (`app.erase_person`) removes a person's
  checks and requests and anonymises them in reports.

## The LOK plugin (`plugins/se-lok`)
- RF's rules **versioned by the activity's date**: 2023 rules before
  2025-07-01, 2025 rules from then: a group activity counts with at least one
  appointed leader of 13 or older, at least 3 participants active at the same
  time and at least 45 minutes; participants who turn 6–25 in the calendar
  year (no upper age with a disability), leaders who turn 13–25 count as
  participants too; at most 20 participants per leader present; at most one
  group activity per person, sport and day; at most 3 (ages 6–9) or 5
  (10–25) per person, sport and calendar week. **Read RF's rules of 22 May
  2025 before coding them** (link in ADR 0051's research; the network
  allow list of the development environment may need rf.se).
- Periods 1 Jan–30 Jun (deadline 25 Aug) and 1 Jul–31 Dec (deadline
  25 Feb), 25 % less when one week late, refused later.
- Outputs: the period's report as CSV and PDF (sessions, people, what counts
  and why) for checking and manual registration; the municipal XML file
  ("kommunfil") if its format can be confirmed; the RF integration platform
  as an integration behind configuration, **stubbed** until a vendor
  agreement and specification exist (a `decision-needed` issue).
- Replaces the Swedish pack's `grantRules`: the attendance view's hints come
  from `evaluateSession`; with the plugin disabled there are no hints.
- Swedish municipal variants (e.g. 60 minutes, residence) are configuration
  of the same plugin or plugins of their own: show one variant as a test.

## The LAM plugin (`plugins/no-lam`)
- Membership snapshot for "samordnet rapportering": members and active
  members on 31 December, of them aged 6–19, parasport and children's sport
  school, per sport; reported 1–30 April.
- Output: the counts as CSV and PDF for entering in the federation's
  system; no integration yet.

## Permissions
New in the catalogue: `ops.plugins.manage` (built-in, operators, MFA),
`club.plugins.manage` (club managers), `club.grants.read` and
`club.grants.report` (registrars, treasurers; the template keys get them by
default), `club.background_checks.manage` (ADR 0051 table in the roadmap).
Allow and deny tests for each; matrix rows for every endpoint.

## UI
- Club settings → Plugins: the plugins for the club's country, enable,
  configure (generated from the manifest's schema), with their texts.
- Grants: per enabled scheme the current period and its deadline, the
  report (sessions and people, what counts and why, totals), missing data
  with links to fix it, lock, export, submit; reminders before deadlines
  (notifications, P1.7).
- The attendance view keeps its hints (now from the plugin).
- Operators: installed plugins and compatibility.

## Privacy and security
Plugins see only what they declare; personal identity numbers only for
plugins that declare them, decrypted for the call and never logged or kept
in reports (reports hold IDs and codes). Every export and submission is
audited. Plugin code is trusted (reviewed, licence allow-listed); network
calls only to declared hosts; secrets encrypted. Background checks follow
ADR 0051 (no documents, codes or negative outcomes). Update `gdpr.md`
(processing for grants, the plugins as part of the processor's service,
an integration's recipient as a recipient).

## Protected identity
Reports name people only through `PersonPresenter` in the UI; files sent to
a grant authority contain what the scheme requires (the authority is a
recipient by legal basis); protected people are included without addresses.

## Locale and country packs
Plugin texts in sv, en and nb (checked by the conformance suite). The
Swedish pack's `grantRules` is removed (ADR 0015 amended by ADR 0051).

## Performance
Evaluating a period for the load scenario's club of 10,000 members reads
sessions and attendance by the club and date indexes in a bounded number of
statements and runs the plugin in memory; a budget for the report endpoint;
the attendance view's budget unchanged.

## Tests (many; they are how the contract is judged)
The contract is good when plugins for many countries can be written against
it without changes. Build:

1. **A conformance suite** (`packages/plugin-api/testing`) every plugin
   runs: manifest valid, texts complete in sv/en/nb for every code, outputs
   valid against the schemas, determinism (same input, same output),
   only declared data used, no network outside `network`, timeouts handled.
2. **LOK scenario tests** with exact expectations: the 2023/2025 boundary
   (a period spanning 1 July 2025), ages counted by calendar year (6 and 25,
   7 before July 2025), disability without upper age, leaders 13–25 counted
   as participants, 3 participants exactly, 44/45 minutes, 20/21
   participants per leader and with two leaders, two sessions the same day
   and sport (one counts), different sports the same day, the weekly caps
   for 6–9 and 10–25 across Monday–Sunday, missing birth dates and missing
   personal identity numbers listed as missing data, late submission rules,
   a municipal variant with 60 minutes.
3. **LAM scenario tests:** counts on 31 December by age band and sport,
   members who joined or left around the date, active vs. passive members.
4. **Expressiveness tests with fixture plugins** (in the test tree, not
   shipped) proving the contract fits other countries: Denmark (members
   under 25 on a reference date, fee paid), Germany (A- and B-numbers per
   age, sex and sport; a leader-based scheme), the Netherlands (municipal
   youth grant with residence and a minimum of 20 youth members), Finland
   (active under 20 and led sessions per group).
5. **Background-check fixtures:** Sweden (the person presents, at most 6
   months old, code checked during the call and not stored), Norway (the
   club requests, consent states, from 15 years), Germany (3 months old,
   re-check after 5 years), France (federation feed, yearly), Ireland (the
   organisation receives the result), Spain (code lookup); the club's
   stricter setting; a negative outcome closes without a stored reason.
6. **Host tests:** data minimisation (a plugin without
   `personalIdentityNumber` never receives it), errors and timeouts contained,
   incompatible plugin-API versions refused at start, licence allow list,
   secrets never in logs or history, tenant isolation of every table and
   endpoint, allow/deny for every policy, erasure, request budgets.
7. **e2e:** a registrar in the Swedish demo club enables LOK, sees the
   period's report with reasons, fixes a missing birth date and exports.

## Seed data
SVK enables LOK with a period that has counting and non-counting sessions
(too short, too few, a second session the same day); FIL (NO) enables LAM;
`seed-data.md` names where to look.

## Out of scope
- The out-of-process (HTTP) transport and plugin marketplace.
- Background-check plugins for Sweden and Norway and their screens (P2.8).
- The RF integration itself (needs RF's agreement), Danish CFR, German
  LSB exports as real plugins.
- Paying grants or fees.
