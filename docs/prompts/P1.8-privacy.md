## Goal
A parent decides, per child and club, whether the club may publish photos
of the child, stream matches or use the email for marketing, reads exactly
the text they agree to, and withdraws it again with one click; a teenager
from the consent age decides for themselves. Anyone downloads everything
the system holds about them and their children as a file within minutes.
A member leaves a club themselves and sees what the club keeps and for how
long. A person deletes their account and is anonymised everywhere,
permanently, also after a restore. The retention periods in `gdpr.md` are
enforced by nightly jobs, each club sees its rules on one page and sets the
configurable ones within the legal limits, and the documentation lists the
same rules as the code.

## Users and roles involved
Members with their own login (their own consents, export, leaving, account
deletion); full guardians (`parent`, `legal_guardian`) with
`act_on_behalf` (for wards: consents below the consent age, export,
leaving, erasure of a ward without login); linked guardians (relatives,
step and foster parents, contact persons) never; young people from the
consent age (their own consents); registrars (record a consent given on a
paper form, see consent status); club managers (consent texts, enabled
purposes, retention settings, with MFA); group leaders and owners (consent
status of their group, so they know whom not to photograph); the worker as
system and as `clubadmin_retention`; platform operators (no access to club
data; the ledger and the jobs' metrics only).

## User stories
- As a guardian, I want to see per child and club which consents the club
  asks for, with the club's text, and give or withdraw each one, so that I
  decide what is published about my child.
- As a 14-year-old with my own login, I want to give or withdraw my own
  photo consent, and see that my parent gave one before I turned 13.
- As a member, I want consents to be withdrawn as easily as they were
  given, with immediate effect.
- As a registrar, I want to record a consent a parent gave on the paper
  membership form, with a note, so that the register is complete.
- As a club manager, I want to choose which purposes the club asks for and
  publish the club's own text for each purpose, so that members agree to
  what we actually do; a new text version does not silently cover old
  consents.
- As a coach, I want to see which players in my group lack photo consent
  before the cup, without seeing anyone I am not allowed to see.
- As a member, I want to download everything about me (and my children) in
  a readable and a machine-readable form, so that I can check it or take it
  elsewhere.
- As a member or guardian, I want to leave a club myself, and see what
  ends now and what the club keeps and for how long.
- As a person, I want to delete my account; I want a few days to change my
  mind, and afterwards nothing that identifies me should remain, not even
  in a restored backup.
- As a full guardian, I want to erase my child (without a login) from the
  system when we stop using it.
- As a club manager, I want to see the club's retention rules on one page
  and set the ones the club may change, within the allowed limits.
- As a former member, I want the club to stop holding my contact details
  once the retention period after I left has passed.
- As the maintainer, I want every retention rule in `gdpr.md` to have a
  job and every job to be in `gdpr.md`, checked by CI.

## Data model
Club-owned tables: `club_id`, tenant RLS, record history, `updated_at`
(CLAUDE.md rule 3). Global tables: RLS with `app.*` helpers (ADR 0023).
Person references in `privacy.references.ts` (merging covers them).
A new ADR (the next free number) records the decisions of this package.

- **`consents` (expand):** `source` (`self`, `guardian`, `club_record`;
  default `self` for existing rows, backfilled from `given_by_person_id`),
  `club_note` (max 500 characters, `club_record` only: "paper form
  2026-08-14"; never in audit events), `revoked_reason` (`withdrawn`,
  `left_club`, `superseded`, `erased`, `not_applicable`; the enum may grow,
  ADR 0029). `text_version` now names a published text: `default:<n>`
  (platform default version `n`) or `club:<n>` (the club's version `n`).
  Revoking sets `revoked_at`, `revoked_by_person_id`, `revoked_reason`;
  rows are never deleted by the app roles (revoke DELETE from
  `clubadmin_app`), only by the former-member purge. Index
  `consents_club_purpose_idx (club_id, purpose, person_id) WHERE revoked_at
  IS NULL` for group overviews (the unique index starts with `person_id`
  after `club_id` and does not serve "who in this group lacks X").
- **Platform default texts:** `packages/shared/src/consent-texts.ts`: per
  purpose the current default version and the message key of each version
  (`consents.texts.photo_publication.v1`, …) in sv, en and nb. A version is
  never edited once released (unit test against a committed hash list); a
  new wording is a new version.
- **`consent_texts` (club-owned):** a club's own text for a purpose:
  `purpose`, `version` (integer, per club and purpose, from 1), `texts`
  (JSONB `{ sv?, en?, nb? }`, each 20–4000 characters, at least the club's
  default locale), `published_by_person_id`, `published_at`. Unique
  `(club_id, purpose, version)`. Immutable after insert (UPDATE and DELETE
  revoked from the app roles). History.
- **`clubs` (expand, defaults keep rows valid):**
  `consent_purposes` (text[], the purposes the club asks for; default
  `{photo_publication, video_publication}`; `marketing` only when the club
  sends marketing, `streaming` when it streams), `consent_reconfirm_on_new_version`
  (boolean, default false: a new text version leaves open consents valid;
  true: open consents of the older version are revoked with `superseded`
  and the family is asked again), `former_member_retention_months`
  (integer, default 24, check 6–120).
- **`data_exports` (global):** "download my data": `subject_person_id`,
  `requested_by_person_id`, `status` (`queued`, `ready`, `failed`,
  `expired`), `object_key` (`exports/people/<id>.zip`, under the existing
  `exports/` lifecycle rule), `size_bytes`, `locale`, `expires_at` (seven
  days after ready), `error_code`. RLS: SELECT and INSERT for the requester
  (`requested_by_person_id = app.current_person_id()`, subject self or a
  ward per `app.is_full_guardian_of`), `app.is_system()` for the worker.
  Insert trigger `app.enqueue_data_export` queues `privacy:export`.
  Index `(requested_by_person_id, created_at DESC)`. History. Rows are kept
  12 months as the record that an access request was answered, then
  deleted by `privacy:retention`.
- **`erasure_requests` (global):** `person_id`, `requested_by_person_id`,
  `kind` (`account_deletion`, `ward_erasure`), `status` (`scheduled`,
  `cancelled`, `running`, `done`, `failed`), `scheduled_for` (now + the
  grace period), `include_ward_ids` (uuid[]: wards without login the
  requester chose to erase with their account), `cancelled_by_person_id`,
  `cancelled_at`, `done_at`, `error_code`. One open request per person
  (partial unique index `WHERE status IN ('scheduled', 'running')`); index
  `(scheduled_for) WHERE status = 'scheduled'` for the job. RLS: the person
  and their full guardians; system. IDs only, no personal values. History.
  Kept 24 months after `done_at`, then deleted (the ledger keeps the fact).
- **`club_profiles` (expand):** `club_data_purged_at` (timestamptz): the
  former-member purge ran for this person and club (the job is idempotent
  and a re-joining member gets a fresh start). Index
  `club_memberships_left_idx (club_id, valid_to) WHERE status = 'left'`
  for the purge's candidate query.
- **Retention registry:** `packages/shared/src/retention.ts` lists every
  retention rule once: `key`, the data it covers, `defaultMonths` (or hours
  or days), `min`/`max` and the club setting (column) if configurable, the
  job that enforces it, and whether it is implemented yet. The web's club
  privacy page, `GET /clubs/:clubId/privacy` and a docs test read it (every
  key appears in the `gdpr.md` table and vice versa; every job named exists
  in the worker's task list). Rules for modules that do not exist yet
  (chat, accounting, background checks, waiting lists of P1.10) are listed
  as `planned`.
- **Former-member purge (`privacy.purge_former_member(club, person)`,
  SECURITY INVOKER, run as `clubadmin_retention`):** for a person with no
  open relationship in the club (no active or paused membership, role,
  group function, employment, ownership, pending join request or waiting
  entry) whose last membership ended more than the club's
  `former_member_retention_months` ago: clear `club_profiles.shared_fields`,
  `emergency_contacts` and `notes` (the member number, the membership
  dates and type, group memberships and roles stay as minimised facts for
  annual reports and grant reports; `protected_identity_club` stays);
  delete the person's consents in the club; clear the notes on their
  answers, `join_requests.message`/`decision_message` and waiting-list
  notes in the club; scrub those values in the club's history rows; set
  `club_data_purged_at`; audit `person.club_data.purged` (club, person,
  field names). A person without login, with no open relationship in any
  club, every club's data purged and no open guardianship (as ward or
  guardian) is then erased with `privacy.erase_person(..., 'retention')`.
- **`privacy.erase_person` (CREATE OR REPLACE in a new migration; never
  edit 0005 or 0012):** additionally clears `join_requests.message` and
  `decision_message` of the person's requests,
  `group_waitlist_entries.applicant_note`, `guardianships.consent_note` and
  `ended_reason` where the person is ward or guardian, club `invitations`
  whose `email` was the person's account email (replaced like guardian
  invitations), unaccepted `guardian_invitations` and
  `account_invitations` to that email; revokes open consents
  (`revoked_reason = erased`); scrubs the same keys in the history; and
  extends `privacy.unerased_people()` with the new checks. Grants for
  `clubadmin_retention` on exactly the columns it changes. Stays
  idempotent; the ledger replay test covers the new tables.

Under `/api/v1`. Writes refused for clubs that are not active (ADR 0031),
except leaving, withdrawing consents, exports and erasure (a person's
rights do not depend on the club's subscription). Problem codes, never
values: `consent_purpose_not_asked`, `consent_not_applicable`,
`consent_age_required`, `consent_already_given`, `consent_text_outdated`,
`last_club_manager`, `not_a_member`, `export_limit_reached`,
`export_expired`, `erasure_pending`, `erasure_blocked`, `ward_has_login`,
`recent_login_required`, ….

## Permissions
New permissions in `packages/shared/src/permissions.ts`, one policy each
in `authorization.policy.ts`, allow and deny tests, entries in the
permission matrix (tenant isolation: a user of club B gets 404 for club A):
- `person.consents.read` (person, `?clubId`): the person; full guardians
  with `act_on_behalf`; club managers and registrars; owners and leaders of
  the person's groups (the service returns status only, no history, to
  them). Not members of the same group, not relatives, not support access.
- `person.consents.manage` (person, `?clubId`): the person (the service
  requires the person to have reached the club's country pack
  `consentAge`); full guardians with `act_on_behalf` (the service allows
  giving only while the ward is under `consentAge`, and withdrawing until
  `adultAge`); registrars and club managers (the service allows only
  `club_record` with a note, and withdrawing on request). Never relatives,
  step or foster parents or contact persons, never leaders. With
  `X-On-Behalf-Of` only that ward.
- `group.consents.read` (group): the group's leaders (any non-member
  function) and owners (also of parent groups), club managers, registrars.
  Not members or guardians.
- Existing `club.settings.update` (club managers, with MFA) covers
  `PATCH /clubs/:clubId/privacy` and publishing consent texts; existing
  `club.read` (insiders) the privacy page and the texts: every member may
  read the club's rules.
- Tests: a 12-year-old with a login cannot give a consent (`consent_age_required`)
  but their parent can; at 13 they can, and the parent can still withdraw;
  Gunilla (relative) gets 403; a coach of F2017 sees status for F2017 but
  404 for a member of Herr senior he does not lead; a user of IKN gets 404

## Leaving a club
`POST /me/clubs/:clubId/leave` runs, in one tenant transaction:
`MembershipsService.leaveClub` (membership, group memberships, ownerships,
roles; `last_club_manager` refused), withdraws pending join requests and
open waiting-list entries in the club (P1.10 statuses; withdraw only),
revokes open consents (`left_club`), audits `membership.ended` with
`changedFields: ["status", "valid_to"]` and on-behalf-of, publishes
`membership.changed` (the authorization cache) and emails the club's
managers and registrars ("a member left"; the name through the mail
presenter, never for a protected member to someone who may not see them).
Guardianships are global and stay; the club stops seeing the family once
the ward is no longer a member (domain model). The club keeps the profile
and the history until `former_member_retention_months` after the end, then
the purge minimises it (above). Adults leave themselves; a minor's full
guardians leave for them (a minor with a login cannot leave alone; the
answer explains who can). Rejoining within the period keeps the member
number and profile.

Once no relationship to the club remains, the club's profile-view log
(P1.12, `profile_views`) is anonymised for the person, as viewer and as
viewed person, and recorded in `privacy.anonymization_ledger`, which the
backup restore replays like the deletion ledger. If P1.12 is not yet
implemented, P1.12 adds this hook; if it is, leaving through this endpoint
must trigger it (the outbox handler on `membership.changed`).

## Account deletion and erasure
- `POST /me/account/deletion` creates an `erasure_requests` row
  (`account_deletion`, grace period 14 days), audits
  `account.deletion.scheduled`, emails the account ("your account will be
  deleted on …; log in to cancel"). The account works during the grace
  period; the web and the app show a banner with "Cancel deletion".
- Blockers: being the last club manager of a club (hand over first, ADR
  0018). Wards without login whose only full guardian with a login is the
  caller: the caller chooses per ward to erase them too or to keep them
  (they stay as people without a guardian with a login; clubs keep their
  memberships); the preview explains both.
- The worker's `privacy:erase` job (every ten minutes) takes due requests
  (`FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED`, status `running`) and, idempotently, per
  person (the requester, then each chosen ward): in each of the person's
  clubs (system transaction per club) leaves as above with the system as
  actor; ends open guardianships in which the person is guardian or ward
  (`guardianship.ended`); deletes the person's data export files and rows;
  then, as `clubadmin_retention`, `privacy.erase_person(person,
  'account_deleted' | 'ward_erased')`; then, after commit, deletes the
  Zitadel user (management API with the `clubadmin-api` machine user;
  `pnpm auth:configure` grants it the user-delete permission), revokes
  BFF sessions and deny-lists mobile sessions; then marks the request
  `done`. A failure after the database part leaves `error_code` and is
  retried; the database erasure is never rolled back. After a restore the
  ledger replay erases the person again; a Zitadel user that still exists
  cannot log in (no `idp_subject` match) and the replay report lists it.
- No email after the erasure (the address is gone, and job payloads carry
  IDs only); the scheduling email names the date.
- Ward erasure (`/me/wards/:personId/erasure`) follows the same path for a
  ward without login; every other full guardian is emailed and may cancel
  during the grace period.
- Erased people disappear from the register (`anonymized_at` filter
  exists), the presenter shows them as "Deleted user" where an ID remains
  (history, attendance), and they are counted in nothing new.

## "Download my data"
- `privacy:export` (per request, insert trigger) builds a ZIP in the
  requester's language: `data.json` (machine-readable, documented schema
  `docs/api/data-export.md`, versioned) and `index.html` (human-readable,
  self-contained, no external resources, no scripts) with a `README` page
  explaining each section, the controller of each part (the club, or the
  platform for the account), and how to ask a club for more.
- Contents for the subject: the person row (own fields), the account
  (email, locale, time zone, preferences; never the IdP subject or
  sessions), per club (only clubs where the subject has or had a
  relationship): club profile (member number, shared fields, emergency
  contacts, protected flag), memberships, group memberships with functions,
  roles, employments, consents with the text versions they agreed to (the
  text itself included), join requests and waiting-list entries, answers
  (with their notes) and attendance, call-ups; guardianships with the other
  party's name; invitations the subject sent; data exports and erasure
  requests; the record history of the subject's own rows (who changed what,
  actors through the presenter). Not included: club notes about the person
  (`club_profiles.notes`, restricted; the README says the club answers a
  full access request), other people's contact details, audit IP hashes,
  backups.
- Wards: a full guardian's export of a ward contains the ward's data as
  above; one request per subject (the UI offers "me and my children" as
  several requests).
- Size: written in batches to a multipart upload (as `members:export`);
  never held in memory. The file is deleted after seven days
  (`privacy:expire_exports`, hourly) and by the `exports/` lifecycle rule;
  the row stays as the record. A failed export gets an `error_code`; the
  requester may retry.

## Retention jobs
New worker tasks (registered in `tasks/index.ts`, crontab and the
`architecture.md` table; payloads IDs only; logs counts only):

| Task | Schedule | Does |
|---|---|---|
| `privacy:export` | per export (insert trigger) | Writes a "download my data" file |
| `privacy:expire_exports` | hourly | Deletes export files seven days after ready; fails exports still queued after two hours |
| `privacy:erase` | every 10 minutes | Runs due erasure requests (above) |
| `privacy:retention` | daily 05:31 UTC | Per club: former members past `former_member_retention_months` (`privacy.purge_former_member`, batches of 500); then people without login and without any remaining relationship (erased); `data_exports` rows after 12 months; `erasure_requests` after 24 months |
| `audit:retention` | daily 05:43 UTC | Deletes `audit.events` older than `AUDIT_RETENTION_MONTHS` (installation setting, default 24, at least 12), in batches of 10,000 |

The worker gets a second database handle for `DATABASE_RETENTION_URL`
(`deps.retentionDb`, used only by `privacy:*` and `audit:retention`;
`withSystem` on it); without it these jobs log an error and do nothing,
and `/health/ready` of the worker reports it. `HISTORY_RETENTION_MONTHS`
stays an installation setting (partitions cannot be dropped per club).

## Privacy and security
- Legal bases (record in `gdpr.md` and `domain-model.md`): consents are
  Art. 6(1)(a) (and Art. 8 for minors through the country pack's consent
  age); exports, leaving and erasure are the data subject's rights
  (Art. 15, 17, 20); retention is storage limitation (Art. 5(1)(e)); the
  former-member period exists for re-joining and annual and grant reports
  (legitimate interest, documented per rule in the registry).
- Withdrawal is as easy as giving (one action, no confirmation dialog
  beyond the text) and takes effect immediately: publishes
  `consent.changed` (IDs and purpose) for later consumers (photos,
  streaming, marketing) and the register's missing-consent filter reflects
  it on the next request.
- Audit events (register in `audit-actions.ts`), in the same transaction:
  `consent.given`, `consent.withdrawn`, `consent.recorded` (club record;
  never the note), `consent_text.published`, `membership.ended` (leaving,
  existing), `privacy.export.requested`, `privacy.export.downloaded`,
  `account.deletion.scheduled`, `account.deletion.cancelled`,
  `person.erasure.scheduled` (ward), `person.erasure.cancelled`,
  `person.club_data.purged`, `person.erased` (existing). Each with actor and
  on-behalf-of. Audit events of global actions keep `club_id` NULL.
- Domain events with IDs only: `consent.changed`, `privacy.export.ready`
  (mail), `erasure.scheduled` and `erasure.cancelled` (mail to the
  requester and the other full guardians), `person.erased` (bumps the
  authorization cache; later modules such as chat react).
- The export file is the most sensitive artefact in the system: only the
  requester downloads it, with a recent login, through a five-minute link;
  never emailed (the email says it is ready and links to the page); never
  logged; ZIP entries contain IDs and values but the object key holds only
  the export ID.
- Personal data never in logs, job payloads or outbox events. Retention
  jobs log counts per club ID.
- `clubadmin_retention` gets exactly the grants it needs (listed in the
  migration); a test asserts the app role still cannot call
  `privacy.erase_person` or delete from `consents`, `audit.events` or the
  history.

## Protected identity
- Consent lists for groups name only people the viewer may see
  (PersonPresenter); protected members are counted and never named for
  others. Leaders who may see them see them with the crossed-camera icon.
- Photo, video and streaming consents are not applicable to a protected
  person (effective flag in that club): the UI shows "Not photographed or
  streamed (protected identity)" instead of buttons and the API refuses
  `consent_not_applicable`; existing consents are revoked
  (`not_applicable`) when the flag is set (in the protection change's
  transaction; `person.protection.changed` already exists). Marketing and
  federation sharing stay possible.
- Leave notices to managers go through the mail presenter; the export names
  other people (guardians, co-guardians, those who recorded attendance)
  only as the subject may see them, as the API would.
- History and audit rows of erased people show "Deleted user" (presenter
  change in P1.9; here the people row is anonymised and the names are
  empty).

## Locale and country packs
- Strings in sv, en and nb: purposes, statuses, default consent texts (v1
  for each purpose, reviewed for plain language and the age of the reader),
  the export's HTML and README, emails (export ready, deletion scheduled,
  ward erasure scheduled, member left), retention rule names and hints,
  problem codes.
- Country packs decide `consentAge` (who gives a consent for a minor) and
  may add `retentionLimits` (min/max per registry key; `se`: former members
  at least 6 months; nothing stricter yet for `no`). The core reads the
  limits from the club's pack, never from country codes; the strictest of
  the registry and the pack applies.

## Performance
- `GET /me/consents` < 50 ms p95 (one statement over the caller's and
  wards' memberships and `consents_person_idx`).
- `GET /groups/:groupId/consents` for a team of 25 < 50 ms, for
  Storklubben's largest group < 100 ms (`consents_club_purpose_idx`).
- Export of a person with five years of attendance (about 400 marks) in
  under a minute; memory bounded by batches.
- `privacy:retention` on the load scenario (1,000 clubs, 200,000 people,
  every 20th membership left three years ago) under ten minutes, no
  sequential scans on `club_memberships` (`club_memberships_left_idx`) or
  the history (scrubbing by `record_changes_record_idx`); query-plan test.
- `audit:retention` deletes in batches with an index on
  `audit.events (created_at)` (new; the club index starts with `club_id`),
  so it never holds long locks.
- `privacy.erase_person` for a person with history in three clubs < 1 s
  (extend `privacy.integration.test.ts`).

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] A guardian gives and withdraws a ward's consents per club with the
      club's current text; a young person from the consent age does it
      themselves; relatives and leaders cannot (403); registrars record a
      paper consent with a note.
- [ ] A new club text version is a new version; consents keep the version
      they were given for; with reconfirm on, older open consents are
      revoked `superseded` in the same transaction.
- [ ] Protected persons cannot get photo, video or streaming consents, and
      setting the flag revokes them.
- [ ] Leaders see their group's consent status; protected members are only
      counted for viewers who may not see them.
- [ ] A member leaves a club themselves (a guardian for a minor); groups,
      roles, ownerships, pending requests, waiting entries and consents end;
      the last manager is refused; the club's managers are emailed.
- [ ] "Download my data" produces a ZIP (JSON and HTML) in the requester's
      language within minutes, for self and wards, downloadable only by the
      requester with a recent login, deleted after seven days.
- [ ] Account deletion: preview with blockers, a 14-day grace period that
      can be cancelled, then `privacy.erase_person`, ended guardianships and
      memberships, deleted export files, the Zitadel user deleted and all
      sessions revoked; chosen wards erased too; idempotent on retry.
- [ ] `privacy.erase_person` covers the new columns; the ledger replay test
      erases a person again after a restore, including the new tables;
      `privacy.unerased_people()` stays empty.
- [ ] `privacy:retention` purges former members after the club's period
      and erases orphaned people without login; `audit:retention` deletes

- [ ] Clubs see their retention rules and set the configurable ones within
      the registry's and the country pack's limits.
- [ ] A docs test fails when a registry rule is missing from `gdpr.md` or a
      named job does not exist.
- [ ] Performance budgets met (query-plan and timing tests on the load
      scenario).
- [ ] Tenant-isolation test for every new endpoint
- [ ] Allow/deny tests for every new policy
- [ ] Audit events for every change to consents, memberships, exports and
      erasure
- [ ] Translations complete for sv and en (and nb)
- [ ] Docs updated: `gdpr.md` (retention table from the registry, rights as
      implemented, legal bases), `domain-model.md` (`consents`,

## Out of scope
- Effects of consents in modules that do not exist yet (photo galleries,
  streaming, marketing email, federation export); they consume
  `consent.changed` later.
- The History panel and audit log UI (P1.9); the profile-view log, its
  anonymisation on leaving and its ledger (P1.12).
- Erasing or exporting chat messages, documents, accounting records and
  background-check metadata (their modules add themselves to the export,
  the erasure function and the registry).
- Waiting-list retention (closed entries after 12 months, P1.10).
- Dormant accounts (no login and no club for years): a later issue.
- The record of processing activities (ROPA) template per club.
- A club manager erasing a person or purging a former member early; an
  operator console for erasure requests.
- Streaming audit events to separate storage (`security.md`, "Audit").

## Open decisions
1. **Grace period before account deletion.** Recommended: 14 days,
   cancellable, account usable meanwhile; an installation setting
   (`ERASURE_GRACE_DAYS`, 0–30) for self-hosters.
2. **Who decides consents between the consent age and the adult age.**
   Recommended: the young person gives and withdraws their own consents
   from `consentAge`; full guardians may still withdraw (never give) until
   `adultAge`, and a guardian's consent given before the consent age stays
   valid until someone withdraws it.
3. **Wards when a guardian deletes their account.** Recommended: wards
   without login and without another full guardian with a login are listed
   in the preview and erased only if the guardian chooses so per ward;
   default choice in the UI is "erase too".
4. **Former-member period and limits.** Recommended: default 24 months,
   club setting 6–120 months; memberships, group memberships, roles and
   the member number stay as minimised facts after the purge (no names are
   added to them), contact data, notes and consents go.
5. **Automatic erasure of orphaned people.** Recommended: a person without
   login, with no open relationship anywhere, every club's data purged and
   no open guardianship is erased by `privacy:retention`; people with a
   login are never erased automatically in this package.
6. **Club notes in "download my data".** Recommended: excluded from the
   self-service export (they may concern third parties); the README tells
   the person to ask the club, which answers a full access request.
7. **Export format.** Recommended: a ZIP with `data.json` and a
   self-contained `index.html`, no PDF (no new rendering dependency); one
   ZIP dependency with an allowed licence (e.g. `fflate`, MIT), checked by
   `pnpm licenses:check`.
8. **Club-specific consent texts.** Recommended: supported, optional; the
   platform default texts apply until a club publishes its own; texts are
   immutable versions; reconfirming on a new version is the club's choice
   (default off).
9. **Audit log retention.** Recommended: installation-wide
   `AUDIT_RETENTION_MONTHS` (default 24, at least 12), not a club setting;
   deletion in batches by a new `created_at` index rather than converting
   `audit.events` to a partitioned table now.
10. **Minors leaving a club.** Recommended: only full guardians leave for a
    minor; a minor with a login sees who can do it; adults leave
    themselves.
