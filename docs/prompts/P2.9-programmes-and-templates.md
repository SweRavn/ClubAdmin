# P2.9 — Programmes and templates (cups, camps, match days)

## Context
Read first: `CLAUDE.md`, ADR 0054 (this prompt's decisions), ADR 0034
(activities and the schedule read model), ADR 0038 (events and sign-up),
ADR 0050 and ADR 0052 (duty shifts are tasks), ADR 0040 (responsiveness),
ADR 0029 (`/api/v1` only grows), ADR 0046 (permissions in a catalogue).

What exists already:

- Activities of every kind (`training`, `match`, `meeting`, `other` with a
  `kindLabel` such as "Lunch", P1.6b) in one or more groups, with a place
  (`locations`) or a free-text place, meeting time and place, transport
  mode, response mode; location conflicts as warnings (P1.4).
- Events with sign-up whose sessions are activities (P1.6, ADR 0038),
  created together with the event (`activities` in `CreateEventSchema`),
  drafts that only organisers see.
- Duty shifts (P2.6): tasks of kind `duty_shift` with a type, a time slot, a
  place, the number needed, tied to an activity, an event or standalone, in
  a group or the club; the country rule on contact with children.
- P1.6 left "recurring events and copying an event to next year" out of
  scope; this prompt is where it lands.
- Tournaments with generated match schedules are Phase 4 and will fill
  programmes; this prompt does not generate matches.

## Goal
A team manager or a club manager builds a whole programme (a cup, a camp,
a match day, a cleaning day): days, halls and pitches, matches and other
activities, meals and other programme items, duty shifts, with or without
an event to sign up for. The programme is seen as one timeline. It can be
saved as a template and laid out again on new dates, so that next year's
cup is a few minutes' work: everything is copied except people.

## Users and roles involved
Team managers and coaches (their group's programmes and templates), club
managers (the club's programmes and templates, every group), members and
guardians (see the programme, as they see its activities today).

## User stories
- As a team manager, I build "Höstcupen": two days, two halls, eight
  matches, lunch and dinner both days, a kiosk and a score-table shift at
  every match, and sign-up for the players.
- As a team manager, I see the cup as a timeline per day and hall and spot
  a hall that is double booked.
- As a team manager, I save the cup as a template, and next year I lay it
  out on new dates, choose which groups play (P2012 is now a year older,
  the template asks for "Lag 1" and "Lag 2"), see a preview with
  warnings, and confirm; everything is created at once.
- As a coach, I save our usual home match day (match, kiosk, score table)
  as a template and use it for every home match of the season.
- As a coach, I copy last Saturday's programme to next Saturday without
  saving a template.
- As a parent, I open the cup in the app and see my child's matches, the
  meals and where everything is, and my own shifts.
- As a club manager, I make "Städdag" a club template that every team can
  use for its own groups.

## Decisions (ADR 0054)
1. **A programme is a container for activities and shifts**, any kind of
   activity, with or without an event. It has a title, a start and end
   date (derived from its items), the groups it is for, the template and
   edition it came from, and optionally the event it belongs to (an event
   gets a programme when it is laid out from a template or when its
   organiser opens the programme view; one event has at most one
   programme). Items are the existing activities and duty shifts with a
   `programme_id`; they keep all their behaviour (schedule, responses,
   notices, feeds, call-ups, car pools, duties).
2. **Meals and other programme items are activities** of kind `other` with
   a label ("Lunch", "Middag", "Invigning"), response mode `none` by
   default, so that they appear in the participants' schedules and on the
   timeline. No portions, special diets or catering orders (out of scope;
   diets are health data). A meal can have duty shifts like any activity
   (a "Matlag" duty type).
3. **Templates belong to the club** (never shared with other clubs): a
   group template (managed by those who manage the group's activities) or a
   club template (managed by club managers, usable by anyone who manages
   activities in a group of the club, for their own groups). A template
   stores **no people**: no participants, call-ups, responses,
   registrations, assignments, sign-ups, drivers or notes about people.
4. **Relative times in the club's time zone.** A template item has a day
   offset from the programme's first day (0–30) and a wall-clock start and
   end (end may be on a later day offset); laying out converts with the
   club's time zone, so a match at 09:00 stays at 09:00 across daylight
   saving time. Meeting time is stored as minutes before the start; the
   event's sign-up window as days before the first day.
5. **Group slots.** A template names its groups as slots ("Lag 1", "Lag 2",
   "Alla"), each with a default group; laying out maps every slot to a
   group of the club (default: the slot's default group if it still
   exists and is not archived). Activities and shifts follow their slots.
   A slot may map to several groups (a joint training).
6. **Places by reference.** Items keep the club's `location_id` (or the
   free-text place). Laying out refuses an archived location unless it is
   mapped to another, and warns about location conflicts with existing
   activities (the P1.4 conflict check), never blocking.
7. **Duty shifts copy type and need.** A shift item keeps its duty type,
   title, note, number needed and time relative to its activity (or to the
   day); laying out checks the duty rules (the country rule on contact
   with children, archived types) through the duties service.
8. **Saving as a template** from: a programme, an event (its sessions and
   shifts), the activities of a group in a date range (at most 31 days), or
   one activity with its shifts. The template is a snapshot: later changes
   to the source do not change it.
9. **Laying out is a preview, then one transaction.** The preview lists
   every item with its new time, groups and place, the warnings (location
   conflicts, a slot without a group, a past date) and the refusals
   (archived places or duty types, the country rule, missing
   permissions). Confirming creates the programme, the event (as a draft
   when the template has one), the activities and the shifts in one
   transaction through the existing services, with their audit events,
   history and notices (activities created in the future notify as they do
   today; a draft event's sessions do not until it is published). It is
   all or nothing. "Copy to new date" is the same with a template made on
   the fly and not kept.
10. **Editions.** A programme laid out from a template records the template
    and an edition label (default: the year, "Höstcupen 2027"); the
    template shows its earlier editions. Later changes to the template do
    not change programmes already laid out, and vice versa (no sync).
11. **Removing a programme** deletes it with all its items only while none
    of them has responses, registrations, assignments, sign-ups or
    car-pool offers (a mistake right after laying it out); otherwise items
    are cancelled one by one as today and the programme stays as their
    container.
12. **The timeline**: per day, a column per place (halls and pitches; items
    without a place in their own column), items as blocks with time,
    title, groups and the shifts' filled/needed; a list per day on narrow
    screens and in the app. Participants see the items they can see today
    (their groups' activities, their own and their family's shifts); the
    organisers see everything, with conflicts marked.

## Data model
Club-owned, tenant RLS (`app.enable_tenant_rls`), history
(`history.enable_history`), `updated_at`, UUIDv7, expand-only changes to
existing tables. Person references (`created_by`) are declared in
`programmes.references.ts`.

- `programme_templates` (club_id, group_id null for a club template, name,
  description, day_count, event_settings jsonb null: title, description,
  audience, capacity, waitlist mode, questions, price text, sign-up opening
  and closing as days before the first day; archived, created_by).
- `programme_template_slots` (template_id, club_id, key, label,
  default_group_id null; unique (template_id, key)).
- `programme_template_items` (template_id, club_id, kind `activity` |
  `duty_shift`, parent_item_id null (a shift on an activity item), sort,
  day_offset, start_time, end_day_offset, end_time, slot keys text[]; for
  activities: activity_kind, kind_label, title, notes, location_id,
  location_text, meet_minutes_before, meet_place, transport_mode,
  home_away, opponent, response_mode; for shifts: duty_type_id, title,
  note, needed). Check constraints per kind. At most 300 items per
  template (shared constant).
- `programmes` (club_id, title, template_id null, edition null, event_id
  null unique, first_day, last_day, created_by).
- `programme_groups` (programme_id, club_id, group_id) for "the programmes
  of my groups".
- `activities.programme_id` and `tasks.programme_id` (null, indexed on
  (programme_id, starts_at)).
- Erasure: templates and programmes hold no personal data beyond
  `created_by` (set to null by `privacy.erase_person`, as elsewhere).

## API
| Method | Path | Permission | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| GET | `/clubs/:clubId/programme-templates` | `group.activities.manage` in some group or `club.programmes.manage` | The group's and the club's templates the caller may use, keyset |
| GET/PATCH/DELETE | `/clubs/:clubId/programme-templates/:id` | the template's group: `group.activities.manage`; club: `club.programmes.manage` | Read, rename, change items, archive |
| POST | `/clubs/:clubId/programme-templates` | as above for the chosen scope | From a source: `{ source: { programmeId } \| { eventId } \| { groupId, from, to } \| { activityId } }` |
| POST | `/clubs/:clubId/programme-templates/:id/preview` | `group.activities.manage` in every mapped group (and `group.duties.manage` for shifts, `club.events.create` for an event) | `{ firstDay, slots: { key: groupIds[] }, locations?: { from: to }, edition? }`: items, warnings, refusals; writes nothing |
| POST | `/clubs/:clubId/programme-templates/:id/apply` | as preview | Same body; creates everything or nothing; returns the programme |
| POST | `/clubs/:clubId/programmes/:id/copy` | as preview | Copy to a new date without keeping a template |
| GET | `/clubs/:clubId/programmes?groupId=&from=` | `group.activities.read` / `club.activities.read` | Programmes of a group or the club, keyset |
| GET | `/programmes/:id` | can read the programme (sees one of its activities) | Header, days, places |
| GET | `/programmes/:id/timeline?day=` | as above | Items of one day as the caller may see them: activities, shifts (filled/needed, the caller's own), conflicts for organisers |
| DELETE | `/clubs/:clubId/programmes/:id` | the programme's organisers | Only while untouched (decision 11) |
| GET | `/me/programmes` | authenticated | Coming programmes of the caller's and their family's groups (app) |

Expected failures as `DomainError` codes, for example
`programme_template_too_large`, `programme_slot_unmapped`,
`programme_location_archived`, `programme_duty_type_archived`,
`programme_not_untouched`, `programme_day_offset_invalid`; the duties
service's own codes (the country rule) pass through.

## Permissions
| Permission | Scope | Allows | Given to these template roles |
|---|---|---|---|
| `club.programmes.manage` | club | Club templates; programmes in every group | `club_manager` |

Group templates and programmes use the existing `group.activities.manage`
(and `group.duties.manage` for shifts, `club.events.create` for an
event); laying out checks each of them for every group a slot maps to and
refuses with the list of what is missing. Policies with allow and deny
tests; matrix rows and tenant isolation (club B gets 404) for every
endpoint.

## UI
- **Web, group and club:** "Program" with the programmes and templates;
  a programme's timeline (days as tabs, a column per place, conflicts
  marked, links to each activity and shift); "Spara som mall" on a
  programme, an event, an activity and a group's calendar range; "Kopiera
  till nytt datum".
- **Web, laying out:** choose template, first day, groups per slot, places
  to replace; the preview (new times, warnings in amber, refusals in red);
  confirm. An edition label.
- **Web, template editor:** the items as a list per day (add, change, move
  between days, remove; shifts under their activity), slots and their
  default groups, the event settings.
- **App:** a programme's timeline as a list per day (cache first; prefetch
  the next programme of the person's groups), from the activity screen
  ("Del av Höstcupen") and from the schedule. Editing and laying out stay
  on the web.
- Empty states, loading states and errors as elsewhere; all texts sv, en,
  nb.

## Privacy and security
Templates hold no personal data. A programme shows people only where its
items do today (the activity's participants and the shifts' visibility
setting, ADR 0050). Audit events for creating, changing, archiving and
deleting templates and programmes, and one `programme.applied` event with
the counts (the created items have their own audit events). Retention:
templates until deleted; programmes follow their items.

## Protected identity
The timeline shows no names except a shift's people, through the duties
presenter and its visibility setting (`PersonPresenter`); nothing about
people is copied to templates.

## Locale and country packs
sv, en, nb. The duty rules of the country pack apply when shifts are laid
out. Times through the club's time zone (decision 4).

## Performance
- Laying out creates up to 300 items in one transaction with bulk inserts
  (a constant number of statements per kind of item, not one per item),
  then one schedule refresh per affected group; measured and listed in
  `request-budgets` (the write is not repeatable, so in `NOT_HERE` with its
  cost) and timed in a test with a full template.
- The timeline reads one day of one programme from
  `(programme_id, starts_at)` on activities and tasks; budgets for
  `GET /programmes/:id`, `/timeline` and `/me/programmes` that do not grow
  with the club.
- The preview's conflict check reads the mapped places' activities in the
  programme's days (the existing location index).

## Seed data
- **SVK F2017 "Höstcupen i Strandviken"** (the P1.6 event) becomes a
  programme: two halls (Strandvikshallen A and B), the matches, lunch and
  dinner on both days, kiosk and score-table shifts (SVK gets the duty
  types Kiosk and Sekretariat), and the template "Höstcupen" with last
  year's edition laid out in the past from it.
- **IKN P2012 "Hemmamatchdag"**: a group template (match, kiosk two
  places, score table one place) made from P2.6's home match.
- **IKN club template "Städdag"** (a morning with two work shifts and
  coffee) for every team.
- Update `docs/seed-data.md`.

## Parts
- **A:** migration, shared schemas and constants, the programmes module
  (templates from each source, preview, apply in one transaction, copy,
  remove while untouched, timeline and lists), permission, policies,
  matrix rows, integration tests (time zones across daylight saving time,
  slots, refusals, all-or-nothing, no people copied), audit and history.
- **B:** web (programmes, timeline, laying out with preview, template
  editor, "Spara som mall" and "Kopiera till nytt datum" entries), app
  (timeline, from activities and the schedule, cache first), seed,
  budgets, e2e, docs (`domain-model.md`, `seed-data.md`, ADR 0054 "As
  implemented").

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] A programme with activities of every kind, meals as programme items,
      duty shifts and an optional event; seen as a timeline per day and
      place
- [ ] Save as template from a programme, an event, a group's range and one
      activity; no people in any template
- [ ] Lay out on a new first day with slots mapped to groups: times kept in
      the club's time zone across daylight saving time, preview with
      warnings and refusals, all or nothing
- [ ] Copy to a new date without keeping a template; editions per template
- [ ] Duty rules (country rule, archived types) and location checks when
      laying out
- [ ] Remove a programme only while untouched
- [ ] Club templates usable by every team for its own groups; templates
      never visible to other clubs
- [ ] Tenant isolation, allow/deny tests, matrix rows, audit, history
- [ ] Request budgets for the reads; laying out with a constant number of
      statements per kind of item
- [ ] Translations sv, en, nb; docs and ADR 0054 "As implemented"

## Out of scope
- Sharing templates between clubs or with a federation.
- Generated match schedules, classes, results and standings (Phase 4,
  tournaments), which will fill programmes.
- Portions, special diets and catering orders.
- Laying out automatically every year; keeping templates and programmes in
  sync.
- Public programme pages (Phase 3, public club pages) and facility
  booking (Phase 3).
- Moving a whole laid-out programme to other dates (copy and remove
  instead while untouched).
