# P1.5 — Responses (attending / not attending) and attendance taking

## Context
Read first: `CLAUDE.md`, ADR 0034 (activities, `schedule_entries`, the
`activity` resource kind), `docs/domain-model.md` ("Activities",
"guardianships" and the rights of linked kinds, "Protected identity",
"Effective permissions"), ADR 0006 (`X-On-Behalf-Of`), ADR 0017 (record
history), ADR 0021 (what calendar data may contain; answer links come in
P1.11 and build on this package), ADR 0022 (protected identity), ADR 0025
(authorization engine), ADR 0028 (mobile app: "writes and an offline outbox
come with P1.5"), ADR 0029 (API only grows), ADR 0031 (club access state),
`docs/performance.md` (budgets, "offline outbox for attendance"),
`docs/seed-data.md` ("Demo scenario: activity and other data").

What exists already:
- **Activities (P1.4):** `activities` (materialised sessions, `kind`,
  `status`, `series_id`, `detached`, `sequence`, `changed_at`),
  `activity_series`, `activity_groups`, `locations`, and the read model
  `schedule_entries` (one row per activity and group, rewritten in the same
  transaction as every change, `member_read` policy across clubs). Endpoints
  for the group and club calendars, the activity detail and
  `GET /me/schedule` (me and my wards, `view_schedule` enforced for linked
  kinds). Permissions `group.activities.read/manage`, `activity.read/manage`,
  `club.activities.read`, `club.locations.manage`. Domain events
  `activity.changed` and `activity_series.changed`, consumed by nothing yet.
  `DELETE /activities/:id` waits for this package's "has responses" check.
- **Families (P1.3, P1.3b):** guardianship rights per link; `sign_up` is
  defined but nothing reads it yet ("sign-up P1.5/P1.6"). Full guardians
  always hold it; relatives, step and foster parents and contact persons
  only when chosen.
- **Groups:** `group_memberships` with functions and validity periods (who
  was in the group on a given day), owners, parent groups.
- **Mobile (P0.10, P1.4):** "My schedule" as the start screen and the
  activity detail, read-only, from a persisted per-account encrypted cache;
  requests pause offline (NetInfo). No writes from the app yet.
- **Country packs:** `se`, `no` with public holidays; nothing about grant
  rules yet.
- **Nothing yet** for responses, attendance, notifications (P1.7) or answer
  links (P1.11).

## Goal
A parent answers "Elsa is coming" or "not coming, she has a fever" in two
taps from "My schedule", on the web or in the app, for each child; a
teenager answers for themselves. The coach sees before the training how many
are coming and who has not answered, and in the hall ticks off who is
present on the phone, also without coverage: the marks are saved on the
phone and sent when the phone is online again. Over the season the coach
and the registrar see each member's attendance per group, the basis for
LOK-stöd reporting later (Phase 2). Nobody sees a protected member in any
response or attendance list except the leaders and managers allowed to.

## Users and roles involved
Members with their own login (answer for themselves); full guardians and
linked people with `sign_up` (answer for a ward); linked people with only
`view_schedule` see the schedule but cannot answer. Group leaders
(`head_coach`, `assistant_coach`, `team_manager`, `other_leader`), group
owners and club managers see all responses and take attendance; registrars
and club managers see attendance summaries and correct marks after the lock.

## User stories
- As a guardian, I want to answer attending or not attending for each of my
  children, with an optional short note to the leaders, so that the coach
  can plan.
- As a member with my own login, I want to answer for myself, and to see
  that my parent answered for me.
- As a coach, I want to choose per activity (and per series) whether answers
  are not asked for, optional, or requested by a deadline, so that matches
  ask for answers and ordinary trainings need not.
- As a guardian, I want to change my answer until the deadline, and after
  it still say "not coming" (illness), so that the coach is never surprised.
- As a coach, I want to see per activity how many are coming, not coming
  and have not answered, with names and notes, so that I know before the
  session whether it can go ahead.
- As a member or guardian, I want to see how many are coming (and who, where
  I may already see the group's members), so that I know if there will be
  enough players.
- As a coach, I want to take attendance from a list of the group's members
  (prefilled from the answers), mark leaders present, and add a club member
  from another group who joined as a guest, also offline in a hall without
  coverage.
- As a coach or registrar, I want each member's attendance over a period
  per group, so that I see who is drifting away and have the basis for
  grant reports.
- As a club manager or registrar, I want to correct an attendance mark after
  the leaders' editing window has closed, with a reason that is kept, so
  that the records stay trustworthy.
- As a relative with only `view_schedule`, I want to see the schedule but
  get no answer buttons.

## Data model
All club-owned tables: `club_id`, tenant RLS, record history, `updated_at`
(CLAUDE.md rule 3). Person references declared in
`activities.references.ts` (or the new module's `*.references.ts`) so
merging duplicate people covers them.

- **Columns on `activities` and `activity_series`** (expand only, defaults
  keep existing rows valid): `response_mode` (`none`, `optional`,
  `requested`; defaults by kind: trainings `optional`, matches and meetings
  `requested`, other `none`), `respond_by` on activities (timestamptz,
  nullable) and `respond_by_minutes` on series (relative to each session's
  start, copied to the sessions). Changing them follows the series rules of
  ADR 0034 (from a date, detached sessions untouched) and bumps neither
  `sequence` nor `changed_at` (calendars do not show them).
  `schedule_entries` gets `response_mode` and `respond_by` so "My schedule"
  needs no extra statement.
- `activity_responses`: `activity_id`, `person_id` (whose answer),
  `status` (`attending`, `not_attending`; the enum may grow, ADR 0029),
  `note` (max 200 characters, leaders only), `responded_by_person_id` (the
  person or the guardian who answered), `responded_at`. Unique
  `(activity_id, person_id)`; indexes `(club_id, activity_id)` for the
  lists and counts and `(person_id, activity_id)` for "My schedule".
- `attendance_records`: `activity_id`, `person_id`, `status` (`present`,
  `absent`, `excused`; the enum may grow), `role` (`participant`, `leader`;
  leaders count separately in grant reports), `guest` (not a member of the
  activity's groups on that day), `recorded_by_person_id`, `recorded_at`
  (the time on the device that took it, see the outbox), `corrected_reason`
  (set on corrections after the lock; never personal data, the form says
  so). Unique `(activity_id, person_id)`; indexes `(club_id, activity_id)`
  and `(club_id, person_id, activity_id)` for summaries.
- **The roster** of an activity is the open `member`-function memberships of
  its groups on the activity's start (a membership valid at `starts_at`),
  plus everyone already marked. Leaders are the leader functions of its
  groups on that day. A member who joined later or left earlier is not on
  it; their responses are kept but not counted.
- **Lock:** leaders may record and change attendance from one hour before
  the start until `attendance_lock_days` after it (club setting, default
  30). Later changes are corrections: club managers and registrars only,
  with a reason.
- **Cancelled activities** keep their responses; new responses and
  attendance are refused (`activity_cancelled`). Restoring reopens them.
- **Deleting and series changes:** `DELETE /activities/:id` is refused with
  `activity_has_responses` when the activity has responses or attendance. A
  series change that drops a session with responses cancels it instead of
  deleting it (update ADR 0034); sessions with attendance are in the past
  and never rewritten anyway.
- **Retention:** decided in P1.8 ([#18](https://github.com/SweRavn/ClubAdmin/issues/18)):
  attendance as the country pack's grant rules require, responses to past
  activities deleted after a period (proposal 13 months). Erasure already
  removes the notes on a person's answers (P1.5).

ADR 0035 decides: responses and attendance as separate records (intent
versus fact), the roster definition, the lock and corrections, who sees
which names and counts, the offline outbox semantics (ordering, idempotency,
conflicts, clock skew), the response modes and deadlines, and how answer
links (P1.11) and notifications (P1.7) will use them.

## API
Under `/api/v1`. Writes refused for clubs that are not active (ADR 0031).
Problem codes, never values: `response_closed`, `response_not_requested`,
`activity_cancelled`, `activity_not_started`, `attendance_locked`,
`not_on_roster`, `not_a_club_member`, `correction_reason_required`,
`activity_has_responses`, ….

| Method | Path | Permission | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| PUT | `/activities/:activityId/responses/:personId` | `activity.respond` | Status and note; `personId` is the caller or a ward (full guardian, or linked with `sign_up`) on the activity's roster. Until `respond_by`; after it only a change to `not_attending`. Idempotent |
| DELETE | `/activities/:activityId/responses/:personId` | `activity.respond` | Withdraw an answer (same rules) |
| GET | `/activities/:activityId/responses/summary` | `activity.read` | Counts (attending, not attending, not answered, among the roster) and the caller's own and wards' answers; for viewers who may read the group's members also the names of those attending (PersonPresenter; protected people only counted) |
| GET | `/activities/:activityId/responses?status=&cursor=` | `activity.responses.read` | Leaders' list: the whole roster with answer, note, who answered and when (keyset by person) |
| GET | `/activities/:activityId/attendance` | `activity.attendance.manage` | The roster and leaders with their marks, prefilled suggestions from the answers (not saved until sent), whether the activity is locked |
| PUT | `/activities/:activityId/attendance` | `activity.attendance.manage` | Bulk marks `{ personId, status, role, recordedAt }[]` (up to 200); guests must be active members of the club; one statement per kind of change; after the lock only with `correctedReason` and for managers and registrars (checked by the service against the policy's resource) |
| GET | `/groups/:groupId/attendance?from=&to=&cursor=` | `group.attendance.read` | Per member of the period: sessions held (not cancelled), present, absent, excused, rate; and per session the number present (participants and leaders). Range max 400 days, keyset by person |
| GET | `/me/schedule` (changed) | own data | Each entry gains `responseMode`, `respondBy` and per person in `people` their answer and whether the caller may answer for them (new optional fields, ADR 0029) |
| GET | `/groups/:groupId/activities` (changed) | `group.activities.read` | For those who manage the group: per activity the counts of the summary |

`PUT` responses and attendance accept an `Idempotency-Key` (UUID); the
writes are idempotent by content (the same answer or mark again changes
nothing), so the key needs no store on the server (ADR 0035). Ordering of
offline writes follows `recordedAt` (attendance: the newer mark wins; an
older one is not applied and comes back in the answer's `stale` list,
while the request succeeds for the other marks; responses: the deadline is
checked when the request arrives, never against the device's clock).
`recordedAt` more than 7 days old or in the future (beyond a 5-minute
skew) is refused (`recorded_at_out_of_range`).

## Permissions
New permissions, one policy each in `authorization.policy.ts`, allow and
deny tests, entries in the permission matrix (tenant isolation: 404 for
club B):
- `activity.respond` (resource `activity`): active members of one of the
  activity's groups, their full guardians, linked people with `sign_up`
  (through the ward). Not linked people with only `view_schedule`, not
  leaders as such (a leader who is also a member answers as a member). The
  service checks that `personId` is the caller or such a ward and on the
  roster; with `X-On-Behalf-Of` only that ward.
- `activity.responses.read`: those who manage the activity (owners of its
  groups and parent groups, `head_coach`, `assistant_coach`,
  `team_manager`, club managers) and `other_leader`. Not members, not
  guardians, not `physio`.
- `activity.attendance.manage`: the set of `activity.responses.read` plus
  registrars. The service lets leaders and owners record until the lock and
  only club managers and registrars correct after it (registrars only
  correct).
- `group.attendance.read`: the group's leaders and owners (also of parent
  groups), club managers, registrars. Not members or guardians (their own
  attendance is out of scope here).
- Response counts in `/responses/summary` and the calendars are visible to
  everyone who may read the activity; names of those attending only to
  viewers who also hold `group.members.read` on one of its groups.

## UI
- **Web, "My schedule":** per entry and person, "Coming" / "Not coming"
  buttons (with the note in a small field behind "Add a note to the
  leaders"), the current answer and who gave it ("answered by Anna"), the
  deadline, an "Answer needed" mark for `requested` entries without an
  answer. Works without JavaScript (server actions; optimistic updates on
  the web are left for later, the app shows answers at once).
  No buttons where the caller may not answer.
- **Web, activity page:** the summary (counts and, where allowed, names of
  those attending), the caller's family's answers with the buttons; for
  leaders the full list (attending, not attending with notes, not
  answered) and "Take attendance".
- **Web, attendance:** the roster with present / absent / excused per
  person, prefilled from the answers (attending → present, not attending →
  excused), leaders in their own section, "Add a guest" (search among the
  club's active members), save; after the lock read-only for leaders and a
  correction form with a reason for managers and registrars.
- **Web, group page:** an "Attendance" tab for leaders, owners, managers
  and registrars: the per-member summary for a period (this season by
  default), sortable by rate, with the sessions' counts; the grant-rule
  hints below.
- **Web, activity and series forms:** response mode and deadline (series:
  "hours before each session").
- **Mobile:** answer buttons in "My schedule" and on the activity detail
  (per person, the note); for leaders an "Attendance" screen from the
  activity detail (large touch targets, prefilled; guests are added on the
  web). Writes go through
  the **offline outbox**: a persisted queue per account in the encrypted
  cache, each write with its `Idempotency-Key` and `recordedAt`, sent in
  order when online, shown as "Waiting to send" with a count; a refused
  write (a closed deadline, a stale mark) is shown with the reason and the
  current server value. Logging out with unsent writes asks first.
- Loading, empty ("Nobody has answered yet"), offline and error states;
  translations in sv, en, nb; times in the club's time zone.

## Privacy and security
- Attendance and responses are personal data about children; the legal
  basis is the membership (running the activity) and, for attendance, the
  club's grant reporting. Record in `domain-model.md`.
- Notes to leaders may be about health ("fever"): leaders only, never in
  counts, calendars, exports, push or emails; the field says not to write
  more than needed; 200 characters; never logged.
- Audit events in the same transaction: `activity.response.set` and
  `.withdrawn` (with `respondedBy` and whether on behalf; no note text),
  `attendance.recorded` (one event per save, with the number of marks, not
  per person), `attendance.corrected` (per corrected mark, with the reason).
  Record history on both tables.
- Domain events with IDs only: `activity.response.changed`,
  `attendance.recorded` (P1.7 may notify leaders of late "not coming"; no
  consumer in this package).
- A guardian answering on behalf of a ward is recorded as on-behalf-of
  (ADR 0006); a revoked `sign_up` or guardianship takes effect at once
  (answers already given stay, attributed).
- Rate limits on the write endpoints per account (P0.10).

## Protected identity
- Response lists for members and guardians name only people they may see
  (PersonPresenter); protected members are counted ("12 attending") and
  never named for them.
- Leaders' lists, attendance and summaries name protected members with the
  crossed-camera icon (they may see them, ADR 0022); the mobile attendance
  screen sets `FLAG_SECURE` / blurs the snapshot while it shows a protected
  person (ADR 0028).
- "Answered by" names the guardian through the presenter; a protected
  guardian is shown as "a guardian" to viewers who may not see them.
- Nothing about responses or attendance appears in calendar data
  (`schedule_entries` holds no people; feeds in P1.11 show only the
  subscriber's own answer).

## Locale and country packs
- Strings in sv, en and nb (statuses, modes, deadline phrases such as
  "Answer by Thursday 18:00", outbox states, problem codes).
- **Grant-rule hints** (country pack, pure function, tests): `se` exposes
  the LOK-stöd rules a session must meet to count (RF:s föreskrifter,
  version of 15 January 2023: at least three participants aged 7–25 in the
  calendar year, a leader aged 13 or more, at least 45 minutes); the attendance
  screen and summary show "does not qualify for LOK-stöd" with the reason
  for leaders. `no` returns no hints (no equivalent per-session rule).
  Reporting and export are Phase 2.

## Performance
- `GET /me/schedule` stays < 50 ms p95 with answers (one statement; the
  answers through `(person_id, activity_id)`); extend
  `schedule-plans.integration.test.ts`.
- Response summary and leaders' list for a group of 25 < 50 ms; the group
  calendar with counts for a season < 100 ms (counts in one grouped
  statement per page, never per activity).
- Saving attendance for 30 people < 200 ms (one statement per kind of
  change); a group's attendance summary over a season < 100 ms.
- Load scenario: responses for upcoming sessions and attendance for past
  ones (`seed-data.md`, "Load scenario": about 0.8 million attendance rows,
  the six latest sessions per team: every past session took the scenario
  over its ten minutes); query-plan tests for the summary, the leaders' list, the group
  summary and the schedule on it (no sequential scans on
  `activity_responses` or `attendance_records`).

## Acceptance criteria
- [x] A guardian answers for each child from "My schedule" (web and app); a
      teen answers for themselves; a relative without `sign_up` gets no
      buttons and `403` from the API.
- [x] After the deadline only "not coming" is accepted; cancelled
      activities refuse answers and attendance.
- [x] Leaders see counts, names, notes and who has not answered; members
      and guardians see counts and only the names they may see; protected
      members are only counted for them.
- [x] A coach takes attendance offline in the app; the marks are sent when
      online, in order, without duplicates (same `Idempotency-Key`), and a
      stale mark is reported with the current value.
- [x] Leaders cannot change attendance after the lock; managers and
      registrars can, with a reason recorded in audit and history.
- [x] The roster follows group membership on the activity's date; guests
      must be club members.
- [x] `DELETE /activities/:id` refuses activities with responses or
      attendance; a series change cancels such sessions instead of deleting
      them.
- [x] Grant-rule hints for `se` with tests; none for `no`.
- [x] Performance budgets met (query-plan and timing tests on the load
      scenario; the timed tests in the `--mode timed` pass).
- [x] Tenant-isolation test for every new endpoint
- [x] Allow/deny tests for every new policy
- [x] Audit events for every change to responses and attendance
- [x] Translations complete for sv and en (and nb)
- [x] Docs updated: `domain-model.md` (responses, attendance, retention,
      `sign_up`), `performance.md`, `seed-data.md`, `architecture.md`
      (responses and attendance in the activities module, ADR 0035; the

      the outbox), ADR 0034 (dropped sessions with responses are

## Out of scope
- Call-ups (selecting a squad for a match, "kallelse" to some members only)
  and reserves; a later package ([#19](https://github.com/SweRavn/ClubAdmin/issues/19)).
- Events with sign-up forms, capacity and waiting lists (P1.6).
- Reminders to those who have not answered and notices to leaders of late
  "not coming" (P1.7; the domain events exist).
- One-click answer links in calendar feeds (P1.11) and iMIP replies
  (Phase 2).
- LOK-stöd reporting, IdrottOnline export and other grant reports
  (Phase 2); this package stores the data and shows hints.
- Members' and guardians' view of their own attendance history; statistics
  across groups for the club.
- The retention jobs themselves (P1.8).
