# P1.2 — Member register: search, filter, export, with field-level permissions

## Context
Read first: `CLAUDE.md`, `docs/domain-model.md` ("club_memberships",
"club_profiles", "Field visibility", "Effective permissions",
"Protected identity"), ADR 0006/0025 (authorization), ADR 0011 (object
storage), ADR 0022 (protected identity), ADR 0029 (API only grows),
ADR 0031 (club access state: exports always work), ADR 0032 (transactional
email), `docs/performance.md`, `docs/security.md` ("Rate limits", insider
threat), `docs/gdpr.md`.

What exists already:
- **Permission** `club.members.read` (club managers, registrars) with the
  web app's "Members" navigation entry and a placeholder page
  (`apps/web/src/app/(app)/clubs/[clubId]/members/page.tsx`).
- **Field visibility (P0.7):** `GET /api/v1/people/:personId?clubId=` returns
  fields by relationship (`profileAccess`: self, admin, leader, member);
  phone, address and gender reach a club only if shared with it
  (`club_profiles.shared_fields`). Names only through `PersonPresenter`.
- **Rosters (P0.7):** keyset-paginated group members with
  `app.visible_group_members` (protected members filtered in the database for
  non-privileged viewers), `bulkRead` budgets per club and per account.
- **Memberships (P0.5, P1.1):** `club_memberships` (status active, paused,
  left; `applied` is legacy), `membership_type`, member numbers in
  `club_profiles`; join requests decide applications (P1.1).
- **Consents (P0.5):** `consents` per purpose (photo, video, streaming,
  marketing, federation sharing); schema and seed rows, no UI.
- **Worker and email (P1.1):** outbox handlers, the `mail` handler and its
  ICU templates (`packages/i18n`, namespace `email`).
- **Object storage:** Garage in development (`S3_*` in `.env.example`,
  bucket `clubadmin-files`), backed up and replicated (P0.11); no S3 client
  in the application yet (ADR 0011).
- **Club access state (P0.12):** `clubAccess: "export"` keeps an endpoint
  working for read-only and suspended clubs.

## Goal
A club's registrar or manager finds any member in a second, filters the
register the way annual reports and grant applications need, sees exactly
the fields their role allows, and exports a list without anyone being able
to take more data than their role allows, or doing it unnoticed. Board
members see a limited list.

## Users and roles involved
Club manager, registrar (full register), board roles (`chair`,
`vice_chair`, `treasurer`, `secretary`, `board_member`,
`deputy_board_member`: limited list), members whose data is shown.

## User stories
- As a registrar, I want to search members by name or member number and get
  results as I type, so I can answer a question on the phone.
- As a registrar, I want to filter by membership status and type, group,
  role, birth year range, gender (where shared) and missing consent, so I can
  produce the lists the board and federations ask for.
- As a registrar, I want to sort by name, member number or join date and page
  through thousands of members without waiting.
- As a club manager or registrar, I want to export the current filter as CSV
  with the columns I choose, so I can use it in a spreadsheet; the export is
  audited and all managers are told.
- As a board member, I want to see who the members are (name, member number,
  membership type, groups), without contact details or birth dates.
- As a registrar, I want to pause, resume or end a membership and change its
  type, so the register stays correct.
- As a member, I want my protected identity to keep me out of lists and
  exports others make, unless they may see me (and then marked).

## Data model
- `member_exports` (club-owned, tenant RLS, history, `updated_at`):
  `requested_by_person_id`, `status` (`queued`, `ready`, `failed`,
  `expired`), `filter` (JSON: the register filter, IDs and enums only),
  `columns` (text[] from the allowed column list), `include_protected`
  (bool), `row_count`, `object_key` (S3, per-club prefix), `expires_at`
  (24 hours), `error_code`. Person reference declared in
  `members.references.ts`.
- Indexes for the register, justified in the PR with `EXPLAIN` on the load
  scenario (10 000 members in one club, 1 million people in total):
  - `club_memberships (club_id, status, membership_type)`;
  - a trigram index on the normalised full name of `people`
    (`lower(given_name || ' ' || family_name)`), used through a join on the
    club's profiles; member number search uses the existing unique index;
  - `club_memberships (club_id, valid_from, id)` for "joined" sorting.
- Register reads go through a database function (like
  `app.visible_group_members`) that leaves out protected people for viewers
  who may not see them, so pages and counts stay correct.
- No new columns on people; nothing is copied out of `people` into the
  club.

## API
All under `/api/v1/clubs/:clubId`. Keyset pagination: `cursor` is the last
item's person ID; the sort key is resolved from it. Filters are validated
with shared Zod schemas.

| Method | Path | Permission | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| GET | `/members?q=&status=&type=&groupId=&role=&bornFrom=&bornTo=&gender=&missingConsent=&sort=&cursor=&limit=` | `club.members.read` | Fields by register access (below); `totalCount` of all matches (an aggregate, protected people included without identity); `bulkRead` |
| GET | `/members/facets` | `club.members.read` | Counts per status, type and group for the filter panel (no people) |
| PATCH | `/members/:personId/membership` | `club.members.manage` | Pause, resume, end (with date), change type; ending follows `leaveClub` (groups, ownerships, roles end; never the last manager) |
| POST | `/member-exports` | `club.members.export` | Body: filter, columns, `includeProtected`; queues the export (202); recent login required |
| GET | `/member-exports` | `club.members.export` | The caller's and others' recent exports (who, when, row count, columns), keyset |
| GET | `/member-exports/:exportId/download` | `club.members.export` | Only the requester; a pre-signed URL valid 5 minutes, while not expired; `clubAccess: "export"` |

## Permissions
New permissions, one policy each, allow and deny tests, entries in the
permission matrix (tenant isolation: 404 for club B):
- `club.members.read` (changed): club managers and registrars (full
  register), and the board roles listed above (limited register). A
  function `registerAccess(actor, clubId)` returns `full`, `limited` or
  null, like `profileAccess`.
- `club.members.manage`: club managers (MFA), registrars.
- `club.members.export`: club managers (MFA), registrars (MFA); with
  `@RecentAuth(15)`.

Field sets (the API leaves out what the viewer may not see; tests per
access level):

| Access | Fields |
|---|---|
| full | name, member number, birth date, membership status, type and dates, groups with functions, roles, shared phone, address and gender, guardians (names, through the presenter) with their phone if shared, consents missing |
| limited | name, member number, membership type, groups |

## UI (web)
- `/clubs/[clubId]/members`: search box (debounced, results as you type),
  filter panel with facet counts, sortable columns, keyset "load more" with
  list virtualisation above 200 rows (`DataList`), total count. Server
  component for the first page; the table is a client component only where
  it must be.
- Member row → the person's profile in the club (P0.7), which gains the
  membership actions (pause, resume, end, change type) for
  `club.members.manage`.
- Export dialog: columns (only allowed ones offered), "include protected
  members (marked, do not share)", explanation that managers are notified;
  the exports list with status and download while valid.
- Protected members show the crossed-camera icon for viewers who may see
  them; the limited list never shows them.
- Loading, empty and error states; translations in sv, en, nb.

Mobile is out of scope.

## Privacy and security
- Personal data: everything in the register; the export file is personal
  data at rest: stored encrypted at rest by the bucket, under the club's
  prefix, deleted after 24 hours (worker job and bucket lifecycle rule),
  never linked publicly (pre-signed URL, 5 minutes, only for the
  requester).
- Every export writes an audit event (`members.exported`: filter, column
  names, row count, `include_protected`; never values) and a domain event
  that emails all club managers (ADR 0032 pipeline; new template).
- Membership changes write audit events (`membership.paused`,
  `membership.resumed`, `membership.ended`, `membership.type.changed`).
- Bulk reads: the register list counts against the `bulkRead` budgets;
  exports are limited per account and club per day (configurable).
- The CSV is generated by the worker (not in the request), streamed to
  object storage; formula injection is neutralised (cells starting with
  `= + - @` are prefixed); UTF-8 with BOM for spreadsheet programs.
- The export is an `export` route for read-only and suspended clubs
  (ADR 0031): a club can always take its data out.

## Protected identity
Register and exports go through the visibility rules: managers and
registrars see protected members with the icon; the limited register and
everyone else never sees them (counts include them without identity).
Exports leave protected members out unless `includeProtected` is set by a
viewer who may see them; then a "Protected identity: do not share" column
marks them. Guardians' names in the register pass through the presenter.

## Locale and country packs
All strings in sv, en, nb. Column headers of the CSV in the requester's
language. Birth dates as ISO dates in the CSV. No country-specific columns
yet (federation exports such as IdrottOnline come with Phase 2).

## Performance
- Register page (50 rows, any filter combination): p95 < 100 ms on the load
  scenario (10 000 members in one club); ≤ 5 queries per request (page,
  names, groups of the page, protected set); facets < 100 ms.
- Search as you type: trigram index, results in < 100 ms.
- Exports of 10 000 members finish in < 30 s in the worker, streaming (no
  full result in memory).
- `query-plans.integration.test.ts` covers the register page, search and
  facets without sequential scans on large tables.
