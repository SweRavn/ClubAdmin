# P2.3 — Car pooling, mileage and a general attestation flow

## Context
Read first: `CLAUDE.md`, ADR 0048 (this prompt's decisions), ADR 0047 (no
adult alone with a child), ADR 0022 (protected identity), ADR 0034
(activities, series, locations), ADR 0039 (push), ADR 0040, ADR 0046
(roles and permissions).

What exists already:

- Activities, series and sessions with meeting time and place and call-ups
  (`invitees_only`, `activity_invitees`); events with sessions (P1.6).
- `locations` (name, street, postal code, city, coordinates, directions,
  archived, created by); **no distance column yet**.
- `club.locations.create` lets leaders (`managesAnyGroupActivities`) add
  places and edit only their own; `club.locations.manage` holds the rest.
- Guardianships, ages, the country pack's adult age, `PersonPresenter`.
- No car pools, no expense claims.

## Goal
For matches, cups and trainings far away, the leaders say how people get
there; parents offer seats, children get a ride, nobody is left without one
and no adult is alone in a car with someone else's child without the
guardian's confirmation. Drives become mileage statistics the members
settle among themselves or claims the club attests and pays (payment in
Phase 3). The attestation flow is general and serves all expenses later.

## Users and roles involved
Leaders and activity creators, drivers (adult participants, guardians),
guardians, children, attesters (group and club), treasurer, protected
children.

## User stories
- As a coach, I set "Deltagarna tar sig dit själva" on Saturday's away
  match and see who has no ride yet.
- As a parent, I offer my car with three seats, and "kan köra om det
  behövs" for the next match.
- As a parent, I book seats for my son both ways; as a 14-year-old whose
  guardian allows it, I book myself.
- As a guardian, I am asked to confirm when my daughter would be the only
  child with one adult who is not her parent.
- As a driver, I correct the distance after the trip and turn my month's
  drives into a claim; the coach who created the match attests it.

## Decisions (ADR 0048)
1. **Transport mode** per activity, series (inherited) and event session:
   not set | own transport (meeting time and place required, car pool
   opens) | shared transport (a bus: only the meeting place and time).
2. **Offers**: a car with N seats, departure place and time; or "kan köra
   om det behövs". The return trip is a copy of the outward one, edited
   separately (another time, place, or none).
3. **Booking**: a guardian for their child, an adult for themselves; a
   child for themselves from the age their guardian chooses (default the
   country pack's age, else 13); the guardian sees and changes the child's
   bookings. The return seat is booked in the same car and can be changed.
   Push to drivers and bookers on bookings and changes.
4. **One adult and a single child not their own** → pending until the
   child's guardian confirms actively (with the driver's name), whoever
   drives; a child's own booking into such a car always needs it. Changes
   that create the situation (another passenger cancels) ask again.
5. **Protected child**: seen by the driver only; the guardian is told
   before booking.
6. **Distance** is a field of the place (`locations.distance_km` from the
   club's starting point; the club's home place is a club setting). The
   driver may correct the distance per drive afterwards. No routing service,
   no home addresses.
7. **Places**: `club.locations.create` is widened to create and edit all
   places with their distance; every leader holds it by default;
   `club.locations.manage` keeps archiving and merging.
8. **Statistics**: per person and period, drives, distance, passengers.
   Seen by the driver, the group's leaders and attesters; a **group
   setting** (group leaders) makes them public in the group so members can
   settle among themselves.
9. **Reimbursement**: a club setting turns it on with a rate per km (the
   country pack gives the tax-free rate as a guide, Sweden per mil). A
   driver turns drives into a claim.
10. **Attestation flow (`expense_claims`)**: kind (`mileage` first),
    amount, lines, claimant, the activity or group it belongs to. Routed
    first to the activity's creator if they may attest, otherwise to the
    holders of the permission (group, then club). Never one's own claim;
    approve or reject with a reason; audited; the claimant is told;
    approved claims export to CSV. The flow is generic: new kinds plug in
    with their lines and routing.
11. **Kept until deleted** explicitly: a driver deletes their own offers
    before the trip; `club.carpools.manage` deletes older data. Attested
    claims follow accounting retention. Erasure anonymises the person in
    drives and statistics (`app.erase_person`, deletion ledger).
12. **Events** use the same tables (an event session is a "trip context"
    like an activity).

## Data model
Club-owned, tenant RLS, history, `updated_at`, person references declared.

- `activities`, `activity_series`, `event_sessions`: `transport_mode`.
- `locations.distance_km`; club setting for the starting place.
- `carpool_offers` (club_id, context activity | event session, driver,
  direction `out` | `back`, linked offer, seats, departure place and time,
  willing_only, distance_km override, done_at).
- `carpool_bookings` (offer_id, passenger, booked_by, status `held` |
  `pending_guardian` | `cancelled`, confirmed_by, confirmed_at).
- `carpool_self_booking_ages` (guardian setting per child).
- `expense_claims` (club_id, kind, claimant, group_id, activity_id,
  amount, currency, status `submitted` | `approved` | `rejected` |
  `exported` | `paid`, decided_by, decided_at, reason) and
  `expense_claim_lines` (claim_id, offer_id for mileage, description,
  quantity, unit_amount).

## API
| Method | Path | Permission | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| GET | `/me/carpools` | authenticated | "Mina samåkningar": my drives, my children's seats, pending confirmations |
| GET | `/clubs/:clubId/activities/:id/carpool` | can see the activity | Cars, free seats; leaders: who has no ride |
| POST/PATCH/DELETE | `…/carpool/offers[/:offerId]` | participant or guardian of one | Offer, edit, copy return trip |
| POST/DELETE | `…/carpool/offers/:offerId/bookings[/:bookingId]` | guardian, adult, child from the allowed age | Book, cancel |
| POST | `/me/carpool-bookings/:id/confirm` | the child's guardian | Confirm the one-adult-one-child case |
| PUT | `/me/children/:personId/carpool-age` | guardian | Self-booking age |
| PATCH | `/clubs/:clubId/locations/:id` | `club.locations.create` | Distance included |
| GET | `/clubs/:clubId/groups/:groupId/carpool-stats` | leaders, attesters, or members when public | Statistics |
| POST | `/me/expense-claims` | authenticated (own drives) | Create a mileage claim |
| GET | `/clubs/:clubId/expense-claims` | `group.expenses.attest` / `club.expenses.attest` | To attest, keyset |
| POST | `/clubs/:clubId/expense-claims/:id/decision` | attest permission, not own | Approve/reject |
| GET | `/clubs/:clubId/expense-claims/export` | `club.expenses.attest` | CSV |
| DELETE | `/clubs/:clubId/carpools?before=` | `club.carpools.manage` | Delete old data |

## Permissions
| Permission | Scope | Allows | Default |
|---|---|---|---|
| `club.locations.create` (widened) | club | Create and edit all places, distance included | every leader in the club |
| `group.expenses.attest` | group | Attest claims in the group, never one's own | `head_coach`, `team_manager` |
| `club.expenses.attest` | club | Attest claims anywhere in the club | `treasurer` |
| `club.carpools.manage` | club | Delete old car-pool data; set the mileage rate and starting place | **proposed:** `club_manager`, `treasurer` |

Settings, not permissions: the group's "statistics public" (group leaders);
a guardian's self-booking age per child (the guardian). Allow/deny tests
for every rule of 3–5, including a driver who is a leader, a parent of
another child, and the child's own parent.

## UI
- Activity and event session: transport mode in the form; the car-pool
  section (cars, seats, book, offer, the return trip), leaders' "utan
  skjuts" list.
- "Mina samåkningar" (app and web), with pending confirmations first.
- Places: distance field; club settings: starting place, reimbursement and
  rate.
- Claims: "Mina utlägg", attesters' queue, decision dialog, CSV export.
- Group settings: statistics public.

## Privacy and security
Who rides with whom is personal data about children: visible to the car's
driver and passengers, the guardians and the group's leaders (protected
children: driver only). Audit for confirmations, attestations, deletions.
No home addresses, no live location.

## Protected identity
Passenger lists and statistics through `PersonPresenter`; protected
children hidden from everyone but the driver and their guardians.

## Locale and country packs
`mileageRate` (tax-free rate and unit) and `childSelfBookingAge` in the
country pack (SE: rate per mil); sv, en, nb.

## Performance
Car-pool section loaded with the activity in one query set; "Mina
samåkningar" from the person's bookings and offers (indexes on person);
statistics aggregated per group and period with an index on (group, date);
budgets for each endpoint.

## Seed data
IKN F2015 away match with own transport: Anna offers three seats, two
children booked, one booking pending a guardian's confirmation, one child
without a ride; a mileage claim attested by Erik; one rejected claim.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Transport modes on activities, series and event sessions
- [ ] Offers, return trips, bookings, self-booking age
- [ ] The guardian-confirmation rule enforced in the service and tested
- [ ] Distances on places, per-drive correction, statistics with the
      public setting
- [ ] Attestation flow generic, never own claim, audited, CSV
- [ ] Tenant isolation, allow/deny, matrix rows, budgets, history, erasure
- [ ] Translations sv, en, nb; docs (`domain-model.md`, ADR 0048 "As
      implemented", `seed-data.md`)

## Out of scope
- Paying claims (Phase 3), receipts and other expense kinds (Phase 3).
- Routing services and home addresses.
- Rates per group or vehicle, passenger supplements.
