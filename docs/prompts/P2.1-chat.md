# P2.1 — Chat: channels, real time, "Mina kanaler", child safety

## Context
Read first: `CLAUDE.md`, ADR 0016 (chat built in-house), ADR 0047 (child
safety in chat: the rules this prompt implements), ADR 0022 and P1.12
(protected identity; the chat acceptance rules decided there), ADR 0039
(notifications, push), ADR 0040 (responsiveness, cache-first app), ADR 0046
(roles and permissions), ADR 0010 (Valkey), `docs/dpia-chat.md` (the impact
assessment this implementation must match), `docs/security.md`.

What exists already:

- Groups, group memberships with roles (participant role `member`,
  leaders), guardianships with full guardians, ages from birth dates and the
  country pack's adult age, protected identity and `PersonPresenter`.
- Notifications: inbox, push through Expo, daily email, categories, quiet
  hours, lock-screen text setting (P1.7, ADR 0039).
- The `authz.changed` event and the client's authorisation version (P1.15
  A2), so far delivered by refetching.
- No WebSocket gateway, no chat tables (ADR 0016 says Postgres + WebSocket +
  Valkey pub/sub; the architecture lists a `chat` module).

Decisions (maintainer, 2026-11-12) are in ADR 0047 and below.

## Goal
Members talk in their groups in real time, on web and in the app, with
channels per group that their creator opens to the group or keeps closed;
children chat with each other; no adult is ever alone with a child, and
guardians can see and join their children's chats, visibly. "Mina kanaler"
gathers every chat a person is in. The chat is the product's unique selling
point: fast, simple, safe.

The prompt is delivered in parts, each with green CI:
**A** real time and channels (gateway, tables, channels, direct messages,
editing, the app and web views); **B** child safety and moderation (ADR 0047
rules, guardians' insight, reporting, safeguarding cases); **C** push per
channel, "Mina kanaler" polish, docs (README, help, privacy notice).

## Users and roles involved
Members of all ages, guardians, leaders, group owners, supporters (e.g. a
grandparent in the group), safeguarding officers (`safeguarding_officer`
template role), club managers.

## User stories
- As a coach, I create a channel "Matchinfo P2014" open to the group and a
  closed channel for the coaches, so that each message reaches the right
  people.
- As a player of 11, I create a chat with my teammates, so that we can talk;
  I see that our parents may join and which have.
- As a parent, I see which chats my child is in and who is in them, and I
  can join one and read its history.
- As a coach writing to a player directly, the player's guardians are added
  automatically, so that I am never alone with a child.
- As anyone, I edit a message with a typo; the earlier version is kept.
- As a member, "Mina kanaler" lists my chats across groups and clubs,
  unread first, and I set push per channel.
- As a child or parent, I report a message with one tap; the club's
  safeguarding officers handle it.

## Rules (ADR 0047, with the decisions of 2026-11-12)
1. **Channels in groups.** A group has a default open channel for everyone
   in it and more as needed. The creator chooses the members (people of the
   group or a subset) and whether the channel is **open** (people of the
   group see it and may join) or **closed** (only the members added; others
   of the group neither see nor join it).
2. **A chat is not owned by one group.** Its members are its own list with
   the context that explains why (a group, several groups, none for direct
   messages); every rule is evaluated from the members' ages, guardianships
   and roles, so chats across groups can come later without a new model.
3. **No adult alone with a child.** Whenever a leader is in a chat with a
   child (a direct message, a leader joining a children's chat), the
   children's guardians are added automatically with the whole history.
   Only guardians, not other leaders.
4. **Other adults** (other parents, supporters) cannot start a direct
   message with someone else's child; guardians always can with their own.
5. **Children's chats.** Children of every age, also under 13, may chat and
   create channels (the participant role holds `group.channels.create`);
   for now with children of their own group.
6. **Open to guardians, visibly.** A guardian sees which chats their child
   is in (also closed ones) and may join any of them with the whole
   history; guardians are not added automatically except under rule 3. The
   chat always shows children that parents can join and which have joined.
   Guardians of a group's children see who is in the group's chats that have
   children in them (membership, not messages), also chats they are not in.
   The group's leaders may join the children's chats of their group (rule 3
   then adds the guardians). A closed channel without children (leaders,
   the board) is hidden from everyone else.
7. **Protected people** are never added without accepting (ADR 0022, P1.12
   rules); names through `PersonPresenter`.
8. **Nothing disappears.** Messages are kept without a time limit; a message
   its sender deletes is hidden, not removed; editing keeps every earlier
   version (each with its time), shows that the message was edited, and
   those who may read the chat may see the versions. Erasure anonymises the
   sender ("en tidigare medlem") inside `app.erase_person`, so the deletion
   ledger applies it again after a restore; the text stays.
9. **Background checks.** Where the club's background-check requirement
   applies (P2.8: the plugin's requirement or the club's stricter setting),
   a leader writes to children and joins children's chats only with a valid
   check. Until P2.8 the rule is a hook that allows.
10. **Reporting** takes one tap for children and guardians; the reported
    person does not see who reported; the club's safeguarding officers
    receive it (`club.safeguarding.handle`), read the reported chat under a
    two-person rule with every read logged, and close the case.
11. **Moderation**: channel managers (the creator,
    `group.channels.manage`) remove members and hide messages in their
    channels; safeguarding officers may hide any reported message and mute
    a member in the club's chats for a time; every such act is audited and
    the person is told.
12. **Old messages** (DPIA): messages older than three
    years are readable by the chat's members and, on a report, by the
    safeguarding officers; not shown in searches or exports to others; the
    club reviews the retention decision every three years.
13. **Push** for chats with children shows "Nytt meddelande i <kanal>"
    without text; for others the P1.7 lock-screen setting applies.

## Data model
Postgres, messages partitioned by month (ADR 0016); every table club-owned
with tenant RLS (or per-person RLS for direct messages across clubs, decided
in the design note), history (`history.enable_history`; message bodies
redacted in history views: the message's own version table is the record),
`app.enable_updated_at`; person references declared for merging.

- `chats` (club_id, kind `channel` | `direct`, name, visibility `open` |
  `closed`, context groups as a list, created_by, archived_at).
- `chat_members` (chat_id, person_id, reason `added` | `joined` |
  `guardian_auto` | `guardian_joined` | `leader_joined`, role in the chat,
  joined_at, left_at, last_read_message_id, push setting `all` | `mentions`
  | `off`).
- `chat_messages` (chat_id, sender, body, reply_to, created_at, edited_at,
  hidden_at, partition by month).
- `chat_message_versions` (message_id, body, created_at): every edit.
- `chat_message_attachments`: the table and API shape exist, **unused** in
  this revision (text only; files come later with the storage of P2.2).
- `chat_reactions`, read receipts (per member, the last read message).
- `safeguarding_reports` and `safeguarding_case_reads` (who read what, the
  second person's confirmation).

## API
REST for history, membership, settings; the **WebSocket gateway** for live
messages, typing and read events, with Valkey pub/sub between API instances
and the same authorisation as REST (the connection's actor, re-checked on
`authz.changed`). Every endpoint declared with `@Endpoint`; matrix rows;
request budgets (listing "Mina kanaler" and a chat's latest page must not
grow with the number of messages: keyset by message ID).

## Permissions
New in the catalogue (P1.15 decision 5, template roles by default):

| Permission | Scope | Allows | Given to these template roles |
|---|---|---|---|
| `group.channels.create` | group | Create channels in the group, choose members | `member` (children included), `head_coach`, `assistant_coach`, `team_manager`; owners |
| `group.channels.manage` | group | Manage channels others created (members, archive) | `head_coach`; owners |
| `club.channels.create` | club | Club-wide channels (the board, all leaders) | `club_manager`, `chair` |
| `club.safeguarding.handle` | club | Reports and safeguarding cases | `safeguarding_officer` |

Rules 3–6 are not permissions: they are enforced in the chat service and in
SQL for every write and read, with allow/deny tests for each.

## UI
- App: "Mina kanaler" (unread first, the latest message, mute state), each
  group's channels on the group screen, the chat (cache first, sends queued
  offline), member list with reasons ("förälder till …", "ledare"),
  the banner for children ("Föräldrar kan gå med. Med i chatten: …"),
  editing with "redigerat" and versions, report, push per channel.
- Web: the same views.
- Guardians: their children's chats and who is in them; join.
- Safeguarding officers: cases, reading under the two-person rule.

## Privacy and security
`docs/dpia-chat.md` (complete it with what was built), ADR 0047. No message
text in logs; encrypted in transit and at rest; no end-to-end encryption.
Audit events for membership changes made by others, guardian joins,
safeguarding reads and closes, hides. README, help pages (sv, en, nb) and
the privacy notice describe the child-safety rules clearly; children see
them in the app in words they understand.

## Protected identity
Member lists and senders through `PersonPresenter`; protected people join
only by accepting; hidden from those who may not see them.

## Locale and country packs
sv, en, nb for every text; the child-safety texts written for children too.

## Performance
Budgets for "Mina kanaler", a chat's page and sending; the gateway's fan-out
through Valkey; load test with a club of 10,000 members and busy channels.

## Seed data
IKN F2015: an open team channel, a closed coaches' channel, a children's
chat with a parent who joined, Samira's accepted "Föräldrar" room (the
P1.12 story), a direct message from Erik to a player with the guardians
added, a reported message handled by a safeguarding officer.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Parts A, B and C delivered with green CI
- [ ] Real time on web and app through the gateway; cache first in the app
- [ ] Channels open and closed; direct messages; editing with versions;
      attachments prepared, not accepted
- [ ] Every rule above with allow/deny tests (children, guardians, leaders,
      other adults, protected people, closed channels, old messages)
- [ ] Reporting and safeguarding cases with the two-person rule
- [ ] Push per channel; no text in push for chats with children
- [ ] Erasure anonymises messages; the deletion ledger replays it
- [ ] Tenant isolation, matrix rows, budgets, history, audit
- [ ] README, help pages, privacy notice and `dpia-chat.md` updated;
      translations complete for sv, en and nb

## Out of scope
- The reviewing bot (ADR 0047: later).
- Files and images in chat (the model is prepared).
- Chats between children of different groups or clubs (the model allows
  them; the rules come later).
- Video broadcasts from a channel (Phase 4).
