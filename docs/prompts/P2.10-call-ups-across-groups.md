# P2.10 — Call-ups across groups, for series, and revoking a call-up

## Context
Read first: `CLAUDE.md`, ADR 0056 (this prompt's decisions), ADR 0036
(call-ups), ADR 0034 and P1.14 (series, answer requests and `request_at`),
ADR 0035 (roster, answers, attendance, guests), ADR 0043 (waiting lists and
capacity), ADR 0046 (club-defined roles), ADR 0038 (events set their own
call-ups), ADR 0055 (forms update in place).

What exists already:
- `activities.invitees_only` and `activity_invitees` (ADR 0036), set with
  `PUT /api/v1/activities/:id/invitees`, limited to participants of the
  activity's groups (`not_on_roster` otherwise); web page
  `/activities/:id/invitees`; domain event `activity.invitees.changed`.
- The roster query (`attendance.repository` `roster`), "Mitt schema"
  (`schedule_entries`), counts, attendance (with `guest` marks), calendar
  feeds, all reading `activity_invitees`.
- Answer requests: `request_lead`, `request_at`, `requested_at` on
  activities and series; the worker's `notifications:response_requests`.
- Group roles copied from the club type (`roles` and `club_type_roles`,
  template keys). Group memberships keep a `function` (a CHECK constraint
  allows six values) and a `role_id`; a trigger maps one to the other and
  sets `participates`/`leads` from the role.
- `app.group_places` counts every open membership with `participates`
  (ADR 0043), the duties' quotas and eligible players (P2.6) read
  `participates` too.
- The worker notifies `callup.added` and `callup.removed` at once on every
  `activity.invitees.changed` (ADR 0036 decision 7); this prompt changes
  that (below).
- `PUT /activities/:id/invitees` is allowed until the activity ends and
  does not refuse an event's sessions today.
- Series are split (`activity.series.split`), extended by the worker
  (`extendSeries`) and have skipped dates that can be restored (P1.4,
  P2.7d).

## Adjustments of 2026-11-28 (before implementation)
Checked against the code after P2.9; these override anything below that
says otherwise, and ADR 0056 is amended with them.

1. **When the call-up goes out** (amends ADR 0056 decision 5). With an
   answer request: at `request_at` (or when `requested_at` is set). Without
   one: `CALLUP_DEFAULT_LEAD_HOURS` (72) before the start. An activity
   saved, or a call-up changed, inside that window goes out when saved;
   changes by its leaders within `CALLUP_GRACE_MINUTES` (15) of it going
   out are still silent (corrections right after creating it). The API
   returns `callUpSentAt` (null until it has gone out) so that the web and
   the app warn before a revocation.
2. **Notifications before and after** (amends ADR 0036 decision 7): the
   worker no longer notifies every change at once. Nothing before the
   call-up goes out; `callup.added` after it; `callup.revoked` (new, with
   the reason) for a removal after it. `callup.removed` stays a valid type
   in the API (`/api/v1` only grows, ADR 0029) but is no longer sent.
3. **Table names**: the guest flag is `roles.guest` and
   `club_type_roles.guest`, not `club_roles`.
4. **Guest memberships**: a guest membership has `function = 'member'` and
   the guest role's `role_id` (the trigger keeps `participates`). Guests do
   **not** take places: `app.group_places`, the waiting list's offers and
   the group's `occupied` count exclude memberships whose role has
   `guest = true`. `GROUP_FUNCTIONS` is unchanged; the template key
   `guest_player` is added to the built-in role template, the club types'
   template roles and `app.builtin_role_template` (copied to existing
   clubs as in 0059/0060).
5. **The channel setting**: `groups.guests_in_channel boolean NOT NULL
   DEFAULT false` (in the data model below).
6. **Duties**: permanent guests are not players of the group for duty
   quotas, eligible players or the season's statistics (P2.6); they take
   shifts only as any member of the club may.
7. **Series life cycle**: a series' call-up follows a split (the successor
   gets the series' list and the overrides of its sessions), new sessions
   made by `extendSeries` and restored dates get the series' list, and a
   skipped date drops its overrides.
8. **Events**: `PUT /activities/:id/invitees` (and the per-person endpoint)
   refuse an event's sessions with `event_session` (ADR 0038: the
   confirmed participants are its call-up); P2.9's programmes lay out
   event sessions already called up to nobody.
9. **Until the start**: changes are refused after the start
   (`activity_started`), where today they are allowed until the end.
10. **Who can be called up ad hoc**: members of the club with an `active`
    membership (not paused, not left); leaders are never called up.
11. **Programmes (P2.9)**: laying out or copying a programme copies no
    call-ups, guests or revocations (no people); its activities start
    without a call-up.
12. **Budgets**: request budgets are required for the endpoints the app
    calls (the activity's call-up and the per-person toggle, the person's
    revocations in "Mitt schema"); the grid is web only and gets a budget
    as well, as it reads a page of sessions.

## Goal
A coach can call up players from other teams of the club, either as
permanent guests of the team or for single activities; decide person by
person who is called up to which session, also for a whole series, and
change single sessions until their call-up goes out; and take a call-up
back up to the start, with a warning and an explanation that reaches the
player and their guardians.

## Users and roles involved
Group owners and leaders who manage activities (`activity.manage`,
`group.members.manage`); leaders who read answers; players, guardians.

## User stories
- As a coach, I add Wilma from F2016 as a permanent guest of F2017 for the
  autumn, so that she is called up and answers like the others without
  being one of F2017's members (no place taken, no waiting list affected).
- As a coach, I call up two players from other teams to Saturday's match
  only, by typing part of their names.
- As a coach, I set who is called up to all of the series' Tuesday
  sessions at once, and then take Oskar out of next week's session because
  he is away, before the call-up goes out, without anyone being told.
- As a coach, I see a grid of the coming sessions and the players and
  switch single cells.
- As a coach, I take back a call-up that has already gone out, up to the
  start; I am warned and must write why; the player and the guardians get
  "Kallelsen har återkallats" with my reason.
- As a player or guardian, I am told when I am called up after the
  call-up went out, and when a call-up is taken back, with the reason; the
  revoked session shows the reason in my schedule until it has ended.

## Data model
Migration (forward-only, expand):
- `roles.guest boolean NOT NULL DEFAULT false` and the same on
  `club_type_roles`; template role `guest_player` (group scope,
  `group.participate`, `guest = true`, names sv "Gästspelare", en "Guest
  player", nb "Gjestespiller") added to the built-in type and copied to
  existing clubs (not required: a club may delete it).
- `groups.guests_in_channel boolean NOT NULL DEFAULT false`.
- `activity_series.invitees_only boolean NOT NULL DEFAULT false`.
- `activity_series_invitees` (club-owned: `club_id`, `series_id`,
  `person_id`, `added_by_person_id`, timestamps; unique series+person).
- `activity_invitee_overrides` (club-owned: `club_id`, `activity_id`,
  `person_id`, `kind` `added`|`removed`, `set_by_person_id`, timestamps;
  unique activity+person).
- `activity_callup_revocations` (club-owned: `club_id`, `activity_id`,
  `person_id`, `reason` text 1–500 (personal data), `revoked_by_person_id`,
  `revoked_at`, timestamps; unique activity+person, deleted when the
  person is called up again).
- All with RLS (`app.enable_tenant_rls`), history, `updated_at`, indexes
  starting from the series or activity (and person for "Mitt schema").
  Person columns declared in the module's `references.ts`. Retention: the
  attendance retention job also deletes overrides and revocations of
  activities past the period; erasure removes a person's revocation
  reasons.
- `activity_invitees` stays the materialised read model (ADR 0056
  decision 3); a series change rewrites it for the sessions that have not
  started, in the same transaction.

## API
| Method | Path | Permission | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| GET | `/api/v1/activities/:id/invitees` | `activity.responses.read` | Adds `candidates` from the whole club (paged, `q` search), `guest` per person, `callUpSentAt`, `revocations` |
| PUT | `/api/v1/activities/:id/invitees` | `activity.manage` | Any active club member; after the call-up went out, removals need `revocationReason` (`revocation_reason_required`); after the start `activity_started`; an event's session `event_session` |
| GET | `/api/v1/activity-series/:id/invitees` | `activity.responses.read` | The series' list and the grid: next sessions (keyset by start) × people, effective state, overrides, sent |
| PUT | `/api/v1/activity-series/:id/invitees` | `activity.manage` | The series' list; returns the sessions whose call-up went out where people would be revoked unless `revocationReason` is given |
| PUT | `/api/v1/activities/:id/invitees/:personId` | `activity.manage` | One cell: `{ calledUp: boolean, revocationReason? }` (an override on a series session) |

Group guests use the existing membership endpoints with the guest role.
Every endpoint in the permission matrix and in the request budgets; cost
independent of the club's size.

## Permissions
No new permissions. Policies as listed; allow and deny tests for each, and
tenant isolation (club B gets 404).

## Notifications
- `callup.added` when someone is added after the call-up went out (to the
  person and guardians with `view_schedule`), the answer buttons included.
- `callup.revoked` with the activity (or the series' sessions), who
  revoked it (through the person presenter) and the reason; push, email
  and the in-app list per the person's settings.
- Nothing is sent for changes before the call-up goes out (adjustment 1);
  the answer request (`response.requested`) goes to the effective list at
  `request_at`. `callup.removed` is no longer sent (adjustment 2).

## UI
Web (ADR 0055: in place, one tap per choice):
- Group page "Medlemmar": a "Gäster" section; "Lägg till gäst" with search
  as you type across the club (shows each person's own groups); end date.
- Activity call-up page: the activity's groups first, then guests, then
  "Kalla från andra grupper" (search as you type). Each person a toggle.
  After the call-up went out: removing opens a confirmation ("Kallelsen
  har redan gått ut <tid>. Spelaren och vårdnadshavarna får ett meddelande
  om återkallelse.") with a required reason field.
- Series page: "Kallelse för serien" (the list) and the grid of the next
  eight sessions (more on request); sent sessions marked; a cell change on
  a sent session asks for the reason as above.
- Schedule and activity page for the person: "Kallelsen återkallades" with
  the reason and who revoked it, until the session has ended.
Mobile: the person's view of a revoked call-up (schedule and activity
screen) and the notifications; leaders toggle single people on an
activity's call-up with the same warning and reason. The grid stays web.

## Privacy and security
The revocation reason is personal data about a child's participation:
visible to the person, their guardians and the activity's leaders; never
logged; audit `activity.callup.revoked` with IDs only; history on the
tables. Guests see only what participants of the group see. Guests are not
in the group's default chat channel unless the owners turn it on
(`groups.guests_in_channel`, default off).

## Protected identity
Candidates, guests, the grid, revocations and notifications show people
through `PeopleService.summaries` and `PersonPresenter`; protected people
are listed only for leaders who may see them, and a notification never
names a protected person to someone who may not see them.

## Locale and country packs
sv, en, nb for every new string and the template role's names.

## Performance
A series has at most a few hundred sessions and a group a few dozen
players: the grid reads one page of sessions and their invitees and
overrides by series (indexes by series and activity). A series change
rewrites at most the future sessions' invitees in one statement per table.
Request budgets for every new endpoint.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Permanent guests: answer, are counted, are marked as guests, take no
      place (`app.group_places`) and leave the waiting list alone; not in
      the channel by default; not players for duty quotas
- [ ] When the call-up goes out (adjustment 1), silent changes before it
      and within the grace period; `callUpSentAt` in the API
- [ ] A series' call-up follows splits, extensions and restored dates;
      events' sessions refused
- [ ] Ad hoc guests on one activity from the whole club
- [ ] Series call-up with per-session overrides; silent until sent
- [ ] Revocation until the start: warning, required reason, notification
      to the person and guardians, shown on their view; after the start
      refused
- [ ] Domain events and audit for every change; history on every table
- [ ] Permission matrix, tenant isolation, allow/deny tests, request budgets
- [ ] Playwright: Erik adds a guest from Minivolley; sets the series
      call-up; removes Elsa from one unsent session silently; revokes a
      sent call-up with a reason, and Sara (Alva's guardian) sees it
- [ ] Maestro: a guardian sees a revoked call-up with its reason
- [ ] Translations sv, en, nb
- [ ] Docs: `domain-model.md`, ADR 0036 (decision 7 amended) and 0056
      ("As implemented"), ADR 0043 (guests take no places),
      `roles-and-permissions.md` (regenerated), seed-data.md

## Out of scope
- Calling up players of another club.
- Reserves and automatic squad selection.
- Guest fees or LOK-stöd rules for guests beyond today's attendance marks.
