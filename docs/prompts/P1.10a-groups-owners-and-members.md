# P1.10a — Groups admin: owners, seasons, members, archiving

Part one of P1.10 (split 2026-11-06). P1.10b builds the waiting lists:
applying, offers, admission, the expiry and auto-offer job and their
notifications. This part lets club managers and owners run a group's
structure, owners and members on the web.

## Context

and ADR 0025 (protected identity, the authorization engine, owners of
parent groups), ADR 0029 (API only grows), ADR 0031 (club access state),
ADR 0039 (notifications), ADR 0040 (request budgets, one transaction per
request), ADR 0041 (leaving, assignments), ADR 0042 (history views),
`docs/domain-model.md` ("`groups`", "`group_memberships`", "`group_owners`",

What exists already (checked against the code 2026-11-06):
- **Groups (P1.6b):** `POST /clubs/:clubId/groups` (`club.groups.create`:
  club managers; owners create child groups of a group they own, the
  service checks the parent), `PATCH /groups/:groupId` (`group.update`;
  name, kind, details, admission settings, parent; no cycles),
  `POST /groups/:groupId/archive` (refused with unarchived child groups,
  `group_has_children`; today it only sets `archived_at`),
  `/restore` (club managers), `DELETE` (only never-used groups,
  `group_in_use`). Web: the group tree, "New group" / "New subgroup" and the
  edit form (`groupForm.*`).
- **Club settings (P1.6b):** `PATCH /clubs/:clubId/settings`
  (`club.settings.update`, MFA) with `UpdateClubSettingsSchema`, which
  includes `ownersCanAppointCoOwners` and `groupOwnersAdmitToClub`; the web
  settings page has no "Groups" section yet.
- **Services without endpoints:** `GroupsService.addOwner`/`removeOwner`
  (checks `owners_can_appoint_co_owners` and ownership through the
  ancestors; audit `group.owner.added`/`.removed`, event
  `group.owner.changed`), `createSeason`, `setGroupSeason`;
  `MembershipsService.addToGroup(InTx)` (active or paused club members;
  `already_in_group`), `endGroupMembership`.
- **Policies:** `group.owners.manage` (club managers; owners when the club
  allows co-owners) and `group.members.manage` (club managers, owners) have
  allow/deny tests but guard no endpoint.
- **Read side:** `GET /groups/:groupId` (owners through the presenter),
  `GET /groups/:groupId/members` (roster), `GET /groups/:groupId/history`
  (P1.9, club managers and registrars).
- **Leaving (P1.8a):** leaving a club ends the person's group memberships
  and ownerships and withdraws their open waiting-list entries
  (`withdrawWaitlistEntriesOfPersonInTx`, status `withdrawn`).
- **Notifications (P1.7):** the `waitlist` category is reserved in the
  catalogue with no types.

## Goal
A club manager appoints owners for each group; an owner runs their group
without the board: adds members with a function (player, head coach, …)
and ends them, sets the capacity per season, appoints co-owners where the
club allows it. Archiving a group that still has people in it is one clear
step that leaves nothing half-open.

## Users and roles involved
Club managers (owners, seasons, settings, everything an owner can do);
group owners, including owners of a parent group (members and functions,
seasons of their groups; co-owners only with the club setting); registrars
(read only here); members see the roster as today.

## User stories
- As a club manager, I want to appoint and remove group owners, and to let
  owners appoint co-owners if the club wants that.
- As a group owner, I want to add club members to my group with a function
  and end memberships, so that the roster is right without asking the
  registrar.
- As a group owner, I want to set the number of places per season.
- As a club manager, I want to archive a team at the end of its life: its
  players and leaders leave the team, its waiting list closes and the
  applicants are told, and the team's history stays.

## Decisions (2026-11-06)
- **Archiving a group with people in it** (decision 3): allowed. In one
  transaction, after the existing refusal for unarchived child groups:
  1. every open `group_memberships` row of the group, all functions
     (players and leaders), gets `valid_to = now()` (audit
     `group.membership.ended` with `changedFields: ['valid_to']` and the
     reason code `group_archived`); the people stay members of the club;
  2. every open waiting-list entry (`waiting`, `offered`, `accepted`)
     becomes `removed` with `closed_reason = 'group_archived'`, and the
     requester (and the applicant if they have a login) gets the
     notification `waitlist.entry.closed`;
  3. the ownerships stay open, so owners still find the archived group
     (read-only) and club managers can restore it;
  4. activities are not touched: with no members left they drop out of
     everyone's schedule, and they stay in the group's history.
  Restoring a group (club managers) brings back the group, not the
  memberships or waiting-list entries; the confirmation dialog says so
  ("12 players and 2 leaders leave the group; 5 people on the waiting
  list are told").
- **Who may own a group** (decision 4): a member (active or paused) of the
  club who has reached the country pack's `adult_age` and has a login
  (`owner_not_eligible` otherwise). Teenage assistants become leaders.
- **Candidate search for owners** (decision 5): owners search the club's
  active and paused members by name, with minimal fields.
- **Club settings** (decision 6): the existing whole-schema endpoint; the web
  adds only the "Groups" section.

## Data model
Expand-only; club-owned tables keep `club_id`, tenant RLS, record history
and `updated_at`.
- **`group_waitlist_entries.closed_reason`** (text, nullable; codes
  `declined`, `expired`, `withdrawn`, `removed_by_owner`,
  `join_request_rejected`, `left_club`, `group_archived`; a CHECK on the
  list, the enum may grow). Added here because archiving and leaving use
  it; P1.10b adds the rest of the waiting-list columns. Leaving a club now
  sets `left_club` on the entries it withdraws.
- **Effective capacity:** the group's `group_seasons.capacity` for the
  season covering today (club time zone), else `groups.capacity`; null is
  unlimited. **Occupied places** here = open `member`-function memberships
  (P1.10b adds outstanding offers and accepted entries). Leader functions
  never count. One function in the groups module, used by the members
  endpoint, the group page and later the waiting list.
- Move the person references of `group_waitlist_entries` from
  `JOINING_PERSON_REFERENCES` to `groups.references.ts`.
- Classify the new column in the history view policy
  (`apps/api/src/modules/audit/history-columns.ts`: `closed_reason` as an
  enum value with the new `waitlist.closedReasons` labels).

## API
Under `/api/v1`. Writes are refused for clubs that are not active
(ADR 0031). Problem codes: `owner_not_eligible`, `already_group_owner`,
`not_a_member`, `already_in_group`, `group_full` (`overCapacity: true`
overrides it), `group_archived`, `group_has_children`, ….

| Method | Path | Permission | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| POST | `/groups/:groupId/archive` (changed) | `group.update` | Ends memberships and closes the waiting list as decided above; the response says how many memberships ended and entries closed |
| GET | `/groups/:groupId/archive-preview` | `group.update` | The counts for the confirmation dialog (open memberships by member/leader, open waiting-list entries) |
| GET | `/clubs/:clubId/seasons` | `club.read` | The club's seasons |
| POST | `/clubs/:clubId/seasons` | `club.settings.update` | Name, start, end |
| PUT | `/groups/:groupId/seasons/:seasonId` | `group.update` | Per-season capacity and notes (idempotent) |
| POST | `/groups/:groupId/owners` | `group.owners.manage` | `{ personId }` (eligibility above) |
| DELETE | `/groups/:groupId/owners/:personId` | `group.owners.manage` | Ends the ownership; owners may remove themselves. A group may have no owners (club managers run it) |
| GET | `/groups/:groupId/candidates?q=&function=&cursor=` | `group.members.manage` | Active and paused club members matching a name, not in the group with that function: person (presenter), birth year, member number. Protected members only for viewers who may see them. Keyset, at most 20; bulk-read rate limit |
| POST | `/groups/:groupId/members` | `group.members.manage` | `{ personId, function, overCapacity? }`; a `member` to a full group needs `overCapacity: true` (audited). P1.10b adds the queue-bypass reason |

| GET | `/groups/:groupId` (changed) | `group.read` | Adds `capacity` (effective), `occupied` for those with `group.update` (optional fields, ADR 0029) |

Request budgets (ADR 0040): every endpoint the web calls gets a case in
`request-budgets.integration.test.ts`; one transaction per request.

## Permissions
- Existing policies now guard endpoints: `group.owners.manage`,
  `group.members.manage`, `group.update` (seasons, archive preview),
  `club.settings.update` (seasons). Add their matrix rows; the owner of the
  matrix scenario's section acts on its team; the team's head coach is
  denied; club B gets 404.
- No new permissions. Update the permissions table in `domain-model.md`.

## UI (web)
- **Group page:** "Owners" with add (club managers: the member register
  search; owners with the co-owner setting: `/candidates`) and remove;
  "Members" with "Add member" (search, function) and per row "End"; a
  capacity line ("8 of 10 places") and the full-group warning with the
  override checkbox; per-season capacity in the edit form.
- **Archive:** the confirmation page shows the archive preview's counts and
  what restoring does not bring back.
- **Club settings:** a "Groups" section with "Owners may appoint co-owners"
  and "Group owners admit new club members" (used by P1.10b; MFA prompt).
- Works without JavaScript (server actions); empty, error and conflict
  states.

## Privacy and security
- Owners see of candidates only name, birth year and member number; no
  phone numbers or addresses.
- Audit in the same transaction: `group.owner.added`/`.removed`,
  `group.membership.created`/`.ended` (with `overCapacity` and, on
  archiving, the reason code), `group.archived` (with the counts),
  `club.season.created`, `group.season.updated`, `club.settings.updated`.
- Notification `waitlist.entry.closed` (catalogue type in the `waitlist`
  category, email mandatory like other transactional notices; the code's
  text, never an owner's note), IDs only in the event.

## Protected identity
- Owners, members and candidates go through `PeopleService.summaries` and
  `PersonPresenter`. The candidate search leaves out protected members for
  owners who may not see them; club managers and registrars see them with
  the icon.

## As implemented (2026-11-07)
- Migration `0023_groups_admin`: `group_waitlist_entries.closed_reason`
  with a CHECK on the codes (`WAITLIST_CLOSED_REASONS`).
- The group's own module keeps groups, owners, seasons and the capacity
  (`capacityInTx`, `archiveGroupInTx`, `closeWaitlistOfGroupInTx`,
  `addOwnerInTx`); the memberships module orchestrates what touches
  `group_memberships` in one transaction: `addGroupMember`,
  `endMembershipInGroup`, `groupCandidates`, `archivePreview`,
  `archiveGroup`, `addGroupOwner` (eligibility from one statement with
  `app.person_has_account`). So `POST /groups/:groupId/archive` moved to the
  memberships controller (same path and response, plus `ended`).
- Extra read endpoint `GET /groups/:groupId/seasons` (the edit form needs
  the per-season values). `GET /clubs/:clubId` gains `groupSettings`;
  `GET /groups/:groupId` gains `effectiveCapacity`, `occupied`,
  `canManageMembers`, `canManageOwners`; the roster gains each function's
  membership ID for those who manage members. The candidate search's
  `function` is optional (without it nobody is left out: owner search).
- Web: `/groups/[groupId]/manage` (places, owners, members, seasons, a new
  season for club managers), `/groups/[groupId]/archive` (the preview and
  the confirmation; the edit page links to it), the "Groups" section of the
  club settings.
- Notification `waitlist.entry.closed` (category `waitlist`, mandatory
  email, at once) with the group's name and the reason code; it links to
  `/me/waitlists`.
- Not done here: moving the waiting-list person references from
  `JOINING_PERSON_REFERENCES` to `groups.references.ts` (left for P1.10b,
  which owns the waiting-list code).
- Request budgets: `GET /groups/:groupId` 6 statements for an owner (one
  more for the places), candidates 3, archive preview 4, group seasons 2,
  club seasons 2.

## Out of scope
- Waiting lists beyond closing them on archive (P1.10b).
- Mobile screens (#17); moving a member between groups in one step; season
  roll-over.
