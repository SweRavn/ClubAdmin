# P1.6 — Events with sign-up forms, deadlines, capacity and waiting lists

## Context
Read first: `CLAUDE.md`, ADR 0034 (activities, `schedule_entries`, the
`activity` resource kind), ADR 0035 (responses and attendance, the roster),
ADR 0036 (call-ups: `invitees_only` and `activity_invitees`, which this
package reuses for the participants of an event's activities), ADR 0019
(group waiting lists: the offer semantics this package mirrors for events),
ADR 0032 (transactional email through the outbox and a `MailProvider`),
ADR 0022 (protected identity), ADR 0025 (authorization engine), ADR 0028
(mobile app), ADR 0029 (API only grows), ADR 0031 (club access state),
`docs/domain-model.md` ("guardianships" and the rights `sign_up` and
`see_medical`, "Activities and My schedule", "Responses and attendance",
"Waiting lists", "Protected identity", "Effective permissions"),
`docs/security.md` (field-level encryption), `docs/gdpr.md` (retention),
`docs/performance.md`, `docs/seed-data.md` ("One cup weekend (event with
sign-up, deadline …) with events (P1.6)").

What exists already:
- **Activities (P1.4):** `activities` (single activities and series
  sessions; kinds `training`, `match`, `meeting`, `other`; the enum may grow,
  ADR 0029), `activity_groups`, `locations`, the read model
  `schedule_entries` and `GET /me/schedule`, group and club calendars.
  Permissions `group.activities.read/manage`, `activity.read/manage`.
- **Responses and attendance (P1.5, ADR 0035):** `activity_responses`,
  `attendance_records`, the roster (members of the activity's groups on its
  start), the offline outbox in the app, `activity.respond` (members, full
  guardians, linked people with `sign_up`), `activity.responses.read`,
  `activity.attendance.manage`.
- **Call-ups (ADR 0036):** `activities.invitees_only` and
  `activity_invitees`; with a call-up only the invitees are participants,
  answer, are counted and have the activity in "My schedule". Set through
  `AttendanceService.setInvitees` (`PUT /activities/:id/invitees`).
- **Families:** guardianship rights per link (`act_on_behalf`,
  `receive_messages`, `view_schedule`, `sign_up`, `see_medical`); full
  guardians hold every right. `X-On-Behalf-Of` for acting as a ward.
- **Group waiting lists:** `group_waitlist_entries` (schema since P0.5,
  behaviour in P1.10): a separate queue for group admission; events need
  their own, shorter-lived queue.
- **Email:** the worker's mail handler renders transactional emails from the
  ICU catalogues in the recipient's language and sends them through a
  `MailProvider` (SMTP, Mailpit in development); names of protected people
  are left out unless the recipient may see them. Preferences, push and
  in-app notifications come with P1.7.
- **Field-level encryption:** `FieldEncryption`
  (`apps/api/src/platform/crypto/field-encryption.ts`, per-club data keys in
  `crypto.data_keys`).
- **Retention:** the `attendance:retention` job deletes answers, attendance
  and call-ups after the club's `attendance_retention_months` (#18).
- **Nothing yet** for events, registrations or sign-up forms.

## Goal
A team manager announces the autumn cup weekend: two days of matches, at
most 12 players, sign-up until Wednesday evening, with a T-shirt size and
"allergies or other things the leaders should know". Parents sign their
children up on the web or in the app in a minute; when the cup is full, the
next ones go on a waiting list and get a place automatically, or an offer
to accept, when someone cancels. The team manager sees the list with the
answers, exports it for the cup organiser, and the cup's matches appear in
"My schedule" of exactly those signed up, with answers and attendance
working as for any activity. The same works for a club-wide end-of-season
party or a training camp. Health information given in a form is seen only
by the organisers and the child's guardians with `see_medical`, and is
deleted soon after the event.

## Users and roles involved
Organisers: those who manage the event's groups (owners, `head_coach`,
`assistant_coach`, `team_manager`) and club managers; for club-wide events
club managers and the board. Participants: members of the event's audience;
full guardians and linked people with `sign_up` sign up their wards; members
with their own login sign up themselves. Linked people with only
`view_schedule` see the event but cannot sign up. Registrars see
registrations (read-only) for the club's records.

## User stories
- As an organiser, I want to create an event for one or more groups or the
  whole club, with a description, a place, dates, a price shown as text, a
  sign-up period, a cancellation deadline and a capacity, so that families
  know what they sign up for.
- As an organiser, I want to add questions to the sign-up form (short text,
  long text, one choice, several choices, number, yes/no; required or not;
  some marked "sensitive"), so that I get the information I need.
- As an organiser, I want the event's matches or sessions in the calendar,
  either by creating them with the event or by linking existing activities,
  so that participants have them in "My schedule".
- As a guardian, I want to sign up each of my children, answer the form and
  change my answers until the sign-up closes, so that the organiser has
  current information.
- As a member with my own login, I want to sign myself up, and to see that
  my parent signed me up.
- As a guardian, I want to see whether my child has a place or is on the
  waiting list, and which number, so that I can plan.
- As a guardian, I want to cancel before the cancellation deadline, and see
  that after it I must contact the organiser.
- As someone on the waiting list, I want to get the place automatically, or
  an offer I can accept or decline within a stated time, when a place
  becomes free, and to be told by email.
- As an organiser, I want to see the registrations, the waiting list and
  the answers, move someone from the waiting list or remove a registration
  with a reason, and export the list (CSV), so that I can hand it to the cup
  organiser.
- As an organiser, I want to cancel an event, which cancels its activities
  and tells those signed up.
- As a relative with only `view_schedule`, I want to see the event but get
  no sign-up button.

## Data model
All club-owned tables: `club_id`, tenant RLS, record history, `updated_at`
(CLAUDE.md rule 3). A new module `events` (`apps/api/src/modules/events/`)
owns these tables and uses the activities module only through its exported
services. Person references in `events.references.ts`.

- `events`: `title`, `description` (plain text, 4,000 characters),
  `audience` (`groups`, `club`), `status` (`draft`, `published`,
  `cancelled`; the enum may grow), `starts_at`, `ends_at`, `location_id` or
  `location_text`, `price_text` (shown only, no payments), `registration_opens_at`
  (nullable: open when published), `registration_closes_at`,
  `cancellation_closes_at` (nullable: same as registration closes),
  `capacity` (nullable: unlimited), `waitlist_mode` (`none`: refuse when
  full; `auto`: move the next one up; `offer`: offer the place with
  `offer_reply_hours`, default 24), `offer_reply_hours`,
  `questions` (JSONB, validated with a shared Zod schema: an ordered list of
  `{ id, type, label, required, sensitive, options? }`, at most 20; labels
  in the organiser's language, never translated), `created_by_person_id`,
  `cancelled_reason`. Index `(club_id, starts_at)`.
- `event_groups`: the audience groups (`event_id`, `group_id`); empty for
  `audience = club`. Index `(club_id, group_id)`.
- `event_activities`: the event's activities (`event_id`, `activity_id`,
  unique `activity_id`: an activity belongs to at most one event). Each
  linked activity gets `invitees_only = true`, and its invitees are kept
  equal to the event's confirmed registrations in the same transaction as
  every registration change (ADR 0036): answers, attendance and "My
  schedule" then work unchanged. An activity with its own call-up cannot be
  linked (`activity_has_call_up`).
- `event_registrations`: `event_id`, `person_id` (the participant),
  `registered_by_person_id` (the person or a guardian), `status`
  (`confirmed`, `waitlisted`, `offered`, `cancelled`, `declined`, `expired`,
  `removed`; the enum may grow), `queued_at` (waiting-list order),
  `offer_expires_at`, `answers` (JSONB: answers to non-sensitive questions,
  by question ID), `sensitive_answers` (sealed with `FieldEncryption`: the
  answers to sensitive questions as one sealed JSON value),
  `removed_reason` (organisers only), `decided_by_person_id`, `decided_at`.
  One open registration per event and person (partial unique index on
  `(event_id, person_id)` where `status` is not `cancelled`, `declined`,
  `expired` or `removed`). Indexes `(club_id, event_id, status, queued_at)`
  for the lists and the queue, `(person_id, event_id)` for "My events".
- **Capacity under concurrency:** a registration locks the event row
  (`SELECT … FOR UPDATE`) and counts confirmed and offered registrations in
  the same transaction; never more than `capacity` places taken.
- **Moving up:** when a confirmed registration ends (cancelled, removed) or
  the capacity grows, the first waiting registration in `queued_at` order
  is confirmed (`auto`) or offered (`offer`) in the same transaction. An
  offer that is not accepted by `offer_expires_at` expires (worker job) and
  the next one is offered. Out-of-order moves by organisers need a reason
  (audited), as in ADR 0019.
- **Deadlines:** sign-up and changes to answers until
  `registration_closes_at`; cancelling until `cancellation_closes_at`; after
  that only organisers change registrations. Offers may still be accepted
  until they expire, also after the sign-up has closed.
- **Cancelling an event** cancels its linked activities (with the reason)
  and keeps the registrations; a cancelled event takes no registrations.
  Deleting is only possible for drafts.
- **Retention:** sensitive answers are deleted 30 days after the event ends
  (worker job; the organiser's export says so); registrations follow the
  club's `attendance_retention_months` like answers and attendance (extend
  `app.purge_expired_attendance` or add an events purge in the same job).
  Erasure (`privacy.erase_person`) deletes a person's sensitive answers and
  scrubs both answer columns from history.

Write a new ADR (the next free number) for: events as a separate sign-up container linked to
activities through call-ups, the waiting-list modes and offers, form
questions as validated JSONB with sealed sensitive answers, and their
retention.

## API
Under `/api/v1`. Writes refused for clubs that are not active (ADR 0031).
Problem codes, never values: `registration_closed`, `registration_not_open`,
`event_full`, `already_registered`, `not_in_audience`, `cancellation_closed`,
`offer_expired`, `event_cancelled`, `answer_required`, `answer_invalid`,
`activity_has_call_up`, ….

| Method | Path | Permission | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| GET | `/clubs/:clubId/events?from=&to=&cursor=` | `club.events.read` | Published events the caller may see (organisers also drafts); keyset by `(starts_at, id)` |
| GET | `/groups/:groupId/events?from=&to=&cursor=` | `group.activities.read` | The group's events |
| POST | `/clubs/:clubId/events` | `club.events.create` | Audience groups the caller manages (all of them), or the whole club (club managers, board); optionally creates its activities (title, kind, times, place) in the same transaction |
| GET | `/events/:eventId` | `event.read` | The event, its activities, counts (confirmed, waiting, places left), the caller's family's registrations with status and waiting-list position, and the questions |
| PATCH | `/events/:eventId` | `event.manage` | Fields above; capacity cannot go below the confirmed count; raising it moves people up |
| POST | `/events/:eventId/publish` / `/cancel` | `event.manage` | Cancel with a reason; cancels the linked activities |
| DELETE | `/events/:eventId` | `event.manage` | Drafts only |
| PUT | `/events/:eventId/activities` | `event.manage` | Link or unlink existing activities of the audience groups (the activities' managers only) |
| PUT | `/events/:eventId/registrations/:personId` | `event.register` | Sign up or change the answers; `personId` is the caller or a ward with `sign_up`; returns the status and position |
| DELETE | `/events/:eventId/registrations/:personId` | `event.register` | Cancel (until the cancellation deadline) |
| POST | `/events/:eventId/registrations/:personId/accept` / `/decline` | `event.register` | Answer an offer |
| GET | `/events/:eventId/registrations?status=&cursor=` | `event.registrations.read` | Organisers: everyone with status, position, answers; sensitive answers only for organisers |
| POST | `/events/:eventId/registrations/:personId/confirm` / `/remove` | `event.registrations.manage` | Move up out of order (reason required) or remove (reason) |
| GET | `/events/:eventId/registrations.csv` | `event.registrations.read` | Export (organisers; sensitive answers only with `?sensitive=true` and MFA); audited |
| GET | `/me/events?cursor=` | own data | Events the caller and their wards may sign up for or are registered to |
| GET | `/me/schedule` (changed) | own data | Entries of an event's activities carry `eventId` (new optional field) |

Answers are validated against the event's questions (types, required,
options) with a shared Zod schema built from the questions.

## Permissions
New permissions, one policy each in `authorization.policy.ts`, allow and
deny tests, entries in the permission matrix (tenant isolation: 404 for
club B). A new resource kind `event` (club, audience groups with their
ancestors, `audience`, `status`), resolved like `activity`.
- `club.events.read` (club): everyone with a relationship to the club
  (members, guardians of members, leaders, roles); `/clubs/:id/events`
  filters to events whose audience includes the caller or a ward, plus all
  events for organisers and club managers.
- `club.events.create` (club): club managers, the board (club-wide events)
  and those who manage at least one group (group events; the service checks
  each audience group).
- `event.read`: the audience (members of the audience groups or of the
  club, their guardians and linked people with `view_schedule` or
  `sign_up`), organisers, registrars; drafts only for organisers.
- `event.manage`: club managers; for group events those who manage every
  audience group (as `activity.manage`); for club-wide events also the
  board.
- `event.register`: members in the audience, their full guardians, linked
  people with `sign_up`; the service checks that `personId` is the caller or
  such a ward, with `X-On-Behalf-Of` only that ward.
- `event.registrations.read`: organisers (`event.manage`), the leaders of the
  audience groups (`other_leader` included), registrars.
- `event.registrations.manage`: organisers.
- Counts (confirmed, places left, waiting) for everyone who may read the
  event; names of participants only for viewers who hold
  `group.members.read` on an audience group (as for "who is coming",
  ADR 0035).

## UI
- **Web, club and group pages:** an "Events" list (upcoming, open for
  sign-up first; "Full", "Waiting list", "Closes Wednesday 20:00" badges);
  "New event" for organisers.
- **Web, event form:** details, audience, sign-up period, cancellation
  deadline, capacity and waiting-list mode, the price text, the questions
  (add, reorder, required, sensitive, options; works without JavaScript:
  one form, "add question" posts and returns), and the activities (create
  sessions with the event, or link existing ones).
- **Web, event page:** description, dates and place, the activities, places
  left, the caller's family with "Sign up" / "Change answers" / "Cancel" /
  "Accept" / "Decline" per person and the status ("Place confirmed",
  "Number 3 on the waiting list", "Offered: answer by Friday 18:00"); the
  sign-up form with the questions; for organisers the registrations list
  (confirmed, offered, waiting, cancelled), the answers in a table, "Move
  up" and "Remove" with a reason, "Export CSV".
- **Web, "My schedule" and home:** events open for sign-up for me and my
  wards ("Sign up by …"); the event's activities appear for those confirmed
  (through the call-up).
- **Mobile:** an events list from "My schedule" (events open for my family,
  my registrations), the event detail with sign-up, the form (all question
  types, large touch targets), cancel, accept and decline. Registrations
  need a connection (capacity is decided by the server): offline the
  buttons are disabled with "Needs a connection", not queued in the outbox.
  Organiser tools stay on the web.
- Loading, empty ("No events yet"), full, closed, offline and error states;
  times in the club's time zone; translations in sv, en, nb.

## Privacy and security
- Registrations and answers are personal data, often about children; the
  legal basis is the membership (running the event). Questions marked
  sensitive (health, allergies, medication) are special-category data:
  sealed with field-level encryption, shown only to organisers and to the
  participant and guardians with `see_medical`, never in lists for others,
  exports without `?sensitive=true`, emails, push, logs or calendars; the
  form shows "Only the organisers see this" next to them; deleted 30 days
  after the event.
- The form builder tells organisers not to ask for more than needed and
  never for personal identity numbers (a text next to the questions; no
  automatic check of the labels).
- Audit events in the same transaction: `event.created`, `.updated`,
  `.published`, `.cancelled`, `.deleted`; `event.registration.created`,
  `.updated` (changed question IDs, never values), `.cancelled`,
  `.offered`, `.accepted`, `.declined`, `.expired`, `.confirmed` (out of
  order, with the reason), `.removed` (with the reason);
  `event.registrations.exported` (with whether sensitive answers were
  included). Record history on all tables (sealed values only).
- Domain events with IDs only: `event.changed`, `event.registration.changed`
  (status changes, for P1.7), `event.offer.made` (consumed by the mail
  handler now: "You have a place" / "A place is offered to you, answer by
  …" to the participant and guardians with `receive_messages`, in their
  language; P1.7 adds preferences and push).
- A guardian signing up on behalf of a ward is recorded as on-behalf-of
  (ADR 0006); a revoked `sign_up` stops further changes, registrations
  already made stay, attributed.
- Rate limits on the registration endpoints per account (P0.10).

## Protected identity
- Participant lists for members and guardians name only people they may
  see (PersonPresenter); protected participants are counted, never named.
- Organisers' lists, the export and the waiting list name protected
  participants with the crossed-camera icon (they may see them, ADR 0022);
  the export is marked "contains protected persons: do not share" and
  leaves protected persons out unless the caller confirms; the mobile event
  detail does not list participants.
- Emails about offers never name other participants; "registered by" names
  the guardian through the presenter.
- Nothing about registrations appears in calendar data; the event's
  activities follow ADR 0036 (only those confirmed have them).

## Locale and country packs
- Strings in sv, en and nb: statuses, waiting-list phrases ("Number 3 on
  the waiting list"), deadlines, question types, the sensitive notice,
  problem codes, the offer emails.
- Questions and options are written by the organiser and shown as written.
- No country-pack rules in this package; the age of consent for answering
  sensitive questions for oneself follows the existing guardianship rules
  (a ward answers for themselves only with their own login).

## Performance
- `GET /events/:id` with the caller's family's registrations < 50 ms;
  `GET /me/events` < 50 ms (index `(person_id, event_id)` and the audience
  through the caller's open group memberships).
- A sign-up, including the capacity check, the call-up sync of up to five
  linked activities and the audit event, < 100 ms p95; 100 parallel sign-ups
  to an event with 20 places confirm exactly 20 (integration test).
- The organisers' list and the CSV export for 500 registrations < 300 ms
  (one statement for the registrations, one for the people, decryption
  only when sensitive answers are asked for).
- Load scenario: a few events per club with registrations (about 50 per
  event); query-plan tests for the lists and "My events".

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] An organiser creates a group or club-wide event with questions,
      capacity, deadlines and its activities; members of the audience see
      it, others get 404.
- [ ] A guardian signs up each child with answers (web and app); a teen
      signs up themselves; a relative without `sign_up` gets no button and
      `403` from the API.
- [ ] Capacity is never exceeded under parallel sign-ups; the next ones go
      on the waiting list in order; a cancellation moves the next one up
      (`auto`) or offers the place (`offer`), and an unanswered offer
      expires and moves on (worker job).
- [ ] Sign-up, changes and cancellation follow their deadlines; organisers
      change registrations after them, with reasons for out-of-order moves
      and removals.
- [ ] Confirmed participants, and only they, have the event's activities in
      "My schedule"; answers and attendance work on them unchanged
      (ADR 0036).
- [ ] Sensitive answers are encrypted, visible only to organisers and to
      the participant's guardians with `see_medical`, left out of the
      export unless asked for with MFA, and deleted 30 days after the
      event.
- [ ] Offer emails reach the participant and guardians with
      `receive_messages` (Mailpit), in their language, naming no other
      participant.
- [ ] Cancelling an event cancels its activities and refuses new
      registrations.
- [ ] Performance budgets met (timing and query-plan tests).
- [ ] Tenant-isolation test for every new endpoint
- [ ] Allow/deny tests for every new policy
- [ ] Audit events for every change to events and registrations
- [ ] Translations complete for sv and en (and nb)
- [ ] Docs updated: `domain-model.md` (events, registrations, retention,
      `sign_up` and `see_medical`), `architecture.md` (the `events` module,
      worker jobs), `performance.md`, `seed-data.md`, `gdpr.md` (retention
      of registrations and sensitive answers), ADR 0036 (call-ups set by
      events), the new ADR; Maestro flow for signing up from the app

## Out of scope
- Payments for events and fees (Phase 3; `price_text` is shown only).
- Car pools (Phase 2) and volunteer duties.
- Events open to non-members or the public (public club pages, Phase 3).
- Several participant categories or ticket types (players, parents,
  helpers), group sign-up of a whole team at once, and quotas per group.
- Reminders before the sign-up closes and notification preferences (P1.7;
  the domain events exist).
- Recurring events and copying an event to next year.
- Questions with file uploads.

## Open decisions
1. **Event or activity?** An event is a separate sign-up container linked
   to activities, which reuse call-ups for their participants. Recommended,
   as above; the alternative (sign-up fields on activities) mixes the
   roster of a group with a sign-up list.
2. **Waiting-list default:** `offer` with 24 hours (fair, gives families a
   choice) or `auto` (simpler, for parties). Recommended: `offer` for group
   events, `auto` for club-wide ones, chosen per event.
3. **Retention of sensitive answers:** 30 days after the event. Recommended
   as above; longer only if a club needs them for insurance (then a club
   setting).
4. **Who creates club-wide events:** club managers and the board.
   Recommended as above; a dedicated "event organiser" role can come later.
5. **Offer emails before P1.7:** sent now through the transactional email
   pipeline (no preferences yet). Recommended, because a waiting list
   without notice is unfair; P1.7 adds preferences and push.
