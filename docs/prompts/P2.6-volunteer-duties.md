# P2.6 — Volunteer duties (arbetspass)

## Context
Read first: `CLAUDE.md`, ADR 0050 (this prompt's decisions), ADR 0047 and
ADR 0051 (background checks for duties alone with children), ADR 0049
(instructions as documents), ADR 0034, ADR 0040, ADR 0044 (calendar feeds).

What exists already:

- Activities, events, "Mitt schema" (`person_schedule`), calendar feeds.
- Guardianships and families; club and group settings with the club
  deciding whether groups may change them (P1.6b, settings registry).
- After P2.4: documents; after P2.7: background-check records.

## Goal
Clubs replace their duty spreadsheets: a catalogue of duty types, shifts at
matches, cups and cleaning days, a quota per player and season that the
family fulfils, sign-up and assignment, free swaps, reminders, and an
overview for the leaders. Prepared for automatic distribution and the
qualifications register (Phase 4) and for buying out (Phase 3).

## Users and roles involved
Club managers (catalogue), group leaders and team managers (shifts,
quotas), guardians, adult members, young members from a duty type's
minimum age.

## User stories
- As a team manager, I create kiosk shifts for every home match and set two
  shifts per player this season.
- As a parent, "Mina pass" shows my children's quotas and open shifts; I
  sign up for a kiosk shift that counts for my son.
- As a parent, I offer my shift; another parent takes it and confirms.
- As a coach, I see unstaffed shifts and the quota per player.

## Decisions (ADR 0050)
1. **Duty types**: name, instruction (documents attached), minimum age,
   background-check requirement (category per ADR 0051: e.g. alone with
   children); required qualifications prepared in the model, not checked.
2. **Shifts**: type, time, place, number needed, note; tied to an activity,
   an event (session) or standalone; in a group or the club. In "Mitt
   schema" and calendar feeds.
3. **Quota per player**: per group and season; a shift counts towards one
   player's quota whoever in the family does it (guardian, older sibling,
   a family member); adult members count their own.
4. **Sign-up** (first come, open shifts shown to those who may take them:
   age, background check) and **assignment** by leaders.
5. **Free swaps**: offer or ask; the taker confirms; leaders see, do not
   approve.
6. Reminder push the day before; afterwards done or missed (missed does not
   count); leaders mark. **Proposed:** a shift past without marking counts
   as done after 7 days unless marked missed.
7. **Visibility setting** (who sees whom on which shift, others' quotas):
   the club's default and whether groups may change it either way; leaders
   always see everything in their group.
8. **"Mina pass"** and the group overview; **statistics** of hours and
   shifts per person, player, group and season (CSV, also for grant
   reporting of voluntary work).
9. Prepared: unavailability periods and quota fields for Phase 4's
   automatic distribution; `bought_out` on a shift assignment or quota share
   for Phase 3.

## Data model
Club-owned, tenant RLS, history, `updated_at`, person references declared.

- `duty_types` (club_id, name, instruction document ids, min_age,
  check_category nullable, required_qualifications JSON prepared).
- `duty_shifts` (club_id, group_id nullable, type_id, activity_id or
  event_session_id nullable, starts_at, ends_at, location_id, needed,
  note).
- `duty_assignments` (shift_id, person_id doing it, counts_for_person_id
  (the player), source `signup` | `assigned` | `swap`, status `planned` |
  `done` | `missed`, bought_out).
- `duty_swap_offers` (assignment_id, offered_by, taken_by, confirmed_at).
- `duty_quotas` (group_id, season_id, shifts_per_player).
- `duty_unavailability` (person_id, from, to) prepared.
- Settings: `duties.visibility` (club default, group override allowed).

## API
| Method | Path | Permission | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| GET | `/me/duties` | authenticated | "Mina pass": mine and my children's, quotas, open shifts |
| GET/POST/PATCH | `/clubs/:clubId/duty-types[/:id]` | `club.duty_types.manage` | Catalogue |
| GET/POST/PATCH/DELETE | `/clubs/:clubId/groups/:groupId/duty-shifts[/:id]` | `group.duties.manage` | Shifts in the group |
| GET/POST/PATCH/DELETE | `/clubs/:clubId/duty-shifts[/:id]` | `club.duties.manage` | Club shifts |
| POST/DELETE | `…/duty-shifts/:id/signups` | eligible member or guardian | Sign up, cancel |
| POST | `…/duty-shifts/:id/assignments` | `group.duties.manage` | Assign |
| POST | `/me/duty-assignments/:id/swap-offers` · `/…/take` | assignee · eligible | Swap |
| POST | `…/duty-assignments/:id/status` | `group.duties.manage` | Done/missed |
| GET/PUT | `/clubs/:clubId/groups/:groupId/duty-quotas` | `group.duties.manage` | Quotas |
| GET | `/clubs/:clubId/groups/:groupId/duty-stats` | `group.duties.manage` | Statistics, CSV |

## Permissions
| Permission | Scope | Allows | Default |
|---|---|---|---|
| `group.duties.manage` | group | Create, assign and mark shifts; quotas | `head_coach`, `assistant_coach`, `team_manager`; owners |
| `club.duties.manage` | club | Club shifts, every group | `club_manager` |
| `club.duty_types.manage` | club | The catalogue of duty types | `club_manager` |

Eligibility (age, background check) is a rule, tested allow/deny.

## UI
- "Mina pass" (app and web): upcoming, quotas left per child, open shifts,
  swap offers.
- Group: shifts list and calendar, quota overview, unstaffed first.
- Activity and event pages: their shifts.
- Club: duty types, club shifts, visibility setting.

## Privacy and security
Who works when is personal data; visibility per setting; the background
check only as valid/not valid from P2.8 records. Audit for assignments by
others and status changes.

## Protected identity
Shift lists through `PersonPresenter`; protected people shown only to
leaders.

## Locale and country packs
sv, en, nb; no country rules (checks come from the plugins).

## Performance
"Mina pass" from the person's family and groups; quota sums per group and
season with an index on (group, season); budgets.

## Seed data
IKN F2015: kiosk type, shifts for three home matches, quota two per player,
Anna signed up for her child, one swap offered, one unstaffed shift.
