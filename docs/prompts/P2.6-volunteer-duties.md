# P2.6 — Volunteer duties (arbetspass)

## Context
Read first: `CLAUDE.md`, ADR 0050 (this prompt's decisions, with its
amendment of 2026-11-18), ADR 0052 (shifts and meeting action items are one
system of assigned tasks), ADR 0047 (no adult alone with a child), ADR 0051
(background checks come with the plugins), ADR 0049 (instructions as
documents), ADR 0034, ADR 0040, ADR 0044 (calendar feeds), ADR 0041 (the
settings registry).

What exists already:

- Activities, events (their sessions are activities), "Mitt schema"
  (`person_schedule`), calendar feeds.
- Guardianships and families; seasons (`seasons`, `group_seasons`, P1.10a);
  club and group settings with the club deciding whether groups may change
  them (P1.6b, the settings registry).
- Documents (P2.2). The `tasks` and `task_assignments` tables with meeting
  action items, `counts_for_person_id`, the statuses planned, done and
  missed, and "Mina uppgifter" (P2.4, migration 0046).
- Notices with categories and push (P1.7); the worker's scheduled jobs.
- Owners of a group get its group permissions (`OWNER_PERMISSIONS`, P2.5).
- Background-check records come with P2.7 and P2.8: P2.6 has **no**
  background-check requirement at all (decision 3 below).

## Goal
Clubs replace their duty spreadsheets: a catalogue of duty types, shifts at
matches, cups and cleaning days, a quota per player and season that the
family fulfils, sign-up and assignment, free swaps (offered and asked for),
reminders, and an overview for the leaders. Prepared for automatic
distribution and the qualifications register (Phase 4) and for buying out
(Phase 3).

## Users and roles involved
Club managers (catalogue), group leaders and team managers (shifts,
quotas), guardians, adult members, young members from a duty type's
minimum age.

## User stories
- As a team manager, I create kiosk shifts for every home match and set two
  shifts per player this season.
- As a parent, "Mina uppgifter" shows my children's quotas and open shifts; I
  sign up for a kiosk shift that counts for my son (with two children in the
  group I choose which one).
- As a parent, I offer my shift; another parent takes it.
- As a parent, I ask to take over another parent's shift; they accept.
- As a coach, I see unstaffed shifts and the quota per player.
- As a club manager in Sweden, I cannot create a duty type where an adult is
  alone with children.

## Decisions (ADR 0050 and its amendment of 2026-11-18)
1. **Duty types**: name, instruction (documents attached), minimum age, the
   **contact with children** it means (`none`, `with_children`: with
   children together with other adults, `alone_with_children`); required
   qualifications prepared in the model, not checked. **No background-check
   requirement in P2.6**: requirements per category come with the
   background-check plugins (P2.8, ADR 0051), on the same field.
2. **Country rule: no adult alone with a child.** Every country pack has
   `dutyRules.aloneWithChildren`: `forbidden` or `allowed`. Sweden:
   `forbidden` (ADR 0047, RF's "Trygg idrott"); Norway: `allowed` until its
   rule is confirmed (a Norwegian club can forbid it itself). A club may
   be stricter than its pack (forbid where the pack allows; the club
   setting `duties.alone_with_children_forbidden`), never less strict (the
   registry's limits, ADR 0041). Where forbidden, a duty type with
   `alone_with_children` can be neither created nor changed to it
   (`DomainError("duty_alone_with_children_forbidden")`), and existing types
   with it (a club that later forbids it) cannot get new shifts. The rule is
   checked in the service and tested per pack.
3. **Shifts**: type, time slot, place, number needed, note; tied to an
   activity, an event session or standalone; in a group or the club. They
   appear in "Mitt schema" and the calendar feeds of those assigned (and of
   the player it counts for), with their own request budgets.
4. **Quota per player**: per group and season (`group_seasons`); a shift
   counts towards one player's quota whoever in the family does it
   (guardian, older sibling, a family member); adult members count their
   own. **Whom it counts for** is chosen at sign-up; with one eligible
   player in the group it is chosen automatically.
5. **Sign-up** (first come, open shifts shown to those who may take them:
   the duty type's minimum age) and **assignment** by leaders.
6. **Free swaps, both ways**: a person **offers** their shift (anyone who
   may take it takes it, which is the confirmation) or **asks** to take over
   someone else's shift (the person holding it accepts or declines). The
   counterpart always confirms; leaders see swaps, never approve them.
   Withdrawing an open offer or request is allowed until it is confirmed.
7. **Notices**: a new category `duties` with fixed texts: a push reminder
   the day before, an assignment by a leader, a swap request to the holder,
   a completed swap to both. Afterwards the leaders mark done or missed. Only
   done counts towards the quota; a past shift nobody marked stays planned
   (never counted automatically) and is listed for the leaders under "Att
   markera"; the quota shows done and planned apart.
8. **Visibility setting** `duties.visibility`, three levels: `open` (everyone
   in the group sees who has which shift and everyone's quotas), `names`
   (names on shifts, only one's own and one's children's quotas), `leaders`
   (only leaders see names; others see filled or open). The club's default
   and whether groups may change it either way; leaders always see
   everything in their group.
9. **"Mina uppgifter"** and the group overview; **statistics** of hours and
   shifts per person, player, group and season (CSV, also for grant
   reporting of voluntary work).
10. Prepared: unavailability periods and quota fields for Phase 4's
    automatic distribution; `bought_out` on a shift assignment or quota share
    for Phase 3.

## Data model
Club-owned, tenant RLS, history, `updated_at`, person references declared,
erasure in `privacy.erase_person`.

- `duty_types` (club_id, name, instruction document ids, min_age,
  child_contact `none` | `with_children` | `alone_with_children`,
  required_qualifications JSON prepared, archived).
- Shifts are **tasks** (ADR 0052) of kind `duty_shift` in the `tasks` and
  `task_assignments` tables P2.4 built: P2.6 widens the `tasks_kind` check
  (expand only) and adds `duty_type_id`, `location_id`, `needed` and the
  time slot (`starts_at`, `ends_at` exist) to `tasks`, `bought_out` to
  `task_assignments` (`counts_for_person_id` is the player).
- `task_swaps` (assignment_id, kind `offer` | `request`, created_by,
  counterpart, counts_for for the taker, status `open` | `done` |
  `declined` | `withdrawn`, decided_at).
- `duty_quotas` (group_id, season_id, shifts_per_player).
- `duty_unavailability` (person_id, from, to) prepared.
- Settings: `duties.visibility` (club default, group override allowed),
  `duties.alone_with_children_forbidden` (club, limited by the pack).
- Country packs: `dutyRules: { aloneWithChildren: "forbidden" | "allowed" }`.

## API
| Method | Path | Permission | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| GET | `/me/tasks` (from P2.4, extended) | authenticated | "Mina uppgifter": shifts and action items, mine and my children's, quotas, open shifts, swaps |
| GET/POST/PATCH | `/clubs/:clubId/duty-types[/:id]` | `club.duty_types.manage` | Catalogue; the country rule |
| GET/POST/PATCH/DELETE | `/clubs/:clubId/groups/:groupId/duty-shifts[/:id]` | `group.duties.manage` | Shifts in the group |
| GET/POST/PATCH/DELETE | `/clubs/:clubId/duty-shifts[/:id]` | `club.duties.manage` | Club shifts |
| GET | `/activities/:activityId/duty-shifts` | `activity.read` | An activity's shifts (event sessions too) |
| POST/DELETE | `…/duty-shifts/:id/signups` | eligible member or guardian | Sign up (`countsForPersonId`), cancel |
| POST | `…/duty-shifts/:id/assignments` | `group.duties.manage` | Assign |
| POST | `/me/duty-assignments/:id/swaps` | assignee (offer) · eligible (request) | Offer or ask |
| POST | `/me/duty-swaps/:id/take` · `/accept` · `/decline` · `/withdraw` | eligible taker · holder · holder · creator | Confirm or withdraw |
| POST | `…/duty-assignments/:id/status` | `group.duties.manage` | Done/missed |
| GET/PUT | `/clubs/:clubId/groups/:groupId/duty-quotas` | `group.duties.manage` | Quotas |
| GET | `/clubs/:clubId/groups/:groupId/duty-stats` | `group.duties.manage` | Statistics, CSV |

## Permissions
| Permission | Scope | Allows | Given to these template roles |
|---|---|---|---|
| `group.duties.manage` | group | Create, assign and mark shifts; quotas | `head_coach`, `assistant_coach`, `team_manager`; owners |
| `club.duties.manage` | club | Club shifts, every group | `club_manager` |
| `club.duty_types.manage` | club | The catalogue of duty types | `club_manager` |

Eligibility (minimum age, the family's players for "counts for") is a rule,
tested allow/deny; the country rule is tested per pack.

## UI
- "Mina uppgifter" (app and web): upcoming, quotas left per child, open shifts,
  swap offers and requests (to take, to accept).
- Group: shifts list and calendar, quota overview, unstaffed first, "Att
  markera".
- Activity and event pages: their shifts.
- Club: duty types (the contact with children; the alone option missing
  where forbidden), club shifts, visibility setting.

## Privacy and security
Who works when is personal data; visibility per setting. No background-check
data in P2.6. Audit for assignments by others, swaps and status changes.

## Protected identity
Shift lists through `PersonPresenter`; protected people shown only to
leaders.

## Locale and country packs
sv, en, nb; the country pack's `dutyRules` (SE forbids an adult alone with
children; NO allows it until confirmed); background checks come from the plugins.

## Performance
"Mina uppgifter" from the person's family and groups; quota sums per group and
season with an index on (group, season); shifts in "Mitt schema" and the
feeds without growing with the club; budgets for every endpoint the app
calls.

## Seed data
IKN F2015: a kiosk type (contact `none`), a "Sekretariat" type (contact
`with_children`), shifts for three home matches, quota two per player,
Anna signed up for her child, one swap offered and one asked for, one
unstaffed shift, one past shift to mark. The Swedish pack refuses an "alone
with children" type (tests, not seed).

## Parts
- **A:** migration, country-pack rule, permissions and policies, API for
  types, shifts, sign-up, assignment and quotas, tests and matrix rows.
- **B:** swaps (offer and request), notices and the reminder job, done and
  missed with "Att markera", the visibility setting, statistics CSV,
  shifts in "Mitt schema" and the feeds.
- **C:** web and app ("Mina uppgifter", group overview, activity and event
  pages, club catalogue), seed, budgets, e2e, docs.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Types, shifts, quotas, sign-up (counts for), assignment, swaps both
      ways, reminders, done/missed
- [ ] The country rule: no alone-with-children type where the pack or club
      forbids it
- [ ] Visibility setting (three levels) with group override per club permission
- [ ] "Mina uppgifter", group overview, statistics CSV
- [ ] Prepared fields for Phase 3 and 4 exist and are unused
- [ ] Tenant isolation, allow/deny, matrix rows, budgets, audit, history
- [ ] Translations sv, en, nb; docs (`domain-model.md`, ADR 0050 "As
      implemented")
