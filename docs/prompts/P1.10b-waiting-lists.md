## Goal
An owner runs a fair waiting list for their group. A parent
puts their child on the waiting list of a full team (also from outside the
club, after finding it in the club search), sees "Viktor is number 2", gets
an email when a place is offered and accepts it with one click; acceptance
makes the child a member of the team, and of the club if needed. Offers
expire after the group's reply time and, if the owner wants, the next in
line gets the place automatically. Admitting anyone out of turn needs a
reason that club managers can see.

## Users and roles involved
Club managers (everything an owner can do, the club-wide view of waiting
lists and out-of-order decisions); group owners, including owners of a
parent group (the waiting list); registrars (approve the club
join request when owners may not admit to the club); members and full
guardians or guardians with `act_on_behalf` (apply, see the position,
accept or decline offers, withdraw); signed-up people without a club (apply
through the club search, which also creates a join request); the worker
(expiry and auto-offers, as a system job).

## User stories
- As a group owner, I want to choose whether offers go out automatically.
- As a parent, I want to put my child on a team's waiting list, with a
  short note, and see their position and status, so that I know where we
  stand.
- As someone outside the club, I want to find the club's groups that take
  applications in the club search and apply, so that I need no contact at
  the club.
- As a group owner, I want the waiting list in fair order (priority, then
  time) with birth year, gender category and waiting time, so that I can
  pick who fits the group.
- As a group owner, I want to admit directly or send a time-limited offer,
  and to give a reason when I skip someone, so that the decision is fair
  and traceable.
- As a parent, I want an email when my child is offered a place and to
  accept or decline it in one step before it expires.
- As a group owner, I want an expired or declined offer to move on to the
  next in line automatically when I turn auto-offers on.
- As a club manager, I want to see all waiting lists and every
  out-of-order decision with its reason.
- As a registrar, I want to approve the club membership of someone an owner
  admitted when the club does not let owners admit to the club.

## Data model
All club-owned tables keep `club_id`, tenant RLS, record history and
`updated_at` (CLAUDE.md rule 3); new columns are expand-only with defaults
that keep existing rows valid. Move the person references of
`group_waitlist_entries` from `JOINING_PERSON_REFERENCES` to
`groups.references.ts` (the waiting list belongs to the groups module, as
`architecture.md` plans).

- **`groups.waitlist_auto_offer`** (boolean, default false): when a place
  is free or an offer expires or is declined, the worker offers the place
  to the next `waiting` entry. Only meaningful with `admission_mode =
  waitlist`.
- **`group_waitlist_entries`** gains `offered_at` (timestamptz),
  `offered_by_person_id` (null for auto-offers), `join_request_id`
  (nullable, same club: the pending join request created with the
  application of a non-member), `priority_reason` (`sibling`,
  `returning_member`, `other`; the enum may grow; required when
  `priority > 0`). (`closed_reason` exists since P1.10a.) Classify the new
  columns in the history view policy. Index
  `(club_id, status, offer_expires_at) WHERE status = 'offered'` for the
  expiry job, and `(club_id, decided_at) WHERE out_of_order_reason IS NOT
  NULL` for the managers' view.
- **Status machine** (enforced in the service and a CHECK on the pairs that
  need data):
  `waiting → offered → admitted` (or `→ accepted → admitted` while a club
  join request waits for approval), `offered → declined | expired`,
  `offered → waiting` (the owner withdraws an offer: back in line with the
  original `queued_at`), `waiting | offered | accepted → withdrawn`
  (applicant) or `removed` (owner, club). `declined`, `expired`,
  `withdrawn`, `removed`, `admitted` are closed. Applying again creates a
  new entry with a new `queued_at`.
- **Occupied places** (extends P1.10a's function) = open `member`-function
  memberships plus outstanding offers plus `accepted` entries. A group is
  **full** when occupied ≥ effective capacity.
- **Position** = rank among the group's `waiting` entries by
  `(priority DESC, queued_at, id)`. Applicants cannot read other entries
  under RLS, so positions come from `app.waitlist_positions(entry_ids
  uuid[])`, a SECURITY INVOKER function that sets `app.lookup =
  'waitlist_position'` for its one query (a `lookup_read` policy on
  `group_waitlist_entries` applies only then) and returns `(entry_id,
  position, waiting_count)` for entries the caller may read, nothing else
  (ADR 0032 pattern).
- **Groups open for applications** to people outside the club:
  `app.club_open_groups(club_id)` (same pattern, `app.lookup =
  'open_groups'`) returns, for clubs the club search lists (not
  `invite_only`, active, not suspended), the unarchived groups with
  `admission_mode = waitlist` and `waitlist_visible_publicly`: name, kind,
  parent name, sport, birth years, gender category, level, whether full and
  the number waiting. No people.
- **Retention:** closed entries are deleted 12 months after `decided_at`
  (or `updated_at`) by `joining:retention` (extend it, as
  `clubadmin_retention`; query-plan test; update `docs/gdpr.md`). Erasure
  already clears the notes (P1.8a).
- No new tables. The owner and member writes use the existing tables and
  services.

Write ADR 0043, "Waiting lists: capacity, offers, admission and
positions", for the status machine, capacity and occupied places, the
position lookup, auto-offers by job, admission across the club membership
and join requests, and the visibility of applicants (below); amend ADR

## API
Under `/api/v1`. Writes are refused for clubs that are not active (ADR
0031). Problem codes, never values: `group_cycle`, `group_parent_invalid`,
`group_archived`, `group_has_children`, `group_not_managed`,
`already_group_owner`, `owner_not_eligible`, `not_a_member`,
`already_in_group`, `admission_closed`, `already_on_waitlist`,
`not_on_waitlist`, `waitlist_entry_closed`, `offer_expired`,
`group_full` (`overCapacity: true` overrides it for owners),
`out_of_order_reason_required`, `queue_bypass_reason_required`,
`priority_reason_required`, `join_request_rejected`, ….

Members (extends P1.10a): `POST /groups/:groupId/members` adding a
`member` to a group with people `waiting` needs `queueBypassReason`
(`queue_bypass_reason_required`, audited like an out-of-order admission).
Ending a `member` function frees a place for auto-offers.

Waiting lists, the applicant's side (login only; the service checks that
`personId` is the caller or a ward with `act_on_behalf`, else 404):

| Method | Path | Permission | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| GET | `/clubs/:clubId/open-groups` | authenticated | Through `app.club_open_groups`; insiders of the club also see groups with `admission_mode = waitlist` that are not publicly visible. Rate limit `joining` |
| POST | `/groups/:groupId/waitlist` | authenticated | `{ personId, note? }` (note max 500, "for the group's owners"). Allowed for `admission_mode = waitlist`, or `direct` when full; `closed` → `admission_closed`. For a non-member of the club also creates (or reuses) a pending join request with this group as preference, in the same transaction; `invite_only` clubs answer 404. Already in the group → `already_in_group`. Rate limit `joining` |
| GET | `/me/waitlists` (changed) | own data | Exists (P1.6b); adds closed entries of the last 12 months, the offer expiry and the closed reason (optional fields, ADR 0029) |
| POST | `/me/waitlists/:entryId/withdraw` | own data | Open entries; withdrawing the last open entry also withdraws a join request created with it |
| POST | `/me/waitlists/:entryId/accept` | own data | Before `offer_expires_at` (server clock, `offer_expired`). Admits (see "Admission") or moves to `accepted` |
| POST | `/me/waitlists/:entryId/decline` | own data | Closes the entry (`declined`); triggers the next auto-offer |

| Method | Path | Permission | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|

| GET | `/clubs/:clubId/waitlists` | `club.waitlists.read` | Per group: waiting, offers, free places; and out-of-order decisions and queue bypasses of a period with reason, decider (presenter) and group. Keyset by decision time |
| GET | `/groups/:groupId` (changed) | `group.read` | Adds `waitlistVisiblePublicly`, `offerReplyDays`, `waitlistAutoOffer` for those with `group.update`, and `waitingCount` for everyone who may read the group (new optional fields, ADR 0029) |

**Admission** (one transaction, the entry locked `FOR UPDATE`, the group
row locked for the capacity check): if the person is an active or paused
member of the club, or `group_owners_admit_to_club` is on (then the
pending join request is approved through the joining service, or a
membership created with `createMembershipInTx` when there is none), the
group membership `member` is added and the entry becomes `admitted`.
Otherwise the entry becomes `accepted` and stays an occupied place; when a
club manager or registrar approves the join request (existing endpoint),
the joining service admits the person's `accepted` entries in that club in
the same transaction; rejecting it closes the person's open entries in the
club (`join_request_rejected`). Leaving the club closes the person's open
entries there (`left_club`). Joining service and groups service call each
other only through exported `…InTx` methods.

**Worker:** `waitlists:offers` every five minutes (`?max=1`), as a system
job, club by club, set-based: expire offers past `offer_expires_at`
(`expired`), then for groups with `waitlist_auto_offer` and free places
offer the next `waiting` entries in order, one per free place (never out of
order, `offered_by_person_id` null). The job catches up after downtime and
is idempotent. Auto-offers on a freed place (a membership ended, an offer
declined) wait for the next run (at most five minutes).

**Notifications** in the recipient's language, as catalogue types of the
`waitlist` category (ADR 0039; in-app and email, the offer's email
mandatory), with recipients tested per type as in P1.7. Events with IDs only
`waitlist.entry.created` (confirmation to the requester),
`waitlist.offer.sent` (to the applicant if they have a login and to the
requester and the applicant's full guardians with a login: the group, the
expiry in the club's time zone, a link to `/me/waitlists`),
`waitlist.offer.expired`, `waitlist.admitted`, `waitlist.entry.closed`
(exists since P1.10a; now also removed by the owner and join request
rejected; with the code's text, never the owner's note).

**Request budgets** (ADR 0040): every endpoint the web calls gets a case in
`request-budgets.integration.test.ts`; one transaction per request
(admission calls the joining service's `…InTx` methods inside it).

## Permissions
New permissions, one policy each in `authorization.policy.ts`, allow and
deny tests, entries in the permission matrix (tenant isolation: 404 for
club B):
- `group.waitlist.manage` (group): club managers, owners of the group or a
  parent group. Deny: leaders who are not owners, registrars, members,
  guardians, support access. Archived groups: club managers only.
- `club.waitlists.read` (club): club managers; registrars (they approve the
  club memberships that follow). Deny: owners (they see their own lists),
  board roles, members.
- Applicant endpoints need only a login; the controller limits `personId`
  to the caller and wards with `act_on_behalf` (404 otherwise), as joining
  does; `X-On-Behalf-Of` is honoured (only that ward). Offer acceptance by
  a relative without `act_on_behalf` → 404.
- Update the permissions table in `domain-model.md`.

## Privacy and security
- Personal data: who applies for which group (often children), birth year,
  notes. Legal basis: the application (steps before a membership at the
  data subject's request); retention 12 months after the entry closed.
  Record in `domain-model.md` and `docs/gdpr.md`.
- `applicant_note` is for the owners: the form says not to write health
  information; `owner_note` is never shown to the applicant (but is part of
  "download my data", P1.8). Neither is logged, sent in email or put in
  events.
- Owners see of applicants only: name, birth year, the fit with the group's
  birth years and gender category (computed, not the gender itself unless
  the person shared it with the club), the requester's name, the notes and
  waiting time. No phone numbers or addresses of non-members.
- Audit events in the same transaction: `group.created`, `.updated`,
  `.archived`, `.restored`, `group.owner.added`/`.removed` (exist),
  `group.membership.created`/`.ended` (exist; with `queueBypassReason` when
  given), `club.settings.updated`, `waitlist.entry.created`,
  `.priority_changed`, `.offered` (with `outOfOrder: true` and the reason),
  `.offer_withdrawn`, `.accepted`, `.declined`, `.expired` (system),
  `.admitted` (with the reason when out of order), `.withdrawn`,
  `.removed`. On-behalf-of is recorded for guardians.
- Fairness: out-of-order offers and admissions and queue bypasses always
  carry a reason and are listed for club managers.
- Rate limits: applying and the open-groups lookup use the `joining`
  account budget; offers and admissions the normal account budget.

## Protected identity
- Every list (waiting list, candidates, owners, members, the managers'
  decisions) goes through `PeopleService.summaries` and `PersonPresenter`.
- Extend `PersonVisibility` (ADR 0022 amendment, recorded in the new
  waiting-list ADR): **owners of the group or a parent group, club
  managers and registrars may see an applicant on that group's waiting
  list** (with the crossed-camera icon),
  as managers see join-request applicants. Nobody else sees applicants;
  the waiting count includes them.
- Applicants never see other applicants, only their position and the count.
- The candidates search leaves protected members out for owners who may
  not see them; managers and registrars see them with the icon.
- Emails name a protected applicant only to recipients who may see them
  (the applicant's own family).

## Performance
- Waiting list page of a group with 500 entries < 100 ms (order index,
  keyset); the summary in one grouped statement; `/me/waitlists` with
  positions < 50 ms (one call to `app.waitlist_positions` for the page).
- Offer, admit, accept < 200 ms (the capacity count uses
  `group_memberships_group_idx` and the order index).
- `waitlists:offers` per run set-based (one statement to expire, one to
  pick and offer per club with work), under 1 s on the load scenario.
- `/candidates` < 100 ms on a 10,000-member club (the register's name
  index; no `OFFSET`).
- Load scenario: add waiting lists (e.g. 20 entries for every second team,
  some offers past expiry); query-plan tests for the owner's list, the
  positions, the summary, the expiry job and the managers' view (no
  sequential scans on `group_waitlist_entries`).

## Decisions (2026-11-06, as recommended)
1. **Declining an offer** closes the entry; the applicant may apply again at
   the back of the queue; owners can restore fairness with priority.
2. **Non-members** applying get a pending join request in every club,
   settled on admission; nobody becomes a member without a group.
3. **Archiving** (P1.10a) ends the group's memberships and closes its
   waiting list.
4. **Owners** are adult members with a login (P1.10a).
5. **Candidate search** for owners (P1.10a).
6. **Club settings**: the whole schema (P1.10a).
7. **Auto-offers** by the five-minute job `waitlists:offers`.
