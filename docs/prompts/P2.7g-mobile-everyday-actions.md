# P2.7g — Mobile: leaders' and members' everyday actions

To do before P2.8. Wish from the maintainer (2026-11-26): leaders take
attendance, create activities and send announcements from the phone;
everyone who may offers and books car pools; the chats are easy to find.

## Context
Read first: `CLAUDE.md`, ADR 0028 (the mobile app), ADR 0055 (updates in
place: the same rules apply to the app's screens), ADR 0040 and
`docs/performance.md` (cache first, budgets), ADR 0035/0036 (roster,

(chat), ADR 0053 (announcements), ADR 0048 (car pools, amended in P2.7f),
`docs/development.md` "Mobile app" and `docs/android-emulator.md`.

What exists already in `apps/mobile` (checked 2026-11-26):
- **Attendance** (`app/(app)/attendance/[activityId].tsx`): marks through the
  offline outbox, corrections after the lock with a reason; reached only from
  an activity's page. No guests from outside the roster, no list of the
  sessions waiting for marks.
- **Chats** (`app/(app)/chats/…`): list, chat with send, edit, delete,
  reactions, reports, a ward's chats, a direct chat from a person's page.
  Reached only through a link on Home; no new channel in a group; no unread
  count in the navigation.
- **Car pools** (`components/carpool.tsx`, `ride-choices.tsx`): offer (seats,
  place, note, there and home with the return's place), book (also for a
  ward), cancel, confirm, "Åk med" from the schedule. Missing compared with
  the web: the departure and return times, editing and removing one's own
  car, "Skapa samåkning" with one's defaults, the defaults themselves, the
  default return time (`defaultReturnAt`), the "too young to book" message
  (`selfBookingAge`).
- **Announcements**: read only (board, group board, mark read).
- **Activities**: read and answer only; no creating, changing, cancelling.
- **Navigation**: one stack, Home with links; no tab bar.
- No date or time picker, no file picker among the dependencies.

The API already has everything below (the web uses it): `POST
/groups/:groupId/activities` (with `notCalledUp`, `carpoolReturnMinutes`),
`PATCH /activities/:id`, cancel/restore, `PUT /activities/:id/invitees`,
`GET /groups/:groupId/members`, the attendance endpoints and guests,
`POST /clubs/:clubId/announcements` and `…/groups/:groupId/announcements`
with `…/preview`, edit and withdraw, `POST /clubs/:clubId/groups/:groupId/chats`,
the car-pool endpoints, `GET/PUT /me/carpool-settings`. No new endpoints
are expected; add one only if a screen needs data the API does not give in
a budgeted request, and say so in the PR.

## Goal
A coach does the week's work from the phone: sees which sessions wait for
attendance and marks it, also offline; creates or changes a match or
training with who is called up; sends an announcement to the team. Every
adult who takes part (or whose child does) offers a car with one tap or in
full, and books, changes and cancels seats. The chats are one tap away from
everywhere, with the number of unread messages.

## Users and roles involved
Group leaders and owners (`group.activities.manage`, `activity.manage`,
`activity.attendance.manage`, `group.channels.manage`), club roles that
send announcements (`club.announcements.send`), members and guardians
(answers, car pools, chats).

## User stories
- As a coach, I open "Närvaro" and see my groups' sessions from the last
  days that have no marks yet, and mark one in a few taps, also without a
  connection (the outbox sends it later).
- As a coach, I add a player from another group who came as a guest.
- As a coach, I create a match for Saturday: the title is "Match" until I
  write one, the time with the phone's pickers, the place from the club's
  places, own transport and the way home 20 minutes after the end; I untick
  two players who are not called up.
- As a coach, I move a training, cancel one with a reason, and restore it.
- As a team manager, I write an announcement to my group, see how many it
  reaches before sending, and send it; I withdraw it if it was wrong.
- As a parent, I tap "Skapa samåkning" under Saturday's match and my car
  (from my defaults) is offered there and home; later I change the seats
  and the departure time, or remove the car.
- As a parent, I set my car-pool defaults in the app's settings.
- As a 12-year-old, I see why I cannot book a seat myself and from what age
  I can.
- As anyone, I reach the chats from the tab bar and see how many messages
  are unread; as a leader, I start a new channel in my group.

## UI (mobile)
All screens: cache first (show what is cached at once, refresh behind),
the same permissions the web shows (from `useMyPermissions`/the resource's
`canManage` flags; the API stays the authority), empty, loading, offline
and error states, field errors from the API's problem details next to
their fields (as the web, P2.7c/P2.7d), and `testID`s for Maestro.

1. **Navigation.** A tab bar: Hem (the schedule), Chatt (unread badge),
   Anslagstavla, Mer (the rest of today's Home links and settings). Deep
   links and notifications keep working; the stack stays inside each tab.
2. **Attendance.**
   - "Närvaro" (from Mer, and from a group's page for its leaders): the
     sessions of my groups from the last `attendanceLockDays` that have
     started and have no marks (or not all), newest first; a tap opens the
     existing attendance screen.
   - On the attendance screen: "Lägg till gäst" (search the club's members
     as you type, as the web), shown as guests.
   - Everything else stays as it is (outbox, corrections, locked).
3. **Activities.**
   - "Ny aktivitet" on a group's page for those who manage its activities:
     kind, title (the kind's name until written, P2.7e), date, start and
     end with native pickers, all day, place (club places, or free text),
     meeting time and place, opponent/home-away/competition for matches,
     answers (mode, deadline), transport mode and "Hemfärd, minuter efter
     slut", notes for participants and for leaders, and the call-up list
     (the group's players, all ticked; `notCalledUp` for those unticked).
   - Editing the same fields on the activity's page; cancel with a reason;
     restore; a series' session changes only itself (series stay on the
     web).
   - Creating and changing need a connection: offline, the button says so
     (no queued creates).
4. **Announcements.**
   - "Nytt anslag" on the board and on a group's page for those who may
     send: to the group or (with the club permission) the club; title,
     text (the web's Markdown subset, plain text editor with a preview),
     "important"; the recipients' count from the preview endpoint before
     sending; scheduled sending if the web offers it.
   - Withdraw and edit one's own as on the web.
5. **Car pools.**
   - The offer form gains the departure time and the way home's time
     (prefilled from `defaultReturnAt`), and starts from one's defaults.
   - One's own car: edit seats, places, times and note; remove it (the
     passengers' bookers are told, as on the web).
   - "Skapa samåkning" under an activity on a group's page and in the
     schedule for those with defaults (`canOffer` in the summaries), one
     tap, as the web (P2.7f).
   - Settings → "Samåkning": the defaults (adults; `canSet`).
   - The activity's car pool shows "Du bokar plats själv från {age} år …"
     when `selfBookingAge` is set, instead of "not taking part".
6. **Chats.**
   - The tab with the unread count (from the chat list; realtime updates as
     today).
   - "Ny kanal" in a group's chats for those with `group.channels.manage`
     (name, who: the group or its leaders, as the web). Members, archiving
     and safeguarding stay as they are (web).

Dependencies: a date and time picker (and nothing more). Only licences on
the mobile allow list (`docs/licensing.md`, `pnpm licenses:check
--mobile`); run `pnpm --filter @clubadmin/mobile licenses:generate`; keep
the bundle within its budget (`bundle:check`, ADR 0028).

## Permissions
No new permissions. The app hides what the person may not do; every action
is checked by the API's existing policies (already in the matrix).

## Privacy and security
No new personal data. People appear through the API's presenter as today
(protected identity: names hidden, screenshots blocked where protected
people are shown, as the attendance screen does). Announcement texts and
chat messages are never logged; nothing personal goes into the outbox
beyond what the attendance marks already hold.

## Locale
All new strings in sv, en, nb (the web's keys reused where they fit).

## Performance
Each new screen's requests are ones the web already makes and are in
`request-budgets.integration.test.ts`; add any request the app makes that
is not (e.g. the sessions waiting for attendance, if it needs its own) with
its budget. The tab bar adds no request (the unread count comes with the
chat list the app already loads). Cold start budget unchanged
(`docs/performance.md`).

## Out of scope

  archiving), safeguarding, roles and club settings: they stay on the web.
