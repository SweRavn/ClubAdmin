## Goal
On the hosted service a new club tries ClubAdmin for a month, its club
manager chooses a plan with a second factor, enters the association's
invoice details and pays by invoice (bankgiro with an OCR reference) or by
card. Every period the club gets a correct Swedish invoice with VAT, as a
PDF by email and in the app, and the treasurer finds all invoices on one
page. When an invoice is not paid the club is reminded, then sees a
warning, and after the grace period becomes read-only: nothing is deleted,
exports keep working, and paying restores it at once. The operator sees
every club's subscription and invoices (never member data), records bank
payments, issues credit notes and exports invoices for bookkeeping. A
self-hosted installation with `BILLING_ENABLED=false` sees no difference at
all.

This package is large; deliver it in two PRs (see Open decisions): **A**
billing details, plan selection, invoices with VAT and PDF, manual
(bankgiro) payments, trial end, dunning, the club page and the operator
console; **B** the card provider behind `PaymentProvider`.

## Users and roles involved
Club managers (choose and cancel the plan, with MFA; billing details;
read invoices), treasurers (billing details, payment method, invoices; not
the plan, see Open decisions), other club members (only the read-only
notice), platform operators acting as operators with MFA (plans, any
club's subscription, payments, credit notes, exports; never member data).
No member's personal data is processed by billing.

## User stories
- As a club manager in a trial, I want to see when it ends and what each
  plan costs (with and without VAT), so that I can choose in time.
- As a club manager, I want to choose a plan and add-ons, monthly or
  yearly, with a second factor and the terms accepted, so that the club
  keeps its features after the trial.
- As a treasurer, I want to enter the association's name, organisation
  number, invoice address, invoice email and our reference, so that
  invoices reach the right place and can be booked.
- As a treasurer, I want to choose invoice (bankgiro) or card, and to see
  and download every invoice and credit note as a PDF, so that our books
  are complete.
- As a club manager, I want to upgrade at once and downgrade or cancel at
  the end of the period, so that we never pay twice.
- As a club manager or treasurer, I want reminders before anything happens,
  and a clear notice of what read-only means and how to restore it.
- As a member of a read-only club, I want to see why I cannot change
  things, and still export and read everything.
- As an operator, I want to list clubs by subscription status, record a
  bank payment against an invoice, issue a credit note, extend a trial and
  export invoices and payments for bookkeeping, so that I can run the
  service without database access.
- As a self-hoster, I want nothing of this to appear or run when billing is
  off.

## Data model
All new club tables: `club_id`, tenant RLS through
`app.enable_tenant_rls`, the operator and restrictive reader/writer
policies of migration 0006, `app.enable_updated_at`,
`history.enable_history`, no `DELETE` grant. Global tables: RLS with the
`app.*` helpers (ADR 0023). **No person references anywhere in `billing`**
(the existing test stays green); who did what is in audit and history.
Amounts are integer minor units with an ISO 4217 currency; VAT rates in
basis points (2500 = 25 %). Expand only (rule 13).

- **`billing.accounts`** (club-owned, one per club): `legal_name`,
  `organization_number` (validated by the club's country pack where it has
  a format), `vat_number` (optional), `address` (jsonb, the
  `PostalAddressSchema` shape), `invoice_email` (an organisation address;
  the form says so), `reference` (free text ≤ 60, "Er referens"),
  `invoice_locale` (`sv`, `en`, `nb`), `payment_method` (`invoice`,
  `card`), `provider`, `provider_customer_id`, card summary (`card_brand`,
  `card_last4`, `card_exp_month`, `card_exp_year`; never card numbers),
  `terms_version`, `terms_accepted_at`. Club managers and treasurers may
  insert and update it (a new permissive policy with
  `app.has_club_role(ARRAY['club_manager','treasurer'])`); operators read
  and change it.
- **`billing.plan_prices`** (global catalogue, operators write, everyone
  reads): `plan_id`, `currency`, `period` (`month`, `year`), `price_minor`
  (excluding VAT), `active`. Unique `(plan_id, currency, period)`.
  `billing.plans.price_minor`/`currency`/`period` stay (contract later) and
  are backfilled into `plan_prices`.
- **`billing.subscriptions`** (new columns): `billing_period` (`month`,
  `year`), `currency`, `cancel_at_period_end` (bool), `scheduled_plan_id`,
  `scheduled_add_on_plan_ids` (applied at the period end), `past_due_since`,
  `trial_reminded_at`. Index `(status, current_period_end)` for the daily
  job.
- **`clubs.access_state_source`** (`operator`, `billing`; null when
  active): payment restores `active` only when billing set the restriction;
  an operator's manual `read_only` or `suspended` is never lifted by a
  payment.
- **`billing.invoices`** (new columns; `number` becomes nullable until
  issued, the unique index stays): `kind` (`invoice`, `credit_note`),
  `credited_invoice_id`, `subtotal_minor`, `vat_minor` (`total_minor` =
  both), `seller` and `buyer` (jsonb snapshots at issue: the legal content
  of the invoice never changes when details change later), `payment_method`,
  `ocr_reference` (bankgiro OCR with check digit), `pdf_object_key`,
  `pdf_sha256`, `reminder_level`, `last_reminded_at`. Unique
  `(subscription_id, period_start) WHERE kind = 'invoice'` (the job is
  idempotent). Partial index `(due_at) WHERE status = 'open'`. A trigger
  refuses changes to an issued invoice except `status`, `paid_at`,
  `reminder_level`, `last_reminded_at` and the PDF columns; corrections are
  credit notes. The enum `status` may grow (ADR 0029).
- **`billing.invoice_lines`** (new columns): `plan_id`, `period_start`,
  `period_end`, `proration` (bool), `vat_rate_bp`, `vat_minor`.
- **`billing.invoice_number_series`** (global, jobs and operators only):
  `key`, `prefix`, `next_value`. Numbers are assigned in the issuing
  transaction under a row lock: one gapless series per seller.
- **`billing.payments`** (club-owned): `invoice_id`, `provider`
  (`manual`, `card`, …), `provider_reference` (unique per provider),
  `amount_minor`, `currency`, `status` (`pending`, `succeeded`, `failed`;
  may grow), `failure_code`, `received_on` (date, for bank payments),
  `recorded_at`. Several payments may settle one invoice; the invoice is
  `paid` when succeeded payments reach the total.
- **`billing.provider_events`** (global): `provider`, `event_id` (unique per
  provider), `type`, `received_at`, `processed_at`, `error_code`. No payload
  is stored (it may contain contact data); the handler fetches what it needs
  from the provider by ID. Deleted after 90 days.
- **Retention:** invoices, credit notes, lines, payments and accounts are
  accounting records: kept 7 years after the end of the calendar year of
  the invoice (Bokföringslagen; `docs/gdpr.md`), also after the club is
  archived or deleted, then removed by `billing:retention` (yearly) through
  a `SECURITY DEFINER` purge function (the app roles cannot delete), with
  the PDFs. `billing.accounts` contact fields of a club without invoices in
  the retention window are cleared by the same job.

A new ADR (the next free number) decides: invoices generated by
ClubAdmin (not by the payment provider), periods, proration, VAT
handling, the payment-provider interface, the dunning schedule and the
access-state source; ADR 0031 and `hosting-and-billing.md` are updated.

## Payment providers
`PaymentProvider` (rewrite of the placeholder in `billing.interfaces.ts`,
one adapter per provider, chosen by configuration):
`name`; `methods` (`invoice`, `card`); `setup(club, returnUrl)` → redirect
URL or nothing; `collect(invoice)` → `{ reference, status, actionUrl? }`;
`parseWebhook(rawBody, headers)` → verified events or a signature error;
`fetchPayment(reference)`. The `InvoiceGenerator` interface is replaced by
an internal `InvoiceIssuer` service (the plan says what to invoice; no
pluggability needed).
- **`manual` (PR A, always available):** invoice with bankgiro and an OCR
  reference (Luhn check digit). Nothing is collected automatically; an
  operator records payments from the bank statement. Works with no external
  service, so self-hosters who turn billing on (a federation charging its
  clubs) can use it.
- **`card` (PR B):** the provider's hosted pages for saving a card and for
  paying a single invoice (no card data on our pages or in our logs:
  PCI DSS SAQ A; no provider script on our pages, so the CSP is
  unchanged); invoices are charged off-session when issued; failed charges
  are retried on days 1, 3 and 7 and the club gets a pay link. The provider
  and its SDK must be on the licence allow list (the Stripe Node SDK is
  MIT) and on the published sub-processor list. Configuration:
  `BILLING_CARD_PROVIDER`, `BILLING_CARD_SECRET_KEY`,
  `BILLING_CARD_WEBHOOK_SECRET` (dummy values in `.env.example`).

## Lifecycle, invoices and dunning
- **Trial:** a new hosted club starts on `BILLING_TRIAL_PLAN` (new; default
  `BILLING_DEFAULT_PLAN`, so existing installations behave as today) for
  `BILLING_TRIAL_DAYS`. Emails 7 days and 1 day before the end to club
  managers. At the end: with a selected paid plan and complete details →
  `active` and the first invoice; otherwise → `active` on the default plan
  (never read-only for not choosing).
- **Periods:** aligned to calendar months (or calendar years for yearly
  billing) in the club's time zone; the first period is prorated by day.
  Invoices are issued in advance on the first day of the period.
- **Changes:** upgrades and added add-ons at once with a prorated line on
  the next invoice; downgrades, removed add-ons, period changes and
  cancellations at the period end. No refunds for unused time (credit notes
  only by operators).
- **Invoice content** (mervärdesskattelagen 2023:200 and Bokföringslagen):
  date of issue, a unique number from one series, seller's name, address,
  organisation and VAT numbers (from `BILLING_SELLER_*` configuration),
  buyer's name and address (and VAT number where reverse charge applies),
  what is sold with period and quantity, price excluding VAT, VAT rate and
  amount per rate, total, due date, payment instructions (bankgiro, OCR),
  and the reverse-charge or "outside the scope of Swedish VAT" note where it
  applies. VAT is decided by a pure `vatFor(seller, buyer)` function in the
  billing module with tests per case (see Open decisions); rates come from
  configuration, not code.
- **Rendering:** the worker renders the PDF (`billing:render_invoice`, per
  invoice, after `billing.invoice.issued`) with a library on the allow list,
  in the buyer's `invoice_locale`, stores it in the files bucket under
  `billing/<club_id>/<invoice_id>.pdf` with its SHA-256, and emails it to
  the invoice email (attached) and a notice to club managers and
  treasurers.
- **Jobs:** `billing:daily` (replaces the monthly `billing:generate_invoices`
  crontab; daily 04:41 UTC): trial reminders and endings, scheduled changes,
  issuing invoices for periods that have started, dunning steps, card
  retries. Club by club, one transaction per club, idempotent (the unique
  index on invoices, the reminder levels). `billing:provider_event` per
  stored webhook event; `billing:retention` yearly.
- **Dunning (defaults, see Open decisions):** invoice due 30 days after
  issue (card: charged at issue); reminder email on day 1 overdue → status
  `past_due` (`past_due_since`, a banner for managers and treasurers, full
  access); second reminder on day 14 naming the read-only date; on day 30
  → `read_only` (`access_state_source = billing`; every member sees the
  existing read-only notice, exports work, nothing is deleted); a payment
  covering all overdue invoices returns the club to `active` at once.
  Long-term non-payment never deletes anything.
- **Domain events (IDs only):** `billing.invoice.issued`,
  `billing.invoice.paid`, `billing.invoice.overdue`,
  `billing.subscription.changed`; `club.entitlements.changed` as today on
  every plan, status or access-state change.

- RLS tests: a treasurer of club A reads A's invoices but not B's; a

## Privacy and security
- Billing data is about the club as a customer. The invoice email and
  "reference" may identify a person: the form asks for an organisation
  address and a role, they are never logged, and they are kept as
  accounting records (legal obligation, 7 years). Record it in `gdpr.md`.
- No person IDs in `billing`; recipients of billing emails (club managers,
  treasurers) are looked up when sending; job payloads and domain events
  carry IDs only.
- Audit events in the same transaction: `billing.account.updated` (changed
  fields only), `billing.subscription.selected`, `.cancelled`, `.resumed`,
  `.trial.changed`, `billing.invoice.issued`, `billing.credit_note.issued`,
  `billing.payment.recorded`, `billing.payment.failed`,
  `billing.plan.created`/`.updated`, `club.access_state.changed` (existing,
  now with the source). Record history on every table.
- Webhooks: signature verification with a timestamp tolerance, raw body,
  idempotency by event ID, no payload stored, no secrets or contact data in
  logs; provider secrets only from the environment.
- Issued invoices are immutable (trigger); numbers are gapless (row lock);
  money is never a float; every total is checked against its lines in tests.
- Operators act as operators (ADR 0031): MFA, audited, no member data.
- With `BILLING_ENABLED=false`: the module, routes, worker handlers and
  jobs are not loaded; the seller and provider configuration is not
  required; the web and app hide everything (`billingEnabled` false);
  the access state is only changed by operators as today. Both CI legs
  prove it.

## Protected identity
- Billing shows no members. The only people shown are in history panels
  ("changed by"), through `PersonPresenter` (a protected actor appears as
  "someone with protected identity"), and the greeting of billing emails to
  club managers and treasurers through the worker's mail-name presenter.
- Invoices and PDFs never contain a member's name; the free-text
  reference is the club's own input (the form advises a role, not a name).

## Locale and country packs
- Strings in sv, en and nb: plans UI, statuses, banners, emails (invoice,
  receipt, reminders, trial ending, read-only, restored), PDF labels, problem
  codes. The PDF uses the account's `invoice_locale`; Swedish legal labels
  ("Moms", "Omvänd skattskyldighet", "Godkänd för F-skatt" if configured).
- Organisation number formats come from the country packs (`se`
  organisationsnummer with check digit, `no` organisasjonsnummer); VAT
  rules live in the billing module (they depend on the seller), not in the
  country packs.
- Currency: the club's `currency` selects the price; a plan without a price
  in that currency cannot be selected (`price_not_available`).

## Performance
- Club billing page: subscription, plans and the first invoice page each
  < 100 ms p95; ≤ 5 queries.
- Entitlement checks are unchanged (in-memory; the existing timing test).
- `billing:daily` for 10,000 subscriptions in < 5 minutes (batches of 100
  clubs, the `(status, current_period_end)` and open-invoice indexes; no
  per-club query to find work).
- Webhook endpoint < 100 ms (insert and enqueue only). PDF rendering off the
  request path, < 1 s per invoice.
- Operator lists keyset-paged < 100 ms with 10,000 clubs and 120,000
  invoices; add billing rows to the load scenario (one year of monthly
  invoices for every club) and query-plan tests for the operator lists and
  the daily job's selections.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] With billing off, nothing changes: no routes (404), no jobs, no
      handlers, no UI, no required configuration; the billing-off CI leg
      passes unchanged.
- [ ] A club manager chooses a plan with MFA and accepted terms; upgrades
      apply at once with a prorated line, downgrades and cancellations at
      the period end; treasurers cannot choose plans but manage details.
- [ ] Trials end: reminders at 7 and 1 days; a selected plan becomes active
      with an invoice, otherwise the club falls back to the default plan.
- [ ] Invoices are issued once per period (idempotent), numbered without
      gaps, immutable after issue, with correct VAT per the tested cases,
      rendered as PDF in the account's language and emailed.
- [ ] Operators record bank payments (partial and full), issue credit notes,
      extend trials and export invoices and payments as CSV.
- [ ] Dunning moves an unpaid club to `past_due` and then `read_only`
      (exports still work, nothing deleted); a payment restores `active`
      at once; an operator's manual restriction is never lifted by a
      payment.
- [ ] Card payments (PR B): hosted setup, off-session charge on issue,
      retries, pay link; webhooks verified and idempotent; no card data
      stored or logged.
- [ ] Billing tables still hold no person references (test); operators read
      no member data (RLS test).
- [ ] Performance budgets met (timing and query-plan tests on the load
      scenario).
- [ ] Tenant-isolation test for every new endpoint
- [ ] Allow/deny tests for every new policy
- [ ] Audit events for every change to money, plans and billing details
- [ ] Translations complete for sv and en (and nb)
- [ ] Docs updated: `hosting-and-billing.md`, ADR 0031, the new billing ADR,
      `architecture.md` (jobs, `PaymentProvider`), `gdpr.md` (billing
      contact data, retention), `security.md` (webhooks, PCI scope),
      `licensing.md` (new dependencies), `.env.example`, `seed-data.md`,
      the permission table in `domain-model.md`

## Out of scope
- Member fees, club invoicing of members, Swish and the team cash desk
  (Phase 3, `finance` module, independent of `BILLING_ENABLED`).
- Automatic bank reconciliation (Bankgirot BgMax files, open banking);
  operators record payments by hand.
- E-invoicing (Peppol, Svefaktura) and bookkeeping integrations (SIE,
  Fortnox) for the operator's books; the CSV export only.
- Usage-based billing (metered streaming hours), coupons and discounts,
  refunds through the provider (credit notes only; refunds by hand in the
  provider's dashboard).
- Enforcing limits after a downgrade (e.g. `members_max` with more members
  than allowed): file an issue; nothing is ever deleted.
- Debt collection, reminder fees and late-payment interest.
- Billing inside the mobile app.

## Open decisions
1. **Two PRs.** Default: PR A (details, plans, invoices with VAT and PDF,
   manual bankgiro payments, trial end, dunning, club page, operator
   console) and PR B (card provider). Alternative: one PR, or card first.
2. **Invoicing entity.** Which legal entity issues the invoices (name,
   organisation number, VAT number, F-skatt, address, bankgiro, email,
   invoice number prefix)? Default: configured by `BILLING_SELLER_*`
   environment variables, required at startup when billing is on; the
   maintainer supplies the values for the hosted service.
3. **Plan catalogue and prices.** Which plans, prices, features and limits
   at launch? Default: none in code; operators create them in the console;
   the demo catalogue (Free, Standard 499 SEK/month, Streaming 199 SEK/month)
   stays seed-only. Also: offer yearly billing (default yes, priced as
   10 × monthly).
4. **Prices and VAT display.** Default: prices are stored excluding VAT and
   shown both excluding and including VAT (most associations cannot deduct
   VAT).
5. **VAT treatment.** Default for a Swedish seller: Swedish clubs 25 %;
   clubs outside Sweden no Swedish VAT with the matching note. Norwegian
   clubs that are not VAT-registered may make the seller liable to register
   in Norway's VOEC scheme: confirm with an accountant. Default until then:
   paid plans only for Swedish clubs; Norwegian clubs stay on the default
   plan or an operator-assigned plan without invoices.
6. **Currencies.** Default: SEK only at launch; NOK prices (and KID
   references for Norwegian bank payments) when decision 5 allows.
7. **Payment methods and provider.** Default: invoice with bankgiro and OCR
   (manual reconciliation) first, as Swedish associations expect; card
   second through Stripe (Stripe Payments Europe, Ireland; US transfers
   under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, listed as sub-processor).
   Alternatives: an EU provider (Mollie, Adyen) for cards, or an invoicing
   service (e.g. Billogram) for sending and reconciling invoices. Swish is
   not used for subscriptions.
8. **Payment terms and dunning schedule.** Default: due 30 days after issue;
   reminders on days 1 and 14 overdue; `past_due` from day 1; `read_only`
   from day 30; no reminder fee or interest.
9. **Trial.** Default: 30 days on `standard` (new `BILLING_TRIAL_PLAN`); at
   the end without a chosen plan the club moves to the default (free) plan,
   never to read-only.
10. **Who chooses the plan.** Default: club managers with MFA choose,
    change and cancel; treasurers manage billing details and payment method
    and read invoices. Alternative: treasurers may also choose plans.
11. **Proration and periods.** Default: calendar-aligned periods with a
    prorated first period; upgrades immediate and prorated; downgrades and
    cancellations at the period end; no refunds.
12. **Price changes for existing subscribers.** Default: new prices apply
    from the first period starting at least 30 days after the club is
    notified by email.
13. **Long-term non-payment.** Default: the club stays read-only until it
    pays or an operator decides; an operator may cancel (default plan,
    write access back) only after the open invoices are paid or credited.
14. **Terms acceptance.** Default: the club manager accepts the terms of
    service and DPA version (`BILLING_TERMS_VERSION`, URL) when choosing a
    paid plan, recorded on the account and in audit; no check that the
    manager is an authorised signatory (*firmatecknare*).
15. **Invoice archive.** Default: the database rows are the accounting
    record, the PDF is stored with its hash in the files bucket for 8 years
    (lifecycle rule); PDF library `pdfkit` (MIT). Alternative: an
    immutable (object-locked) archive bucket.
16. **Invoice numbering.** Default: one continuous gapless series per
    seller with a configurable prefix (e.g. `CA-000123`), credit notes in
    the same series.
17. **Demo organisation numbers.** Default: none seeded (the field is
    optional); alternative: a documented fictional range that is verified
    not to exist.
