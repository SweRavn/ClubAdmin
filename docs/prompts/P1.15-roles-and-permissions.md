  declares one; each has exactly one policy in
  `apps/api/src/modules/authorization/authorization.policy.ts` (deny by

- **Roles are hard-coded and named in about 37 places of the API**, not only
  in the policies:

    `ownsGroup(...)`, reaches child groups through `ancestorIds`).

    `person-visibility.service.ts` (who sees protected people),
    `profileAccess` (the profile's field tiers admin / leader / member),
    the events, activities, attendance, groups, group-members and consents
    controllers (e.g. `events.controller.ts`: board drafts, "all events"),
    `memberships.service.ts` and `waitlists.service.ts`.
  - SQL: `app.has_club_role(ARRAY['club_manager', …])` in RLS policies of
    migrations 0006 (entitlements), 0010 (imports) and 0027 (billing); the

    (0001, 0007).
  - Worker: `notifications/recipients.ts` (`gm.function IN ('head_coach',
    …)`), `notifications/handler.ts` (managers and registrars, billing
    recipients: managers and treasurers), `mail.ts` (managers),
    `tasks/members.ts`.
- **Group owners** (`group_owners`, ADR 0019) are an administrative
  relationship separate from functions, inherited by child groups.
- **The relationship set** is cached **per person** in Valkey under the
  person's version counter (one MGET of version and value,
  `authorization-cache.ts`); the worker bumps it on domain events. It holds
  the person's roles and functions; policies run on it per request.
- **No realtime channel** to clients yet (the WebSocket gateway is planned
  for chat, Phase 2). The web app is server-rendered per request; the mobile
  app caches what it was shown (cache first, ADR 0040). Push goes through
  the notification pipeline (ADR 0039).
- **No "view as"** exists; this prompt does not add impersonation.
- Seed data and the load scenario assign roles and functions by name.
- Merged migrations are never edited (rule 13): every SQL change below is a
  new migration (0030 onwards).

**Moved here from P1.14** (finding 6 there): invitations to several groups,
each with a role. They are built on this prompt's roles (part A4).

## Goal
A club decides which roles it has and what each role may do, as in AWS IAM
or Google Workspace. ClubAdmin defines the **permissions** (what can be
done); a club administrator creates **roles**, gives them permissions and
assigns them to people, in the club or in a group. A new club starts with a
ready set of roles from its **club type** (sports club, chess club,
pensioners' association, …), which operators maintain, so creating a club
stays as simple as today. Everything is explained in plain language, so that
an administrator without IT experience understands who may do what. Taking a
permission away takes effect at once.

## Concepts (for the ADR and the help texts)
- **Permission (behörighet):** something one may do, defined by ClubAdmin,
  e.g. "Se medlemsregistret", "Planera gruppens aktiviteter". A club cannot
  invent permissions, only combine them. Each permission has a **scope**:
  - *club*: applies in the whole club (e.g. the member register);
  - *group*: applies in the groups where the role is held. **Not in their
    child groups** (maintainer's decision 2026-11-11, as leader functions
    today); a coach of "P2015" who should also plan "P2015 Blå" is given the
    role there too. Group owners keep their inherited rights as a
    relationship (ADR 0019, decision 4). A group permission may declare
    `inherits: true` in the catalogue only where today's behaviour already
    reaches child groups; in A1 no held role does.
- **Role (roll):** a named set of permissions in one club, e.g. "Kassör" or
  "Huvudtränare". A role is either a **club role** (held in the club) or a
  **group role** (held in a group). Club roles may contain group permissions;
  they then apply in every group of the club.
- **Assignment (tilldelning):** a person holds a role from a date, until a
  date or further notice, in the club or in a group. Today's
  `club_role_assignments` and `group_memberships` become assignments.
- **Administrator (föreningsadministratör):** anyone who holds
  `club.roles.manage` through any role (part A3).
- **Template (mall):** a club type's set of roles and permissions, copied
  into a club when it is created. Later changes to the template never change
  existing clubs (decision 6).

## Part A1 — Permissions instead of role names (no change in behaviour)

### A1.1 The permission catalogue
- Each permission in `PERMISSIONS` is classified in code
  (`packages/shared/src/permission-catalogue.ts`):
  - `assignable`: may be put in a role, with its scope (`club` | `group`),
    `inherits` (group scope; default `false`), its **area** (Medlemmar,
    Grupper, Aktiviteter, Evenemang, Ekonomi, Integritet, Föreningen) and
    whether it is **sensitive** (gives access to many people's personal
    data, protected identity, money, history, or to roles themselves).
  - `built_in`: never in a role, decided by relationships alone (one's own
    profile and consents, a guardian's rights for a ward, a member's own
    answers, public pages, operators, billing operations, club creation,
    confirming a suspension, part A3).
- **Capabilities that are not endpoints today become permissions** too, so
  that nothing outside the policies needs a role name:
  - "Deltar i gruppens aktiviteter" (`group.participate`; today `member`):
    the group's activities in My schedule and answer requests;
  - "Räknas som ledare" (`group.lead`; today `LEADER_FUNCTIONS`): shown as a
    leader in attendance and on activities, leader answers, leader
    recipients;
  - "Se medlemmarnas uppgifter" (`club.people.details`, club scope) and
    "Se gruppmedlemmarnas uppgifter" (`group.people.details`, group scope):
    the profile tiers (A1.3);
  - "Se personer med skyddad identitet" (`club.people.protected`, club
    scope, sensitive): A1.3;
  - "Se föreningens utkast till evenemang" and the other board checks in
    `events.controller.ts`.
- Each assignable permission has catalogue texts (sv, en, nb): a name, one
  sentence in plain language of what it allows, and an example ("Kan till
  exempel lägga till en träning eller ställa in en match"). A test fails for
  an assignable permission without texts.
- MFA follows the permission, not the role: sensitive permissions keep the
  policies' `mfa` requirement as today.

### A1.2 Policies and services ask for permissions
- `Actor` gains `holds(permission, scope)`: club scope (`clubId`) or group
  scope (a `GroupRef`: the group itself, or an ancestor only for a
  permission with `inherits: true`). `hasClubRole(...)`,
  `isClubManager(...)`, `hasGroupFunction(...)` and `leadsGroup(...)` are
  replaced by `holds(...)` everywhere: policies, `profileAccess`,
  `PersonVisibility`, controllers and services. Relational conditions stay
  (archived group, `onBehalfOf`, the resource's club, MFA, protected
  identity). One policy per permission stays (ADR 0006).
- Checks that sat in controllers and services move into policies or into
  `holds(...)` calls on a declared permission (CLAUDE.md: no ad hoc role
  checks).
- **A lint rule** refuses role and function names (`CLUB_ROLES`,
  `GROUP_FUNCTIONS`, `LEADER_FUNCTIONS`, `BOARD_ROLES` values, and the
  methods above) anywhere in `apps/api/src` and `apps/worker/src`, outside
  the roles module itself and the locked roles (A2.1); tests and seed are
  exempt.
- **Group owners** stay a relationship (ADR 0019) and keep their rights
  (decision 4); policies keep `ownsGroup(...)` alongside `holds(...)`.

### A1.3 Profile tiers and protected identity
`profileAccess` and `PersonVisibility` read permissions instead of roles:
- tier **admin**: `club.people.details` in the person's club;
- tier **leader**: `group.people.details` in one of the person's groups, or
  ownership (inherited as today);
- tier **member**: unchanged (sharing a group, guardian, support access,
  contact consents);
- **seeing protected people** (ADR 0022): `club.people.protected`; in A1
  held by club managers and registrars, as today.

The built-in template gives `club.people.details` and `group.people.details`
to exactly the roles that have the tiers today, so A1 changes nothing.

### A1.4 SQL and the worker
- A function `app.holds_permission(p_club uuid, p_permission text,
  p_group uuid DEFAULT NULL)` (STABLE, `SECURITY INVOKER`, the `app.*`
  helper pattern of ADR 0023) answers for the current actor.
- A new migration **recreates** the RLS policies of 0006, 0010 and 0027
  that call `app.has_club_role(...)`, with `app.holds_permission(...)`
  (DROP POLICY / CREATE POLICY in one transaction; the merged migrations
  stay as they are). `app.has_club_role` stays for the locked manager role
  (the guard of 0021, the handover tables).
- The worker's recipients ask "who holds `<permission>` in this club or
  group" through one shared SQL helper (`holders(clubId, permission,
  groupId?)`, indexed), replacing the role and function lists in
  `recipients.ts`, `handler.ts`, `mail.ts` and `tasks/members.ts`.

### A1.5 Data and migration (expand/contract, forward-only)
- New tables, all with `club_id`, RLS (`app.enable_tenant_rls`), history
  (`history.enable_history`), `created_at`/`updated_at`:
  - `roles (id, club_id, scope club|group, template_key NULL, locked,
    name_sv, name_en, name_nb NULL, description per language NULL,
    sort_order, archived_at, version)`; the display name falls back to the
    catalogue text while a template role is not renamed (decision 9);
  - `role_permissions (club_id, role_id, permission)`, unique, index on
    `(club_id, permission)` for "who holds".
- Columns: `club_role_assignments.role_id`, `group_memberships.role_id`
  (nullable first, backfilled, then `NOT NULL` in a later migration, at the
  latest in A2); `role`/`function` stay, filled from the role's
  `template_key` (or `NULL` for custom roles, which exist from A2) by a
  trigger, so old readers keep working.
- The migration creates every existing club's roles from the **built-in
  template** (today's roles with today's effective permissions, from
  `domain-model.md` "Permissions" and the code paths in the context above)
  and backfills the assignments, per club in batches, safe on a live
  database.
- New clubs get the built-in template when created.
- Person references: assignments are already declared in
  `<module>.references.ts`; check the merge of duplicates covers `role_id`.

### A1.6 Caching
- The relationship set holds role IDs; the **club's role map** (role →
  permissions) is cached per club under a **club authorization version**.
  A request reads, in the same MGET as today, the person's version and set
  plus one version and map **per club the person relates to** (a guardian
  with children in two clubs: two more keys). Holders' permissions are
  expanded in memory.
- Request budgets must not grow; add a case for a person in several clubs.

## Part A2 — The club manager's split, the role editor, immediate effect

### A2.1 Locked roles and the club manager's split
- **Club manager (Föreningsansvarig) administers access; it does not hold
  everything** (maintainer's decision 2026-11-08). The locked part of the
  role is:
  - **`club.roles.manage`** ("Administrera roller och behörigheter"):
    create, change and delete roles and assign any role to anyone,
    including permissions the manager does not hold;
  - **`club.people.protected`** ("Se personer med skyddad identitet"),
    maintainer's decision 2026-11-11: the person who assigns roles must be
    able to find every member, protected people included, and see that
    they are protected, or protected members could not be given roles or
    would be handled blindly. It gives the name and the protection marker
    in the club's lists (through the person presenter), **not** the
    register's details: those need `club.people.details`. The ADR and the
    help page say this in plain language ("Föreningsansvariga ser alltid
    vilka medlemmar som har skyddad identitet, men inte deras uppgifter,
    om de inte också har en roll som ger det").
- What the role may do besides that is an ordinary, editable set of
  permissions. The built-in template gives it the club's administration
  (club settings, groups and owners, invitations and join requests,
  handover of managers, the subscription and plan, the roles' and the audit
  log's read access) and nothing of the day-to-day work of other roles (the
  member register's details, money beyond the subscription, attendance,
  consents); the ADR lists the split per permission.
- To find whom to assign, role administration includes a name list of the
  club's members (through the person presenter), nothing else about them.
- **Self-assignment** is allowed, so that a small club where one person
  does everything is never stuck and a club can never lock itself out: a
  manager may give themselves any role. It is shown, audited
  (`role.assigned`, `self: true`) and told at once to the club's other
  administrators and the chair (notice, mandatory email); the roles page
  lists "Roller som föreningsansvariga gett sig själva".
- **Chair (Ordförande)** stays locked for recovery
  (`club.managers.claim`), its other permissions editable (decision 3).
- The locked role cannot be deleted and its locked permissions cannot be
  removed, so the SQL that names `club_manager` stays valid. The rest of
  ADR 0018 (handover, recovery) stays.
- **No escalation for everyone else:** a person without
  `club.roles.manage` (e.g. a group owner assigning group roles) may assign
  a role, or invite with it, only if every permission in it is one they
  hold themselves. Club managers are exempt through `club.roles.manage`,
  not through holding everything.
- Deleting a role that people hold needs a replacement role or ends the
  assignments (the dialog says which and how many); history keeps them.
- A role keeps a stable `template_key` (e.g. `head_coach`) while it comes
  from a template, for the API's old enum fields and for new permissions in
  later releases (decision 5).

### A2.2 Migrating the split
- Existing clubs get the split in a migration; the managers are told
  (notice and email) which permissions moved to which roles and how to give
  them back to themselves (one click: "Ge mig rollen Registeransvarig").
- The demo managers who also do that work (e.g. Anna in SVK) get the
  matching roles in the seed.
- The matrix test gets **one table of intended changes** (persona,
  endpoint, before, after); any other difference fails.

### A2.3 Immediate effect when permissions change
- **Server side, at once:** changing a role's permissions bumps the club's
  authorization version (A1.6); changing an assignment bumps the person's
  version as today. The next request of every holder is decided on the new
  permissions; there is nothing to log out of, since the API authorizes
  every request. The bump is in the same transaction's outbox, published as
  a domain event for the worker.
- **Jobs:** background jobs started by a person (exports, imports) check
  the permission again when they run and stop with a notice if it is gone.
- **Clients:** every authenticated API response carries `x-authz-version`
  (the person's version and the club's). The web app re-reads the menu and
  page on the next navigation anyway; a form posted without the permission
  gets a friendly page ("Dina behörigheter har ändrats") instead of an
  error. The mobile app compares the header with its cached value,
  refreshes its permissions and drops cached screens it may no longer see.
  A silent push (`authz.changed`, IDs only) to the holders' devices makes
  this happen without a tap, **best effort** (iOS throttles background
  pushes; the header is what guarantees it). When the WebSocket gateway
  arrives (Phase 2), it carries the same event and closes subscriptions the
  person may no longer hold.
- **People are told:** holders of a role get a notice when a permission is
  removed from it or their assignment ends (category `club_admin`, in-app),
  naming the role and the permission in plain language.

### A2.4 The role editor (web; pedagogical first)
- **Roles page** (club settings → "Roller och behörigheter"): club roles
  and group roles as cards, each with its name, a one-line summary in plain
  language generated from its permissions ("Kan se medlemsregistret och
  planera gruppens aktiviteter"), how many hold it, and badges for
  sensitive permissions.
- **Editing a role:** permissions grouped by area, each with its name,
  sentence and example, a "?" that opens the longer help; sensitive ones
  marked with an explanation of why. "Kopiera roll" starts from an existing
  role; "Återställ till mallen" restores a template role.
- **Before saving:** a summary of the change in sentences, and its effect:
  "3 personer får inte längre se medlemsregistret: Anna L, …" (names
  through the person presenter; protected people as "en medlem").
- **Who may do what:** per permission, the list of roles and the number of
  people who hold it ("Vem kan …?"); per person (their profile), every role
  and what it allows.
- **"Förhandsvisa rollen"** (instead of a "view as", which does not exist):
  the menu and the list of pages and actions the role reaches, computed
  from the catalogue, without switching identity or showing anyone's data.
- **Help:** a short page "Så fungerar roller" with a diagram (permission →
  role → person, club or group), linked from every role screen; first-visit
  hints on the roles page; texts reviewed for plain language (no
  "permission", "scope" or "policy" in the UI: "behörighet", "gäller i",
  "roll"). The help says that a group role applies in that group only, not
  in its subgroups, and what a club manager always sees (A2.1).
- Undo is through history (ADR 0042: the role's history panel shows each
  change and who made it).
- Assignment: club roles on the person's page as today; group roles in the
  group's member list, where the function choice becomes a choice of the
  club's group roles.
- Mobile: read-only (one's own roles and what they allow); editing is web
  only.

## Part A3 — At least two administrators; suspending one

Maintainer's decisions 2026-11-08 and 2026-11-11: a club always needs two
administrators, three are recommended, and an administrator can be taken
out quickly (for example after a break-in, or when the task passes to
someone else).

### A3.1 Who counts
An administrator counts when the person:
- holds `club.roles.manage` through an assignment valid today (any role);
- has an **active account** that can log in with MFA (pending invitations
  and people without login do not count);
- is an **adult** by the club's country pack (minors may hold the role, but
  do not count towards the minimum).

Distinct people are counted, not assignments.

### A3.2 Minimum two
- A club with fewer than two administrators is in the state **"needs an
  administrator"**: it can do everything it did before except **add
  members** (approving join requests, invitations other than for an
  administrator, adding by hand, imports, admitting from waiting lists).
  Existing members, activities and logins are unaffected. Each refusal says
  why and links to "Utse en till föreningsadministratör"
  (`club_needs_administrators`).
- **A new club** starts in this state: creating a club (and its creation
  request) asks for a second administrator's email; the club can invite
  the second administrator (an invitation with the administrator role) at
  once, and opens for members when that person accepts.
- **Existing clubs with one administrator** (migration) get a **30-day
  grace period** (maintainer's decision 2026-11-11): a banner and a notice
  with mandatory email at the start, reminders after 14 and 25 days, and
  then the state above. `clubs.administrators_grace_until` records it; the
  operator console can extend it once per club (audited). No member is
  removed.
- Leaving the role (stepping down, leaving the club, a role change that
  removes the permission) is refused if it would leave fewer than two,
  except suspension (A3.4) and erasure, which put the club in the state
  instead. The handover flow (ADR 0018) stays for one-to-one hand-overs.
- **In the database:** a deferred constraint trigger on assignments and
  role permissions refuses a change that leaves an active club (after its
  grace period) with fewer than two administrators, as the last-manager
  guard does today. The exception for a confirmed suspension or an erasure
  is a transaction-local marker that **only the `SECURITY DEFINER`
  functions performing them can set** (a setting the application role
  could set itself would not be an exception but a bypass); a test proves
  the application role cannot.

### A3.3 Three recommended
With exactly two, the club and its administrators see a recommendation
(banner on the roles page and the club start page, dismissible for 90
days): with three, one can be suspended by the other two without outside
help.

### A3.4 Suspending an administrator (two-person rule)
- Any administrator (MFA, recent login) can start "Stäng av som
  föreningsadministratör" for another administrator, with a reason code
  (`compromised`, `left_task`, `other`) and a note.
- **Confirming** is a built-in permission,
  **`club.administrators.confirm_suspension`**, with its own policy:
  allowed for an administrator, or for the chair when the club has exactly
  two administrators; never for the target or the initiator. The request
  must be confirmed within 72 hours. On confirmation, in the same
  transaction:
  - every role assignment that carries `club.roles.manage` ends; with
    "Degradera till vanlig medlem" (default on for `compromised`) every
    other club role and group leader role ends too (membership stays);
  - the target's pending handovers and role changes are cancelled, their
    open invitations with roles revoked;
  - with `compromised`: the target's sessions are revoked everywhere: the
    app's own sessions and devices, **and the identity provider's sessions
    and refresh tokens (Zitadel)**, so that the target cannot simply log
    in again with a still-valid session. They must log in again, also for
    other clubs, which the dialog says. The dialog lists the target's role
    and permission changes of the last 14 days, each linked to its history
    entry, to review and undo;
  - the authorization version is bumped (A2.3): it takes effect on the
    next request;
  - the target, every administrator and the chair are told (notice,
    mandatory email); audit `administrator.suspended`.
- With exactly two administrators and the chair being the target (or no
  chair), an operator confirms through the documented recovery (ADR 0018).
  The club then drops to "needs an administrator" (no grace period).
- The initiator can cancel before confirmation; an unconfirmed request
  expires after 72 hours. The target cannot confirm, cancel or act on
  their own suspension, and is told of the request at once (so that a
  mistaken or malicious request is visible).
- Restoring a suspended administrator is an ordinary assignment, told to
  everyone as below.

### A3.5 Adding an administrator
Assigning a role with `club.roles.manage`, or adding the permission to a
role people hold, is told to every administrator and the chair (notice,
mandatory email); one administrator is enough to add one.

## Decisions (confirmed by the maintainer)
1. **Name in the UI:** "Behörighet" for permission, "Roll" for role,
   "Föreningstyp" for club type (default).
2. **Club roles and group roles both** become club-defined in this prompt
   (default), not only group roles.
3. **Locked roles:** the club manager role is locked only in
   `club.roles.manage` and `club.people.protected` (administering roles and
   assigning any role, without holding the permissions; self-assignment
   allowed, shown and told to the other administrators); its other
   permissions are editable. The chair is locked only for recovery.
   (2026-11-08, protected identity 2026-11-11.)
4. **Group owners stay** a relationship next to roles (default).
5. **New permissions in later releases:** each new permission declares the
   template role keys that get it; a migration adds it to the clubs' roles
   with that `template_key`, never to custom roles, and never if sensitive
   (those are offered to the club managers in a notice) (default).
6. **Template changes never reach existing clubs** by themselves (default);
   clubs can copy roles from the template.
7. **Group owners may invite to their own groups** (default yes).
8. **One role per group per invitation** (default).
9. **Role names:** a name per club language with fallback to the
   catalogue text for template roles not renamed (default), so that a
   Norwegian member sees "Hovedtrener" until the club renames the role.
10. **Administrators:** `club.roles.manage` may be in several roles; at
    least two administrators, three recommended; with fewer than two the
    club cannot add members; suspension by two administrators (2026-11-08).
11. **Grace period:** existing clubs with one administrator get 30 days
    (2026-11-11).
12. **Club managers see protected people** (name and marker, not details),
    documented in the ADR, the domain model and the help (2026-11-11).
13. **Group permissions are not inherited by child groups** by default;
    ownership keeps its inheritance (2026-11-11).
14. **The prompt is implemented in parts A1–A4 and B**, A1 without any
    change in behaviour (2026-11-11).

## Users and roles involved
Club managers (edit roles), registrars and the chair (read roles), every
member (sees their own roles and what they allow), group owners (assign
group roles in their groups), operators (grace periods, recovery, club
types).

## Privacy and security
- Changing roles changes who sees personal data: sensitive permissions are
  marked, need MFA, and every change is audited and in history; holders are
  told when they lose a permission.
- Club managers administer access without holding every permission;
  self-assignment is visible and told; no escalation for others; the
  locked role prevents lock-out; permissions apply on the next request.
- The administrator minimum's exception cannot be set by the application;
  a compromised administrator loses the identity provider's sessions too.

## Protected identity
Holder lists, the change preview, the administrator list and invitation
lists show people through `PersonPresenter`; nothing in roles stores names.
Who sees protected people is a permission (`club.people.protected`),
always held by club managers (decision 12) and by default by registrars.

## Locale and country packs
Catalogue texts (name, sentence, example) for every assignable permission
and every template role in sv, en and nb; the help page in all three;
club type names in all three; country packs set the adult age (A3.1) and
may set default types.

## Performance
- `holds(...)` is a set lookup on the relationship set plus the club's
  role map (A1.6). Request budgets must not grow; cases for a person in
  several clubs, the roles page, the holders list (keyset) and
  `/me/permissions`.
- `app.holds_permission` and the worker's `holders(...)` are indexed by
  `(club_id, permission)` and `(club_id, role_id)`; query plans tested on
  the load scenario.
- The migrations run per club in batches.

## Out of scope
- Conditions on permissions beyond club and group scope (e.g. "only
