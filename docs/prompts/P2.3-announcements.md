# P2.3 — Announcements: the board in the app, email and push in one message

**Reviewed 2026-11-14** against what P1.7, P2.1 and P2.2 built: audiences
are the shared `audiences` list, push carries no free text, "important"
works as a mandatory email, unsubscribing stays per category, and a shared
Markdown renderer is part of this prompt.

**Status (2026-11-14):** implemented: migration 0044, the `announcements`
module, the worker's fan-out and `announcements:due`, the shared Markdown
parser, web (board, archive, composer, an announcement, a group's board)
and app (board, announcement, group board), seed, budgets and ADR 0053
(with its "As implemented"). The club's archive is
`GET /clubs/:clubId/announcements`; a group's senders have
`GET /clubs/:clubId/groups/:groupId/announcements/archive`.

## Context
Read first: `CLAUDE.md`, ADR 0039 (notifications: inbox, push, email modes,
bounces, unsubscribing), ADR 0047 (chat: no text in push), ADR 0049
(audiences: the same list decides who receives an announcement), ADR 0022
(protected identity), ADR 0040 (responsiveness), `docs/gdpr.md`.

What exists already:

- Notifications (P1.7, ADR 0039): a catalogue of types in
  `packages/shared/src/notifications.ts`, each with a category (`schedule`,
  `responses`, `leader`, `events`, `membership`, `club_admin`, `family`,
  `account`, `waitlist`, `chat`, `documents`) and optionally
  `mandatoryEmail` (sent whatever the settings say) and `immediate`
  (emailed at once instead of in the daily email). Channels `email` and
  `push`; email modes `always` (in the daily email) | `no_app` | `never`;
  quiet hours; bounces; one-click unsubscribe per account and category
  (turns the category's email to `never`). Notices are rendered from ICU
  messages and their params never hold free text or names.
- Audiences (P2.2, ADR 0049): `audiences` and `audience_entries`, with the
  entry kinds `club` (members, their guardians whose link receives
  messages, the club's leaders and role holders), `group` (the group's
  members and their guardians who receive messages), `group_roles`,
  `club_role` and `public`; `app.audience_includes(audience, person)` and
  `app.audience_people(audience)` decide it in SQL. The web's audience
  editor (`AudienceFields`) and its text (`AudienceText`) exist.
- Chat (P2.1): group channels for conversation; push never shows a
  message's text or sender.
- Club roles include `communications`; groups have owners (P1.10a).
- No board, no announcements, no Markdown rendering anywhere yet.

## Goal
A club or a group sends one message (a newsletter, "klubbstugan är stängd",
the date of the annual meeting) to the people it concerns and chooses how it
reaches them: the **board in the app**, **push** and/or **email**, one text.
The message board and bulk email are one feature. Members read
announcements in one place ("Anslagstavlan", across their clubs and groups)
and never miss the important ones.

## Users and roles involved
Club managers, the chair, the secretary, communications, group leaders and
owners (senders); members and guardians of all ages (recipients); protected
people.

## User stories
- As a club manager, I write "Årsmöte 12 mars" to the whole club, pin it
  on the board, mark it important and send it by email and push, so that
  everyone knows.
- As a coach, I tell my team's players and guardians that the field is
  closed this week, by push and on the board.
- As a parent, I see the announcements of my children's groups and of the
  club on one board, unread first, and pinned ones on top.
- As a member who never opens the app, I get announcements by email and
  can unsubscribe from announcements without losing activity notices.

## Decisions (write ADR 0053, amend ADR 0039)
1. **One message, chosen channels.** The sender picks any of board, push,
   email; the text is the same on the board and in the email (Markdown
   limited to what email renders: paragraphs, emphasis, lists, links).
2. **Push carries no free text** (ADR 0039, as chat in ADR 0047): it says
   "Nytt anslag från {group or club}" ("Viktigt anslag …" when important)
   and opens the announcement; the title and body are shown only in the
   app, so nothing the sender wrote reaches a lock screen.
3. **Audience** = an ADR 0049 audience (`announcements.audience_id`): the
   whole club, groups (optionally some roles in them), club roles. Guardians
   receive what is sent to their wards' groups when their link receives
   messages (`receive_messages`), as for documents. `public` cannot be
   chosen (public announcements belong to the public pages, Phase 3). A
   group sender's audience stays within the group (`group` or
   `group_roles` of that group).
4. **The board** keeps announcements per club and group: pinned first
   (until `pinned_until`), then newest; read/unread per person; an expiry
   date hides an announcement from the board (it stays in the archive).
   The board is an aggregated view ("Anslagstavlan" across clubs and
   groups) and each group's own on the group page. Who may read an
   announcement is decided by its audience in SQL, as for documents.
5. **Delivery through P1.7.** Sending creates one notice per recipient of
   the new type `announcement.sent` in the new category `announcements`
   (defaults: email `no_app`, push on), `immediate` (the email goes at
   once, not in the daily email). The email holds the title and body (an
   email template rendering the Markdown, escaped) and the usual footer
   with the unsubscribe link, which turns off announcements' email only.
   Reading the announcement marks its notice read and the reverse, so the
   inbox and the board never disagree.
6. **Important** announcements (e.g. the annual meeting notice) are sent
   like `mandatoryEmail`: by email to everyone in the audience whatever
   their settings, but never to an address that bounced. Push still follows
   the recipients' settings and quiet hours.
7. **Unsubscribing stays per account and category** (ADR 0039): there is no
   per-club setting; the email footer says which club sent it.
8. **Sending later**: an announcement may be scheduled (a worker job sends
   it); until sent it can be edited or withdrawn. After sending, editing
   changes the board and the archive (marked "redigerat"), not the emails
   or notices already sent; withdrawing removes it from the board.
9. **Markdown** through one shared, safe renderer (no raw HTML, links only
   `https:` and `mailto:`, a licence on the allow list), used by the web,
   the app and the email template; P2.4 reuses it for statutes and minutes.
10. **Email volume**: a per-club limit on announcement emails per day
    (operator setting, `ANNOUNCEMENT_EMAILS_PER_CLUB_DAY`, unlimited when
    unset); over the limit the email channel is refused before sending
    (`announcement_email_limit`), the board and push still work.
11. Kept until deleted by the sender or a club manager; erasure anonymises
    the sender.

## Data model
Club-owned, tenant RLS, record history, `updated_at`, person references
declared in `announcements.references.ts`.

- `announcements` (club_id, group_id nullable, audience_id, title, body
  (Markdown), channels (`board`, `push`, `email`), important, pinned_until,
  expires_at, scheduled_at, sent_at, edited_at, withdrawn_at, created_by,
  recipient counts per channel when sent).
- `announcement_reads` (announcement_id, person_id, read_at).
- Recipients resolved when sent with `app.audience_people` in the worker,
  in batches, into notices of type `announcement.sent` (IDs only); delivery
  through the P1.7 pipeline.

## API
| Method | Path | Permission | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| GET | `/me/announcements` | authenticated | The board across clubs and groups, keyset; unread and pinned first |
| GET | `/clubs/:clubId/announcements/:id` | in the audience, or may send it | One announcement |
| POST | `/clubs/:clubId/announcements/:id/read` | in the audience | Mark read (and its notice) |
| GET | `/clubs/:clubId/groups/:groupId/announcements` | in the audience (filtered) | The group's board |
| GET | `/clubs/:clubId/announcements/archive` | `club.announcements.send` | The club's archive incl. drafts and scheduled |
| POST | `/clubs/:clubId/announcements` | `club.announcements.send` | Draft, schedule or send |
| POST | `/clubs/:clubId/groups/:groupId/announcements` | `group.announcements.send` | Draft, schedule or send to the group |
| PATCH/DELETE | `/clubs/:clubId/announcements/:id` | the sender, or the permission it was sent with | Edit, withdraw, delete |
| GET | `/clubs/:clubId/announcements/:id/recipients` | as PATCH | Counts per channel, delivered, bounced and read (counts only, never names) |
| POST | `/clubs/:clubId/announcements/preview` | either send permission | Recipient counts per channel for an audience before sending |

## Permissions
| Permission | Scope | Allows | Given to these template roles |
|---|---|---|---|
| `club.announcements.send` | club | Announcements to the club, any group or club role | `club_manager`, `chair`, `secretary`, `communications` |
| `group.announcements.send` | group | Announcements to the group's members and guardians, or roles in it | `head_coach`, `assistant_coach`, `team_manager`; owners |

Allow/deny tests for each policy; matrix rows for every endpoint; a group
sender cannot address people outside the group; a reader outside the
audience gets 404.

## UI
- App and web: "Anslagstavlan" (unread first, pinned on top), a group's
  board on the group page, an announcement's page; a push or inbox notice
  opens the announcement.
- Composer (web): audience picker (the P2.2 editor without `public`),
  channels, important, pin, expiry, schedule, preview per channel (the
  email as rendered, the push text), the number of recipients per channel
  before sending.
- The archive with delivery and read counts.

## Privacy and security
Recipients' addresses never shown to the sender; delivery data kept as in
P1.7; audit events for send, edit, withdraw and delete. No free text in
push or in notice params. Bodies are not logged.

## Protected identity
Protected people receive announcements but appear in no recipient list or
count breakdown that could single them out; the sender's name through
`PersonPresenter`.

## Locale and country packs
sv, en, nb: the category, the push texts, the email template and footer,
the composer.

## Performance
Fan-out in the worker in batches; the board query starts from the
person's clubs and groups (as `app.my_documents`) with indexes on
(club_id, sent_at) and (group_id, sent_at); request budgets for every
endpoint the apps call (`/me/announcements`, one announcement, a group's
board, marking read). The app is cache first.

## Seed data
IKN: a pinned club announcement about the annual meeting (important, all
channels), a group announcement for F2015, one scheduled draft; Anna (a
parent of Oskar in IKN) has one unread.

## Acceptance criteria
- [x] Board, push and email from one message; push without free text;
      important ignores the category setting, not bounces
- [x] Audiences resolve guardians who receive messages; group senders
      limited to their group; readers outside the audience get 404
- [x] Scheduling, editing, withdrawing; inbox and board read status agree
- [x] Shared Markdown renderer (web, app, email), safe links only
- [x] Per-club email limit (operator setting)
- [x] Tenant isolation, allow/deny, matrix rows, budgets, audit, history
- [x] Translations complete for sv, en and nb; docs updated
      (`domain-model.md`, ADR 0053, ADR 0039 amendment for the category
      and the push rule)

## Out of scope
- Public announcements on the club's website (Phase 3).
- Comments and reactions on announcements (use the chat).
- Email to people who are not members (mailing lists).
- Per-club notification settings.
