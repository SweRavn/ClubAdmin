# P2.8 — Background-check plugins for Sweden and Norway; tracking screens

## Context
Read first: `CLAUDE.md`, ADR 0051 (decision 6, the background-check
contract), ADR 0047 (chat: a check where required; a club may only be
stricter), ADR 0050 and its amendment of 2026-11-18 (a duty type's contact
with children; no adult alone with a child where the country forbids it),
P2.7 (the plugin contract
and host, background-check tables), `docs/gdpr.md` (Art. 10: criminal
records), `docs/security.md`.

What exists already (after P2.7): `packages/plugin-api` with the
`background-checks` capability, the host's storage of check metadata, test
plugins; `club.background_checks.manage`; the hook in chat (P2.1) that asks
whether a person has a valid check. Duties (P2.6) have **no** check yet: a
duty type states its contact with children (`child_contact`: `none`,
`with_children`, `alone_with_children`), the country pack's
`dutyRules.aloneWithChildren` (Sweden: forbidden) and the club's stricter
setting decide whether `alone_with_children` may be used at all, and
eligibility checks only the minimum age. P2.8 adds the check to duty
eligibility.

## Goal
Clubs in Sweden and Norway follow up their leaders' background checks in
ClubAdmin: what is required for which role or duty, who has a valid check,
who must renew and when, without storing the certificate. Chat and duties
enforce the requirement.

## Users and roles involved
Club managers and the registrar (follow-up), leaders and volunteers (the
checked), the safeguarding officer.

## User stories
- As the registrar, I see which leaders lack a valid check and remind them.
- As a coach in Sweden, I order the extract from Polisen, show it, and the
  registrar records it as verified without keeping a copy.
- As a club in Norway, the "politiattest" is requested with the
  confirmation of purpose from the club and recorded when seen.
- As a leader, I see when my check expires.

## Plugins
Each declares check types, requirements per duty category and the flows.
The exact current national rules are verified against the authorities'
pages when the prompt starts and written into the plugin with sources.

- **`plugins/se-background-check`** (Sweden): "utdrag ur
  belastningsregistret" for work with children, requested by the person
  from Polisen, shown to the club (paper or digital); at most a set age when
  shown; re-check interval set by the club (default from the federation's
  recommendation); verified by looking (and by the authority's check where
  available); never a copy, never the reference code.
- **`plugins/no-background-check`** (Norway): "politiattest" (barneomsorg)
  requested by the person with the club's confirmation of purpose, shown to
  the club; re-check per the federation's rules; same storage limits.

## Decisions
1. **Requirements** come from the plugin per duty category (regular contact
   with children, alone with children, overnight, board); the club may add
   stricter ones (more roles, shorter intervals), never weaker (ADR 0047).
2. **Who needs a check is set on roles**, in the role editor of P1.15:
   a role (club or group) can carry the **requirement** "kräver
   registerutdrag" with a category from the plugin (default: regular
   contact with children). Duty types need no field of their own: their
   `child_contact` (P2.6) maps to the plugin's categories (`with_children`
   → regular contact with children, `alone_with_children` → alone with
   children, only where the country and club allow it at all); the club may
   map a type to a stricter category (`duty_types.background_check_category`,
   never a weaker one). It is a
   requirement in the role catalogue, not a permission: permissions grant,
   and owners' inheritance and the "holds" checks must never be what
   imposes a duty (decision 2026-11-12, maintainer asked for "a permission"
   and accepted a requirement in the same editor). Template defaults:
   `head_coach`, `assistant_coach`, `team_manager`.
3. **Checking off (Sweden):** the person shows their extract to someone
   in the club who holds `club.background_checks.verify` and ticks it off
   in ClubAdmin (type, issued date, seen, valid until); nothing is
   uploaded. Norway: the same, after the club's confirmation of purpose.
4. **Records** store type, country, issued, verified, by whom, method,
   valid until; never the document, a picture, a code or a negative outcome
   (a negative outcome closes the case and ends the role without a reason).
5. **Follow-up**: reminders before expiry (to the person and the
   registrar), a list of missing and expiring.
6. **Enforcement**: the chat rule (P2.1 rule 9) asks the host; P2.8 adds
   the check to duty eligibility (P2.6 checks only the minimum age): open
   shifts of a type that needs a check are not offered to people without a
   valid one, sign-up, assignment and taking a swap are refused
   (`DomainError("background_check_required")`), and people already on such
   a shift without a valid check are listed for the leaders (the shift is
   not cancelled automatically). A leader without a required check keeps
   the role but cannot write to children or take such duties; the screens
   show why.

## Data model
Uses P2.7's tables (`background_check_records`, requests, requirements);
adds the requirement on roles (`club_roles` / role templates:
`background_check_category` nullable, set in the role editor) and the
club's stricter mapping on duty types (`duty_types.background_check_category`
nullable; none: from `child_contact`). Records are kept until someone with `club.background_checks.manage`
deletes them (no automatic deletion). Club-owned, tenant RLS, history.

## API
| Method | Path | Permission | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| GET | `/clubs/:clubId/background-checks/overview` | `club.background_checks.manage` | Missing, expiring, valid (keyset) |
| GET/PUT | `/clubs/:clubId/background-checks/requirements` | `club.background_checks.manage` | Plugin requirements, club's stricter additions, mappings |
| POST | `/clubs/:clubId/background-checks/records` | `club.background_checks.verify` (MFA) | Tick off a shown extract |
| POST | `/clubs/:clubId/background-checks/requests` | `club.background_checks.manage` | Start a request (NO flow) |
| GET | `/me/background-checks` | authenticated | My status and what is required of me |

## Permissions
| Permission | Scope | Allows | Given to these template roles |
|---|---|---|---|
| `club.background_checks.manage` (from P2.7) | club | Overview, the club's stricter requirements, reminders, deleting records | `club_manager`, `registrar` |
| `club.background_checks.verify` | club | Tick off an extract a person shows (never one's own) | `registrar`, `safeguarding_officer` |

The requirement "kräver registerutdrag" on roles is edited by whoever may
edit roles (`club.roles.manage`). Reading one's own status is
authenticated. Allow/deny tests for each.

## UI
Club → Trygghet → Registerutdrag: overview, requirements,
record dialog (no upload field), reminders. "Mitt konto": my checks and
what is required. Explanations in chat and duties when blocked.

## Privacy and security
Art. 10 data: only metadata; access logged; no negative outcomes. Records
are kept until actively deleted (decision 2026-11-12); the overview shows
records of people who no longer hold a role that needs them, so they can be
deleted. DPIA note in `gdpr.md`.

## Protected identity
Overview lists through `PersonPresenter`.

## Locale and country packs
Plugins carry their texts in sv, en, nb; the UI translated.

## Performance
Overview from the club's leaders with an index on (club, valid_until);
budgets.

## Seed data
IKN: three leaders with valid checks, one expiring, one missing (blocked
from writing to children in chat and from P2012's "Sekretariat" shifts,
contact `with_children`, P2.6's seed); a Norwegian demo club with the NO
plugin.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] SE and NO plugins pass the conformance suite of P2.7
- [ ] Requirements, club stricter additions, mappings
- [ ] Records without documents, codes or negative outcomes
- [ ] Reminders; enforcement in chat and duties (open shifts, sign-up,
      assignment, swaps; the leaders' list of shifts staffed without a
      valid check)
- [ ] Tenant isolation, allow/deny, matrix rows, budgets, audit, history
- [ ] Translations sv, en, nb; docs (`gdpr.md`, ADR 0051 "As implemented",
      `docs/plugins.md`)

## Out of scope
- Automatic verification against the authorities' APIs where none is
  open to clubs; other countries (third-party plugins).
- Federation feeds (e.g. France) as real plugins.
