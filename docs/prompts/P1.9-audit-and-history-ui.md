# P1.9 — Audit log and record-history UI ("History" panel on detail pages)

## Context
Read first: `CLAUDE.md`, ADR 0017 (record history: what is captured, who
may read it, scrubbing on erasure, "a History panel on every detail page
and a filterable history view for club managers"), `docs/security.md`
("Audit": club managers view their club's audit log), ADR 0022 (protected
identity: "history/audit actor names" are hidden from those who may not
see the person), ADR 0023 (global tables RLS), ADR 0025 (authorization
engine, 404 versus 403), ADR 0029 (API only grows), ADR 0030 (erasure core),
`docs/domain-model.md` ("Record history", "Protected identity", "Field
visibility", "Permissions"), `docs/architecture.md` ("History, audit,
outbox", "Person visibility"), `docs/performance.md`, `docs/seed-data.md`
("Record history" in the demo scenario), `docs/gdpr.md` (retention of the
audit log and the history), `docs/prompts/P1.8-privacy.md` (erasure,
audit retention).

What exists already:
- **Record history (P0.4):** `history.record_changes`, partitioned by month
  (`record_changes_YYYY_MM` plus a default partition), columns `id`,
  `changed_at`, `table_name` (schema-qualified), `record_id`, `club_id`
  (NULL for global tables), `operation`, `changed_columns`, `old_values`,
  `new_values` (changed columns only for updates, the full row for inserts
  and deletes), `actor_person_id`, `on_behalf_of_person_id`, `actor_kind`
  (`person`, `operator`, `system_job`, `migration`, `db_role`),
  `request_id` (`job:<name>` for worker jobs, `privacy:erasure`, …),
  `transaction_id`, `db_role`. Indexes `record_changes_record_idx
  (table_name, record_id, changed_at DESC, id DESC)` and
  `record_changes_club_idx (club_id, changed_at DESC)`. RLS: `club_read`
  for `clubadmin_app`/`clubadmin_operator` only where `club_id =
  app.current_club_id()`; **rows of global tables (`people`, `accounts`,
  `guardianships`, `guardian_invitations`, `account_invitations`,
  `people_merges`, …) are unreachable for the app role** ("reached through
  dedicated functions", none exist yet). Redacted columns are stored as
  `"[changed]"` (`people.personal_identity_number` and its hash,
  `club_profiles.notes`, token hashes, `member_imports.columns`); ignored
  columns are not recorded (`clubs.next_member_number`,
  `member_imports.counts`). Erasure scrubs values to `"[erased]"`
  (`privacy.scrub`). Partitions older than `HISTORY_RETENTION_MONTHS`
  (default 24) are dropped daily by `history:maintain_partitions`.
  Exclusions with reasons in `packages/db/src/history-exclusions.ts`
  (`schedule_entries`, `member_import_rows`, the infrastructure schemas).
- **Reader:** `getRecordHistory(tx, table, recordId, { cursor, limit })`
  in `packages/db/src/history.ts`: one record, newest first, keyset on
  `(changed_at, id)` with an opaque base64url cursor, limit 1–200.
  `packages/db/src/history.integration.test.ts` covers it. **No HTTP
  endpoint, no UI, nothing reads more than one record.**
- **Audit events (P0.4):** `audit.events` (`id`, `created_at`, `club_id`,
  `actor_person_id`, `on_behalf_of_person_id`, `actor_kind`, `action`,
  `resource_type`, `resource_id`, `changed_fields` (names only), `ip_hash`,
  `request_id`); indexes `events_club_idx (club_id, created_at DESC, id
  DESC)` and `events_resource_idx (resource_type, resource_id, created_at
  DESC)`; RLS `club_read` by `club_id`, insert-only for the app roles. About
  90 registered actions in `packages/shared/src/audit-actions.ts`
  (`membership.*`, `group.*`, `activity.*`, `attendance.*`,
  `guardianship.*`, `person.protected.viewed`, `members.exported`, …, and
  from P1.8 `consent.*`, `privacy.*`, `person.club_data.purged`). **No
  endpoint or page lists them.** Retention: `audit:retention` (P1.8).
- **Actors:** `PersonPresenter.presentActors(authorization, clubId,
  actors)` returns `ActorReference` (`{ kind: "person", person }`,
  `{ kind: "protected" }`, `{ kind: "system" }`;
  `packages/shared/src/domain/responses.ts`), tested in
  `authorization.integration.test.ts`. Erased people (P1.8,
  `people.anonymized_at`) have empty names; nothing presents them yet.
- **Field visibility:** `profileAccess` in the person-profiles module
  (`GET /people/:personId?clubId=`) decides which fields a viewer sees;
  `club_profiles.shared_fields` limits phone, address and gender for clubs;
  the family presenter limits consent notes and `ended_reason`; activity
  response notes are for leaders and the family only (ADR 0035).
- **Detail pages (web):** `/people/[personId]` (with `clubId`),
  `/groups/[groupId]`, `/activities/[activityId]`,
  `/activity-series/[seriesId]`, `/clubs/[clubId]` and its admin pages
  (members, invitations, join requests, managers, locations, exports,
  imports); `/me/profile`, `/me/family`. Mobile has people, groups and
  activity details (read-only besides answers and attendance).
- **Access logging:** viewing a protected person's profile writes
  `person.protected.viewed` (ADR 0022).
- **Seed:** `docs/demodata/history.json` replays changes over time (Anna
  moved Elsa from Minivolley to F2017, Erik changed his phone, Anna
  approved Johan, a job expired a waiting-list offer); Sara's attendance
  correction (P1.5); Oskar's and Liam's own logins; P1.8 adds consents, a
  purged former member and an erased person.
- **Nothing yet** for history or audit endpoints, permissions, labels for
  tables, columns or actions, or the indexes a club-wide filtered view
  needs.

## Goal
On every detail page, those who look after a record open "History" and see
who changed what and when, in plain language ("Anna Lindqvist moved Elsa
from Minivolley to F2017", "Phone changed", "Erik for Maja: Not coming"),
with old and new values where they may see the field, and nothing they may
not: no protected person they may not see, no value of a field hidden from
them, "Erased" where a person's data was erased. A club manager also has
the club's audit log ("Sara exported the member register", "Johan
corrected an attendance mark") and a club-wide list of changes, both
filterable by what, who and when, fast on years of data.

## Users and roles involved
Club managers (everything in their club, with MFA: audit log, club-wide
history, every panel); registrars (people, memberships, groups, activities'
attendance); group owners (their groups and the groups' activities);
leaders who manage an activity (its history); the person and their full
guardians with `act_on_behalf` (their own records); others see no history.
Platform operators: nothing in club data (support access as for other club
reads).

## User stories
- As a club manager, I want a History panel on a member's page showing
  every change to their membership, groups, roles, profile, guardians and
  consents in the club, so that I can answer "who changed this, and when?".
- As a registrar, I want to see when a member's membership type changed and
  who did it.
- As a group owner, I want the group's history: who joined and left, who
  became coach, what settings changed.
- As a coach, I want the history of an activity: time and place changes,
  cancellation, who changed which answer and who corrected attendance.
- As a parent, I want to see who changed my child's details and answers,
  including what the club did.
- As a club manager, I want the club's audit log filtered by kind of action
  ("exports", "roles"), by person acting and by date, so that I can
  investigate an incident or answer a member's question.
- As a club manager, I want a club-wide list of all changes filtered by
  kind of record, person acting and period.
- As anyone, I never want to see a protected person I may not see, or a
  value I could not see on the record itself, through history.
- As a member whose data was erased, I want history to keep only the fact
  that something changed, never my values.

## Data model
No new tables. A new ADR (the next free number) records the decisions (who reads which history,
the column policy, the scope of a panel, global-table access).

- **History view policy** (`apps/api/src/modules/audit/history-columns.ts`):
  for every history-enabled table that a panel or the club-wide view shows,
  its label key, the resource it belongs to, and for every column one of
  `value` (shown with old and new value when the viewer may see the field
  on the current record), `name_only` (shown as "changed"), `hidden`
  (never shown: `id`, `club_id`, `created_at`, `updated_at`, token hashes,
  `personal_identity_number_hash`, `sequence`, `changed_at`), `person`
  (a person ID, resolved through the presenter), `ref:<kind>` (a group,
  location, activity, club or season ID, resolved to its name in one
  query per kind and page), or a field-visibility rule (`people.phone`:
  the person, full guardians, and club viewers only while shared;
  `activity_responses.note`: leaders and the family; `guardianships.consent_note`:
  as the family presenter; …). An integration test fails when a
  history-enabled table (from the same catalogue as `findGuardViolations`)
  is neither in the policy nor in an explicit "never shown" list with a
  reason; a new column of a listed table defaults to `name_only` until
  classified (unit test on the policy against the Drizzle schema).
- **Panel scope** (same file): which rows make up a detail page's history.
  The record itself plus related rows found by ID in one query, including
  ended ones:
  - person in a club: `people` (global, see below), `club_profiles`,
    `club_memberships`, `group_memberships` (the person's, in the club),
    `club_role_assignments`, `employments`, `consents`, `guardianships`
    as ward or guardian (global), `activity_responses` and
    `attendance_records` are **not** included (they belong to activities;
    too many rows);
  - group: `groups`, `group_owners`, `group_memberships` of the group,
    `group_seasons`, waiting-list entries (when P1.10 lands);
  - activity: `activities`, `activity_groups`, `activity_responses`,
    `attendance_records`, `activity_invitees` of the activity; series:
    `activity_series`, `activity_series_groups`;
  - club: `clubs`, `locations`, `consent_texts` (P1.8),
    `support_access_grants`.
  The history query is `(table_name, record_id) IN (…)` ordered by
  `(changed_at, id)` descending with the keyset cursor, served by
  `record_changes_record_idx` per partition. The ID lists are capped
  (`history_scope_too_large` above 2,000 IDs; the UI then offers the
  club-wide view filtered to the record).
- **Global rows:** `history.read_global_changes(p_tables text[],
  p_record_ids uuid[], p_before_at timestamptz, p_before_id uuid, p_limit
  int)` (SECURITY DEFINER, owner-owned, `search_path` pinned) returns rows
  of `public.people` and `public.guardianships` only, for record IDs the
  caller may see (`app.can_see_person` for people; for guardianships,
  `app.can_see_person` of both parties), never `accounts` except the
  caller's own account (email changes on `/me/profile`). Authorization in
  the API stays primary; the function is the database backstop. Granted
  to `clubadmin_app` only.
- **Indexes** (new migration; built per partition, see Performance):
  `history.record_changes (club_id, changed_at DESC, id DESC)` replacing
  `record_changes_club_idx` (keyset needs the tie-breaker),
  `(club_id, actor_person_id, changed_at DESC, id DESC)` and
  `(club_id, table_name, changed_at DESC, id DESC)` for the club-wide
  filters; `audit.events (club_id, actor_person_id, created_at DESC, id
  DESC)` and `(club_id, action, created_at DESC, id DESC)`;
  `events_resource_idx` gains `id DESC` (new index, old one dropped in a
  later migration: expand/contract).
- **Retention:** unchanged: history partitions after
  `HISTORY_RETENTION_MONTHS`, audit events after `AUDIT_RETENTION_MONTHS`
  (P1.8). The panels say "History is kept for 2 years" from the setting.

## API
Under `/api/v1`. Reads only, allowed for clubs in any access state.
Keyset pagination with the opaque cursor of `getRecordHistory` (`cursor`
= encoded `(changed_at, id)` of the last item; `PageQuerySchema` with this
cursor format documented as an exception to "last item's ID"); `limit`
default 50, at most 100. Problem codes: `history_scope_too_large`,
`invalid_cursor`, `range_too_large`, ….

| Method | Path | Permission | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| GET | `/people/:personId/history?clubId=&cursor=` | `person.history.read` | The person's panel in one club (scope above) plus their global rows; without `clubId` only for the person and their full guardians: global rows and every club the caller may read |
| GET | `/groups/:groupId/history?cursor=` | `group.history.read` | The group's panel |
| GET | `/activities/:activityId/history?cursor=` | `activity.history.read` | The activity's panel |
| GET | `/activity-series/:seriesId/history?cursor=` | `activity.history.read` | The series' panel (resource `activity` through `:seriesId`, as `activity.read`) |
| GET | `/clubs/:clubId/history?table=&actorId=&from=&to=&cursor=` | `club.history.read` | Club-wide changes; `table` from the policy's labelled tables; `from`/`to` at most 400 days apart (`range_too_large`); without them the newest first |
| GET | `/clubs/:clubId/audit-events?category=&action=&actorId=&resourceType=&resourceId=&from=&to=&cursor=` | `club.audit.read` | The club's audit log; `category` maps to a list of actions (`members`, `roles`, `groups`, `activities`, `attendance`, `families`, `privacy`, `exports`, `settings`, `access`); same range rule |
| GET | `/me/history?cursor=` | own data | Shortcut for the caller's own person without `clubId` (the profile page) |

Each history item: `id`, `changedAt`, `operation`, `table` (label key),
`recordId`, `resource` (`{ kind, id, label }`, the label resolved as
above, hidden people as `{ kind: "person", hidden: true }`), `actor`
(`ActorReference`), `onBehalfOf` (`ActorReference` or null), `changes`
(`[{ column, labelKey, kind: "value" | "changed" | "erased" | "redacted",
old?, new? }]`, values already formatted as the policy says: enum keys,
ISO dates, resolved references), `transactionId` (so the client groups one
action's rows), `automatic` (`{ job }` for system jobs: `attendance:retention`,
`privacy:retention`, …). Audit items: `id`, `createdAt`, `action`,
`actor`, `onBehalfOf`, `resource` (as above), `changedFields` (label keys),
never `ip_hash`.

`ActorReference` gains `{ kind: "erased" }` (a person with
`anonymized_at`) and `{ kind: "operator" }` (actor kind `operator`, name
not shown) and `{ kind: "system", job? }`; clients treat unknown kinds as
`system` (ADR 0029, enum grows; update the generated client and the
mobile app's parser).

## Permissions
New permissions, one policy each in `authorization.policy.ts`, allow and
deny tests, entries in the permission matrix (tenant isolation: a user of
club B gets 404 for club A's history and audit log):
- `person.history.read` (person, `?clubId`): the person; full guardians
  with `act_on_behalf`; in the club: club managers and registrars. Not
  leaders, owners, members of the same group, relatives, support access
  (support access may be added later with its own decision). Reading the
  history of a protected person by anyone but the person and their family
  writes `person.protected.viewed` (as a profile view, ADR 0022).
- `group.history.read` (group): club managers, registrars, the group's
  owners (also of parent groups). Not leaders or members.
- `activity.history.read` (activity): those who manage it (as
  `activity.manage`), club managers, registrars. Notes on answers follow
  `activity.responses.read` (leaders) inside the response; attendance
  marks are visible to all of them.
- `club.history.read` (club): club managers, with MFA.
- `club.audit.read` (club): club managers, with MFA.
- `/me/history` is own data (login only), limited by the service to the
  caller's own rows.
- Tests: Erik (head coach F2017) reads the F2017 match's history but gets
  403 for the F2017 group's history and for Elsa's person history; Anna
  reads Elsa's history in SVK as manager and as parent; Gunilla (relative)
  gets 403 for Alva's; Johan (registrar) gets 403 for the audit log;
  Karin (IKN) gets 404 for SVK's; a manager without a second factor gets
  the MFA problem.

## UI
- **Web, History panel** (a shared server component in `packages/ui` or
  `apps/web/src/components/history-panel.tsx`): a "History" tab or
  collapsible section on the person (club context), group, activity, series
  and club pages, shown only when the viewer holds the permission (from
  `GET /me/clubs` and the page's data). Entries newest first, grouped by
  transaction ("Anna Lindqvist · 12 March 14:05 · moved Elsa to F2017",
  expandable to the column changes), old → new values, "changed" for
  `name_only`, "Erased" for scrubbed values, "Hidden" for redacted ones,
  "Automatic (attendance retention)" for jobs, "Deleted user" for erased
  actors, "Protected member" for hidden actors, "Anna for Elsa" for
  on-behalf-of. "Show older" loads the next page (cursor); works without
  JavaScript (a link with the cursor). Times in the club's time zone.
- **Web, club audit log** (`/clubs/[clubId]/audit`, club managers): a
  table of events with filters (category, person acting through a person
  picker limited to people the viewer may see, resource type, date range),
  translated action sentences ("Sara Ahmed exported the member register
  (name, member number, groups)"), links to the resource's page, keyset
  "Show older". Empty state "No events in this period".
- **Web, club-wide changes** (`/clubs/[clubId]/history`, club managers):
  the same entry layout as the panel, with filters by kind of record, person
  acting and period; each entry links to the record's page.
- **Web, own profile:** "History" on `/me/profile` (my person row, my
  account's email changes, my guardianships).
- Loading skeletons, empty ("No changes recorded yet"), the retention note
  ("History is kept for 2 years; audit events for 2 years"), errors.
- **Mobile:** out of scope (web only).

## Privacy and security
- History and audit are themselves personal data about actors and
  subjects; reading them is limited to those who look after the record
  (ADR 0017 amended: "anyone who can view a record can see who changed it"
  becomes "those who hold the record's history permission"). Update
  ADR 0017 and `security.md`.
- Values follow field visibility on the **current** record: a phone number
  no longer shared with the club is hidden in all its past values for club
  viewers; notes follow their own rules; redacted and erased values never
  show. Column names of fields a viewer may not see at all (unshared
  `people` fields for club viewers) are left out, not shown as "changed".
- Rows whose subject is a person the viewer may not see (a protected
  member added to a group, seen by someone who may not see them) are left
  out entirely, not counted; references to hidden people inside values
  show "Hidden person".
- The audit log shows action names and field names only (it has no
  values), never `ip_hash` or `request_id`.
- Reading history and audit is not itself audited, except the protected
  profile access log above (and rate limited per account, P0.10).
- Nothing new is written to history by reads.
- No exports of the audit log or history in this package (see Open
  decisions).

## Protected identity
- Every actor, on-behalf-of person, subject and person reference goes
  through `PersonPresenter` (`present`/`presentActors`) with the viewer's
  authorization, in one call per page (no N+1).
- Hidden actors are `{ kind: "protected" }` ("Protected member"); hidden
  subjects are left out; protected people the viewer may see carry the
  crossed-camera icon in history as everywhere.
- A leader of IKN F2015 sees in the activity's history that Nadia's answer
  changed, with Samira as "Protected member" if they may not see her.
- Tests: the matrix scenario's protected member and protected guardian in
  panel, club-wide and audit responses for each viewer kind.

## Locale and country packs
- Labels in sv, en and nb for every labelled table, every `value` and
  `name_only` column, every enum value shown, every audit action (as a
  sentence with placeholders for actor and resource), categories, job
  names and the `ActorReference` kinds. A unit test fails when a registered
  audit action or a policy column lacks a key in sv or en (and nb).
- No country-specific rules; dates and times in the club's locale and time
  zone.

## Performance
- History panel, first page (50 entries) < 100 ms p95 for a person with
  five years of changes, a group of 25 members over five seasons and an
  activity with 25 answers and marks; "Show older" pages the same.
- Club-wide changes and the audit log, first page < 100 ms p95 without
  filters and with each filter, on Storklubben in the load scenario (add
  two years of history and audit events for it: about 5 million history
  rows and 500,000 audit events); a date range prunes partitions.
- At most 5 queries per request: scope IDs, history page (club rows), the
  global function, presenter, reference names.
- Query-plan tests on the load scenario: no sequential scans on
  `history.record_changes` partitions or `audit.events`; index scans on
  the new indexes; `MergeAppend` over partitions for the newest-first
  pages.
- Building indexes on the partitioned table: `CREATE INDEX ... ON ONLY
  history.record_changes`, then one `CREATE INDEX` per existing partition
  and `ALTER INDEX ... ATTACH PARTITION` (new partitions inherit them).
  Only the current month's partition takes writes, so a short write block
  while its index builds is accepted and measured on the load scenario
  (note it in the PR, rule 13). `history.maintain_partitions` keeps working
  unchanged.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] History panels on the person (club), group, activity, series and club
      pages show changes newest first with actor, on-behalf-of, time and
      column changes, grouped by transaction, keyset-paginated.
- [ ] Values follow field visibility on the current record; redacted,
      erased and hidden values never appear; unshared fields are left out
      for club viewers.
- [ ] Protected people the viewer may not see never appear as actor,
      subject or reference; protected profile history reads are
      access-logged.
- [ ] Erased actors show as "Deleted user", jobs as automatic with their
      name, operators without name.
- [ ] Global rows (people, guardianships) appear in person panels only
      through `history.read_global_changes`, which refuses people the

- [ ] Club managers (with MFA) read the audit log and the club-wide changes
      with category/action, actor, resource and date filters.
- [ ] The history view policy covers every history-enabled table (or lists

      `name_only`; tests guard both.
- [ ] Performance budgets met (timing and query-plan tests on the load

## Out of scope
- Mobile history views.
- Exports of the audit log or history (CSV); streaming audit events to
  separate storage (`security.md`, "Audit"; an issue).
- Operator-facing audit views (operator actions, global tables' history
  for platform support).
- Restoring or reverting a record from its history.
- The protected-identity access log page (P1.12), which reads the same
  `person.protected.viewed` events.
- History of answers and attendance on the person's page (the activity
  panels show them; a member's own attendance history is a later package).

## Open decisions
1. **Who may read history.** Recommended: only holders of the new history
   permissions (club managers, registrars, group owners, those who manage
   an activity, the person and their full guardians), narrowing ADR 0017's
   "anyone who can view a record"; amend ADR 0017.
2. **Values of fields no longer shared.** Recommended: follow the current
   record: once a person stops sharing a field with the club, its past
   values and even the fact that it changed are hidden from club viewers.
3. **Audit log and club-wide history for registrars.** Recommended: club
   managers only (with MFA); registrars get the person, group and activity
   panels.
4. **Exporting the audit log.** Recommended: not in this package; open an
   issue for a CSV export with the member-export pattern (MFA, recent
   login, worker job, 24-hour file) when a club or authority asks for it.
5. **Panel scope for a person.** Recommended: the person's own rows in the
   club and their global rows, without answers and attendance; the scope
   capped at 2,000 record IDs with a fallback to the filtered club-wide
   view, rather than adding a `subject_person_id` column to the history.
6. **Index build on the partitioned history table.** Recommended: per
   partition inside the migration (a brief write block on the current
   month's partition), rather than adding non-transactional migrations
   with `CREATE INDEX CONCURRENTLY` to the runner now.
7. **Operators in history.** Recommended: shown as "Platform operator"
   without a name, as `{ kind: "operator" }`; the support grant itself is
   in the club's history with the operator's name for managers.
8. **Mobile.** Recommended: no history in the app in this package; the web
   pages are linked from the app's person and activity screens for those
   who hold the permission.
