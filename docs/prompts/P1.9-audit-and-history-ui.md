## Decisions (2026-11-05)
- **Who reads history:** club managers and registrars only (decision 1
  below). No `/me/history`, no panels for group owners, leaders, event
  organisers, the person or their guardians. A person's right of access
  (GDPR Art. 15) to the history of their own rows is served by "download my
  data" (P1.8b), which already includes it.
- **The rest of the open decisions** as recommended (2–8).
- **History must not burden the rest of the system.** It is read rarely,
  so it is not optimised for speed beyond the budgets below; what matters
  is that reading it never slows down the everyday paths: its own small
  connection pool with a statement timeout, optionally a read replica,
  only one index change on the history table, and volume tests outside CI
  (see "Isolation from the rest of the system" and "Performance").
  Capture stays as it is (synchronous trigger, ADR 0017); it is not made
  asynchronous.
- **Few message keys:** sentences are built from a small set of resource
  and verb keys, existing form and enum labels are reused, and only columns
  shown with values get labels (see "Locale and country packs").

## Goal
On every detail page, those who look after a record open "History" and see
who changed what and when, in plain language ("Anna Lindqvist moved Elsa
from Minivolley to F2017", "Phone changed", "Erik for Maja: Not coming"),
with old and new values where they may see the field, and nothing they may
not: no protected person they may not see, no value of a field hidden from
them, "Erased" where a person's data was erased. A club manager also has
the club's audit log ("Sara exported the member register", "Johan
corrected an attendance mark") and a club-wide list of changes, both
filterable by what, who and when, fast on years of data.

## Users and roles involved
Club managers (everything in their club, with MFA: audit log, club-wide
history, every panel); registrars (the person, group, activity, series and
event panels in their club); nobody else sees history (decided 2026-11-05:
not group owners, leaders, organisers, the person or their guardians; the
person gets their own history through "download my data"). Platform
operators: nothing in club data (support access as for other club reads).

## User stories
- As a club manager, I want a History panel on a member's page showing
  every change to their membership, groups, roles, profile, guardians and
  consents in the club, so that I can answer "who changed this, and when?".
- As a registrar, I want to see when a member's membership type changed and
  who did it.
- As a registrar, I want a group's history: who joined and left, who
  became coach, what settings changed.
- As a registrar, I want the history of an activity: time and place
  changes, cancellation, who changed which answer and who corrected
  attendance.
- As a parent, I want to see who changed my child's details, which I get
  from "download my data" for my child (P1.8b), not from a panel.
- As a club manager, I want the club's audit log filtered by kind of action
  ("exports", "roles", "privacy"), by person acting and by date, so that I can
  investigate an incident or answer a member's question.
- As a club manager, I want a club-wide list of all changes filtered by
  kind of record, person acting and period.
- As anyone, I never want to see a protected person I may not see, or a
  value I could not see on the record itself, through history.
- As a member whose data was erased, I want history to keep only the fact
  that something changed, never my values.

## Data model
No new tables. A new ADR (0042, the next free number) records the decisions (who reads which history, the isolation (own pool, replica, one index), the composed messages,
the column policy, the scope of a panel, global-table access).

- **History view policy** (`apps/api/src/modules/audit/history-columns.ts`):
  for every history-enabled table that a panel or the club-wide view shows,
  the resource it belongs to (its label is the resource's label), and for
  every column one of `value` (shown with old and new value when the viewer
  may see the field on the current record; the column's label is the
  existing form label where one exists), `name_only` (not labelled: counted
  in "Other fields changed"), `hidden`
  (never shown: `id`, `club_id`, `created_at`, `updated_at`, token hashes,
  `personal_identity_number_hash`, `sequence`, `changed_at`), `person`
  (a person ID, resolved through the presenter), `ref:<kind>` (a group,
  location, activity, club or season ID, resolved to its name in one
  query per kind and page), or a field-visibility rule (`people.phone`:
  the person, full guardians, and club viewers only while shared;
  `activity_responses.note`: leaders and the family; `guardianships.consent_note`:
  as the family presenter; …). An integration test fails when a
  history-enabled table (from the same catalogue as `findGuardViolations`)
  is neither in the policy nor in an explicit "never shown" list with a
  reason; a new column of a listed table defaults to `name_only` until
  classified (unit test on the policy against the Drizzle schema).
- **Panel scope** (same file): which rows make up a detail page's history.
  The record itself plus related rows found by ID in one query, including
  ended ones:
  - person in a club: `people` (global, see below), `club_profiles`,
    `club_memberships`, `group_memberships` (the person's, in the club),
    `club_role_assignments`, `employments`, `consents`, `join_requests`
    and `group_waitlist_entries` (the person's, in the club; decided
    2026-11-01), `guardianships` as ward or guardian (global);
    `activity_responses`, `attendance_records` and `event_registrations`
    are **not** included (they belong to activities and events; too many
    rows);
  - group: `groups`, `group_owners`, `group_memberships` of the group,
    `group_seasons`, `group_waitlist_entries` (they exist since P0.5/P1.6b;
    P1.10 adds offers and admission);
  - activity: `activities`, `activity_groups`, `activity_responses`,
    `attendance_records`, `activity_invitees` of the activity; series:
    `activity_series`, `activity_series_groups`;
  - event (decided 2026-11-01): `events`, `event_groups`,
    `event_activities`, `event_registrations` of the event (answers as
    the event's organisers see them; `sensitive_answers` always `hidden`);
  - club: `clubs`, `locations`, `club_phone_numbers` (P1.6b),
    `consent_texts`, `club_setting_decisions`, `club_privacy_policies`
    (P1.8a), `support_access_grants`.
  The history query is `(table_name, record_id) IN (…)` ordered by
  `(changed_at, id)` descending with the keyset cursor, served by
  `record_changes_record_idx` per partition. The ID lists are capped
  (`history_scope_too_large` above 2,000 IDs; the UI then offers the
  club-wide view filtered to the record).
- **Global rows:** `history.read_global_changes(p_tables text[],
  p_record_ids uuid[], p_before_at timestamptz, p_before_id uuid, p_limit
  int)` (SECURITY DEFINER, owner-owned, `search_path` pinned) returns rows
  of `public.people` and `public.guardianships` only, for record IDs the
  caller may see (`app.can_see_person` for people; for guardianships,
  `app.can_see_person` of both parties), never `accounts`. Authorization
  in the API stays primary; the function is the database backstop. Granted
  to `clubadmin_app` only (and readable on a replica: it only reads).
- **Indexes** (new migration; built per partition, see Performance). One
  change on the history table only, to keep its write cost as today:
  `history.record_changes (club_id, changed_at DESC, id DESC)` replacing
  `record_changes_club_idx` (keyset needs the tie-breaker; same width, so
  no extra write cost). **No** actor or table indexes on the history:
  the club-wide filters by actor and kind of record require a date range of
  at most 90 days (`range_too_large`) and filter inside the club index's
  range (bounded work, see Performance). `audit.events` is small (a few
  events per action, not per column), so it gets `(club_id,
  actor_person_id, created_at DESC, id DESC)` and `(club_id, action,
  created_at DESC, id DESC)`; `events_resource_idx` gains `id DESC` (new
  index, old one dropped in a later migration: expand/contract).
- **Retention:** unchanged: history partitions after
  `HISTORY_RETENTION_MONTHS`, audit events after `AUDIT_RETENTION_MONTHS`
  (P1.8). The panels say "History is kept for 2 years" from the setting.

## API
Under `/api/v1`. Reads only, allowed for clubs in any access state.
Keyset pagination with the opaque cursor of `getRecordHistory` (`cursor`
= encoded `(changed_at, id)` of the last item; `PageQuerySchema` with this
cursor format documented as an exception to "last item's ID"); `limit`
default 50, at most 100. Problem codes: `history_scope_too_large`,
`invalid_cursor`, `range_too_large`, ….

| Method | Path | Permission | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| GET | `/people/:personId/history?clubId=&cursor=` | `person.history.read` | The person's panel in one club (scope above) plus their global rows; `clubId` required |
| GET | `/groups/:groupId/history?cursor=` | `group.history.read` | The group's panel |
| GET | `/activities/:activityId/history?cursor=` | `activity.history.read` | The activity's panel |
| GET | `/activity-series/:seriesId/history?cursor=` | `activity.history.read` | The series' panel (resource `activity` through `:seriesId`, as `activity.read`) |
| GET | `/events/:eventId/history?cursor=` | `event.history.read` | The event's panel (P1.6 events; decided 2026-11-01) |
| GET | `/clubs/:clubId/history?table=&actorId=&from=&to=&cursor=` | `club.history.read` | Club-wide changes; `table` from the policy's tables; without filters the newest first over the whole retention; with `table` or `actorId`, `from`/`to` are required and at most 90 days apart (`range_too_large`; the UI defaults to the last 30 days) |
| GET | `/clubs/:clubId/audit-events?category=&action=&actorId=&resourceType=&resourceId=&from=&to=&cursor=` | `club.audit.read` | The club's audit log; `category` maps to a list of actions (`members`, `roles`, `groups`, `activities`, `attendance`, `events`, `families`, `privacy` (consents, consent texts, board decisions and policies, purges, erasures), `exports`, `settings`, `access`); `from`/`to` at most 400 days apart |

No `/me/history` (decided 2026-11-05): the person's own history is in
"download my data".

Each history item: `id`, `changedAt`, `operation`, `table` (label key),
`recordId`, `resource` (`{ kind, id, label }`, the label resolved as
above, hidden people as `{ kind: "person", hidden: true }`), `actor`
(`ActorReference`), `onBehalfOf` (`ActorReference` or null), `changes`
(`[{ column, labelKey, kind: "value" | "changed" | "erased" | "redacted",
old?, new? }]`, values already formatted as the policy says: enum keys,
ISO dates, resolved references), `transactionId` (so the client groups one
action's rows), `automatic` (`{ job }` for system jobs: `attendance:retention`,
`privacy:retention`, …). Audit items: `id`, `createdAt`, `action`,
`actor`, `onBehalfOf`, `resource` (as above), `changedFields` (label keys),
never `ip_hash`.

`ActorReference` gains `{ kind: "erased" }` (a person with
`anonymized_at`) and `{ kind: "operator" }` (actor kind `operator`, name
not shown) and `{ kind: "system", job? }`; clients treat unknown kinds as
`system` (ADR 0029, enum grows; update the generated client and the
mobile app's parser).

## Permissions
New permissions, one policy each in `authorization.policy.ts`, allow and
deny tests, entries in the permission matrix (tenant isolation: a user of
club B gets 404 for club A's history and audit log):
- `person.history.read` (person, `?clubId`): club managers and registrars
  of the club. Not the person, guardians, leaders, owners, members of the
  same group, relatives, support access (support access may be added later
  with its own decision). Reading the history of a person is logged
  as a profile view with `surface = 'history'` in `profile_views` under the
  same rules as profile views (P1.12: always for protected people, every
  view in clubs that log all views). **Until P1.12** (decided
  2026-11-01): reading a protected person's history writes
  `person.protected.viewed` with `changed_fields = ['history']`, as a
  profile view does today; P1.12 moves both to `profile_views`.
- `group.history.read` (group): club managers and registrars. Not owners,
  leaders or members.
- `activity.history.read` (activity): club managers and registrars. Not
  those who manage it. Notes on answers are shown (managers and registrars
  may read them on the activity today; check `activity.responses.read`
  and narrow the column to `name_only` for anyone it does not allow);
  attendance marks are shown.
- `event.history.read` (event): club managers and registrars. Not the
  organisers. Answers to sign-up questions as the club managers see them;
  sensitive answers never.
- `club.history.read` (club): club managers, with MFA.
- `club.audit.read` (club): club managers, with MFA.
- Tests: Johan (registrar SVK) reads Elsa's, F2017's and the F2017 match's
  history but gets 403 for the audit log and the club-wide changes; Erik
  (head coach and owner of F2017) gets 403 for the match's, the group's and
  Elsa's history; Anna reads Elsa's history in SVK as manager (not because
  she is the parent: as a parent only she would get 403); Gunilla
  (relative) gets 403 for Alva's; Karin (IKN) gets 404 for SVK's; a manager
  without a second factor gets the MFA problem.

## UI
- **Web, History panel** (a shared server component in `packages/ui` or
  `apps/web/src/components/history-panel.tsx`): a collapsible "History"
  section on the person (club context), group, activity, series, event and
  club pages, shown only when the viewer holds the permission (from
  `GET /me/clubs` and the page's data). **Closed by default and loaded only
  when opened** (a link or `<details>` with its own request), so the
  detail pages' own budgets do not change. Entries newest first, grouped by
  transaction ("Anna Lindqvist · 12 March 14:05 · moved Elsa to F2017",
  expandable to the column changes), old → new values, "Other fields
  changed" for `name_only` columns, "Erased" for scrubbed values, "Hidden" for redacted ones,
  "Automatic (attendance retention)" for jobs, "Deleted user" for erased
  actors, "Protected member" for hidden actors, "Anna for Elsa" for
  on-behalf-of. "Show older" loads the next page (cursor); works without
  JavaScript (a link with the cursor). Times in the club's time zone.
- **Web, club audit log** (`/clubs/[clubId]/audit`, club managers): a
  table of events with filters (category, person acting through a person
  picker limited to people the viewer may see, resource type, date range),
  translated action sentences ("Sara Ahmed exported the member register
  (name, member number, groups)"), links to the resource's page, keyset
  "Show older". Empty state "No events in this period".
- **Web, club-wide changes** (`/clubs/[clubId]/history`, club managers):
  the same entry layout as the panel, with filters by kind of record, person
  acting and period (default the last 30 days once a filter is chosen, at
  most 90); each entry links to the record's page.
- No history on `/me/profile` (decided 2026-11-05); `/me/privacy` says that
  "download my data" includes the history of the person's own data.
- Loading skeletons, empty ("No changes recorded yet"), the retention note
  ("History is kept for 2 years; audit events for 2 years"), errors.
- **Mobile:** out of scope (web only).

## Privacy and security
- History and audit are themselves personal data about actors and
  subjects; reading them is limited to those who look after the record
  (ADR 0017 amended: "anyone who can view a record can see who changed it"
  becomes "those who hold the record's history permission"). Update
  ADR 0017 and `security.md`.
- Values follow field visibility on the **current** record: a phone number
  no longer shared with the club is hidden in all its past values for club
  viewers; notes follow their own rules; redacted and erased values never
  show. Column names of fields a viewer may not see at all (unshared
  `people` fields for club viewers) are left out, not shown as "changed".
- Rows whose subject is a person the viewer may not see (a protected
  member added to a group, seen by someone who may not see them) are left
  out entirely, not counted; references to hidden people inside values
  show "Hidden person".
- The audit log shows action names and field names only (it has no
  values), never `ip_hash` or `request_id`.
- Reading history and audit is not itself audited, except the profile
  view log above (and rate limited per account, P0.10).
- Nothing new is written to history by reads.
- No exports of the audit log or history in this package (see Open
  decisions).

## Protected identity
- Every actor, on-behalf-of person, subject and person reference goes
  through `PersonPresenter` (`present`/`presentActors`) with the viewer's
  authorization, in one call per page (no N+1).
- Hidden actors are `{ kind: "protected" }` ("Protected member"); hidden
  subjects are left out; protected people the viewer may see carry the
  crossed-camera icon in history as everywhere.
- A registrar of IKN who may not see Samira sees in the F2015 training's
  history that Nadia's answer changed, with Samira as "Protected member".
- Tests: the matrix scenario's protected member and protected guardian in
  panel, club-wide and audit responses for each viewer kind.

## Isolation from the rest of the system
History is read rarely; reading it must never slow down the everyday paths
(decided 2026-11-05).
- **Own connection pool:** the `audit` module reads through a separate
  `Database` (`HISTORY_DATABASE_URL`, default the same database as
  `DATABASE_URL`) with at most 2 connections (`HISTORY_POOL_MAX`) and a
  statement timeout (`HISTORY_STATEMENT_TIMEOUT_MS`, default 3000; a
  timeout is the problem `history_timeout`, "Narrow the period"). A burst
  of history reads queues in its own pool and never takes connections from
  the main pool. List the variables in `.env.example` and
  `docs/operations` (self-hosting).
- **Optional read replica:** `HISTORY_DATABASE_URL` may point at a
  streaming replica (same roles, RLS and functions, so nothing else
  changes). The read is one read-only transaction on that database; the
  access log write for protected people (`person.protected.viewed`) is a
  second, short transaction on the primary after it (sequential, never
  nested: rule 7). Note in the panel's help that a replica can lag a few
  seconds.
- **Rate limits** per account on every history and audit endpoint (P0.10;
  e.g. 30 per minute), on top of the pool limit.
- **Capture unchanged:** the record-history trigger stays synchronous in
  the writing transaction (ADR 0017: the history is complete by
  construction). Its write cost does not grow: the replaced club index has
  the same number of indexes and nearly the same width.
- **UI:** panels load only when opened (see UI); detail pages make no
  history call otherwise.

## Locale and country packs
Few keys, no per-language logic (decided 2026-11-05); about 100–150 new
keys in all:
- **Audit sentences are composed**, not one sentence per action: an action
  is `<resource>.<verb>` and the sentence is one ICU pattern per verb
  (`audit.verbs.created`: "{actor} created {resource}") with the resource
  from a resource label (`audit.resources.membership`: "a membership"),
  about 30 verbs and 25 resources instead of a sentence per each of the
  ~120 actions. An action may override with its own sentence key only when
  the composed one reads wrongly (a handful, e.g. `members.exported`).
- **Reuse existing labels:** column labels are the existing form labels
  (`people.fields.*`, `groups.form.*`, …) and enum values the existing enum
  messages; the policy names the key, the test checks it exists.
- **Only `value` columns get labels**; `name_only` columns are not named
  ("Other fields changed").
- Categories, job names (a few) and the `ActorReference` kinds.
- `sv` and `en` complete; `nb` falls back to English for missing keys
  (as the catalogue loader already does). A unit test fails when a
  registered audit action cannot be rendered (its verb and resource, or
  its override) or a `value` column's key is missing in sv or en.
- No country-specific rules; dates and times in the club's locale and time
  zone.

## Performance
History is not tuned beyond these budgets (it is read rarely); the point
is bounded cost, not speed.
- History panel, first page (50 entries) < 300 ms p95 for a person with
  five years of changes, a group of 25 members over five seasons and an
  activity with 25 answers and marks; "Show older" pages the same.
- Club-wide changes and the audit log, first page < 500 ms p95 without
  filters and with each filter (within the 90-day range), on Storklubben
  with two years of history (about 5 million rows) and audit events (about
  500,000); a date range prunes partitions.
- Request budgets (ADR 0040, CLAUDE.md rule 7), **in CI** on the normal
  test data: every new endpoint gets a case in
  `apps/api/src/request-budgets.integration.test.ts` (SQL statements and
  pages read, not growing with the data); at most 5 statements (scope IDs,
  history page of club rows, the global function, presenter, reference
  names) in one transaction per request. For the filtered club-wide view
  the page bound is the 90-day range, not the data.
- **Volume tests outside CI** (decided 2026-11-05): the Storklubben
  history volume is generated by a separate step
  (`pnpm db:seed --history-volume`, on top of the load scenario), and the
  timing and query-plan tests (`*.volume.test.ts`, run with
  `pnpm --filter @clubadmin/api test:volume`) run in the Nightly workflow
  and by hand (`workflow_dispatch`), never in the CI workflow. They check:
  no sequential scans on `history.record_changes` partitions or
  `audit.events`; index scans on the club index; `MergeAppend` over
  partitions for the newest-first pages; the filtered view stays inside
  the 90-day partitions; and the write cost of the history trigger after
  the index change (the `@clubadmin/db` bench) is no worse than before.
- Building indexes on the partitioned table: `CREATE INDEX ... ON ONLY
  history.record_changes`, then one `CREATE INDEX` per existing partition
  and `ALTER INDEX ... ATTACH PARTITION` (new partitions inherit them).
  Only the current month's partition takes writes, so a short write block
  while its index builds is accepted and measured on the load scenario
  (note it in the PR, rule 13). `history.maintain_partitions` keeps working
  unchanged.

  effect as `ON ONLY` plus attach without `CONCURRENTLY`), the audit filter
  indexes, `events_resource_keyset_idx` (the old resource index dropped in

## Out of scope
- Mobile history views.
- Exports of the audit log or history (CSV); streaming audit events to
  separate storage (`security.md`, "Audit"; an issue).
- Operator-facing audit views (operator actions, global tables' history
  for platform support).
- Restoring or reverting a record from its history.
- The profile view log and its pages (P1.12).
- History of answers and attendance on the person's page (the activity
  panels show them; a member's own attendance history is a later package).

## Decided (2026-11-05; formerly open)
1. **Who may read history.** Decided: club managers and registrars only
   (no group owners, leaders, organisers, the person or their guardians;
   no `/me/history`; the person's own history is in "download my data"),
   narrowing ADR 0017's "anyone who can view a record"; amend ADR 0017.
2. **Values of fields no longer shared.** As recommended: follow the current
   record: once a person stops sharing a field with the club, its past
   values and even the fact that it changed are hidden from club viewers.
3. **Audit log and club-wide history for registrars.** As recommended: club
   managers only (with MFA); registrars get the person, group, activity,
   series and event panels.
4. **Exporting the audit log.** As recommended: not in this package; open an
   issue for a CSV export with the member-export pattern (MFA, recent
   login, worker job, 24-hour file) when a club or authority asks for it.
5. **Panel scope for a person.** As recommended: the person's own rows in the
   club and their global rows, without answers and attendance; the scope
   capped at 2,000 record IDs with a fallback to the filtered club-wide
   view, rather than adding a `subject_person_id` column to the history.
6. **Index build on the partitioned history table.** As recommended: per
   partition inside the migration (a brief write block on the current
   month's partition), rather than adding non-transactional migrations
   with `CREATE INDEX CONCURRENTLY` to the runner now. Only the one club
   index is rebuilt.
7. **Operators in history.** As recommended: shown as "Platform operator"
   without a name, as `{ kind: "operator" }`; the support grant itself is
   in the club's history with the operator's name for managers.
8. **Mobile.** As recommended: no history in the app in this package; the
   web pages are linked from the app's person and activity screens for
   those who hold the permission.
9. **Isolation and volume** (new): own pool with a statement timeout,
   optional read replica, one history index, filtered club-wide view
   limited to 90 days, volume tests in Nightly and by hand, synchronous
   capture kept.
10. **Message keys** (new): composed audit sentences, reused labels, only
    `value` columns labelled, nb falling back to en.
