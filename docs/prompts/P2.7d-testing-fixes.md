# P2.7d — Fixes from testing: contact details, own profile, series per day, rides in one menu, Home as the schedule

To do after P2.7c and before P2.8. Findings from the maintainer's testing
on 2026-11-20.

## Context
Read first: `CLAUDE.md`, ADR 0055 (updates in place), ADR 0048 (car pools,
amended in P2.7c), ADR 0034 (series), ADR 0041 (privacy), the P1.8a
decisions on shared fields, `docs/domain-model.md` ("Field visibility",
`accounts`: "Changing email requires verifying the new address").

What exists already (checked against the code on 2026-11-20):
- The chat room (`apps/web/src/components/chat-room.tsx`) has a fixed
  height `calc(100dvh - 12rem)` since P2.7c; with the frame, breadcrumbs
  and title above it the page is taller than the window, so the field to
  write in is below the fold and an empty chat looks like it has no input.
  `open-direct-chat` and the direct chat itself work (checked as Anna →
  Erik).
- Person profiles: `profileAccess` and `PersonProfilePresenter`
  (`apps/api/src/modules/authorization/presenters/person-profile.presenter.ts`):
  phone, address and gender reach a club **only if the person shared them**
  (`club_profiles.shared_fields`, P1.8a), even for holders of
  `club.people.details` (club manager, registrar). In the demo only Anna,
  Erik, Johan and Ahmed share anything. People have **no email column**:
  the email is the account's login (`accounts.email`), shown nowhere to
  the club. Nothing lets the registrar change a member's contact details.
- Own profile: `/me/profile` only completes the name and birth date after
  sign-up; there is no endpoint or page for changing one's own phone,
  address or email.
- Series: weekly rules have `slots` (weekday, start time, length); the
  place (`locationId`/`locationText`) is one for the whole series.
- The series page (`/activity-series/:id`) lists its sessions in an
  `Agenda` without actions; cancelling or deleting a session is on the
  session's own page.
- Rides (P2.7c): "Mitt schema" shows the "Kommer" menu (cars there) and,
  under it, "Åk med dit/hem" buttons (`RideButtons`), so a seat can be
  booked in two places; the menu books the way there only. The menu shows
  only while the person has not answered "Kommer"; once they have, only
  the buttons below remain.
- Home (`/`) lists "Kommande" without the answer buttons; "Mitt schema"
  (`/schedule`) has them, and the navigation has both.

## Findings and what to do

1. **The chat shows no field to write in.** The chat room's height must
   fit the window with everything above it: the room grows with its
   messages up to a maximum (`max-height`, not a fixed `height`), the
   messages scroll inside, and the input is always in view on load
   (desktop and phone). Playwright: an empty direct chat opened from a
   person's "Skicka meddelande" shows the input in the viewport; the
   P2.7c test with 25 messages still passes.

2. **The registrar sees and changes members' contact details.**
   *Decision to confirm (ADR 0057, amends P1.8a/ADR 0041):*
   - Holders of `club.people.details` (club manager, registrar:
     "Medlemsansvarig") see a member's phone and address **whether or not
     the person shared them**: the club holds them to run the membership
     (legal basis: the membership agreement). Sharing (`shared_fields`)
     keeps deciding what *other members and leaders* see.
   - They also see the member's **email** (the account's, or for a child
     without login their guardians' through the guardian list), read-only.
   - They **change** a member's phone and address (new permission
     `club.people.contact.edit`, template roles `club_manager`,
     `registrar`; MFA; audit `person.contact.updated` with field names
     only; history). They do **not** change a login email (that would
     hand over the account): the person changes it (finding 3); for a
     member without a login there is no email to change.
   - Protected identity: no address is stored for a protected person
     (existing check constraint); the registrar sees protected members'
     contact details only with `club.people.protected`.
   - Demo data: most adult members get a phone and an address; a few have
     shared nothing, so the difference to a leader's view shows.

3. **Changing one's own contact details, email included.** "Min profil"
   (`/me/profile`, linked from the user menu "Mitt konto") edits name,
   phone, address (and birth date while it is not confirmed by the club
   register, per the existing rules) and the email:
   - Phone and address: saved at once (ADR 0055), audit and history.
   - Email: the new address gets a verification code or link from the
     identity provider (Zitadel's email change with verification); the
     login email changes only once verified, the old address is told
     (`docs/domain-model.md`). Until then the page shows "väntar på
     bekräftelse". With DEV_AUTH the verification goes to Mailpit.
   - Guardians change their children's phone and address the same way
     (act_on_behalf).
   - Playwright: Anna changes her phone; changes her email and confirms it
     through Mailpit; the old address gets the notice.

4. **A series' place per weekday.** A weekly series' slots each have their
   own place (`slots[].locationId` / `locationText`, the series' place as
   the default): F2017 trains in one hall on Tuesdays and another on
   Thursdays. The series form shows the place per slot; changing it
   follows ADR 0034's rules (this and following sessions, detached ones
   kept). Migration: the slots' JSON gets the optional fields (no data
   change). Playwright: Erik sets Tuesday's and Thursday's halls apart and
   the sessions show them.

5. **Cancel or delete a single session from the series.** The series
   page's list of sessions has, per session, "Ställ in" (with an optional
   reason, as on the session's page) and "Ta bort" (a session without
   answers; the date is skipped from then on, as today), with a
   confirmation, in place. Playwright: cancel one session and delete
   another from the series page.

6. **One menu for a ride, there and home.** Booking a seat happens in one
   menu only: "Kommer ▾" (before answering) or "Åk med ▾" (after answering
   "Kommer"), listing the cars there with free seats (driver, pick-up
   place and time). Choosing a car books the way there **and** the way
   home in the same car (`alsoReturn`) when it drives home and has a
   seat; otherwise there only, and the menu says so. A booked seat shows
   whose car it is, with "Avboka". The separate "Åk med dit/hem" buttons
   under the answer go; the activity's car pool keeps its per-car
   buttons for booking one direction or the other car home.

7. **A car with a free seat is not offered.** Reproduce: Erik's car with
   three seats, a child and an adult booked, one seat free; Alva (Sara's
   daughter) gets no menu to ride with him. Find the cause (likely: Alva
   had already answered "Kommer", and the P2.7c menu only shows before
   answering; finding 6 covers that) and add the case to the tests
   (integration: the summary lists the car for Alva; Playwright as Sara).

8. **Planned rides in the demo.** Several coming trainings and matches
   (SVK F2017, Herr senior, IKN P2012) have cars there and home with some
   seats booked, including the finding 7 case, so the schedule, the menu
   and "Mina samåkningar" can be tried at once. Update `seed-data.md`.

9. **Home is the schedule.** Home's "Kommande" shows exactly what "Mitt
   schema" shows (the same component: per person the answer, the "Kommer"
   menu and the seats, the person filter, the weeks), and "Mitt schema"
   leaves the navigation (`/schedule` redirects to `/`, so links and
   bookmarks still work; the e2e tests and docs that open `/schedule` use
   `/`). Home keeps what it has besides (events open for sign-up, tasks).
   Request budget unchanged (the same calls).
