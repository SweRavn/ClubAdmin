- **Mobile:** Expo SDK 57, Expo Router, deep links with UUIDs only, a

  `apps/mobile/package.json`. `GET /meta/client` returns
  `SERVER_FEATURES` (`packages/shared/src/http/client.ts`).
- **No WebSocket gateway** yet (architecture.md describes it as the
  target); **no** notifications module, inbox, device tokens or
  unsubscribe links.
- **P1.6** (events with sign-up, `P1.6-events-and-sign-up.md`) publishes
  `event.changed`, `event.registration.changed` and `event.offer.made`;

## Goal
A parent hears in time when Elsa's training is cancelled or moved, is
reminded the day before the deadline if nobody has answered for her, and
learns that Oskar was called up to Saturday's match, on the phone as a push
notification, in an inbox on the web and in the app, and by email where
that is what they prefer. A coach hears when a player says "not coming"
after the deadline. Everyone chooses per category and channel what they
get, sets quiet hours for push, and can stop an email category with one
click from the email itself. No notification ever names a protected person
to someone who may not see them, and no push or lock screen shows anyone's
name. A self-hosted installation without push credentials still has email
and the inbox.

## Users and roles involved
Every person with an account: members, full guardians, linked people
(relatives, step and foster parents, contact persons) with
`receive_messages`, young people with their own login. Group leaders
(`head_coach`, `assistant_coach`, `team_manager`, `other_leader`), group
owners and club managers as recipients of leader notices. Club managers as
recipients of the existing administrative emails. People without an
account (most children, Nils, Bo) receive nothing; their guardians do.

## User stories
- As a guardian, I want a push notification when my child's activity in
  the coming two weeks is cancelled, restored or moved, so that we do not
  drive to an empty hall.
- As a guardian, I want a reminder before the answer deadline when nobody
  has answered for my child, and none when we already answered.
- As a member or guardian, I want to know when I or my child was called up
  to a match, or is no longer called up.
- As a coach, I want to know when a player says "not coming" after the
  deadline or on the day, without one notice per click when a parent
  changes their mind.
- As an applicant, I want the decision on my join request in the inbox and
  by email, as today.
- As any user, I want an inbox on the web and in the app with what I was
  notified about, unread ones marked, so that I can catch up.
- As any user, I want to choose per category whether I get email and push,
  and quiet hours for push, so that the club does not wake me.
- As any user, I want to stop a category of emails with one click from the
  email, without logging in.
- As a user with several phones, I want each logged-in app to get push, and
  a phone I logged out of to get nothing.
- As a self-hosting club, I want everything except push to work without
  Expo, Apple or Google accounts.

## Notification catalogue
Types are defined once in `packages/shared/src/notifications.ts`
(`NOTIFICATION_TYPES`: type → category, Zod schema of its `params`, default
channels, whether it is mandatory, link target). The catalogue may grow
(ADR 0029: clients ignore unknown types and show a generic entry).

| Type | Category | From | Recipients | Default channels |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `activity.cancelled`, `activity.restored` | `schedule` | `activity.changed` | Participants on the roster (with a call-up: those called up) and their notifiable guardians; only if the activity starts within 14 days and has not started | in-app, push, email when no app |
| `activity.moved` | `schedule` | `activity.changed` `updated` where `sequence` grew | As above; debounced 10 minutes per activity, one notice per `sequence` | in-app, push |
| `activity.added` | `schedule` | `activity.changed` `created` for a single activity (not series sessions) starting within 14 days | Members of its groups and notifiable guardians | in-app, push |
| `series.changed` | `schedule` | `activity_series.changed` `updated`, `split` | Members of the series' groups and notifiable guardians; one per series change, debounced 10 minutes | in-app, push |
| `callup.added`, `callup.removed` | `schedule` | `activity.invitees.changed` | The people added or removed and their notifiable guardians | added: in-app, push, email when no app; removed: in-app, push |
| `response.reminder` | `responses` | Task `notifications:response_reminders` | Roster participants of `requested` activities with no answer, `REMINDER_HOURS` (24) before `respond_by` (or before the start when unset), via those who may answer (themselves, guardians with `sign_up`) | in-app, push, email when no app |
| `response.late_decline` | `leader` | `activity.response.changed` `set` to `not_attending` after `respond_by` or within 24 hours of the start; debounced 5 minutes per activity and person, dropped if the answer changed back | Those holding `activity.responses.read` on the activity | in-app, push |
| `join_request.decided`, `club_creation.decided` | `membership` | `join_request.decided`, `club_creation_request.decided` (moved from `mail`) | The requester | in-app, email, push |
| `club.managers.changed` | `club_admin` | `club.manager.changed` (moved) | Managers and the person concerned | in-app, email (mandatory) |
| `members.exported` | `club_admin` | `members.exported` (moved) | Club managers | in-app, email (mandatory) |
| `members.import.finished` | `club_admin` | `members.import.finished` (moved) | Club managers | in-app, email |
| `family.*` (accepted, own-login age, adult, adult ended) | `family` | the `guardianship.*` events (moved) | As today | in-app, email |
| `account.email.changed` | `account` | `account.email.changed` | The account (in-app) and the **old** address (email) | in-app, email (mandatory) |

Not in the catalogue: emails to an address without an account
(`invitation.created`, `guardianship.invited`, `account.invited`) keep the
direct email path of `mail.ts`: no inbox, no preferences, no unsubscribe
link (they are the invitation itself). `activity.changed` `deleted` notifies nobody (only
activities without answers can be deleted, and the row is gone);
`attendance.recorded` notifies nobody. Categories `events` (P1.6) and
`waitlist` (P1.10) are reserved in the enum with no types yet.

**Notifiable guardians** of a ward for a type: full guardians, and linked
people holding `receive_messages` (for `schedule` and `responses` also
`view_schedule`, since the notice shows the schedule); only while the
guardianship is open and the rights hold at send time. For
`response.reminder` only those who may answer (`sign_up`). A young person
with their own login gets their own notice too. One notification per
recipient and event even when several wards are affected (Anna for Elsa
and Oskar at a joint session); `about_person_ids` lists the wards.

## Data model
New tables are **global, recipient-owned** tables (ADR 0023): the inbox is
the account's across clubs, so the policies use `app.current_person_id()`
instead of the tenant setting; `club_id` is a nullable column for display,
retention and erasure. Writes come only from the worker (`withSystem`).
Every table gets `updated_at` and record history or an entry in
`packages/db/src/history-exclusions.ts` with the reason. Person references
go in `notifications.references.ts` so merging duplicates covers them.

- `notifications`: `recipient_person_id`, `club_id` (nullable), `category`,
  `type`, `subject_type` (`activity`, `series`, `join_request`,
  `member_export`, `member_import`, `guardianship`, `account`, …) and
  `subject_id`, `about_person_ids uuid[]` (wards concerned; max 10),
  `params jsonb` (IDs, enum values, counts and times only, validated by the
  type's schema; **never names, notes or email addresses**),
  `dedupe_key` (e.g. `activity:<id>:seq:<n>`, `reminder:<activity>:<person>`,
  `event:<idempotencyKey>`), `read_at`. Unique `(recipient_person_id,
  dedupe_key)` makes fan-out idempotent. Indexes: `(recipient_person_id, id
  DESC)` for the inbox (keyset), partial `(recipient_person_id) WHERE
  read_at IS NULL` for the unread count, `(created_at)` for retention, GIN
  on `about_person_ids` for merge and erasure. RLS: `own_read`, and
  `own_update` limited to `read_at` (column grant). History with
  `skipInsert` (ADR 0017). Text is **rendered when read** through the
  presenter, so a later protection change takes effect in the inbox.
- `notification_deliveries`: `notification_id`, `channel` (`email`,
  `push`), `push_device_id` (nullable), `status` (`queued`, `held`, `sent`,
  `failed`, `skipped`; may grow), `skip_reason` (`preference`, `no_address`,
  `no_device`, `no_provider`, `quiet_obsolete`, `budget`), `attempts`,
  `send_after`, `sent_at`, `provider_ref` (Expo ticket ID), `error_code`
  (a code, never provider text with addresses). Indexes: partial
  `(send_after) WHERE status IN ('queued','held')`, `(notification_id)`.
  No API access (system only). History exclusion (delivery log, high
  volume, no personal values).
- `push_devices`: `account_id`, `token_hash` (sha256, unique) and `token`
  (field-encrypted), `provider` (`expo`), `platform` (`ios`, `android`),
  `app_variant`, `session_ref` (the login session the sessions list
  shows, so ending that session removes the device), `last_seen_at`,
  `disabled_at`. RLS `own_read`/`own_write` by account. History with the
  token redacted. Index `(account_id)`.
- `accounts.notification_settings` (exists): a Zod schema
  `NotificationSettingsSchema` in `packages/shared`: per category `{ email:
  "always" | "no_app" | "never", push: boolean }`, `quietHours: { from:
  "HH:mm", to: "HH:mm" } | null` (recipient's time zone; default 21:00–07:00),
  `pushPreview: boolean` (default true: see "Privacy"). Missing keys mean
  the catalogue's defaults, so `{}` stays valid and defaults can change.
  Mandatory types ignore the settings for their mandatory channel.
- **Index on `activities`** for the reminder task: partial `(respond_by)
  WHERE response_mode = 'requested' AND status = 'scheduled'` (justify in
  the PR with the load-scenario plan).
- **Retention** (`notifications:retention`, daily): notifications 90 days
  after creation, deliveries 30 days, devices 90 days after `last_seen_at`
  or at once on a provider's "not registered". Erasure: extend
  `privacy.erase_person` to delete the person's notifications, deliveries
  and devices and remove them from `about_person_ids`. Record in
  `docs/gdpr.md`.

ADR 0038 records: the catalogue and categories, recipient-owned global
tables, rendering at read time, the delivery pipeline and its idempotency,
debouncing with Graphile `job_key`, push provider and payload rules, quiet
hours, unsubscribe tokens, and that the existing email templates keep their
`email.*` keys (amending the "moves the templates" consequence of ADR 0032).

## Delivery pipeline
1. **Fan-out (outbox handler `notifications`)** replaces the account-bound
   cases of `mail`: it reads the subject, resolves recipients in one
   statement per event (no N+1), inserts notifications (`ON CONFLICT
   (recipient_person_id, dedupe_key) DO NOTHING`) and their deliveries
   according to settings, devices and capabilities, and enqueues delivery
   jobs, all in the handler's transaction. Debounced types enqueue a
   `notifications:fanout` job with `job_key` and `run_at` instead, which
   re-reads the current state when it runs.
2. **Delivery jobs** `notifications:send_email` (one per delivery) and
   `notifications:send_push` (batches of up to 100 deliveries, Expo's limit)
   mark each delivery `sent` or `failed` after the provider call; a retry
   skips deliveries already `sent`. The email Message-ID stays derived from
   the dedupe key, so a resend after a crash is recognisable. Five attempts
   with backoff, then `failed` (an error log with IDs only).
3. **Quiet hours** set push deliveries to `held` with `send_after` at the
   end of the recipient's quiet hours; when released, a delivery whose
   activity has started or whose notification was read is `skipped`
   (`quiet_obsolete`). Email is not held.
4. **Push receipts:** `notifications:push_receipts` (every 15 minutes)
   reads Expo receipts; `DeviceNotRegistered` disables the device.
5. **Volume budget:** at most `NOTIFY_PUSH_PER_PERSON_HOUR` (10) pushes and
   `NOTIFY_EMAIL_PER_PERSON_DAY` (20) non-mandatory emails per recipient;
   beyond that deliveries are `skipped` (`budget`), the inbox still has
   them. `MAIL_SEND_PER_SECOND` throttles the SMTP/provider rate.

Recipient resolution for leaders mirrors the policies of
`activity.responses.read`; a consistency test runs both over the matrix
scenario and fails if they differ.

## API
Under `/api/v1`. All endpoints are the caller's own data (`{ authenticated:
true }`), always the logged-in account, also with `X-On-Behalf-Of` (like
`PATCH /me/preferences`); another account's IDs give `404`. Problem codes:
`notification_not_found`, `push_not_available`, `push_token_invalid`,
`unsubscribe_token_invalid`, ….

| Method | Path | Access | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| GET | `/me/notifications?cursor=&unread=&category=` | authenticated | Keyset by ID, newest first, 30 per page; each with `type`, `category`, `club` (id, name), `subject` and link, `about` (PersonPresenter; hidden people left out), rendered `title` and `body` in the caller's language, `readAt` |
| GET | `/me/notifications/unread-count` | authenticated | `{ count }` (capped at 99+), cheap enough to poll; also returned in `GET /me` as `unreadNotifications` (new optional field) |
| POST | `/me/notifications/read` | authenticated | `{ ids: uuid[] }` (max 100) or `{ before: uuid }` (all up to that one); idempotent |
| GET | `/me/notification-settings` | authenticated | The effective settings (defaults filled in) and the catalogue's categories with which channels exist on this server |
| PUT | `/me/notification-settings` | authenticated | Whole settings object; mandatory channels cannot be turned off (`notification_mandatory`); audited |
| GET | `/me/push-devices` | authenticated | Platform, variant, last seen; never the token |
| PUT | `/me/push-devices` | authenticated | Register or refresh `{ token, platform, appVariant }` for the current session (upsert by token hash; a token registered to another account moves to this one); `push_not_available` when the server has no push provider |
| DELETE | `/me/push-devices/:id` | authenticated | Also called by the app on logout; ending a session in the sessions list removes its devices |
| POST | `/notifications/unsubscribe` | public | RFC 8058 one-click (`List-Unsubscribe-Post`) and the web page's button: `{ token }`; sets that category's email to `never`; rate-limited per IP |
| GET | `/meta/client` (changed) | public | `features` gains `notifications.inbox` and, only with a push provider, `notifications.push` |

Unsubscribe tokens are `HMAC-SHA256(NOTIFICATION_TOKEN_KEY, "unsubscribe:"
+ accountId + ":" + category)`, never stored or put in payloads (as ADR
0032 does for invitations); rotating the key invalidates old links.

## Permissions
No new club permissions: every endpoint is own data, declared
`{ authenticated: true }`, with the account taken from the principal. Tests
instead of policy tests:
- Own-data isolation: another account's notification and device IDs give
  `404` (in `/read`, `DELETE`); the inbox never returns another account's
  rows (RLS test with two accounts in one club and in two clubs).
- With `X-On-Behalf-Of` a ward's notifications are never shown; the inbox
  is the guardian's.
- The unsubscribe endpoint accepts only valid tokens and changes only the
  token's account and category.
- Recipient resolution: tests per type that a relative with only
  `view_schedule` gets no `schedule` notice, a contact person with
  `receive_messages` + `view_schedule` does, Bo (`receive_messages` only)
  gets none, a revoked right takes effect for the next notice, a club B
  manager never receives club A notices, and leaders match
  `activity.responses.read` (consistency test above).
- Add the new endpoints to the permission matrix as `authenticated` entries
  so the "every endpoint declared" check covers them.

## UI
- **Web header:** a bell with the unread count (refreshed on navigation and
  every 60 seconds while the tab is visible; no WebSocket yet) linking to
  the inbox.
- **Web inbox** (`/me/notifications`): grouped by day, unread marked, club
  name, the link to the activity, request or page; "Mark all as read";
  filter by category; empty state ("Nothing new"); works without
  JavaScript (server actions).
- **Web settings** (`/me/settings/notifications`): a table of categories ×
  email (always / only without the app / never) and push (on/off, shown
  only when the server has push), mandatory ones shown locked with the
  reason, quiet hours, "show details on the lock screen", and the list of
  phones with push and "Remove".
- **Web unsubscribe page** (`/unsubscribe?token=`): states the category
  and has one button (no automatic change on GET, as mail scanners follow
  links); afterwards a link to the settings.
- **Mobile:** `expo-notifications` (native module: a store release, ADR
  0028); ask for permission after the first "My schedule" load with an
  explanation screen, never at launch; register the Expo push token with
  `PUT /me/push-devices` after login and on token change, delete it on
  logout and account switch before the session is cleared; Android
  notification channels per category with localised names; tapping a
  notification opens its deep link (UUIDs only) after unlock and marks it
  read; an inbox screen and a bell on "My schedule"; iOS badge from the
  unread count; notification settings in the settings screen (same
  options as the web). Builds without an Expo project ID (self-built apps,
  development) show "Push is not available" instead of failing.
- Loading, empty and error states; translations in sv, en, nb; times in
  the club's time zone with the recipient's own added when it differs.

## Email
- Plain text as today, one template per type with subject and body in the
  `notify.<type>` namespace (existing types keep their `email.*` keys),
  rendered in the recipient's language (account locale, the club's
  `default_locale`, `en`), the footer naming the club or ClubAdmin.
- Every non-mandatory email carries `List-Unsubscribe` (an HTTPS URL to
  the API endpoint below; no `mailto:`) and
  `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, plus a footer line
  "Change which emails you get" with the unsubscribe page and settings.
  Mandatory ones say why they cannot be turned off.
- `account.email.changed` goes to the previous address, read by the
  handler from the record history of `accounts` for that change (no payload
  may carry an address); the new address gets nothing (the user just
  verified it).
- Bounce and complaint handling are out of scope (provider webhooks).

## Privacy and security
- Personal data: who was notified about what (recipients, wards concerned),
  device tokens, preferences. Legal basis: the membership (running
  activities) and the account's own settings; record in `domain-model.md`
  and `gdpr.md`, with retention.
- **Push payloads:** the visible title and body never contain a person's
  name, a note, an email address or a health detail: they name the club or
  group, the kind of notice and the activity's title and time ("F2017:
  training Tue 17:30 cancelled"). With `pushPreview` off, the text is
  generic ("New notification from ClubAdmin"). The data payload carries
  only the notification ID and the deep link. Payloads pass through Expo,
  Apple and Google: list Expo as a sub-processor for the hosted service
  (`hosting-and-billing.md`), note the transfer basis.
- Device tokens are secrets (they allow sending to a phone): encrypted at
  rest, hashed for lookup, never logged, never returned by the API.
- Logs and job payloads: notification, delivery and device IDs only; never
  rendered text, addresses or tokens (CLAUDE.md rule 5). Provider errors
  are mapped to codes before they are stored or logged.
- Audit events in the same transaction: `account.notification_settings.updated`
  (changed categories, no values), `push_device.registered`,
  `push_device.removed` (with reason: logout, session ended, provider,
  retention), `notifications.unsubscribed` (category). Marking as read is
  not audited (record history covers it).
- Children: notices about a child go to adults with the rights above;
  a child with their own login sees only notices about themselves. No
  notices to linked people whose rights were removed, from the moment of
  removal.
- `NOTIFICATION_TOKEN_KEY` is required when `SMTP_URL` is set (config
  validation); `.env.example` gets dummy values for it, `PUSH_PROVIDER`
  (`expo`, `fake`, unset), `EXPO_ACCESS_TOKEN` and the budgets.

## Protected identity
- The inbox renders `about` and any person named in the text through
  `PersonPresenter` for the caller at read time: hidden people are left
  out or shown as "a member" / "a guardian".
- Email: the recipient's own wards and the recipient themselves by name;
  anyone else only if the worker presenter (`mail-names.ts`, extended) says
  the recipient may see them; for `response.late_decline` a protected
  member is "a member (protected identity)" in email and named with the
  crossed-camera icon in the leader's inbox (leaders may see them, ADR
  0022).
- Push: no names at all (above), so nothing to check.
- A protected person's notifications to themselves are unchanged;
  notifications never reveal that a hidden person is on a roster (counts
  only, as in P1.5).
- Tests: Samira answering "not coming" late for Nadia gives Erik a notice
  that names Nadia with the icon in the inbox and does not name her in
  email or push; other F2015 parents get nothing about it.

## Locale and country packs
- All titles, bodies, email subjects and footers, category and channel
  names, settings texts and Android channel names in sv, en and nb (nb
  complete for the `notify` namespace, not only falling back).
- Times formatted for the recipient's locale in the club's time zone,
  relative where short ("tomorrow 17:30").
- Quiet-hour defaults are not country-specific; no country-pack changes.

## Billing
Notifications are not an entitlement: they work the same with
`BILLING_ENABLED=false`. A club whose access state blocks writes (ADR
0031) still gets mandatory and family notices; activity notices follow
from activity changes, which are already blocked.

## Performance
- `GET /me/notifications` and `/unread-count` < 50 ms p95 (keyset on
  `(recipient_person_id, id)`; the count from the partial index), measured
  on the load scenario.
- Fan-out of one cancellation to a group of 30 members with guardians
  (about 80 recipients) < 200 ms in the handler (one statement for
  recipients, one insert per table); a club-wide series change (300
  recipients) < 1 s.
- `notifications:response_reminders` runs every 15 minutes; one run on
  the load scenario < 1 s (the partial index on `activities.respond_by`,
  answers through `activity_responses (club_id, activity_id)`).
- Push sends in batches of 100; 1,000 pushes leave within one minute of
  the event (with the fake provider in CI).
- Load scenario: about 2 million notifications (90 days) and their
  deliveries; query-plan tests for the inbox, the count, the reminder scan

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Cancelling, restoring or moving an activity within 14 days notifies
      the roster (or those called up) and their notifiable guardians once
      per change, in their languages, in-app and by push and email per
      their settings.
- [ ] Reminders go only to those who have not answered a `requested`
      activity, once per activity and person, to people who may answer.
- [ ] Leaders get one late "not coming" notice per activity and person,
      none if the answer changed back within 5 minutes.
- [ ] Call-up changes notify those added and removed.
- [ ] The existing account-bound emails go through the new pipeline with
      unchanged content, plus an inbox entry; invitation emails to
      addresses are unchanged.
- [ ] The inbox, unread count and mark-as-read work on web and mobile;
      another account's IDs give 404; `X-On-Behalf-Of` never shows a
      ward's inbox.
- [ ] Settings per category and channel, quiet hours (held, then sent or
      skipped as obsolete) and lock-screen preview work; mandatory
      channels cannot be turned off.
- [ ] One-click unsubscribe (RFC 8058) from Mailpit works and changes only
      that category.
- [ ] The app registers and removes its push token (login, logout, account
      switch, session ended); `DeviceNotRegistered` disables the device.
- [ ] Without `PUSH_PROVIDER` the server reports no push feature, the app
      and web hide push options, email and inbox work; without `SMTP_URL`
      the inbox still works.
- [ ] Push texts contain no person names (test over every type's template);
      job payloads and logs contain IDs only.
- [ ] Protected identity: tests above pass; inbox text follows a later
      protection change.
- [ ] A redelivered outbox event or a retried job creates no duplicate

- [ ] `expo-notifications` and `expo-server-sdk` pass the licence checks
      (`pnpm licenses:check --mobile`; About screen regenerated).
- [ ] Performance budgets met (query-plan and timing tests on the load
      scenario).
- [ ] Tenant and own-data isolation tests for every new endpoint
- [ ] Recipient-resolution tests for every type (allow and deny)
- [ ] Audit events for settings, devices and unsubscribes
- [ ] Translations complete for sv and en, and nb for the new namespace

## Out of scope
- Notification types for P1.6 events (registrations, deadlines, offers)
  and P1.10 waiting-list offers: added with those packages using this
  catalogue.
- Daily or weekly digests; per-club or per-group muting.
- WebSocket invalidation of the unread count (polling until the gateway
  exists).
- HTML emails, bounce and complaint handling, a hosted transactional email
  provider (only SMTP here).
- SMS and other channels.
- A push relay so self-hosted servers can push to the store app (see
  [#12](https://github.com/SweRavn/ClubAdmin/issues/12), server selection);
  direct APNs/FCM providers.
- Messages written by people (board posts, chat, "message to the team"):
  `messaging` and `chat` modules.
- Leader digests ("who has not answered") and attendance notices.
- Notices for deleted activities.

## Open decisions
1. **Push provider.** Expo Push (worker uses `expo-server-sdk`, MIT; app
   uses `expo-notifications`, MIT) behind `PushProvider`, versus direct
   APNs/FCM. Recommended: Expo Push now, direct providers later behind the
   same interface.
2. **Push on self-hosted installations.** The store app's push credentials
   belong to the project, so a self-hosted server cannot push to it without
   a relay. Recommended: no relay in P1.7; push only for the hosted service
   and self-built apps, degrade to email and inbox otherwise; open an issue
   linked from ADR 0038 and #12.
3. **What push text may contain.** Recommended: never person names, notes
   or addresses; club or group, kind of notice, activity title and time;
   a per-user "show details on the lock screen" switch (default on) that
   makes the text generic when off.
4. **Who gets notices about a ward.** Recommended: full guardians; linked
   people with `receive_messages` (plus `view_schedule` for schedule and
   reminder notices, and `sign_up` for reminders); the ward if they have a
   login; never people without an account.
5. **Default email behaviour for schedule, call-up and reminder notices.**
   Recommended: "only when I have no app with push" (`no_app`), so app
   users are not emailed twice; `always` for administrative and family
   notices.
6. **Quiet hours default.** Recommended: on, 21:00–07:00 in the
   recipient's time zone, push only; held pushes are sent at the end or
   skipped if obsolete; no exemption for same-day cancellations.
7. **Notice horizon and debouncing.** Recommended: activity notices only
   for activities starting within 14 days; 10 minutes debounce for
   activity and series changes, 5 minutes for late declines; reminders
   24 hours before the deadline (or the start), fixed in code, not a club
   setting yet.
8. **Storage model.** Recommended: recipient-owned global tables (ADR 0023)
   with nullable `club_id`, text rendered at read time from `type` and
   `params`, history with `skipInsert`, deliveries excluded from history.
9. **Retention.** Recommended: notifications 90 days, deliveries 30 days,
   devices 90 days after last seen.
10. **Volume budgets.** Recommended: 10 pushes per person per hour, 20
    non-mandatory emails per person per day, configurable by environment;
    over budget only the inbox gets them.
11. **Existing email templates.** Recommended: keep the `email.*` keys and
    content, route account-bound ones through the new pipeline, and amend
    ADR 0032's "moves the templates" consequence rather than renaming keys.
12. **Unsubscribe key.** Recommended: a separate `NOTIFICATION_TOKEN_KEY`
    (not `INVITATION_TOKEN_KEY`), required when `SMTP_URL` is set.
13. **Old address on email change.** Recommended: the worker reads the
    previous address from the record history of `accounts`; alternative:
    a short-lived table written by `changeEmail`.
14. **Mandatory notices.** Recommended: email for `account.email.changed`,
    `club.managers.changed` and `members.exported` cannot be turned off;
    everything else can.
