# P1.3 — Guardians and children: add child, co-guardians, relatives, duplicates, own login, age transitions

## Context
Read first: `CLAUDE.md`, `docs/domain-model.md` ("Children without login, and
giving them one later", "guardianships", "Protected identity", "Age and
child-safety rules", "Field visibility"), ADR 0006/0025 (authorization,
`X-On-Behalf-Of`), ADR 0020 (identifiers and login email), ADR 0022
(protected identity), ADR 0023 (global tables), ADR 0029 (API only grows),
ADR 0032 (transactional email), `docs/security.md`, `docs/gdpr.md`.

What exists already:
- **Services without endpoints (P0.5):** `GuardianshipsService.addChild`,
  `addGuardian` (a full guardian, or a club manager in the ward's club),
  `inviteCoGuardian` (`guardian_invitations`, 14 days, token hash only),
  `acceptInvitation`, `updateRights`, `endGuardianship` (own; relatives by a
  full guardian; another full guardian only by a club manager with
  `allowedBy: "club_manager"`), `guardiansOf`, `wardsOf`.
  `PersonMergeService.merge` / `undo` (`people_merges`, 30-day undo, moves
  the references every module declares in `*.references.ts`).
  `AccountsService.createOwnLoginInvitation` (`account_invitations`; a full
  guardian or a club manager; refuses below the country pack's
  `ownAccountMinAge`) and `acceptOwnLoginInvitation` (the web login carries
  `?invitation=` to the API as `x-clubadmin-invitation`; the new login is
  linked to the existing person).
- **Domain events without handlers:** `guardianship.invited`,
  `account.invited` (no email is sent), `guardianship.ward.adult` (the worker
  task `guardianships:flag_adult_wards` sets `ward_adult_flagged_at` with the
  highest `adultAge` of all packs, issue
  [#6](https://github.com/SweRavn/ClubAdmin/issues/6)).
- **Reads:** `GET /me/wards` (my wards with my rights), `GET /people/:id`
  (guardians with phone numbers for those responsible), acting for a ward
  with `X-On-Behalf-Of` (ADR 0006). Joining (P1.1) already lets a guardian
  apply for a ward.
- **Email (P1.1, ADR 0032):** the worker's `mail` outbox handler, ICU
  templates in the `email` namespace, names through
  `apps/worker/src/presenters/mail-names.ts`.
- **Member register (P1.2):** registrars and managers see guardians per
  member; `PATCH .../membership`.
- Known gap: a club manager's merge only moves rows in clubs the manager can
  see (issue [#5](https://github.com/SweRavn/ClubAdmin/issues/5)).

## Goal
A family manages itself: a parent adds their children without email, invites
the other parent and gives a grandparent view-only access; both parents see
and act for the children in every club. When both parents created the same
child, one of them (or the club) merges the two and can undo it. When a child
is old enough, a guardian gives them their own login with their own email,
and nothing about the child's memberships or history is lost. At the adult
age the young person decides which guardian access continues. Clubs confirm
or add guardianships from paper applications.

A ward can have any number of guardians (one open guardianship per
guardian and ward is the only constraint): nothing in this package may assume
two, and the UI never says "the other parent". Tests include a child with
four guardians.

## Users and roles involved
Guardians (`parent`, `legal_guardian`: full rights; `relative`,
`contact_person`: rights granted by a full guardian), children and young
people (with and without login), club managers and registrars (confirm,
add and end guardianships in their club; merge duplicates of their members),
the email recipients of invitations.

## User stories
- As a parent, I want to add my child (name, birth date, optional personal
  identity number) without an email address, so I can apply for them,
  answer for them and see their schedule.
- As a parent, I want to invite the other parent by email, so they get the
  same rights with their own login; I want to see the invitation is pending,
  resend it or revoke it.
- As the invited parent, I want to accept with my own login (signing up if I
  have none), and see the child at once.
- As a parent, I want to link a relative (a grandparent, an older sibling,
  an aunt) to my child and choose what they may do (see the schedule, get
  messages, sign the child up, see medical notes; acting for the child only
  if I explicitly choose it), so they can help without full guardian rights.
- As a parent, I want to link a contact person (a neighbour or a friend's
  parent who drives to training, a family-home contact) with only what they
  need (by default the schedule and messages), so the club knows who may
  pick the child up and who to reach.
- As a relative or contact person, I want to accept the link with my own
  login (or be added without one, reachable by phone), see exactly what was
  shared with me, and end the link myself.
- As a parent, I want to change what a relative or contact person may do,
  or end their access, at any time.
- As an adult member (or a young person with their own login, from the
  consent age), I want to link a relative or contact person to myself and
  choose what they see (for example a parent keeping my schedule, or an LSS
  contact person or trustee who helps me), and end it myself.
- As a club registrar, I want to record a relative or contact person from a
  paper form (with the guardian's or adult member's written consent noted),
  so pick-up and emergency contacts are in the system.
- As a guardian, I want to end my own guardianship.
- As a parent, I want to be told when we both created the same child, merge
  the two into one and undo it within 30 days if it was a mistake.
- As a club registrar or manager, I want to add a guardianship from a paper
  application (an existing person or a new one without login), confirm or
  end one (another full guardian only as a club manager, with a reason), and
  merge two people in my club who are the same person.
- As a parent, when my child reaches the own-login age, I want to enter
  their email so they can log in themselves, keeping everything.
- As a young person reaching the adult age, I want to choose which of my
  guardians keep access to my account and data.

## Relatives and contact persons
Both are guardianship kinds with chosen rights; neither is a guardian in the
legal sense, and neither ever gets rights by default beyond those listed.
- **Who links them:** a full guardian for a minor; the member themselves if
  adult, or from the country pack's `consentAge` with their own login (a
  full guardian may also link them until the adult age); a registrar or
  club manager from a paper form, with a note that written consent exists
  (`consent_note`, never shown outside the club's managers, the ward and
  their guardians).
- **Linked person:** an existing person reached by an email invitation
  (accepted with their own login), a member of the same club chosen from
  the register by the club, or a new person without login (name, phone)
  added by a guardian or the club. People outside the reach of the one who
  links are never searched for or revealed.
- **Rights, chosen per link:** any subset of `view_schedule`,
  `receive_messages`, `sign_up`, `see_medical` and, only for relatives and
  only when a full guardian (or the adult member) explicitly ticks it with a
  warning, `act_on_behalf`. Defaults: relative `view_schedule`; contact
  person `view_schedule`, `receive_messages`. Each later feature enforces
  the right it needs (schedule P1.4, sign-up P1.5/P1.6, messages P1.7,
  medical notes later); here the rights are stored, shown and tested in
  the relationship set.
- **Visibility:** the linked person sees the ward's name and only what the
  rights allow; they never see the ward's other guardians' contact data
  (only names) unless they are a full guardian themselves. For the club the
  link is visible to managers, registrars and the ward's group leaders (a
  leader needs to know who may pick a child up), like guardians.
- **Ending:** the person who linked, any full guardian (for a minor), the
  adult ward, the linked person themselves, or the club for links it
  recorded. At the adult age, relative and contact-person links of a young
  person follow the same decision as guardians (keep or end).
- **Emergency contacts:** `club_profiles.emergency_contacts` (name, phone,
  relation, no person) stay for people the club only needs to call; a
  contact person is a linked person who may also see or receive things.
- Tests: a child with two parents, a grandparent (`view_schedule` only), a
  contact person without login and an older sibling with `sign_up`; an adult
  member with an LSS contact person; nobody gets `act_on_behalf` without the
  explicit choice; a relative cannot link further people or change rights.

## Data model
No new tables for guardianships, invitations, merges or account invitations
(they exist). Additions:
- `guardian_invitations`: `revoked_at` is set by revoke; resending creates a
  new token (old one invalid) and records `resent_at`. Check the columns in
  `packages/db/src/schema/people.ts`; add only what is missing.
- `guardianships.adult_decision` (`kept`, `ended`, null) and
  `adult_decided_at`: the young person's decision per guardian at the adult
  age (the guardianship is only ended when they choose so). Forward-only
  migration; history and the existing RLS policies cover it.
- `guardianships.consent_note` (text, short; set when the club records a
  relative or contact person from a paper form) and
  `guardianships.granted_by_person_id` where `created_by_person_id` does
  not already say who granted the rights.
- `guardianships.ended_reason` (text, short; required when a club manager
  ends a full guardian's guardianship; never shown outside the club's
  managers and the two guardians).
- Duplicate candidates are computed, not stored: same birth date and
  family name (`people_duplicate_idx`) or the same personal identity number
  hash, among people the actor may reach (a guardian's wards; a club's
  members). No new index unless the plans need one (justify in the PR).
- Adult age per ward (issue #6): the worker uses the country packs of the
  ward's clubs (default country without clubs), not the highest of all.

## API
All under `/api/v1`. People appear only as `PersonSummary` (PersonPresenter).

Existing services do the work; endpoints add authorization, presenters and
problem codes. Expected failures are `DomainError` codes (no values).

## Permissions
New permissions, one policy each, with allow and deny tests and entries in
the permission matrix (tenant isolation: club B gets 404):
- `person.guardians.read` (person): the ward themselves, any active guardian
  of the ward (relatives see the guardians' names only, not invitations).
- `person.guardians.manage` (person): full guardians (`parent`,
  `legal_guardian`) with `act_on_behalf`; for relative and contact-person
  links also the ward themselves when adult (or from `consentAge` with own
  login); never a relative or contact person; never acting on behalf of
  someone else.
- `person.merge` (person): a full guardian of both people; a club manager
  (MFA) for two members of their club (moves only that club's rows plus the
  global ones the service already moves; close or update issue #5).
- `person.own_login.invite` (person): full guardians; club managers and
  registrars of a club the person is a member of.
- `club.guardians.manage` (club): club managers and registrars; ending a
  full guardian's guardianship only club managers with MFA (in the service).

Child-safety rules (tests): a relative never gets `act_on_behalf` by
default; a guardian acting for a ward (`X-On-Behalf-Of`) cannot invite,
merge or give logins; a young person with their own login sees their
guardians but cannot remove a full guardian before the adult age.

## UI (web)
- `/me` "Family" section (or `/me/family`): my wards with age, clubs and my
  rights; "Add child" form; per ward its guardians (badges for kind and
  pending invitations with resend and revoke), "Invite guardian, relative or
  contact person" (email, or a person without login with name and phone;
  kind; rights checkboxes for relatives and contact persons, with a warning
  on "act on behalf"), "Give own login" (shown
  from `ownAccountMinAge`, with the rule explained below it), "End my
  guardianship" with confirmation.
- Duplicate notice: "Elsa Lindqvist may be in ClubAdmin twice" with both
  records side by side (birth date, clubs, groups, guardians) and "Merge";
  after a merge a notice with "Undo" until the deadline.
- `/guardian-invitations/[token]` landing page: who invites to what, log in
  or sign up, accept; expired, revoked and used invitations say so without
  revealing whom they were for.
- "People who help me" on `/me` for adult members: link, change and end
  relatives and contact persons for oneself.
- "Shared with me": for a relative or contact person, each linked person
  with exactly the rights given, and "End".
- Adult-age page for the young person: each guardian with "keep access" or
  "end access", explained in plain words.
- Club side: on a member's profile (P0.7, P1.2) a "Guardians" section for
  `club.guardians.manage` with add (search the register or a new person),
  end (reason for full guardians); `/clubs/[clubId]/duplicates` list with
  merge.
- Loading, empty and error states; translations in sv, en, nb.

Mobile: a read-only "Family" list (wards and their guardians) is in scope;
forms stay web-only for now.

## Emails (worker, ADR 0032)
New templates in the recipient's language: guardian invitation (with the
link; the ward's first name only if not protected), invitation accepted (to
the inviter), own-login invitation (to the young person's address, with the
link to log in or sign up), "your child can now get their own login"
(guardians, once when the child reaches `ownAccountMinAge`, from a daily
worker task), adult age (to the young person if they have a login, else to
the guardians: what happens next). Names through the worker presenter.
Idempotent per event and recipient.

## Privacy and security
- Personal data: children's names, birth dates, personal identity numbers
  (field encryption, never logged), guardians' emails (invitations only;
  never in outbox or job payloads, logs or URLs other than the token link).
- Invitation tokens: 32 random bytes, hash stored, single use, 14 days;
  lookup and accept rate-limited; unknown, expired, revoked and used ones
  are indistinguishable.
- Audit events for every change: child created, invitation sent, resent,
  revoked, accepted, rights changed, guardianship ended (with who and, for a
  club manager, that a reason exists, never the reason text), merge, undo,
  own-login invitation, adult decision.
- A merge never moves rows the actor could not see; undo restores exactly
  what was moved or refuses (`merge_undo_conflict`).
- The adult decision is the young person's alone; the guardian who is
  ended is told by email that access ended (not why).
- Retention: unaccepted invitations deleted 12 months after expiry (extend
  `joining:retention` or a new task); merges keep their record for the undo
  period and then the audit trail.

## Protected identity
A protected child or guardian is shown only to those who may see them
(PersonVisibility). Invitation emails and the landing page never name a
protected ward or inviter. Duplicate detection never reveals a protected
person to someone who may not see them: such pairs are listed only for
people who see both. Guardians of a protected child are prompted, after
adding or accepting, to protect themselves too (link to the existing flag).

## Locale and country packs
All strings and emails in sv, en, nb. `ownAccountMinAge`, `consentAge` and
`adultAge` come from the country pack of the person's clubs (the strictest
when several; the default country without clubs). Personal identity numbers
are validated by the country pack.

## Performance
Families are small (a handful of rows); duplicate detection per guardian
reads only their wards and the co-guardians' wards; per club it uses
`people_duplicate_idx` and the personal identity number hash index and is
paged. `GET /me/wards` and the family page stay within the read budget
(≤ 5 queries, no N+1 over wards).

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Anna adds a child, invites Mikael, Mikael accepts with his own login
      and sees the child in every club; Gunilla gets view-only access and
      cannot apply or answer for Alva.
- [ ] Anna sees the duplicate Elsa, merges, sees the history kept, and
      undoes within the period; a person with a login is never merged away.
- [ ] A guardian gives Vera her own login; Vera signs up with that email and
      keeps her memberships and history; below the age it is refused.
- [ ] Sara changes Gunilla's rights (relative of Alva) and links a contact
      person without login (schedule and messages) to Alva; the rights are
      shown and are what the relationship set holds; neither can act for
      Alva unless Sara ticks it; Gunilla ends her own link.
- [ ] An adult member links an LSS contact person to themselves and ends
      it; a registrar records a contact person from a paper form with a
      consent note.
- [ ] A registrar adds a guardian from a paper application; a club manager
      (MFA) ends a full guardian's guardianship with a reason; another
      guardian cannot.
- [ ] At the adult age the young person keeps one guardian and ends the
      other; the adult age follows the ward's clubs' country packs (#6).
- [ ] Emails sent for invitations, acceptance, own login, the own-login
      age and the adult age, in the recipient's language; none names a

## Out of scope
- Importing members or families from files, adding members by hand, and the
  `step_parent` and `foster_parent` kinds (P1.3b).
- Notification preferences, push and in-app notices (P1.7): emails only.
- Consents given by guardians (P1.8).
- Protected identity UI beyond the prompt to protect oneself (P1.12).
- Mobile forms (read-only family list only).
