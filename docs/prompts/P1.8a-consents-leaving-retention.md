# P1.8a — Privacy I: consents, leaving a club, the club's privacy policy and retention

P1.8 is split in two. **P1.8a** (this prompt): consents, leaving a club
yourself, the club's privacy settings and privacy policy (decided by the
board), the settings and retention registry, the former-member purge, the
erasure of long-orphaned people and audit retention. **P1.8b**
([`P1.8b-export-and-erasure.md`](P1.8b-export-and-erasure.md)): "download
my data", account deletion and the erasure of wards. The decisions of both
are recorded in ADR 0041, which this package writes.

## Goal

A parent decides, per child and club and separately for each use (photos,
video, streaming, marketing, sharing with the federation, and whether the
family's contact details are visible to the rest of the team or the
club), reads exactly the text they agree to, and withdraws it again with
one tap; a teenager from the consent age decides for themselves. A member
leaves a club themselves and sees what ends, what the club keeps and for
how long, and what must be settled first. Each club has a privacy policy
that members can read, built from a template and the club's settings; the
privacy settings change only with a recorded decision by the club's board,
and the policy shows which decision each value rests on. The retention
periods in `gdpr.md` are enforced by nightly jobs, and the documentation
lists the same rules as the code.

## Users and roles involved

Members with their own login (their own consents, leaving); full
guardians (`parent`, `legal_guardian`) with `act_on_behalf` (consents for
wards below the consent age, leaving for minors); linked guardians
(relatives, step and foster parents, contact persons) never; young people
from the consent age (their own consents); registrars (record a consent
given on a paper form, see consent status); club managers (record board
decisions on privacy settings, consent texts, with MFA); group leaders and
owners (consent status of their group); every member (the club's privacy
policy); the worker as system and as `clubadmin_retention`; platform
operators (no access to club data; jobs' metrics only).

## User stories

- As a guardian, I want to see per child and club which consents the club
  asks for, with the club's text, and give or withdraw each one
  separately, so that I decide what is published about my child.
- As a parent, I want to choose whether my phone number is visible to the
  other families in my child's team, and separately to everyone in the
  club, so that coaches and parents can reach me only as far as I want.
- As a 14-year-old with my own login, I want to give or withdraw my own
  photo consent, and see that my parent gave one before I turned 13.
- As a registrar, I want to record a consent given on the paper membership
  form, with a note, so that the register is complete.
- As a coach, I want to see which players in my group lack photo consent
  before the cup, without seeing anyone I am not allowed to see.
- As a member or guardian, I want to leave a club myself, see what ends
  now and what the club keeps for how long, and be told plainly when
  something must be settled first.
- As a club manager, I want to record the board's decision when it changes
  how long we keep former members' data or which consents we ask for, so
  that our privacy policy and the system say the same thing as the
  minutes.
- As a member, I want to read my club's privacy policy and see when and by
  whom each rule was decided, and be told when it changes.
- As a former member, I want the club to stop holding my contact details
  once the retention period after I left has passed.
- As the maintainer, I want every retention rule in `gdpr.md` to have a
  job and every job to be in `gdpr.md`, checked by CI.

## Data model

Club-owned tables: `club_id`, tenant RLS, record history, `updated_at`
(CLAUDE.md rule 3). Global tables: RLS with `app.*` helpers (ADR 0023).
Insider read policies use the array form of `app.related_club_ids()` (ADR
0040). Person references in `privacy.references.ts` (merging covers them).
One migration (0020); expand only.

- **Consent purposes (expand `CONSENT_PURPOSES`, ADR 0029: enums grow):**
  the five existing purposes plus `contacts_group` (the person's contact
  details are visible to the other members of their groups and their
  guardians) and `contacts_club` (visible to every insider of the club).
  Each purpose is given and withdrawn on its own. For a ward the contact
  purposes are about the **guardians' own** contact details as shown on
  the ward's card: each full guardian decides for themselves (a guardian
  never consents for another adult's phone); the ward's own contact
  purposes are decided like the other purposes. Contact details here means
  the fields the person shared with the club (`shared_fields`: phone,
  address); the consent widens who sees them, never what the club holds.
  Without the consent the current field visibility applies unchanged.
- **`consents` (expand):** `source` (`self`, `guardian`, `club_record`;
  existing rows backfilled from `given_by_person_id`), `club_note` (max 500
  characters, `club_record` only; never in audit events), `revoked_reason`
  (`withdrawn`, `left_club`, `superseded`, `erased`, `not_applicable`; may
  grow). `text_version` now names a published text: `default:<n>` or
  `club:<n>`. Rows are never deleted by the app roles (revoke DELETE from
  `clubadmin_app`), only by the former-member purge. Index
  `consents_club_purpose_idx (club_id, purpose, person_id) WHERE revoked_at
IS NULL` for group overviews and the field-visibility check.
- **Platform default texts:** `packages/shared/src/consent-texts.ts`: per
  purpose the current default version and the message key of each version
  (`consents.texts.photo_publication.v1`, …) in sv, en and nb. A released
  version is never edited (unit test against a committed hash list).
- **`consent_texts` (club-owned):** a club's own text for a purpose:
  `purpose`, `version` (per club and purpose, from 1), `texts` (JSONB `{
sv?, en?, nb? }`, 20–4000 characters each, at least the club's default
  locale), `published_by_person_id`, `published_at`. Unique `(club_id,
purpose, version)`. Immutable (UPDATE and DELETE revoked from the app
  roles). History.
- **`clubs` (expand, defaults keep rows valid):** `consent_purposes`
  (text[], default `{photo_publication, video_publication}`),
  `consent_reconfirm_on_new_version` (boolean, default false),
  `former_member_retention_months` (integer, default 24, check 6–120),
  `privacy_contact_email` (text, nullable: where members send privacy
  questions; shown in the policy; not personal data of a member by design,
  the form suggests a function address).
- **Settings registry (`packages/shared/src/club-settings.ts`):** the
  club settings registry of ADR 0037, started here. Every club setting has
  a stable key (never renamed), a type, limits (system; country packs may
  narrow them), its column, and **`decidedBy`**: `board` for the settings
  the club's board decides (every retention period, the consent purposes
  asked, reconfirming on a new text version) or `manager` for operational
  ones (the attendance lock, group and event defaults, the privacy
  contact). Every value is written only through
  `ClubsService.updateSettings`, validated against the registry. The
  retention part of the registry (`packages/shared/src/retention.ts`) lists
  every retention rule once: key, the data it covers, default and limits,
  the setting key if configurable, the job that enforces it, and whether it
  is implemented (`planned` for chat, accounting, background checks,
  restorable club profiles). A docs test checks it against the `gdpr.md`
  table and the worker's task list.
- **`club_setting_decisions` (club-owned):** the record that the board
  decided settings: `body` (`board`, `annual_meeting`, `extra_meeting`),
  `decided_on` (date), `reference` (the minutes' paragraph, e.g. "§ 7",
  max 200 characters), `effective_from` (date, default the decision date,
  never before it), `settings` (JSONB key → value, only `board` settings),
  `recorded_by_person_id`, `recorded_at`. Immutable (a wrong record is
  corrected by a new decision). Index `(club_id, effective_from DESC)`.
  History. A decision whose `effective_from` is in the future is applied
  by the daily `clubs:apply_decisions` job; one effective today is applied
  in the recording transaction. Phase 2 (ADR 0037) links a decision to
  adopted minutes instead of a typed reference; the table stays.
- **`club_privacy_policies` (club-owned):** each published version of the
  club's policy: `version` (from 1), `template_version` (the country
  pack's template), `settings` (JSONB snapshot of the values the policy
  shows), `decision_id` (nullable: the first version, from the defaults,
  has none), `effective_from`. Unique `(club_id, version)`. Immutable.
  A new version is published when a decision takes effect, the template
  changes, or the club asks for a different set of consents. History.
- **Privacy policy template:** per country pack (`se` first; `no` falls
  back to the platform template), versioned like the consent texts, in sv,
  en and nb, with placeholders for registry keys (`{{former_member_retention_months}}`),
  the consent purposes asked, the club's name, organisation number and
  `privacy_contact_email`, and the platform as processor. Rendered when
  read in the reader's language; the stored version is the snapshot. The
  text is reviewed for plain language before release (a checklist item,
  not legal advice).
- **`club_profiles` (expand):** `club_data_purged_at` (timestamptz). Index
  `club_memberships_left_idx (club_id, valid_to) WHERE status = 'left'`.
- **`people` (expand):** `orphaned_since` (timestamptz, nullable): set by
  the retention job when a person without login has no relationship to
  any club and no open guardianship; cleared when one appears. Partial
  index `WHERE orphaned_since IS NOT NULL`.
- **Former-member purge (`privacy.purge_former_member(club, person)`,
  run as `clubadmin_retention`):** for a person with no open relationship
  in the club (membership, role, group function, employment, ownership,
  pending join request, open waiting-list entry or event registration)
  whose last membership ended more than `former_member_retention_months`
  ago: clear `club_profiles.shared_fields`, `emergency_contacts` and
  `notes` (member number, membership dates and type, group memberships and
  roles stay as minimised facts for annual and grant reports;
  `protected_identity_club` stays); delete the person's consents in the
  club; clear the notes on their answers, `join_requests.message`/
  `decision_message`, waiting-list notes and their event registrations'
  answers in the club; scrub those values in the club's history rows; set
  `club_data_purged_at`; audit `person.club_data.purged` (field names).
- **Orphaned people:** a person **without login** whose every club's data
  is purged and who has no open guardianship gets `orphaned_since`; after
  `ORPHAN_RETENTION_MONTHS` (an installation setting, default 36, at least 12) they are erased with `privacy.erase_person(..., 'retention')`.
  Within that time a new relationship (a club adds them again, a guardian
  links them) clears `orphaned_since` and they keep their identity.
  **Accounts with a login are never erased automatically**; they stay
  until the person deletes them (P1.8b; dormant accounts are a later
  issue).
- **`privacy.erase_person` (CREATE OR REPLACE in 0020; never edit an
  earlier migration):** start from the 0017 version and additionally clear
  `join_requests.message` and `decision_message` of the person's requests,
  `group_waitlist_entries.applicant_note` and `owner_note`,
  `guardianships.consent_note` and `ended_reason` where the person is ward
  or guardian, club `invitations` whose `email` was the person's account
  email, unaccepted `guardian_invitations` and `account_invitations` to
  that email, `club_setting_decisions.recorded_by_person_id` stays (an ID,
  shown as "Deleted user"); revoke open consents (`erased`); scrub the same
  keys in the history; extend `privacy.unerased_people()`. Grants for
  `clubadmin_retention` on exactly the columns it changes. The ledger
  replay test covers the new tables.

`request-budgets.integration.test.ts` (statements and pages; the same
after adding the load clubs).

and the group member list show a person's shared phone (and for a ward the

`contacts_club`; the check reads `consents_club_purpose_idx` once per

## Permissions

New permissions in `packages/shared/src/permissions.ts`, one policy each
in `authorization.policy.ts`, allow and deny tests, entries in the
permission matrix (a user of club B gets 404 for club A):

- `person.consents.read` (person, `?clubId`): the person; full guardians
  with `act_on_behalf`; club managers and registrars; owners and leaders
  of the person's groups (status only). Not members of the same group,
  not relatives, not support access.
- `person.consents.manage` (person, `?clubId`): the person from the
  club's country pack `consentAge`; full guardians with `act_on_behalf`
  (giving only while the ward is under `consentAge`, withdrawing until
  `adultAge`; the contact purposes about their own details always);
  registrars and club managers (`club_record` with a note, and withdrawing
  on request). Never relatives, step or foster parents, contact persons or
  leaders. With `X-On-Behalf-Of` only that ward.
- `group.consents.read` (group): the group's leaders and owners (also of
  parent groups), club managers, registrars.
- Existing `club.settings.update` (club managers, with MFA) covers
  decisions and consent texts; existing `club.read` the privacy page, the
  policy and the decisions: every member may read the club's rules.
- Tests: a 12-year-old with a login cannot give a consent but their
  parent can; at 13 they can, and the parent can still withdraw; Gunilla
  (relative) gets 403; Mikael cannot consent to Anna's phone being shown;
  a coach of F2017 sees status for F2017 but 404 for a member of Herr
  senior he does not lead; a user of IKN gets 404 for SVK's consents,
  decisions and policy.

## Leaving a club

`POST /me/clubs/:clubId/leave` runs in one tenant transaction:
`MembershipsService.leaveClub` (membership and group memberships; the
person holds no assignments any more, see the blockers), withdraws pending join requests, open
waiting-list entries and registrations to coming events in the club,
revokes open consents (`left_club`), audits `membership.ended` with
`changedFields` and on-behalf-of, and publishes `membership.changed`
(authorization cache; the notifications handler sends `membership.left`).
Guardianships are global and stay. The club keeps the profile and the
history until `former_member_retention_months` after the end, then the
purge minimises it. Adults leave themselves; a minor's full guardians
leave for them. Rejoining within the period keeps the member number and
profile.

**Blockers** (shown in the preview with what to do, checked again in the
transaction):

- `roles_must_be_removed`: the person holds an **assignment** in the
  club: a club role (manager, registrar, treasurer, a board role, …), a
  leader function in a group (any non-member function) or the ownership of
  a group. **Leaving is refused** until every assignment has ended (decided
  2026-11-01: you do not leave a club with open assignments). The preview
  lists them with the group or role and who can end them. The person can
  step down themselves (`DELETE /me/clubs/:clubId/assignments/:id`,
  below); club managers and group owners end them as today. Stepping down
  from the last club manager role is refused (`last_club_manager`: hand
  over first, ADR 0018). Memberships ended by the club (registrars and
  managers, P1.2) keep ending assignments with them, as today: that is the
  club's own decision.
- `outstanding_invoices`: the person (or, for a ward, the payer) has
  invoices from the club that are neither paid nor written off. **Leaving
  is refused altogether** until the club's treasurer has settled or
  written them off. No module issues invoices to members yet: the
  privacy module defines an `OutstandingObligations` interface
  (`forPerson(tx, clubId, personId)` returning counts and the role to
  contact, never amounts or texts) that modules register (Nest multi
  provider); none is registered in this package, a test registers a fake
  one, and the finance module (member fees, Phase 3) registers itself.

**"Remove my contact details from the club now"** (`removeNow`, off by
default): the former-member purge runs for this person and club at once
instead of after the period (only the facts the purge keeps remain).
Refused like leaving (above).

**Stepping down from an assignment** (`DELETE
/me/clubs/:clubId/assignments/:id`, `@RecentAuth(15)`): ends one of the
caller's own club roles, leader functions or group ownerships (or a
ward's, for a full guardian with `act_on_behalf`) in one tenant
transaction, audited (`club.role.ended`, `group.membership.ended` with the
function, `group.ownership.ended`, as the existing services do) and
published (`membership.changed`); the club managers (and for a group the
other owners) get the notice `assignment.ended` (category `club_admin`).
Refused with `last_club_manager` for the last manager. Stepping down is
also offered on its own, outside leaving (on `/me/privacy` and the
person's club page).

## The club's privacy policy and board decisions

- Every privacy setting with `decidedBy: board` changes only through a
  recorded decision (`POST /clubs/:clubId/setting-decisions`): a club
  manager with MFA records the body, date, the minutes' reference and the
  effective date. ClubAdmin records the decision; it does not judge
  whether the meeting followed the statutes (ADR 0037). The change, the
  decision ID and the recorder are audited (`club.settings.decided`), and
  `club.settings.updated` names the decision as the reason.
- A new policy version is published in the same transaction (or on the
  effective date) and all members with a login (and full guardians of
  member wards) get the notice `club.privacy_policy.updated`; the policy
  page shows "Decided by the board on 12 September 2026, § 7" next to each
  value and "Default value, not yet decided by the board" for defaults.
- Clubs that turn on governing documents in Phase 2 take these values
  from adopted minutes instead (ADR 0037); this package's decisions are
  the manual form of the same record.

## Retention jobs

New worker tasks (registered in `tasks/index.ts`, crontab and the
`architecture.md` table; payloads IDs only; logs counts per club ID):

| Task                    | Schedule        | Does                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| ----------------------- | --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `privacy:retention`     | daily 05:31 UTC | Per club: former members past `former_member_retention_months` (`privacy.purge_former_member`, batches of 500); then marks orphaned people without login (`orphaned_since`) and erases those orphaned longer than `ORPHAN_RETENTION_MONTHS` |
| `audit:retention`       | daily 05:43 UTC | Deletes `audit.events` older than `AUDIT_RETENTION_MONTHS` (installation setting, default 24, at least 12), in batches of 10,000 by a new `created_at` index                                                                                |
| `clubs:apply_decisions` | daily 00:17 UTC | Applies board decisions whose `effective_from` is today (per club time zone) and publishes the policy version                                                                                                                               |

The worker gets a second database handle for `DATABASE_RETENTION_URL`
(`deps.retentionDb`, used only by `privacy:retention` and
`audit:retention`, and by P1.8b's erasure); without it these jobs log an
error and do nothing, and the worker's `/health/ready` reports it. One
transaction at a time per job step (ADR 0040).

## Erasure, backups and ransomware

Backups cannot be changed (object lock), so erasure and purges never
touch them: a person erased or purged today stays in the backups taken
before, encrypted and unreadable without the backup keys, until those
backups expire (35 days, monthly ones 12 months; `gdpr.md`, "Backups").
Whenever a backup is restored, the deletion ledger (IDs of erased people,
kept outside the backups and exported next to them) is replayed before the
database is used, so erased people are erased again. This is the accepted
way to honour erasure with immutable backups (the data is "beyond use"
until it expires) and is stated in `gdpr.md` and the privacy policy
template. Retention and erasure must not weaken the protection of the
backups (`backup-restore.md`): no job deletes or rewrites backups; erasure of
restored data happens through the deletion ledger replay, which this
package's new columns join (replay test). The ledger export goes to the
same object-locked backup target as the database backups (check it in
`ops:verify-backup` and `ops:object-lock`; add the check if missing).
Gaps found while doing this (object storage replica without object lock,
the audit stream to separate append-only storage) are written down as
issues labelled `security` and linked from `backup-restore.md`; they are
not fixed in this package.

## Privacy and security

- Legal bases (record in `gdpr.md` and `domain-model.md`): consents are
  Art. 6(1)(a) (Art. 8 for minors through the country pack's consent age);
  leaving is the person's right to end the relationship (the club's
  retention after it is storage limitation, Art. 5(1)(e)); the
  former-member period exists for re-joining and annual and grant reports
  (legitimate interest, documented per rule in the registry); the orphan
  period exists so that a person a club or guardian adds again keeps their
  identity (legitimate interest, limited by the installation's maximum).
- Withdrawal is as easy as giving (one action) and takes effect at once:
  publishes `consent.changed` (IDs and purpose), the register's
  missing-consent filter and the field visibility follow on the next
  request.
- Audit events (register in `audit-actions.ts`), in the same transaction:
  `consent.given`, `consent.withdrawn`, `consent.recorded` (never the note),
  `consent_text.published`, `club.settings.decided`,
  `club.privacy_policy.published`, `membership.ended` (existing),
  `person.club_data.purged`, `person.erased` (existing). Each with actor
  and on-behalf-of.
- Notification types (catalogue, `notify.*` messages in sv, en, nb;
  recipient tests per type in the worker): `assignment.ended` (category
  `club_admin`, to club managers and for a group its other owners),
  `membership.left` (category
  `club_admin`, to club managers and registrars; names through the
  presenter, never a protected member to someone who may not see them),
  `club.privacy_policy.updated` (category `membership`, to members and
  full guardians of member wards; not mandatory).
- Personal data never in logs, job payloads or outbox events.
- `clubadmin_retention` gets exactly the grants it needs; a test asserts
  the app role still cannot call `privacy.erase_person` or delete from
  `consents`, `audit.events` or the history.

## Protected identity

- Consent lists name only people the viewer may see; protected members are
  counted and never named for others.
- Photo, video, streaming and both contact purposes are not applicable to
  a protected person (effective flag in that club): the UI shows "Not
  photographed, streamed or listed (protected identity)" and the API
  refuses `consent_not_applicable`; existing consents are revoked
  (`not_applicable`) when the flag is set, in the protection change's
  transaction. Marketing and federation sharing stay possible.
- A guardian's `contacts_group` never makes a protected ward visible: the
  contact is shown on the ward's card, and a protected ward has no card for
  those who may not see them.

## Decisions (made 2026-11-01)

1. Consents are given per purpose; the purposes are photo, video,
   streaming, marketing, federation sharing, and contact details visible
   to the team and to the club.
2. Between the consent age and the adult age the young person decides;
   guardians may only withdraw; a guardian's earlier consent stays until
   withdrawn.
3. Former-member period: default 24 months, 6–120, decided by the board;
   memberships, groups, roles and the member number stay as facts.
4. People without login and without any relationship are **not** erased
   at once: they stay `ORPHAN_RETENTION_MONTHS` (default 36) in case a
   club or guardian links them again; accounts with a login are never
   erased automatically.
5. Club notes are not part of self-service exports (P1.8b).
6. Club-specific consent texts are optional, immutable versions;
   reconfirming is the club's (board's) choice, default off.
7. Audit retention is an installation setting (24 months, at least 12).
8. Only full guardians leave for a minor; adults leave themselves.
9. Leaving is refused while the person holds assignments (club roles,
   leader functions, group ownerships) or has outstanding invoices; the
   person steps down first (or the club ends them). "Remove now" is the
   person's choice when leaving.
10. Privacy settings are board decisions recorded in ClubAdmin and shown
    in the club's privacy policy; changing them without a decision is
    refused.
11. Restorable club profiles are deferred.
12. "Member left" and "the privacy policy changed" are notices of the P1.7
    pipeline, not separate emails.
